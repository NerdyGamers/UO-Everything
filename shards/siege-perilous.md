# Siege Perilous

Siege Perilous is the hardcore-ruleset production shard: one character per account, no Trammel, no item insurance, and harsher consequences everywhere. It is the home of UO's most veteran PvP and survivalist community.

## How it works

Siege runs a Felucca-ruleset world with no Trammel facet, so danger is universal. Each account may have only one character on Siege. There is no item insurance — everything you carry can be looted — and travel is restricted compared to production shards (recall and gate travel are limited or unavailable; community recollection on the exact restrictions, so verify in-game). Skill gain is slower than on production shards, and the death penalty is steeper.

The result is a shard where every trip out of town is a risk calculation: players travel light, bank often, and build tight-knit guilds for protection. The population is small but famously dedicated, and Siege has its own economy, events, and long-running rivalries distinct from the [production shards](official-shards.md).

## Era notes

Siege Perilous dates to the early 2000s as the answer for players who wanted the pre-Trammel danger level. Its ruleset has been tuned over the years but the core identity — one character, no insurance, no safe facet — has never changed.

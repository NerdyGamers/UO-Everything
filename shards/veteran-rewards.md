# Veteran Rewards

Veteran rewards are account-age perks: the older a subscription account, the more reward picks it earns, claimed separately on each shard.

## How it works

Reward eligibility is based on the account's age in years. Each year of continuous account age unlocks additional reward picks (community documentation places the tiers at yearly intervals, with the top tiers reaching well past a decade of age). Rewards are claimed per shard — an account's picks can be redeemed on every production shard it plays, not just one.

The reward list spans practical and decorative picks. Notable categories include ethereal mounts (rideable ethereal creatures), house decoration, dye tubs and special dyes, statuettes, and various veteran-only items that have accreted over the years. Some rewards are one-per-account choices that define a character's look or convenience for years; others are consumable or decorative.

Veteran rewards are a loyalty system: they cost nothing beyond the subscription, and older accounts visibly carry their tenure in the rewards they've claimed. The exact reward list and tier breakpoints have been extended over time as the game aged (verify the current list against a current reference).

## Era notes

The veteran reward system dates to the early 2000s and has grown with the game — each era's account-age milestones added new picks, so the oldest accounts hold rewards newer players cannot obtain.

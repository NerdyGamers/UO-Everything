# Shard Transfer Tokens

Shard transfer tokens move a character — and everything they carry — from one production shard to another. They are the official way to relocate without starting over.

## How it works

A transfer token is consumed from the Ultima Store (or equivalent) and applied to a character, which is then moved to the destination shard of your choice. The character keeps their skills, stats, and belongings: bank box contents and worn/carried items transfer with them. What does not transfer: houses and everything in them (sell or pack up first), player vendors, and anything tied to the origin shard.

Transfers have restrictions — young characters, characters with certain flags, and transfers involving the [Siege Perilous](siege-perilous.md) ruleset are limited or excluded (community recollection on the exact current restrictions; check the store listing before buying). Transfers are one-way per token, and each token moves one character.

## Era notes

Paid transfers replaced the older shard-shield system. The token model has been stable for years, with the store as the single source.

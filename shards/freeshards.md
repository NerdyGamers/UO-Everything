# Freeshards

Freeshards are player-run Ultima Online servers, operated independently of Broadsword's official shards, each with its own ruleset, community, and administration.

## How it works

A freeshard is a complete UO server run by players or community groups rather than the official operator. Most are built on open-source server emulators — RunUO historically, and ServUO as its actively maintained successor — which reimplement the server side of the game and let operators script custom behavior.

Rulesets vary enormously. Some freeshards aim for era accuracy, recreating the game as it played in a specific period (the Second Age, Renaissance, and similar milestones are popular targets). Others are custom: altered skill systems, new landmasses, custom items, accelerated progression, or entirely original mechanics. Population ranges from a handful of friends to thousands of active players, and administration quality, uptime, and longevity vary just as widely.

Freeshards exist in a gray area: they are unofficial and unaffiliated with Broadsword, and operators run them at their own risk. Players choose them for the customized experience, the era-specific gameplay, or the community — and accept the tradeoffs of unofficial administration.

## Era notes

Player-run servers have existed since the late 1990s, with the emulator scene maturing through the 2000s. The freeshard landscape shifts constantly as servers open, merge, and close.

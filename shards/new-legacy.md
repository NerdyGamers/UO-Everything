# New Legacy

New Legacy is Broadsword's seasonal fresh-start ruleset for Ultima Online: a separate shard where everyone starts over, progression is accelerated, and the world wipes between seasons.

## How it works

New Legacy runs as its own shard, distinct from the production shards. Each season begins with a fresh start — every character begins from nothing — and ends with a wipe, after which the next season begins anew. Progression is deliberately accelerated compared to production: skill gains come faster, so characters reach competitive templates within a single season.

The ruleset also differs from production in design, not just pacing. Systems are tuned or reworked for the seasonal format, and each season can introduce ruleset changes — for example, Chivalry was added to the New Legacy ruleset for its second season. Because seasons are self-contained experiments, the exact rules evolve from season to season (confirm the current season's rules against the official announcements).

## Era notes

New Legacy is a Broadsword-era initiative (2020s), part of the ongoing effort to give returning and new players a level playing field. Seasons are announced with a soft-launch or beta period ahead of the full launch.

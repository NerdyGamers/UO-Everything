# Official Shards

Ultima Online's production shards are the live, official servers run by Broadsword. They share one ruleset — what differs is the community, the economy, and the population.

## How it works

Each shard is a full copy of the game world: the same facets, the same systems, the same publishes. Characters, houses, and items live on one shard and do not move without a [shard transfer](shard-transfers.md). The shard list spans regions — North America (Atlantic, Baja, Catskills, Chesapeake, Great Lakes, Lake Austin, Lake Superior, Legends, Napa Valley, Pacific, Sonoma, Origin), Europe (Drachenfels, Europa), and Oceania — with Atlantic consistently the most populous and the hub of the rares and high-end trade economy.

Because the rules are identical everywhere, shard choice comes down to people: guild presence, event activity, housing availability, and market depth. Smaller shards often have tighter communities and cheaper housing plots; Atlantic has the deepest markets and the most contested content. [Siege Perilous](siege-perilous.md) is the exception — a separate hardcore ruleset — and [New Legacy](new-legacy.md) runs as its own seasonal ruleset.

## Era notes

The shard list has shrunk over the decades as populations consolidated; several shards were merged or retired in the 2000s and 2010s. Atlantic's dominance as the trade hub is a long-standing community fact rather than an official designation.

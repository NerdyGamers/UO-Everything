# Keeps and Castles

Keeps and castles are the classic large pre-built houses — the biggest fixed designs in the game, and enduring status symbols. They predate [custom housing](custom-housing.md) and remain the anchors of many player neighborhoods.

## How it works

- **Keep** — a large multi-room fortress with thick walls and a compact footprint for its interior space. A classic guild hall and veteran home.
- **Castle** — the largest house in the game, sprawling across multiple courtyards with room for vendors, gardens, and events.

Both are bought as deeds from realtors and placed like other houses (see [house basics](housing-basics.md)), but they demand very large valid plots, and suitable land is scarce — especially for castles. Deeds are among the most expensive in the game (prices are economy-dependent; community recollection).

Keeps and castles cannot be redesigned in the custom house editor; their layouts are fixed. What they offer instead is scale and prestige: huge lockdown and secure counts, room for full vendor rows, and instant recognizability. Guilds prize them as headquarters, and long-standing player towns tend to grow up around them.

## Era notes

Keeps and castles date to the launch-era housing system. Custom housing later gave players flexible alternatives, but the fixed classics kept their prestige and their plots.

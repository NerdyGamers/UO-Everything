# House Decay

Houses stay standing only while their owner stays active: every house must be periodically refreshed, and a house left unrefreshed passes through decay stages until it collapses — the famous IDOC, "in danger of collapsing."

## How it works

A house is **refreshed** when its owner (or a co-owner/friend with access) opens its door — each visit resets the decay timer. If nobody visits, the house enters decay stages: first it shows wear, then it is officially flagged **in danger of collapsing**, and finally it falls. The full timeline from fresh to fallen is long (community recollection commonly cites roughly three months, varying by era), which is why IDOC-watching is a pastime: players scout for decaying houses and gather at the fall to grab what spills.

When a house collapses, its contents — everything locked down, secured, or left in containers — drop to the ground where anyone can take them. This is why IDOC events draw crowds and PvP. Owners going on break hand co-ownership to a trusted friend to keep the refresh cycle going, or pack up with a [moving crate](moving.md) first.

## Era notes

Decay has existed since player housing launched; the stages and timers have been tuned across publishes, and some shards run different decay rules. The IDOC scene is a long-standing part of the game's culture.

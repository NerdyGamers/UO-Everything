# Custom Housing

Custom houses let players design their own homes wall by wall instead of picking a pre-built design. The house designer is a full in-game editor for plots, and most player homes in the modern game are custom-built.

## How it works

Custom plots are bought as deeds in various sizes, then placed with the house placement tool like any other house (see [house basics](housing-basics.md)). Entering design mode opens the editor: the owner places and moves walls, floors, stairs, roofs, doors, windows, teleporters, and other components on a grid, floor by floor.

Every component costs gold, and the designer shows a running total before anything is final. **Committing** the design builds the house and charges the cost; the owner can re-enter design mode later to remodel, paying for the new components. Designs can be saved, reverted, and tweaked freely before committing.

Custom houses support everything classic houses do: lockdowns, secures, vendors, house teleporter tiles, and [addons](house-addons.md). Storage, lockdown, and secure limits scale with the house's size, so larger plots hold more.

## Era notes

Customizable housing arrived in the early 2000s (community recollection on the exact publish), replacing the fixed-design-only era. Later publishes expanded the component palette — new wall, roof, and stair styles, plus goza mats and other decorative pieces.

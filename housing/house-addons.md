# House Addons

House addons are multi-tile fixtures — crafting stations, beds, teleporters, water troughs — placed from deeds and locked down like furniture, but bigger and often functional.

## How it works

Buy an addon deed (from NPCs, crafters, or event rewards), double-click it inside your house, and target the floor: the addon assembles as a fixed multi-tile object. Many are functional: a **forge** lets blacksmiths smelt and repair nearby, a **loom** and spinning wheel serve tailors, an **oven** serves cooks, and a **pentagram** or **dartboard** is pure decoration. **Beds** let owners log out in the house; **teleporters** link two spots (both must be on the same account's houses, per community documentation); **water troughs** hold water for cooking and alchemy.

Addons count against the house's lockdown/secure limits and can be re-deeded (picked back up into deed form) if you rearrange. Craftable addons come from [carpentry](../skills/crafting/carpentry.md), [tinkering](../skills/crafting/tinkering.md), and masonry-type crafts; rare and holiday addons (snowy trees, haunted furniture) are collector pieces. Placement needs clear floor space — the deed shows the footprint before you commit.

## Era notes

Addons arrived with the customizable-housing era and grew with each expansion's craftables and holiday gifts. Teleporter rules in particular have been tightened over the years.

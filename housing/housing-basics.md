# House Basics

Placing a house in Ultima Online starts with a deed and a patch of valid land. Houses are persistent structures that give characters secure storage, a recall point, and a place in the world.

## How it works

House deeds are bought from realtor NPCs (or from other players via [vendors](player-vendors.md)). Using a house placement tool shows a ghost preview of the house's footprint: land is valid only if it is relatively flat, free of trees, rocks, and other obstructions, and outside no-housing zones such as towns, dungeons, and guard-post areas.

Classic pre-built houses come in fixed designs: small houses, log cabins, sandstone and marble patios, two-story houses, small and large towers — and at the top end, [keeps and castles](keeps-and-castles.md). Each deed specifies the house type and its footprint.

Every house has a **house sign** mounted outside. The sign shows the owner's name, the house's name (set by the owner), whether the house is public or private, and its condition — including whether it is in danger of collapsing (see [house decay](house-decay.md)).

Once placed, the owner can lock down items, set secures, place vendors, and control access. Houses can be resized, transferred to another character, or demolished by the owner.

## Era notes

Player housing dates to the game's launch. The placement tool and the "in danger of collapsing" system came later, replacing the earliest manual deed-targeting and adding the decay cycle that frees abandoned plots.

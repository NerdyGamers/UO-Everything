# House Security

House security controls who can enter a house, use its doors, and touch its contents. Every house has an owner, an access list, and a ban list, and the settings distinguish public houses from private ones.

## How it works

Access levels, from most to least privileged:

- **Owner** — full control: transfer or demolish the house, add co-owners, set friends and bans, place and remove vendors and lockdowns.
- **Co-Owner** — can add and remove friends, ban and eject players, and manage lockdowns and vendors, but cannot transfer or demolish the house.
- **Friend** — can enter a private house, open doors, and use items the owner allows, but cannot remove lockdowns or change the house.

A **public** house lets anyone walk in; a **private** house admits only the owner, co-owners, and friends. The **ban list** ejects named players on sight and bars re-entry — the owner's tool against thieves and griefers. Individual doors can also be locked to specific access levels, so a public shop can still keep its stockroom private.

Friends can use containers and crafting stations per the owner's settings, but only the owner and co-owners can lift lockdowns or secure new items. Vendor rental is governed separately (see [player vendors](player-vendors.md)).

## Era notes

The owner/co-owner/friend tiers and ban lists are long-standing systems, refined across publishes as housing exploits were closed.

# Moving House

Moving house packs your entire plot — structure, lockdowns, and secures — into a moving crate so you can relocate to a new plot without losing everything.

## How it works

From the house sign, choose the move option: the house is demolished and everything in it (locked down, secured, and loose items, up to the crate's limits) is packed into a **moving crate** that lands in your bank box. Find a new plot, place a new house, and unpack the crate to restore your belongings. The crate holds a large but finite amount (community recollection on exact limits) — pack rats sometimes need several trips or must trim down first.

Moving is also the safe way to quit a plot: rather than demolishing and dropping everything on the ground, the crate keeps it all banked. Note that the old plot becomes free the moment you move, so have the new spot picked out first on crowded shards. [Vendors](player-vendors.md) must be collected separately, and addons are typically re-deeded before the move.

## Era notes

The moving crate replaced the old days of manually hauling every item to a new house — a much-requested quality-of-life addition. Its capacity has been raised over the years.

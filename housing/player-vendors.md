# Player Vendors

Player vendors are NPC shopkeepers you hire to sell goods from your house: you stock them with items and prices, and they handle the sales while you are offline, taking a daily fee from their earnings.

## How it works

Hire a vendor with a vendor contract (bought from NPC provisioners) and place them on your plot. Stock them by dropping items into their inventory and setting a price per item; the vendor opens a shop gump to browsing passersby and sells at your prices, holding the gold for you to collect. Vendors charge a daily upkeep fee based on the value of the goods they carry (community recollection on the exact formula) — if the fees exceed their held gold, they quit and their goods drop to the ground, so owners check them regularly.

A vendor's location is everything: houses near busy towns and moongates get foot traffic, remote plots don't. Owners often name vendors to advertise their wares ("Cheap Reagents", "Runic Armor"). You can also place **rental vendors** on another player's plot for a rental fee, which is how vendor malls and market towns work — one house plot hosting several shopkeepers.

## Era notes

Player vendors are one of the oldest systems in the game, predating the modern auction-style trade. Vendor search tools on some shards later made remote vendors findable, but the core hire-stock-fee loop is unchanged.

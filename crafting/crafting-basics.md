# Crafting Basics

Crafting in Ultima Online turns raw materials into finished goods through skill-gated menus. Each trade skill uses its own set of tools and works through a crafting gump that lists every recipe the character can attempt.

## How it works

Using a crafting tool — a smith's hammer, sewing kit, tinker tools, fletcher's tools, and so on — opens the crafting menu, which organizes recipes by category and shows the minimum skill each one requires. Attempting an item consumes materials and rolls a success check against the character's skill: the further the skill is above the requirement, the better the odds. A failed attempt consumes some or all of the materials.

Items crafted at high skill can come out **exceptional**, gaining a quality bonus (extra resists on armor, extra damage on weapons) and carrying the crafter's **maker's mark** — the character's name stamped on the item. Exceptional quality is required for [bulk order deeds](bulk-order-deeds.md) and for [enhancing](enhancing.md), and exceptional armor and weapons are the baseline for serious equipment.

Damaged items can be **repaired**: crafters can repair their own goods directly with the relevant tool, and repair deeds let a character without the skill repair an item a limited number of times. Each repair slightly lowers the item's maximum durability (community recollection on the exact penalty).

## Era notes

Menu-based crafting replaced the older use-tool-on-target crafting early in the game's life. Exceptional items and maker's marks date to the earliest eras; [material tiers](material-tiers.md) and runic crafting arrived with later expansions.

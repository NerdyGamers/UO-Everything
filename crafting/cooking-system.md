# Cooking System

Beyond restoring hunger, [Cooking](../skills/crafting/cooking.md) produces magical foods that grant short-term buffs — and a few that solve problems no potion covers.

## How it works

Cooks prepare foods from dough, meats, fish, fruits, and special ingredients. Most food simply fills hunger (which affects natural regeneration), but magical recipes add temporary effects:

- **Enchanted Apple** — eating one cures poison, making it a lightweight cure for characters without magery or alchemy.
- **Grapes of Wrath** — grant a temporary damage bonus (community recollection on the exact amount and duration).
- **Magical fish pies** — baked from rare or dungeon-caught fish, these grant short-term buffs; exact effects vary by recipe and are community-documented.
- **Miscellaneous treats** — holiday and event recipes occasionally add unique consumables with niche effects.

Food buffs are temporary and generally do not stack with each other beyond their own durations. Because cooking is cheap to train and the ingredients are renewable, magical foods are a staple of PvM preparation — enchanted apples in particular are carried by many templates as poison insurance.

## Era notes

Magical foods accreted over many publishes rather than arriving in one expansion; enchanted apples and grapes of wrath are among the longest-standing examples.

# Runic Tools

Runic tools let crafters forge magic directly into their work: each use consumes one of the tool's charges and adds random magical properties to the crafted item. They are the premier reward of the [bulk order deed](bulk-order-deeds.md) system.

## How it works

**Runic hammers** (blacksmithing), **runic sewing kits** (tailoring), and **runic fletcher's tools** (bowcraft) come in tiers named after the [material tiers](material-tiers.md) — dull copper through valorite for hammers, spined through barbed for sewing kits. Higher-tier BODs award higher-tier runics.

Crafting with a runic tool equipped works like normal crafting, except the finished item gains a number of magical properties (resists, hit chance increase, damage increase, luck, and others) with intensities scaled to the runic's tier. Each item crafted this way consumes one charge; when the charges run out, the tool is gone. Exceptional results from runic crafting keep their maker's mark and can be [enhanced](enhancing.md) afterward.

## Era notes

Runic tools arrived with the bulk order deed system and have remained the backbone of player-crafted magical equipment ever since.

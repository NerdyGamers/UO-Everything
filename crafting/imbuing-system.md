# Imbuing System

Imbuing adds magical properties to weapons, armor, jewelry, and spellbooks after they are crafted, using ingredients harvested by unraveling other magic items at a Soul Forge.

## How it works

Unraveling a magic item at a Soul Forge destroys it and yields ingredients scaled to the item's intensity: low-intensity items give Magical Residue, mid-intensity items give Enchanted Essence, and high-intensity items give Relic Fragments. Gem-based ingredients are also required for some properties. Imbuing a property onto an item consumes the appropriate ingredients plus the item's share of a total property-weight budget — every property has a weight, and an item can only carry so much (community recollection commonly cites a 500-weight cap for most items).

Success depends on [Imbuing](../skills/crafting/imbuing.md) skill versus the property's difficulty. A failed attempt consumes the ingredients but leaves the item intact. Imbued items cannot be enhanced afterward, and the most intense properties (notably the highest property weights) cannot be imbued at all — they must come from loot, runic crafting, or reforging.

The Soul Forge sits in the Royal City of Ter Mur; the Queen's Soul Forge offers a higher success chance. Gargoyles can imbue anywhere with the proper tools, per community documentation.

## Era notes

Introduced with Stygian Abyss (2009) alongside the gargoyle race and Ter Mur. It shifted endgame gearing from pure loot hunting toward crafted-and-imbued suits.

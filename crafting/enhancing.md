# Enhancing

Enhancing applies a runic-tier material directly to a finished item, adding that material's bonuses after the fact. It is the final step in producing top-end player-crafted gear — and a gamble, because failure destroys the item.

## How it works

Use the [material tier](material-tiers.md) (ingots, leather, or boards) on an exceptional crafted item. Success adds the material's full tier bonuses — resists on armor, damage increase on weapons — to the item. Only exceptional items can be enhanced.

The catch is the success chance: it falls as the material tier rises, and a failed attempt **destroys the item outright**, runic properties and all. Enhancing is therefore done last, after [runic tool](runic-tools.md) crafting, and crafters typically reserve it for their best pieces. Property intensity added by enhancing is capped below what the highest runic tools can produce (community recollection on the exact caps).

## Era notes

Enhancing was added alongside the mature runic crafting system and has kept its high-risk, high-reward role ever since.

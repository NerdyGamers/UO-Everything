# Reforging

Reforging spends charges from a [runic tool](runic-tools.md) to reshape a crafted item's properties at the moment of creation, offering choices no ordinary crafting attempt provides.

## How it works

When crafting with a runic tool, the reforging menu offers a set of options before the item is made. The broad categories, per community documentation: **Powerful Reforging** adds a random property scaled to the runic tool's intensity; the **Artifice** options (Grand, Inspired, Exalted) re-roll a larger number of the item's properties; **Structural** reforging favors durability and resist-type properties; **Fundamental** reforging favors luck and lower-requirement style properties. Exact option names and effects have been tuned over time — confirm against a current reference before planning a suit around them.

Reforging consumes runic charges like any runic craft. The practical difference from the other two property systems:

- **Reforging** happens at craft time, driven by runic charges, and produces a randomized property set you then refine.
- **[Enhancing](enhancing.md)** happens after crafting, adds one property from a gem or special material, and can destroy the item on failure.
- **[Imbuing](imbuing-system.md)** happens after crafting at a Soul Forge, adds chosen properties within a weight budget, and never destroys the item.

The standard high-end pipeline is: reforge with a runic tool for a strong randomized base, then imbue the remaining weight budget with chosen properties. Reforged items can generally be imbued afterward (community recollection — verify for specific property combinations).

## Era notes

Added in the early 2010s, well after runic tools themselves (Age of Shadows, 2003), as a way to make runic crafting competitive with imbued loot-era gear.

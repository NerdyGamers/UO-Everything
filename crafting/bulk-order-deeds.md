# Bulk Order Deeds

Bulk order deeds (BODs) are crafting contracts offered by NPC shopkeepers: craft exactly what the deed asks for, turn it in, and receive a reward scaled to the order's difficulty. They are the main path to [runic tools](runic-tools.md) and crafting power scrolls.

## How it works

Blacksmith and tailor NPCs offer BODs to characters with sufficient skill. A **small BOD** asks for one item type in a given quantity, quality (normal or exceptional), and [material tier](material-tiers.md). A **large BOD** is a set of small orders that must all be completed and combined before turn-in.

Fill the deed by crafting the requested items and adding them to it, then return it to any NPC of the right trade for a reward. Rewards scale with difficulty: low-end orders pay in gold or basic goods, while top-end exceptional valorite or barbed orders award runic hammers, runic sewing kits, and Blacksmithy or Tailoring power scrolls (105 through 120). NPCs can also be **bribed** to upgrade a deed to a better reward tier, at a gold cost that rises with the deed's quality (bribing was added in a later era).

## Era notes

Smith BODs are the original system; tailor BODs were added later. Bribing and some reward tiers came later still.

# Inscription Craft

[Inscription](../skills/magic/inscription.md) turns blank scrolls, paper, and reagents into the consumables and containers spellcasters depend on: spell scrolls, spellbooks, runebooks, and recall runes.

## How it works

- **Spell scrolls** — scribes copy spells onto blank scrolls, circle by circle; higher circles demand higher skill (community recollection places 7th–8th circle scrolls near the top of the skill range). Scrolls let anyone cast the spell without it memorized, at the cost of the scroll.
- **Spellbooks** — crafted by scribes; a spellbook scribed by a grandmaster scribe grants its caster a spell-damage-increase bonus tied to the caster's own Inscription skill (community recollection commonly cites +1% per 10 points of Inscription).
- **Runebooks and recall runes** — scribes craft recall runes and assemble runebooks, the multi-rune containers that replaced loose rune collections for most travelers. Runebook capacities and upgrade paths have changed across publishes.
- **Bulk order deeds** — scribes also fill inscription [bulk order deeds](bulk-order-deeds.md), trading finished scrolls and books for runic scribing tools.

The [skill page](../skills/magic/inscription.md) covers training and the mana-refund mechanic; this page covers what the craft produces.

## Era notes

Scroll scribing and spellbook crafting date to the earliest eras. The inscription spell-damage bonus and runebook systems are later refinements layered onto the original craft.

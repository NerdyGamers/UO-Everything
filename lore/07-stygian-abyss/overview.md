# Stygian Abyss — The Gargoyle Homelands

**Canon**

Beneath the known world lies the underworld of the gargoyles — the winged race driven from the surface in ages past — whose queen now seeks an uneasy peace with humanity even as the demon Virtuebane rises in the abyssal depths.

## The story

The gargoyles are Britannia's dispossessed. Once dwellers of the surface world, they were driven into the underworld by ancient wars with humanity, and there they built Ter Mur, a city clinging to the walls of the great chasm called the Stygian Abyss. For centuries the two peoples had no contact but old hatreds.

The age of the Stygian Abyss opens with a cautious thaw: the Gargoyle Queen reaches out to the human kingdoms, seeking the peace her people have never known. But the abyss itself is stirring. Virtuebane, a demon of pure malice, gathers strength in the depths, and his shadow falls over both nations. Champions must descend into the abyss — the greatest dungeon of the age — and face what waits at the bottom, or the fragile peace will drown in blood.

The abyss is more than a dungeon: it is the wound of the world, a place of ancient evils and forgotten magics, and what happens there decides the fate of the gargoyle nation.

## Key figures and factions

- **The Gargoyle Queen** — ruler of Ter Mur, who chose the harder path of peace with the humans her people have every reason to hate.
- **Virtuebane** — the demonic antagonist of the age, rising in the abyssal depths.
- **The gargoyle clans of Ter Mur** — warriors, mystics, and craftsfolk, divided over whether the humans can be trusted.
- **The abyssal inhabitants** — the creatures and cults of the Stygian Abyss, from its upper tunnels to the nightmare below.

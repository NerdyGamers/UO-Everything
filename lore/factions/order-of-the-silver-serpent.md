# Order of the Silver Serpent

**Canon**

The knighthood of Britannia: the realm's sworn warriors, named for the silver serpent — the ancient symbol of the land — and headquartered at Serpent's Hold on the Isle of Deeds.

## History

The Order was founded by Lord British as Britannia's standing knighthood, the training ground of the realm's armed militia. Its knights were taught more than warcraft: Honor and Valor were the Order's creed, and its fortress on the Isle of Deeds stood as a bastion of both. Among its known members were the companions Geoffrey and Dupre.

The Order endured through the Age of Enlightenment, but by the time of Ultima IX it was gone — disbanded after disaster and replaced by the Knights of Valoria. In Ultima Online's telling, the Order never fell so much as changed its name: Britannia's knights became the Royal Knights, and in time the Royal Britannian Guard. Community recollection holds that the renaming was forced by an out-of-world rights dispute, never explained in-character — the symbol of the silver serpent survived the change.

## In Ultima Online

The silver serpent remains the faction creature of the True Britannians, a quiet echo of the old knighthood. On some shards, player guilds carry on the Order's name and traditions.

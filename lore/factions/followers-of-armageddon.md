# Followers of Armageddon

**Canon**

The great NPC villains of early Ultima Online: a cabal of mages who sought the end of the world, and whose reckless sorcery accidentally opened the Lost Lands.

## History

They first called themselves the Zog Cabal, after Zog — the ancient being who, in the deep history of Sosaria, learned the Armageddon spell from the Wisps and cast it out of curiosity, destroying all life on the world. The Cabal announced itself at the funeral of one of its early victims, proclaiming that all heroes, institutions, and order would fall to them, for they were legion, and they devoured structure and gave back chaos.

They fought for chaos, destruction, and decay. Their great prize was the Armageddon spell itself, wrested from the Wisps, and with it they sought enough Blackrock to unmake the world. They never gathered enough — but their workings shook the earth, and the geologic upheavals they caused tore open the passage to the Lost Lands. They were hunted by Crawworth, avenging murdered kin, and by Halston Montil, and are commonly held to have met their end in the dungeon Shame — though vestiges linger in the Wisp Dungeon and Sorcerer's Dungeon of Ilshenar, and an offshoot surfaced again when Blackrock returned during the Inu the Crone scenario.

## In Ultima Online

The Followers belong to UO's early live-event history: scripted villains who gave the young world its first taste of a story told through the game itself rather than its manual.

## Notes

- **Canon:** established in official Ultima Online fiction and live events. Details of their end are community recollection; sources differ on where they first announced themselves.
- Era: the opening of the Lost Lands belongs to [The Second Age](../02-the-second-age/overview.md).
- Related: the [Order of Minax](./order-of-minax.md), another power that trafficked with dark magic.

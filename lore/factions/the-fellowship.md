# The Fellowship

**Canon**

The smiling cult of Ultima VII: a philosophical movement that promised Britannia spiritual wealth, and secretly served the Guardian.

## History

The Fellowship was founded some twenty years before the Avatar's return by the trio of Batlin, Elizabeth, and Abraham, with Lord British's blessing. It preached the Triad of Inner Strength — Unity, Trust, and Worthiness — and offered the people of Britannia something to believe in at a time when the old Virtues felt distant. Branches opened in every town; initiates wore the triangular medallion, and the prominent rose quickly into positions of influence.

Beneath the philosophy, the Fellowship was the Guardian's instrument. Its higher ranks were his agents, and its twofold purpose was to rot Britannia's social fabric from within and to seize power outright when the Guardian entered the world through the Black Gate. The Fellowship built the Black Gate and murdered its enemies to protect the secret. When the Avatar exposed and broke it, the Fellowship was outlawed and its halls dismantled — but loyal remnants went into hiding, waiting for their master's return.

## In Ultima Online

The Fellowship belongs to the single-player canon rather than UO's live history, but its shadow falls across the setting: the question of who still serves the Guardian never quite goes away.

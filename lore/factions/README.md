# Factions and Power Groups

The organizations that drive the setting's conflicts — from the knightly orders of old Britannia to the four factions of the Felucca civil war.

## The Four Factions (Ultima Online)

- [True Britannians](./true-britannians.md) — the loyalists of Lord British
- [Council of Mages](./council-of-mages.md) — the dissident mages of Moonglow
- [Order of Minax](./order-of-minax.md) — the evil mage's followers
- [Shadowlords](./shadowlords.md) — the embodiments of the Anti-Principles

# Shadowlords

**Canon**

Three wraith-like demonic entities — Astaroth of Hatred, Faulinei of Falsehood, and Nosfentor of Cowardice — the living embodiments of the three Anti-Principles, and the villains of Ultima V.

## History

The Shadowlords were born from three shards of the Gem of Immortality, recovered from the Underworld by Captain Johne of the ship Ararat. The shards' corruption drove Johne to murder his surviving crew, and from their blood the Shadowlords took form. Each embodies one Anti-Principle, the dark mirror of Love, Truth, and Courage.

At the height of their power they kidnapped Lord British during his expedition into the Underworld and twisted the mind of his regent, Lord Blackthorn, turning the Virtues into instruments of tyranny — enforced by the Inquisition and the Oppression. They haunted Britannia's towns, each descent heralded by a comet, until the Avatar learned their nature and destroyed them by casting their gem shards into the purifying flames of Truth, Love, and Courage.

## In Ultima Online

Followers of the Shadowlords formed one of the four playable factions of the Faction system, evil-aligned alongside the Order of Minax. Their stronghold was the Yew Crypts on the Felucca facet, and the daemon was their allied faction creature. Long only whispered of, the Shadowlords themselves finally manifested in Britannia during the Warriors of Destiny event cycle, when the Mad Mage Kronos's Blackrock Detector drew their ethereal echoes into the world — echoes that could be fought and that sometimes dropped the Cloak of Corruption artifact robe. The faction system was removed in Publish 86 (2014) and replaced by the Vice vs Virtue PvP system.

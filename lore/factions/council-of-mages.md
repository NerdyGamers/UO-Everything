# Council of Mages

**Canon**

The dissident faction of Ultima Online's Faction system: Britannia's mages, who judged Lord British unfit to rule and broke with the throne — opposing Minax and British both.

## History

The rebellion began in Moonglow. As Minax's hordes pressed in and Britannia's people began deserting to the darkness, the mages of the council concluded that Lord British had failed his realm — he had neither protected the land from invasion nor held the loyalty of his own subjects. British's loyalists tried to crush the rumors of secession, jailing and killing dissenters, but the crackdown only made martyrs and hardened the mages' cause.

The Council of Mages holds that Britannia needs a new ruler: one strong enough to defeat Minax and wise enough to keep the peace afterward. As keepers of the realm's arcane knowledge, they have spent their exile preparing notes, artifacts, and plans for something drastic. They stand apart from all sides — enemies of Minax, estranged from the True Britannians, and hunted by both.

## In Ultima Online

The Council of Mages was one of the four playable factions of the Faction system, the second good-aligned faction alongside the True Britannians. Their stronghold stood in the Parliament Building in Magincia, and the wisp was their allied faction creature. The faction system was removed in Publish 86 (2014) and replaced by the Vice vs Virtue PvP system.

# Order of Minax

**Canon**

The evil-aligned faction of Ultima Online's Faction system devoted to Minax the sorceress — Mondain's apprentice, the tyrant of Ultima II, and one of the great villains of the setting.

## History

Minax was the apprentice and lover of the evil wizard Mondain. After the Stranger slew Mondain at the end of Ultima I, Minax rose in his place, wielding powers that by most accounts exceeded her master's. Her reign of ruin across time itself ended only when a hero faced her in her own fiery castle and destroyed her — the tale told in Ultima II.

Centuries later, her name still gathers the cruel and the power-hungry. The Order of Minax holds that strength is the only true law, and its followers warred across Felucca without the restraint shown by the other factions. Their stronghold was a mountain fortress on the Felucca facet. Community recollection holds that Minax herself is not truly gone: no official fiction records her final death, and she is said to correspond with the Travesty, the Peerless that haunts the Citadel dungeon of the Tokuno Islands.

## In Ultima Online

The Order of Minax was one of the four playable factions of the Faction system, one of the two evil-aligned factions alongside the Shadowlords. The ogre lord was their allied faction creature. The faction system was removed in Publish 86 (2014) and replaced by the Vice vs Virtue PvP system.

## Notes

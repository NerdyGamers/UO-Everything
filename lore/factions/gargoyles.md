# Gargoyles

**Canon**

The winged people of the subterranean Realm of the Gargoyles: an ancient race built around the philosophy of Singularity, and the original keepers of the Codex of Ultimate Wisdom.

## History

Gargoyle society holds that every part of the whole is equally vital — winged and wingless, scholar and drone, all functioning as one body. Their holiest site was the Temple of Singularity, where the seer Naxatilor had placed the Codex of Ultimate Wisdom after summoning it from the Ethereal Void. Their virtues were Control, Passion, and Diligence, bound together under the principle of Singularity.

When the Avatar proved enlightened in Britannia's Eight Virtues, the Great Council of Britannia removed the Codex from the Temple and carried it to the surface. The theft devastated the gargoyle realm and tore open the Underworld between the two worlds. The gargoyles named the Avatar the False Prophet and, guided by their Book of Prophecies, invaded Britannia and seized the Shrines of Virtue — the War of the False Prophet told in Ultima VI. Peace came only when the Avatar returned the Codex to the Ethereal Void, leaving each king a lens so both races might still read its wisdom together.

## In Ultima Online

The gargoyles endured as a people, and with the Stygian Abyss their homelands opened to Britannia once more. Gargoyles are a playable race in Ultima Online, with their own cities, queen, and crafts.

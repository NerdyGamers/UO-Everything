# True Britannians

**Canon**

The loyalist faction of Ultima Online's Faction system: warriors and citizens who held to the teachings of Lord British and fought to keep his order alive on the Felucca facet.

## History

When Minax's invasion shattered the old certainties, Britannia's people split. The True Britannians were those who refused to abandon Lord British — the larger of the two good-aligned factions in the civil war that followed, standing against the Council of Mages' rebellion on one side and the evil of Minax and the Shadowlords on the other.

They drew their identity directly from the crown: loyalty to the king, defense of the realm's towns, and the Virtues as British taught them. Their stronghold was Lord British's own castle in Britain, and they rode to war in purple — faction war horses and robes dyed in their colors — with the silver serpent, the realm's ancient symbol, as their allied creature.

## In Ultima Online

The True Britannians were one of the four playable factions of the Faction system, the good-aligned counterpart to the Council of Mages. Faction members warred for control of Felucca's towns, earned silver, and commanded faction guards and allied monsters. The faction system was removed in Publish 86 (2014) and replaced by the Vice vs Virtue PvP system.

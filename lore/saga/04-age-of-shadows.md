# IV. The Upheaval

**Canon**

A generation after the Sundering, the world broke open again — this time from below.

A new landmass tore its way into being: Malas, a shadowed continent of dead cities and darker magic. At its heart stood two cities in eternal opposition. Luna, the shining city, home of paladins who held the light against the dark. Umbra, the lightless city, home of necromancers who found power in death and were not ashamed of it. Between them lay the Doom Gauntlet, a pit of horrors where the mightiest adventurers tested themselves against nightmares made flesh — and where the victors carried out artifacts of legendary, fixed power: blades and helms and shields whose names are still spoken with reverence.

The arrival of Malas changed more than the map. The artifact age began, and the economy of equipment was transformed in a season — a farmer's son with a Doom artifact could stand beside a lord. Necromancy and Chivalry entered the world's magical traditions as rival disciplines, the one drawing on death, the other on devotion. The old certainties cracked: what a warrior could be, what a craftsman was worth, what a house might hold.

When the dust settled, Britannia was a different world than the one that had been mirrored a generation before — harder, stranger, and richer in wonders. The paladins still keep their watch in Luna, the necromancers their councils in Umbra, and the Gauntlet still waits for those bold enough to descend. Some doors, once opened, do not close.

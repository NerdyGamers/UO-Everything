# The Saga of the Ages

**Canon**

Being the true account of the world from its shattering to the present day, as set down by Corvin, scribe of the Lycaeum, for all who would know the whole of the tale and not merely its pieces.

---

I am Corvin, a scribe of the Lycaeum on Verity Isle, and I have spent my life among old books. What follows is the saga as the old books tell it — the shattering of the world, the coming of the Stranger, the founding of the Virtues, and all the ages since, down to our own restless present.

I do not claim every word is true. The minstrels embroider, the old chronicles disagree, and some pages were lost to fire, flood, and war. Where the record is thin I have said so. But the shape of the tale is true, and any Britannian ought to know it.

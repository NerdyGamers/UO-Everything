# IX. The Valley Out of Time

**Canon**

Beyond the charted seas lies Eodon, a valley the maps forgot — a vast green fastness sealed away from the world, where the old ways never died. Tribal humans hunt and farm there as their ancestors did. Great thunder-lizards stalk the jungles, beasts out of the world's deep morning. Strange magics keep the valley hidden from the empires beyond, and for uncounted years it kept itself.

The valley's peace was shattered by the Myrmidex: a relentless insectoid swarm pouring from their hives to consume everything in their path — crops, cattle, people, all one to the swarm. The tribes, brave but outmatched, turned to outsiders for aid, and adventurers found themselves fighting a war of extermination against an enemy that never stops coming and never asks for quarter.

Above it all stands Shadowguard, a fortress of trials older than any kingdom, and its rooftop is the proving ground of legends. Champions climb through its chambers, each a test of a different mastery, to face what waits at the top. The bards have already begun the songs. Give them time; they will embroider.

And so my chronicle reaches the present day — for Eodon's war is barely cooled, and Shadowguard's trials are still being climbed as I write. Here the scribe must lay down his pen, not because the tale is ended but because it is not. The Ages continue. Somewhere a moongate opens; somewhere a young adventurer takes up a rusty sword; somewhere an old evil stirs in its sleep.

I have set down what the old books tell, and what the stones remember. May those who come after remember it truly — and may they be kinder chroniclers than I, for they will have the advantage of knowing how it all turns out.

*Here ends the Saga of the Ages.*

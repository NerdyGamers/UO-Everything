# Love

**Canon**

One of the Three Principles at the foundation of the Ultima virtue system, alongside [Truth](truth.md) and [Courage](courage.md). From Love alone comes the virtue of [Compassion](compassion.md); combined with Courage it yields [Sacrifice](sacrifice.md), and combined with Truth it yields [Justice](justice.md). All three Principles together bring [Spirituality](spirituality.md).

## Meaning

Love is the principle of care for others — the impulse that turns self-interest into compassion and, at its furthest reach, into sacrifice. Empath Abbey in Yew stood as its stronghold in the single-player games.

## Embodiment

Each Principle is embodied by a physical item. Love is embodied by the **Candle of Love**, located in a cavern beneath the winery in Yew. As community recollection has it, the cavern itself is currently closed off, but blessed candles lit from the Candle of Love are owned by some players and are seen from time to time.

## Notes

The Three Principles were introduced in *Ultima IV: Quest of the Avatar*. The philosophy built on them came to be known as the Codex of Ultimate Wisdom.

See also [Truth](truth.md), [Courage](courage.md), and [The Virtue System in Ultima Online](virtue-system.md).

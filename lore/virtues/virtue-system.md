# The Virtue System in Ultima Online

**Canon**

The in-game system through which players perform virtuous deeds and earn tangible powers. It is accessed from the character's paper doll — in the classic 2D client, by double-clicking the symbol above the character's head — and presents each virtue as an icon on the virtue gump.

## How It Works

Each of the Eight Virtues is gained through deeds aligned with it, and each grants a power at sufficient attainment:

- **Compassion** (heart) — gained through NPC escort quests; resurrect other players with up to 80% health
- **Honesty** (open-palmed hand) — gained through the "lost" item system; NPC merchant discount
- **Honor** (chalice) — gained by honoring monsters, then defeating them; walk among monsters unattacked, briefly
- **Justice** (scales) — gained by killing murderers; increased power scroll rewards at champion spawns
- **Sacrifice** (teardrop) — gained by sacrificing Fame; self-resurrection
- **Valor** (sword) — gained by fighting at champion spawns; activate and advance champion spawns
- **Humility** (shepherd's crook) — gained by slaying evil-aligned monsters during the Humility Hunt
- **Spirituality** (ankh) — gained through targeted heals and resurrections on other players

Murderers (player killers) are unable to use the Virtues.

## Notes

The system was designed by Jonathan "Calandryll" Hanna, whose design document was posted on UO.com in October 2001; it went live in the early 2000s. Only five of the eight virtues were active at first — Honesty, Humility, and Spirituality were activated later, as community recollection has it, and virtue quests were added in Publish 38.

See also the individual virtue articles, beginning with [Honesty](honesty.md), and [The Anti-Virtues](anti-virtues.md).

# Valor

**Canon**

The virtue of pure [Courage](courage.md). Its mantra is **Ra**, its town is Jhelom (city of fighters), its color is red, and its symbol is the sword. Its opposing dungeon is Destard, embodying the [anti-virtue](anti-virtues.md) of Cowardice.

## Meaning

Valor is courage to stand against risk — often called the cloak that protects the other virtues, because conviction undefended is soon lost.

## In Ultima Online

On the virtue gump, Valor appears as a sword. It is gained by fighting monsters at champion spawns, and its power grants the ability to activate and advance champion spawns.

See [The Virtue System in Ultima Online](virtue-system.md).

## Notes

Valor's derivation from Courage alone, its mantra, town, color, symbol, and opposing dungeon follow the classic virtue chart used across the Ultima games.

See also [Courage](courage.md), [Honor](honor.md), and [The Anti-Virtues](anti-virtues.md).

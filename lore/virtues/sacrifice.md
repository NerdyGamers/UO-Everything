# Sacrifice

**Canon**

The virtue of [Love](love.md) joined with [Courage](courage.md). Its mantra is **Cah**, its town is Minoc (city of tinkers), its color is orange, and its symbol is the tear. Its opposing dungeon is Covetous, embodying the [anti-virtue](anti-virtues.md) of Covetousness.

## Meaning

Sacrifice is the courage to give of oneself in the name of love — to surrender something precious, up to and including one's life, for the sake of others.

## In Ultima Online

On the virtue gump, Sacrifice appears as a teardrop. It is gained by sacrificing Fame at the shrines, and its power allows a character to resurrect him- or herself.

See [The Virtue System in Ultima Online](virtue-system.md).

## Notes

Sacrifice's derivation from Love and Courage, its mantra, town, color, symbol, and opposing dungeon follow the classic virtue chart used across the Ultima games.

See also [Love](love.md), [Courage](courage.md), [Compassion](compassion.md), and [The Anti-Virtues](anti-virtues.md).

# Compassion

**Canon**

The virtue of pure [Love](love.md). Its mantra is **Mu**, its town is Britain (city of bards), its color is yellow, and its symbol is the heart. Its opposing dungeon is Despise, embodying the [anti-virtue](anti-virtues.md) of Despise.

## Meaning

Compassion is love of others expressed as action: to ease suffering wherever it is found. It is the act of giving that defines it.

## In Ultima Online

On the virtue gump, Compassion appears as a heart. It is gained by completing NPC escort quests, and its power allows a character to resurrect other players with up to 80% of full health, depending on the level of Compassion attained.

See [The Virtue System in Ultima Online](virtue-system.md).

## Notes

Compassion's derivation from Love alone, its mantra, town, color, symbol, and opposing dungeon follow the classic virtue chart used across the Ultima games.

See also [Love](love.md), [Sacrifice](sacrifice.md), and [The Anti-Virtues](anti-virtues.md).

# Honesty

**Canon**

The virtue of pure [Truth](truth.md). Its mantra is **Ahm**, its town is Moonglow (city of mages), its color is blue, and its symbol is the open-palmed hand. Its opposing dungeon is Deceit, embodying the [anti-virtue](anti-virtues.md) of Deceit.

## Meaning

Honesty is respect for Truth: to speak truly, deal fairly, and hide nothing. It is the first virtue on the Avatar's ankh and the foundation of trust between people.

## In Ultima Online

On the virtue gump, Honesty appears as an open-palmed hand. As community recollection has it, Honesty was inactive when the Virtue system first launched and was activated later: it is gained through the "lost" item system, and its benefit is a discount from NPC merchants.

See [The Virtue System in Ultima Online](virtue-system.md).

## Notes

Honesty's derivation from Truth alone, its mantra, town, color, symbol, and opposing dungeon follow the classic virtue chart used across the Ultima games.

See also [Truth](truth.md), [Justice](justice.md), and [The Anti-Virtues](anti-virtues.md).

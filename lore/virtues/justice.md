# Justice

**Canon**

The virtue of [Truth](truth.md) tempered by [Love](love.md). Its mantra is **Beh**, its town is Yew (city of druids), its color is green, and its symbol is the set of scales. Its opposing dungeon is Wrong, embodying the [anti-virtue](anti-virtues.md) of Injustice.

## Meaning

Justice is truth applied with love: to judge fairly, weighing the facts without cruelty and without favoritism.

## In Ultima Online

On the virtue gump, Justice appears as a set of scales. It is gained by killing murderers (player killers), and its power grants increased power scroll rewards based on the actions of designated characters at champion spawns. Murderers themselves are unable to use the Virtues.

See [The Virtue System in Ultima Online](virtue-system.md).

## Notes

Justice's derivation from Truth and Love, its mantra, town, color, symbol, and opposing dungeon follow the classic virtue chart used across the Ultima games.

See also [Truth](truth.md), [Love](love.md), [Honesty](honesty.md), and [The Anti-Virtues](anti-virtues.md).

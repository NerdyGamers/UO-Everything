# Spirituality

**Canon**

The virtue of all three Principles — [Truth](truth.md), [Love](love.md), and [Courage](courage.md) — together. Its mantra is **Om**, its town is Skara Brae (city of rangers), its color is white, and its symbol is the ankh. Its opposing dungeon is Hythloth.

## Meaning

Spirituality is the all-encompassing virtue: to seek Truth, Love, and Courage from one's own self and from the world around. Where the other virtues each capture part of the good, Spirituality aims at the whole.

## In Ultima Online

On the virtue gump, Spirituality appears as an ankh. As community recollection has it, Spirituality was inactive when the Virtue system first launched and was activated later: it is gained through targeted heals and resurrections performed on other players.

See [The Virtue System in Ultima Online](virtue-system.md).

## Notes

Spirituality's derivation from all three Principles, its mantra, town, color, symbol, and opposing dungeon follow the classic virtue chart used across the Ultima games.

See also [Truth](truth.md), [Love](love.md), [Courage](courage.md), and [The Anti-Virtues](anti-virtues.md).

# Courage

**Canon**

One of the Three Principles at the foundation of the Ultima virtue system, alongside [Truth](truth.md) and [Love](love.md). From Courage alone comes the virtue of [Valor](valor.md); combined with Truth it yields [Honor](honor.md), and combined with Love it yields [Sacrifice](sacrifice.md). All three Principles together bring [Spirituality](spirituality.md).

## Meaning

Courage is the principle of acting rightly in the face of risk — the strength that turns conviction into deeds. Valor is often called the cloak that protects the other virtues, because without courage none of them can be defended.

## Embodiment

Each Principle is embodied by a physical item. Courage is embodied by the **Bell of Courage**. As community recollection has it, the Bell was broken at one point in UO's history and later repaired through the use of Blackrock.

## Notes

The Three Principles were introduced in *Ultima IV: Quest of the Avatar*. The philosophy built on them came to be known as the Codex of Ultimate Wisdom.

See also [Truth](truth.md), [Love](love.md), and [The Virtue System in Ultima Online](virtue-system.md).

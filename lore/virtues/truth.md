# Truth

**Canon**

One of the Three Principles at the foundation of the Ultima virtue system, alongside [Love](love.md) and [Courage](courage.md). From Truth alone comes the virtue of [Honesty](honesty.md); combined with Love it yields [Justice](justice.md), and combined with Courage it yields [Honor](honor.md). All three Principles together bring [Spirituality](spirituality.md).

## Meaning

Truth is the principle of seeing the world as it is, without deception or self-deception. It is the foundation on which the other virtues are measured: a virtue practiced without truthfulness is hollow. In the single-player games, the Lycaeum on Verity Isle stood as the stronghold of Truth.

## Embodiment

Each Principle is embodied by a physical item. Truth is embodied by the **Book of Truth**, kept in a chamber beneath the Lycaeum, which players can visit and see in-game.

## Notes

Truth, Love, and Courage were introduced as the moral framework of Britannia in *Ultima IV: Quest of the Avatar*, and the philosophy built on them came to be known as the Codex of Ultimate Wisdom.

See also [Love](love.md), [Courage](courage.md), and [The Virtue System in Ultima Online](virtue-system.md).

# The Anti-Virtues

**Canon**

The oppositions of the Eight Virtues. Where the virtues are the philosophy of the Codex of Ultimate Wisdom, the anti-virtues are its shadow — and the philosophy of the Virtues as a whole is opposed by the philosophy of Chaos, as championed by Lord Blackthorn.

## The Eight Oppositions

Each virtue is opposed by a sin, embodied in Britannia by one of the eight dungeons:

- **Deceit** opposes [Honesty](honesty.md) — the dungeon Deceit
- **Despise** opposes [Compassion](compassion.md) — the dungeon Despise
- **Cowardice** opposes [Valor](valor.md) — the dungeon Destard
- **Injustice** opposes [Justice](justice.md) — the dungeon Wrong
- **Covetousness** opposes [Sacrifice](sacrifice.md) — the dungeon Covetous
- **Dishonor** opposes [Honor](honor.md) — the dungeon Shame
- The dungeon **Hythloth** stands opposed to [Spirituality](spirituality.md)
- **Pride** opposes [Humility](humility.md) — the absence of all three Principles

## Notes

The dungeons' names and their pairing with the virtues come from the classic virtue chart; the exact naming of some oppositions (such as Injustice versus Wrong, or the dungeon paired with Humility) varies between sources and is given here as community recollection. Lord Blackthorn's own Code of Virtues, a Chaos counter-philosophy, is documented on UOGuide for those who want the opposing view in full.

See also [The Virtue System in Ultima Online](virtue-system.md).

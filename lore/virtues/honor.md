# Honor

**Canon**

The virtue of [Courage](courage.md) joined with [Truth](truth.md). Its mantra is **Summ**, its town is Trinsic (city of paladins), its color is purple, and its symbol is the chalice. Its opposing dungeon is Shame, embodying the [anti-virtue](anti-virtues.md) of Dishonor.

## Meaning

Honor is the courage to seek and uphold the truth: to keep one's oaths, face one's foes openly, and accept the consequences of one's deeds.

## In Ultima Online

On the virtue gump, Honor appears as a chalice. It is gained by honoring monsters and then defeating them in combat, and its power allows a character to walk amidst monsters without being attacked, for a brief period of time.

See [The Virtue System in Ultima Online](virtue-system.md).

## Notes

Honor's derivation from Courage and Truth, its mantra, town, color, symbol, and opposing dungeon follow the classic virtue chart used across the Ultima games.

See also [Courage](courage.md), [Truth](truth.md), [Valor](valor.md), and [The Anti-Virtues](anti-virtues.md).

# Humility

**Canon**

The eighth virtue, and the opposite of Pride — which is the absence of all three Principles. Its mantra is **Lum**, its town is New Magincia (Magincia in the classic games, city of shepherds), its color is black, and its symbol is the shepherd's staff.

## Meaning

Humility is the foundation of the Virtues. As the classic teaching has it, without Humility the other virtues fall of necessity into Pride and become meaningless — that which serfs are born with, but nobles must strive to attain. The demon attacks on Magincia, as community recollection tells it, were punishment for the city's Pride.

## In Ultima Online

On the virtue gump, Humility appears as a shepherd's crook. As community recollection has it, Humility was inactive when the Virtue system first launched and was activated later: it is gained by slaying evil-aligned monsters during the Humility Hunt. Its color, black, is the key to the Cloak of Humility quest.

See [The Virtue System in Ultima Online](virtue-system.md).

## Notes

Unlike the other seven virtues, Humility is not derived from a combination of the Principles — it is the opposite of Pride, the lack of all three. Its opposing dungeon is given in some charts as Doom and in others as Hythloth; this is community recollection and may reflect different eras of the lore.

See also [The Anti-Virtues](anti-virtues.md) and [Spirituality](spirituality.md).

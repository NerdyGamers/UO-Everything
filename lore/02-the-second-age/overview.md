# The Second Age

**Canon**

The revelation of the Lost Lands: a hidden continent joined to Britannia through the serpent pillars, and the expansion of the known world.

## The story

For generations, Britannia's scholars believed their world complete — every coast charted, every dungeon delved. Then explorers found the serpent pillars: ancient stone serpents coiled in the lightless bottoms of Britannia's deepest dungeons. Through them ran a passage to a land no living cartographer had mapped: the Lost Lands, a vast continent cut off from Britannia since time immemorial.

The Lost Lands held their own cities — Delucia, Papua — their own dungeons, and the ruins of peoples who had warred over them for ages: the serpentine Ophidians and the insectile Terathans, whose ancient enmity outlived any living memory of its cause. Adventurers poured through the pillars seeking the continent's riches, and the wars of its native peoples became, in time, the wars of Britannia's explorers as well.

The discovery reshaped the world's self-image. Britannia was not the whole of the world — merely the part that had been found first.

## Key figures and factions

- **The Ophidians** — serpent-folk of the Lost Lands, locked in ancient conflict.
- **The Terathans** — their rivals, dwelling in Terathan Keep.
- **The explorers** — Britannian adventurers and cartographers who mapped the new continent.

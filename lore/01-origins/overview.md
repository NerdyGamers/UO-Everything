# Origins

**Canon**

The foundational myths of the Ultima Online world: the land of Sosaria, the wizard Mondain's theft of the Gem of Immortality, and Lord British's founding of Britannia upon the Eight Virtues.

## The story

Long before Britannia, there was Sosaria — a world whose very existence was bound to the Gem of Immortality, a jewel that held all of the world within it. The evil wizard Mondain seized the Gem and sought to enslave Sosaria through its power. A hero from another world — the Stranger — confronted and destroyed Mondain, but the Gem was shattered in the struggle, and Sosaria itself was sundered into countless parallel worlds. One of those shards became the Britannia we know.

Mondain's apprentice Minax took up her master's war, striking across time itself in what the histories call the Time of Legends. After her fall, the two left behind a terrible legacy: Exodus, a dark being of their making, which rose to threaten the lands anew. Lord British — ruler of Britannia — called once more for a hero from beyond. That hero became the Avatar, and under Lord British's guidance the Eight Virtues were established as the moral foundation of the realm: Honesty, Compassion, Valor, Justice, Sacrifice, Honor, Spirituality, and Humility, born of the Three Principles of Truth, Love, and Courage.

## Key figures and factions

- **Mondain** — the wizard whose theft of the Gem of Immortality shattered Sosaria.
- **Minax** — Mondain's apprentice, who waged war across time itself.
- **Exodus** — the dark legacy left behind by Mondain and Minax.
- **Lord British** — ruler of Britannia and founder of the Virtues.
- **The Avatar** — the hero from another world, exemplar of the Virtues.
- **The Stranger** — the hero who destroyed Mondain; in some tellings, the same soul that would become the Avatar.

# The Guardian

**Canon**

The final villain of the Ultima series and its most frequently recurring one — the main antagonist of Ultima VII, Ultima VIII, Ultima IX, and Ultima Underworld II. A powerful extradimensional being that moves across the multiverse, conquering or destroying the worlds in its path.

## History

The Guardian dwells in the Great Ethereal Void, where it holds nearly limitless power — yet it cannot enter a material world like Britannia unassisted. In Ultima VII it worked through the Fellowship, a philosophical society secretly building a Black Gate of blackrock through which it could physically enter. The Avatar exposed the Fellowship and destroyed the gate, barring the Guardian's entry.

Thwarted, the Guardian banished the Avatar to the world of Pagan (Ultima VIII), then resurfaced to corrupt Britannia from within, working through a secretly returned Lord Blackthorn to drive the city-states toward war (Ultima IX). Unmasked and cornered at Stonegate, the Guardian was seemingly slain — but its columns had rooted too deeply in Britannia's fabric. Only the Spell of Armageddon could destroy it: Britannia perished with it, and the Avatar ascended.

The Guardian saw itself as a god, demanding worship under the pretense of being its victims' "guardian" — hence the name — and took perverse pleasure in twisting conquered worlds into corrupt parodies of themselves.

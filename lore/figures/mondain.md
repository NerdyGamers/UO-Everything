# Mondain

**Canon**

The evil wizard of Ultima I and first of the Triad of Evil. His theft and use of the Gem of Immortality set the entire saga in motion.

## History

Mondain was a moody, gifted youth of Sosaria, raised in sorcery and given to rages. His hunger for power led him to the Gem of Immortality, which made him invulnerable and let him twist the land to his will. The Stranger, summoned by Lord British, could not harm Mondain while the Gem protected him — so the hero found a time machine, traveled back to before the Gem's creation, and slew Mondain in the past, shattering the incomplete artifact.

Mondain's death did not end his influence. His skull survived, and ages later the Stranger recovered it from the ruins of Mondain's sunken abode and cast it into the lava of the Great Stygian Abyss — destroying it, opening the Abyss permanently, and knocking loose the shards of the Gem that would one day become the Shadowlords.

# The Time Lord

**Canon**

A powerful being of ambiguous origin who ensures the smooth and orderly flow of time through space — a task, in his own words, beyond the comprehension of mortals. He holds dear the well-being of the universe and opposes those who would bring destruction and chaos.

## History

The Time Lord first intervened in Sosarian history during the events of Ultima III, manifesting in the lowest depths of the Dungeon of Time, where he taught the Stranger the correct order of the Four Cards that would destroy Exodus. He appears across the ages — in Ultima IV, VII, and IX — and in Ultima Online's telling it is hinted he knows more of Lord British's true origins than any other being.

It was later revealed that he has at times worn the seemingly mortal persona of Hawkwind, through whom he guided the hero — first toward Avatarhood, and later toward the final confrontation with the Guardian and ascension. (The Hawkwind identity comes from later tellings; older accounts treat them as separate, and the contradiction is noted here as community recollection.)

# Lord Blackthorn

**Canon**

Regent of Britannia during the crisis of Ultima V, later an exile, and eventually a servant of the Guardian. His name became shorthand for tyranny dressed as virtue.

## History

Blackthorn was a friend and servant of Lord British who assumed the regency when British vanished leading an expedition into the newly opened Underworld. Three Shadowlords — born of shards of the Gem of Immortality — seized on him, and under their influence his rule turned despotic: the Virtues were twisted into oppressive laws, enforced by penalty, and the Avatar's companions were declared outlaws.

When the Avatar rescued Lord British, Blackthorn was offered a choice: face the judgment of the Great Council for his crimes, or accept banishment through the Orb of the Moons. By his own choice he took exile, passing through a red moongate to an unknown land — later discovered to be Serpent Isle.

There the Guardian found him. After Blackthorn killed Batlin for betrayal, the Guardian persuaded the exile into service, and Blackthorn became one of the Guardian's chief agents across Ultima VII Part Two and Ultima VIII. In Ultima IX he secretly advised Britannia's council of lords, goading the city-states toward war; unmasked at Terfin, he was caught and executed at Lord British's command.

# Shamino

**Canon**

Ranger and Companion of the Avatar — a native of Sosaria (unlike British's Earth-born friends) who named the young British and guided his first years in the strange land.

## History

Shamino took in the youth from Cambridge and gave him the name "British." He was among the eight companions of Ultima IV, encountered by the Stranger in Skara Brae contemplating the Ankh of Spirituality.

An early brush with Exodus left Shamino's mind damaged — he was found wandering the groves outside Lord British's castle muttering of vengeance from the depths of hell. Declared an outlaw under Blackthorn's regime, he was the first to greet the returned Avatar in Ultima V, flinging himself before a poisoned bolt meant for the hero. He rescued the Avatar from the gargoyles in Ultima VI, and in Ultima VII lived in Britain, self-conscious about his growing years. In Ultima IX he stood with Iolo at the Avatar's side to the end.

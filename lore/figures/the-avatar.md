# The Avatar

**Canon**

The hero of the Ultima saga — first the Stranger from Earth, summoned again and again to save Sosaria and Britannia, and from Ultima IV onward the living embodiment of the Eight Virtues.

## History

The Stranger was called through the moongates to defeat Mondain, Minax, and Exodus in the Age of Darkness. Afterward, with the world remade by those wars, Lord British sought a new moral code to unite his people. The Stranger returned, mastered the Eight Virtues and their Three Principles, braved the Great Stygian Abyss, and ripped the Codex of Ultimate Wisdom from its depths — and in reading it, became the Avatar.

Across the Age of Enlightenment the Avatar defended Britannia: toppling Blackthorn's regime, making peace with the gargoyles over the stolen Codex, and exposing the Fellowship. But the Guardian — an extradimensional conqueror working through the Fellowship's Black Gate — proved the final enemy. Banished to Pagan, returned, and summoned one last time, the Avatar learned the Guardian could only be destroyed by extinguishing the life force of Britannia itself. The Spell of Armageddon was cast: Britannia was destroyed along with the Guardian and Lord British, the people were saved by evacuation to Skara Brae under the Runes of Virtue, and the Avatar ascended to a higher plane of existence.

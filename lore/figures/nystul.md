# Nystul

**Canon**

Royal magician and Court Mage to Lord British — a mysterious wizard, a good and loyal friend to the king, with the well-being of the realm at heart.

## History

Nystul served as royal magician at Castle Britannia through Ultima VI, Ultima VII, and Ultima Underworld II, advising both king and Avatar. In Ultima Underworld II he worked with the Avatar to shatter the blackrock gem that had sealed the castle's revelers inside the Guardian's dome.

In Ultima Online's telling, Nystul's deeds grew: he created the gate that let the citizens of Trinsic flee the city during the Crisis in Britannia, warned Lord British of the ambush awaiting a Gate Travel to Yew, and opened a gate to the Ethereal Void so that adventurers could retrieve the ghost of Joye. On some shards he decoded Malabelle's book and prepared the lantern lit from the Candle of Love for Dupre's stand at the black temple of Trinsic. (Shard histories vary; these are hedged here as community recollection.)

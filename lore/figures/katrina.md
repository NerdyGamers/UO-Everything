# Katrina

**Canon**

Shepherdess and Companion of the Avatar — one of the eight who joined the Stranger's quest for Avatarhood in Ultima IV.

## History

Katrina appears among the eight companions gathered for the Quest of the Avatar, the shepherdess of the party, each companion exemplifying one of the Eight Virtues. She is found across the later games in Britannia's towns. Details of her later life are sparse in the canonical record, and are hedged here as community recollection.

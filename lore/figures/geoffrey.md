# Geoffrey

**Canon**

Fighter and Companion of the Avatar — who rose to become captain of the guard at Castle Britannia.

## History

Geoffrey was the fighter among the eight companions of Ultima IV. In the years that followed he took service as Lord British's captain of the guard, a post he holds in the Castle Britannia of Ultima VI and Ultima VII, standing watch over the king he once quested beside.

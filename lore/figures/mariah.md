# Mariah

**Canon**

Mage and Companion of the Avatar — a student of ancient languages and a member of the Council of Mages.

## History

Mariah was among the eight companions who joined the Stranger in Ultima IV, the mage of the party. Skilled in magic and scholarship alike, she lent the quest both arcane power and learning. She appears across the later games in Britannia's magical circles.

# Exodus

**Canon**

The offspring of Mondain and Minax and the villain of Ultima III — described in later tellings as "neither human, nor machine": a daemonic, self-aware intelligence bound to a great machine.

## History

Some twenty years after Minax's death, a fiery island rose from the sea and Exodus began its own war on Sosaria. Lord British called for Heroes of the People, and the Stranger's party labored through deep dungeons seeking the means to destroy it.

The answer came from the Time Lord, consulted in the lowest depths of the Dungeon of Time: the Four Cards had to be applied to Exodus's interface in the correct order. Done rightly, the interface keeping the daemon-machine viable was destroyed, and Exodus fell — ending the Third Era of Darkness and the Age of Darkness itself.

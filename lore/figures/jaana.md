# Jaana

**Canon**

Druidess and Companion of the Avatar — one of the eight who joined the Stranger's quest for Avatarhood in Ultima IV.

## History

Jaana appears among the eight companions gathered for the Quest of the Avatar, each exemplifying one of the Eight Virtues. Like her fellow companions, she is found across the later games at the Avatar's side or in Britannia's towns. Details of her later life are sparse in the canonical record, and are hedged here as community recollection.

# Lord British

**Canon**

Ruler of Britannia and the central sovereign of Ultima lore — the in-game persona of series creator Richard Garriott. Nearly every age of Britannian history turns on his decisions, his absences, or his summoning of the Avatar.

## History

In the telling of the cardinal games, British was a youth of Cambridge, on Earth, who crossed into Sosaria through a moongate. The ranger Shamino took him in and gave him his name. Under Shamino's guidance he grew into leadership, and through good deeds — often by summoning the Stranger to do the deed itself — he earned the title of Lord, united the warring city-states of Sosaria under a single banner, and was crowned. The united land was named Britannia in his honor, and he rules from Castle Britannia in the capital city of Britain.

Men from Earth age slowly in Britannia — roughly a tenth of the normal rate — and British's long life spans the entire recorded history of the realm. He invited friends from his homeland to join him, including Blackthorn, Dupre, Iolo, and Chuckles the jester.

British is the summoner of heroes: it was his call that brought the Stranger to defeat Mondain, Minax, and Exodus, and his search for a moral code after the Dark Ages that led to the Eight Virtues and the rise of the Avatar. In Ultima Online's telling, no references to Earth survive, though the Time Lord hints that British comes from another dimension — and unlike other Britannians, he is not the same across all the realities of the shards.

# Dupre

**Canon**

Paladin and Companion of the Avatar — brave, loyal, and among the most steadfast of the eight who quested for Avatarhood in Ultima IV.

## History

Dupre joined the Stranger's party in Ultima IV as the paladin among the eight companions, each exemplifying one of the Eight Virtues. He fought beside the Avatar across the Age of Enlightenment: greeting the hero's return in Ultima V, rescuing the Avatar from the gargoyles' sacrificial altar in Ultima VI alongside Iolo and Shamino, and sailing to Serpent Isle in Ultima VII Part Two.

By the time of Ultima IX, Dupre was long dead — and his ghost was the key to restoring the Shrine of Honor, for only the dead paladin knew its mantra. The Avatar recovered his ashes and spoke with him through the Spirit Speak ritual.

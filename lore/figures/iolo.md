# Iolo

**Canon**

Bard and Companion of the Avatar — a maker of bows and crossbows, an accomplished musician, and one of Lord British's countrymen from Earth.

## History

Iolo was among the friends British brought from his homeland, and among the eight companions who joined the Stranger's quest for Avatarhood in Ultima IV. He appears across the saga: sheltering the wounded Shamino in his forest hut in Ultima V, rescuing the Avatar from the gargoyles in Ultima VI, and sailing to Serpent Isle in Ultima VII Part Two — a voyage prompted by his wife Gwenno's explorations.

In Ultima IX, the aged Iolo stood with Shamino beside the Avatar in the final days, helping uncover Blackthorn's manipulations of the council of lords.

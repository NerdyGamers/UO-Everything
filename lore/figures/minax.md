# Minax

**Canon**

The enchantress of Ultima II, secret apprentice and lover of Mondain, and second of the Triad of Evil. Her powers on maturity are said to have exceeded her master's.

## History

After Mondain's fall, his teachings did not die. Minax rose to challenge the young city-states, raining destruction across the land and — reaching beyond Sosaria itself — twisting time to assault the Stranger's own homeworld of Earth, opening doorways across Earth's history and loosing legions of monsters through them.

The Stranger pursued her with the Quicksword, the one blade that could harm her, and at last faced her in her fiery castle of Shadowguard at the origin of time, destroying her. So ended the Second Era of Darkness.

# The Shadowlords

**Canon**

Three godlike beings of malice — Faulinei, Lord of Falsehood; Astaroth, Lord of Hatred; and Nosfentor, Lord of Cowardice — the hidden power behind Lord Blackthorn's tyranny in Ultima V.

## History

When the Codex of Ultimate Wisdom was raised from the depths of the Great Stygian Abyss, the lifting exposed three shards of Mondain's corrupt Gem of Immortality. The shards possessed three mortals and transformed them into the Shadowlords. They ambushed Lord British's expedition into the Underworld, abducted him, and bent the regent Blackthorn to their will, ruling Britannia through his corrupted laws.

The Avatar destroyed the remaining shards of the Gem — and the three Shadowlords with them — then rescued Lord British from the Underworld by means of the Orb of the Moons.

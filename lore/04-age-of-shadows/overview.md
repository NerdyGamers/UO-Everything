# Age of Shadows

**Canon**

The upheaval: the dark landmass of Malas rises, the paladins of Luna face the necromancers of Umbra, and the artifact era begins.

## The story

A new landmass tore its way into the world: Malas, a shadowed continent of dead cities and darker magic. At its heart stood two cities in eternal opposition — Luna, the shining city of the paladins, and Umbra, the lightless city of the necromancers. Between them lay the Doom Gauntlet, where the mightiest adventurers faced horrors that dropped artifacts of legendary power.

The arrival of Malas changed more than the map. The artifact era began: named items of fixed, extraordinary power entered the world, and the economy of equipment was transformed overnight. Necromancy and Chivalry entered the world's magical traditions as rival disciplines, and the old certainties — of crafting, of housing, of what a warrior could be — were upended in the upheaval. When the dust settled, Britannia was a different world than the one that had been mirrored a generation before.

## Key figures and factions

- **The Paladins of Luna** — champions of light in the shining city.
- **The Necromancers of Umbra** — masters of death in the lightless city.
- **The Doom Gauntlet** — the dungeon at Malas's heart, source of the first [artifacts](../../items/artifacts.md).

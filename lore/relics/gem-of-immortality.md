# Gem of Immortality

A black gem crafted by the evil mage Mondain, meant to render him and his wickedness immortal. Its shattering set the entire history of Britannia — and of Ultima Online's many worlds — in motion.

## History

The gem began as the Sun Ruby of King Wolfgang, Mondain's father, said to harness the power of the Sosarian sun. Wolfgang promised it to Mondain on the condition of a year of study at an abbey; the fifteen-year-old Mondain instead slew his father and, over the rest of his life, twisted the ruby's magic against itself until it became the black Gem of Immortality.

In Ultima I, the Stranger traveled a thousand years through time and shattered the gem at the moment before its enchantment was completed, rendering Mondain mortal. Mondain was slain shortly after.

The gem was believed destroyed, but three shards survived. When Captain Johne's ship, the Ararat, crashed in the Underworld, the shards twisted his companions' souls into the Shadowlords: the Shard of Falsehood birthed Faulinei, the Shard of Hatred birthed Astaroth, and the Shard of Cowardice birthed Nosfentor. In Ultima V the Avatar recovered all three shards from the Underworld and cast them into the Flames of the Principles, destroying the Shadowlords.

## In Ultima Online

The gem's shattering is Ultima Online's in-fiction explanation for its many shards: each shard of the gem holds a divergent reality, and the worlds players inhabit are those shards. Lord British is said to have gathered what gemstone shards he could and secured them in the Ethereal Void to keep them from Minax.

During a UO live event cycle, the Shadowlords sought to recreate the Gem of Immortality to enhance their power. Players thwarted the effort, and shards of the gem were awarded to the top damagers of each Shadowlord — their exact in-game effects were never fully documented, as community recollection has it.

## Notes

**Canon** — established mainline Ultima lore.

Era notes: Age of Darkness (Ultima I); the Shadowlord shards belong to the Age of Enlightenment (Ultima V).

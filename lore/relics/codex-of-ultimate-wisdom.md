# Codex of Ultimate Wisdom

A magical tome said to hold all knowledge that exists or ever existed — the one right and true answer to any question. Bound in leather and gold, its cover bears a sacred seal of six lines and five circles, representing the Eight Virtues and the Three Principles. It always opens to the page its reader seeks, and reads in the reader's own tongue.

## History

The Codex's exact origins are unknown, but it first rested in the Ethereal Void. The great gargoyle seer Naxatilor drew it down to the material plane using specially crafted lenses and an artifact called the Vortex Cube, and for many years it was kept in the Gargish Shrine of Singularity, which appeared to humankind to lie at the bottom of the Great Stygian Abyss.

When the sages of Britannia learned of it, the Codex became the goal of the Quest of the Avatar: the Stranger descended through the eight levels of the Abyss, passed the Codex's trials of the Virtues, and became the first Avatar.

Some years later the Great Council raised the Codex from the Abyss and enshrined it on the Isle of the Avatar in the Shrine of the Codex, guarded by twin Stone Guardians. The upheaval of its raising caused the Abyss to erupt and then fall dormant, and formed the Underworld. During Lord Blackthorn's reign the Codex gave the Avatar the Word of Power for Dungeon Doom. At the height of the Gargoyle War, the Avatar used the Vortex Cube and the two lenses to return the Codex to the Ethereal Void, forging peace between humans and gargoyles. It returned to Britannia by the time of Ultima IX.

## In Ultima Online

The Codex itself does not appear as an obtainable object in Ultima Online; its legacy lives on through the Ethereal Void, which UO lore uses to explain the shards, and through the virtue system it inspired.

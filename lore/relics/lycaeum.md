# Lycaeum

The bastion of Truth on Verity Isle — Britannia's great seat of learning, home of the Council of Wizards, and the stronghold most associated with the mage Mariah.

## History

The Lycaeum is one of the Three Principles' bastions, answering to Truth as Serpent's Hold answers to Courage and Empath Abbey to Love. Years after the Codex was raised, a shrouded girl came to its lord Shalineth with a scrying ball and spoke ominous prophecies of a coming conflict between a hero and abominations of unliving evil; her augury done, the bauble exploded and killed her, and Shalineth recorded her visions.

When her prophecies came true under Lord Blackthorn's Oppression, the Lycaeum stood with Empath Abbey and Serpent's Hold as a bastion of resistance, the Flame of Truth warding off the Shadowlords. Mariah, wounded by a Shadowlord's bolt, found sanctuary here. It was within the Lycaeum that Faulinei, lord of Falsehood, met his end — dispelled when the Avatar cast the Shard of Falsehood into the Flame of Truth.

After Blackthorn's fall the keep's fortifications were dismantled, and its halls slowly merged into the city of Moonglow. By the Gargoyle War it was run by a loose council of wizards, with the scholar Thariand heading its libraries; Mariah, now of the Council, deciphered the silver tablet there and translated the gargoyle Book of Prophecies for the Avatar.

## In Ultima Online

Moonglow stands on Verity Isle in Ultima Online, carrying the Lycaeum's legacy as Britannia's city of mages and scholars, as community recollection has it.

# Great Stygian Abyss

A massive volcanic dungeon on the southern edge of the Isle of the Avatar — long regarded as a haven of evil standing in opposition to the Eight Virtues, and the final trial of the Quest of the Avatar.

## History

In Ultima IV the Abyss was the pilgrim's ultimate goal: within its lowest depths lay the Codex of Ultimate Wisdom, reachable only by those already partially enlightened in the Virtues, and only the Mystic Arms and Armour could protect one inside. The Stranger fought past the pirate fleet of the Bay of Pirates, unsealed the Abyss's mouth with the Book of Truth, the Candle of Love, and the Bell of Courage, then descended eight levels — using the correct Virtue Stone on each level's altar to go deeper — before passing the Codex's trials with the Key of Three Parts and the Word of Passage.

The Abyss later held Cabirus's doomed colony, whose ruins the Avatar explored in Ultima Underworld: warring goblin tribes, the wizard Tyball, the kidnapped Arial, and Garamon's ghost. Gathering the eight talismans of Cabirus and casting them into the volcano, Garamon opened a dimensional gate that banished the Slasher of Veils — at the cost of the settlement, destroyed in the eruption.

## In Ultima Online

Ultima Online reimagined the Abyss as the Stygian Abyss dungeon, added with the 2009 expansion of the same name and entered from the Isle of Fire. Its lower reaches hold the Slasher of Veils as a peerless-style boss, and beyond lies the Tomb of Kings — the passage to the gargoyle land of Ter Mur.

## Notes

**Canon** — established mainline Ultima lore.

Era notes: Age of Enlightenment (Ultima IV, Ultima Underworld); reintroduced for Ultima Online's Stygian Abyss era.

# Ethereal Void

The void between worlds — the original resting place of the Codex of Ultimate Wisdom, the site of the Shrine of Spirituality, and, in Ultima Online's lore, the vault where Lord British hid the shards of the Gem of Immortality.

## History

Before humankind or gargoyle ever saw it, the Codex of Ultimate Wisdom rested in the Ethereal Void, until the seer Naxatilor drew it down with his lenses and the Vortex Cube. The Shrine of Spirituality — the only virtue shrine built outside Britannia — stands within the Void.

At the end of the Gargoyle War, the Avatar returned the Codex to the Ethereal Void, the one act that could make peace between the two races. The Codex would later return to Britannia, as told in Ultima IX.

## In Ultima Online

Ultima Online's lore holds that when Lord British learned the gemstone shards could be used to traverse realities, he gathered as many shards of the Gem of Immortality as he could and secured them in the Ethereal Void, guarding them from Minax's designs. The Void is thus UO's in-fiction explanation for how its many shards remain separate worlds.

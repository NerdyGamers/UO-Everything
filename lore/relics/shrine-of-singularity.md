# Shrine of Singularity

The holiest site of the gargoyle people, deep beneath Britannia — the temple that once held the Codex of Ultimate Wisdom before humankind ever knew of it.

## History

The great gargoyle seer Naxatilor brought the Codex down from the Ethereal Void and housed it here, at what appeared to humankind to lie at the bottom of the Great Stygian Abyss. The Avatar reached it in Ultima IV by descending through the Abyss and proving enlightened in the Eight Virtues, thus becoming the Avatar of Britannia.

Shortly after, the Great Council removed the Codex to the surface world. The loss devastated the gargoyle realm and, in the upheaval, formed the Underworld. When the Avatar returned during the quest against the Shadowlords, only a boiling pit of lava remained — from which the hero retrieved the lost Mystic Arms.

During the Gargoyle War the Avatar reached the temple by hot-air balloon from the gargoyle lands. The shrine demanded to know for whom the Codex was sought; when the hero answered for both races and had shown virtue in the cause of peace, it sent the Avatar to the gargoyle shrines of Control, Passion, and Diligence to learn the mantra of Singularity — then on the sacred quest to return the Codex to the Ethereal Void. It also kept the gargoyles' other two sacred books, the Book of Circles and the Book of Prophecies.

## In Ultima Online

The shrine has no direct in-game presence in Ultima Online; its legacy survives through the gargoyles of Ter Mur and the Stygian Abyss storyline, as community recollection has it.

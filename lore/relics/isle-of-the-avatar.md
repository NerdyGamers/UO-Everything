# Isle of the Avatar

The volcanic island that rose from the sea when the Great Council raised the Codex of Ultimate Wisdom from the depths — Britannia's most sacred ground and one of its most dangerous.

## History

The isle was born of the same upheaval that lifted the Codex from the Great Stygian Abyss in 97 A.B.: volcanic craters, steaming fumaroles, and sulfurous ash cover it, and few brave its hazards. The Great Council built the Shrine of the Codex over the Abyss's former entrance, in a valley warded by twin Stone Guardians that admit only those on a sacred quest. The Shrine of Humility also stands on the isle, and the sealed entrance to dungeon Hythloth lies here.

The raising's disturbances opened the vast cavern network called the Underworld, and the Abyss became one of its entrances. In later years Baron Almric's people settled the isle, until the wizard Tyball's plot, the kidnapping of Arial, and Garamon's banishing of the Slasher of Veils ended in the volcano's eruption and the settlers' flight.

By the account of Ultima IX, the isle vanished during the Cataclysm, leaving only a veil of fog and renewed bitterness between gargoyle and human — though accounts of the Cataclysm itself are disputed among fans, and are best treated as community recollection.

## In Ultima Online

In Ultima Online, the Isle of Fire holds the entrance to the Stygian Abyss dungeon, introduced with the Stygian Abyss expansion — the gateway to Ter Mur and the Tomb of Kings, with the Slasher of Veils waiting in its depths.

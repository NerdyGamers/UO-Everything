# Shrines of the Virtues

The sacred sites of Britannia's virtue system: eight shrines, one for each of the Eight Virtues, plus three earlier shrines to the Three Principles. Seekers meditate at them to grow in virtue, and they anchor the Quest of the Avatar.

## History

At the founding of Britannia (0 A.B.), Lord British established the Eight Virtues and ordered the shrines built: Compassion, Honesty, Valor, Sacrifice, Justice, Honor, Humility, and Spirituality. Each stands near the city embodying its virtue; only the Shrine of Spirituality lies outside Britannia, in the Ethereal Void. Azure of Minoc crafted the eight runes of virtue, and the Council of Wizards set colored stones of virtue within the dungeons.

Lord British also built three shrines to the Three Principles — Truth, Love, and Courage — on the Isle of Fire. They were lost when the isle sank, and were not found again until the time of Ultima VII.

The shrines' great purpose was the Quest of the Avatar: a seeker who had performed virtuous deeds could meditate at a shrine for three cycles and attain partial Avatarhood in that virtue. Each shrine has a mantra learned from the virtuous towns — Honesty AHM, Compassion MU, Valor RA, Justice BEH, Sacrifice CAH, Honor SUMM, Spirituality OM, Humility LUM. The shrines counsel those who meditate and can send them on sacred quests.

During the Gargoyle War the eight shrines were seized by gargoyle warriors and later freed by the Avatar, rune and mantra in hand. Under Blackthorn's Oppression the shrines were targets for desecration, and the Avatar guarded them.

## In Ultima Online

The eight shrines stand in Britannia in Ultima Online, and UO's virtue system — Compassion, Honesty, Honor, Humility, Justice, Sacrifice, Spirituality, Valor — echoes their role, as community recollection has it.

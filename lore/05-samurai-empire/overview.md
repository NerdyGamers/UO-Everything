# Samurai Empire

**Canon**

Tokuno: the island empire of samurai tradition, its emissaries to Britannia, and the Treasures of Tokuno.

## The story

Across the seas lay the Tokuno Islands — Isamu-Jima, Makoto-Jima, and Homare-Jima — home to an island empire of ancient samurai tradition, ruled from the city of Zento. When Tokuno's emissaries reached Britannia, they brought with them the ways of the warrior: [Bushido](../../skills/combat/bushido.md), the code of the samurai, and [Ninjitsu](../../skills/combat/ninjitsu.md), the shadow arts.

The isles held their own dangers. The restless dead walked their haunted places, and the demonic Oni — ogre-magi of terrible power — stalked the islands' wilds, figures straight out of the yokai tales the islanders told their children. In time a great trove surfaced: the Treasures of Tokuno, minor artifacts scattered among the islands' creatures, drew adventurers from across the world to the empire's shores, and the isles' warriors and monsters alike became legend in Britannia.

## Key figures and factions

- **The Emperor of Tokuno** — ruler of the island empire, seated in Zento.
- **The samurai and the ninja** — the warrior traditions of the isles.
- **The Oni** — the demonic ogre-magi of Tokuno's wilds, drawn from the islands' yokai folklore.

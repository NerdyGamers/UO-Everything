# Mondain's Legacy — The Elven Resurgence

**Canon**

In the deep forests of Ilshenar, the elves of Heartwood emerged from long seclusion as their ancient enemies — the corrupted peerless ancients — stirred once more.

## The story

The elves are one of Britannia's oldest peoples, and for ages they kept to themselves in Heartwood, a living city grown within Ilshenar's woods. The age called Mondain's Legacy takes its name from the long shadow the first great evil cast over the land: old corruptions, old bargains, old wounds reopening.

The elves' grief centers on the peerless — beings of terrible power, each a corrupted echo of something once noble or natural. A ki-rin twisted into horror, a sorceress drunk on dark power, a shapeshifter whose true face no one has seen. These ancients could not be met by armies; they could only be confronted by small bands of champions bearing the right keys, in lairs hidden across the land.

The elven return also reopened an old question: what place do the elder races hold in a Britannia dominated by humans? Many elves remember the wars and betrayals of ages past, and their trust is not lightly given. Adventurers who aid Heartwood find cautious allies — and learn that the elves' war against the dark is older than any human kingdom.

## Key figures and factions

- **The elven council of Heartwood** — keepers of elven lore and the source of the Heartwood quests; they direct champions against the peerless.
- **Dread Horn** — a ki-rin, the noble spirit-stag of elven myth, corrupted into a peerless horror. The elves' grief made manifest.
- **Lady Melisande** — a dark sorceress of peerless power, served by twisted followers.
- **Travesty** — a peerless shapeshifter; its true form remains unknown, which is the point.
- **The peerless ancients** — also including Chief Paroxysmus, the Shimmering Effusion, and Meraktus, each with their own lair, keys, and legend (see [peerless encounters](../../mechanics/peerless-encounters.md)).

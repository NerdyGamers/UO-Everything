# Beltran's Guide to Guilds

A guide to Britannia's guild system, attributed to Beltran. It is one of the stealable books found in the world.

## Contents

The book explains how guilds are formed and run: chartering a guild, recruiting members, guild titles, and the role guilds play in Britannian society. Written as a practical handbook for adventurers considering guild membership.

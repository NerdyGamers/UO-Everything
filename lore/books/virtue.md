# Virtue

The classic text on the Eight Virtues, and one of the stealable books found in the world.

## Contents

The foundational statement of the Virtues — Honesty, Compassion, Valor, Justice, Sacrifice, Honor, Spirituality, and Humility — and the Three Principles of Truth, Love, and Courage from which they derive. The closest thing Britannia has to scripture.

## Notes

**Canon** — an official in-game text.

A special printing of this book by Lord British appears as a quest item; the standard text is the stealable volume. See [Quest of the Virtues](quest-of-the-virtues.md).

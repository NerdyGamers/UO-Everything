# A Politic Call to Anarchy

A political tract advocating anarchy, and one of the stealable books found in the world.

## Contents

A fiery argument against kings, councils, and all constituted authority — the author holds that Britannia would be freer and more virtuous without rulers. Circulated quietly; owning a copy raises eyebrows.

## Notes

**Canon** — an official in-game text.

A counterpoint to the loyalist histories; see [History](../../history/).

# Classic Children's Tales, Volume 2

A collection of children's stories, and one of the stealable books found in the world. The second volume of the set.

## Contents

A gathering of short fables and bedtime stories told to Britannian children — moral tales of clever heroes, foolish villains, and the virtues rewarded.

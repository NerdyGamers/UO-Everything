# Dimensional Travel, a Monograph

A scholarly monograph on traveling between places — and perhaps worlds — by magical means, and one of the stealable books found in the world.

## Contents

A mage-scholar's treatise on moongates, recall magic, and the theory of moving through space by arcane means. Dense, academic, and faintly disapproving of dabblers.

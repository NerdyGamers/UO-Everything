# The Burning of Trinsic

A historical account of the sacking and burning of the city of Trinsic, and one of the stealable books found in the world.

## Contents

The book recounts the attack on Trinsic and the city's burning — the panic in the streets, the defense of the walls, and the aftermath. One of the darker chapters of Britannian history as told by its survivors.

## Notes

**Canon** — an official in-game text.

Trinsic is the city of Honor; see [Britannia](../../locations/britannia.md). Details of the account survive as community recollection.

# Britannian Flora: a Casual Guide

A layman's guide to the plant life of Britannia, and one of the stealable books found in the world.

## Contents

An informal survey of Britannia's common plants and flowers — what they look like, where they grow, and which are useful to alchemists and cooks. Less rigorous than a herbalist's manual, and more charming for it.

## Notes

**Canon** — an official in-game text.

See [Alchemy](../../skills/magic/alchemy.md) for the craft that makes use of many of these plants.

# An Alchemist's Abomination

A Page of Lore from the Alchemist's Abomination event, collected in the Book of Lore introduced with Publish 110.

## Contents

The book chronicles the Alchemist's Abomination event — the twisted alchemical horrors unleashed and the adventurers who put them down. Written as a record of the crisis.

## Notes

**Canon** — an official in-game text.

The Book of Lore is an Inscription-crafted book (45 Inscription, 24 blank scrolls) into which Pages of Lore are placed permanently — once added, a page cannot be removed.

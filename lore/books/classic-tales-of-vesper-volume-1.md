# Classic Tales of Vesper Volume 1

A collection of tales from the city of Vesper, and one of the stealable books found in the world.

## Contents

Stories gathered from Vesper — a city of mages and merchants on the edge of the wilderness — including local legends, cautionary tales, and accounts of the strange happenings for which the city is known.

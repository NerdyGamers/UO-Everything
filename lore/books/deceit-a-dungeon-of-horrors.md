# Deceit: A Dungeon of Horrors

A guide to the Deceit dungeon, and one of the stealable books found in the world.

## Contents

A traveler's warning about Deceit — its twisting passages, its undead inhabitants, and the horrors that await the unprepared. Part gazetteer, part survival guide.

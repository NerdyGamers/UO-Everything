# Quest of the Virtues

A two-volume work on the Quest of the Avatar and the Eight Virtues, and one of the stealable books found in the world.

## Contents

Across two volumes, the book traces the Quest of the Virtues — the Avatar's journey to master Honesty, Compassion, Valor, Justice, Sacrifice, Honor, Spirituality, and Humility. Part history, part devotional text.

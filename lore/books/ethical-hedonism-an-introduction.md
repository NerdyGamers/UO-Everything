# Ethical Hedonism: an Introduction

A philosophical tract, and one of the stealable books found in the world.

## Contents

An introduction to the philosophy of ethical hedonism — the pursuit of pleasure tempered by principle. The author argues that joy need not be the enemy of virtue, a mildly scandalous position in conservative Britannian circles.

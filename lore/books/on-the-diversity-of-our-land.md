# On the Diversity of our Land

A survey of the varied lands of the world, and one of the stealable books found in the world.

## Contents

A traveler's survey of the world's diversity — its facets, its climates, its peoples. Written with the wonder of someone who has walked from the deserts of the Lost Lands to the islands of Tokuno and found something new at every step.

# Talking to Wisps

A guide to communicating with wisps, and one of the stealable books found in the world.

## Contents

Practical advice — as practical as advice about wisps gets — on approaching, addressing, and understanding the strange lights of the wilderness. The author claims fluency; readers remain skeptical.

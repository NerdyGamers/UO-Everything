# The Major Trade Association

A book about Britannia's trade associations, and one of the stealable books found in the world.

## Contents

An account of the major trade associations — the merchant leagues and craft fellowships that move goods across Britannia. Covers their charters, their rivalries, and their influence on prices from Britain to Vesper.

## Notes

**Canon** — an official in-game text.

Companion volume to [The Rankings of the Trade](the-rankings-of-the-trade.md).

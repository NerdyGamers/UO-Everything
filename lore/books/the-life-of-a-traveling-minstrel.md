# The Life of a Traveling Minstrel

The memoir of a wandering minstrel, and one of the stealable books found in the world.

## Contents

First-person recollections of life on the road — the taverns, the patrons, the songs, and the occasional narrow escape. Warm, funny, and fond of exaggeration, as minstrel memoirs tend to be.

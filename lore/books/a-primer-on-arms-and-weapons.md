# A Primer on Arms and Weapons

An introductory guide to arms and armor, and one of the stealable books found in the world.

## Contents

A beginner's survey of Britannian weaponry — swords, bows, polearms, and the armor worn against them. Practical rather than scholarly, aimed at young fighters choosing their first blade.

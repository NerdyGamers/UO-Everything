# Regarding Llamas

The famous treatise on llamas, and one of the stealable books found in the world.

## Contents

A deadpan, thorough, and faintly obsessive study of the llama — habits, humors, and uses. Beloved by readers precisely because it treats its humble subject with utter seriousness.

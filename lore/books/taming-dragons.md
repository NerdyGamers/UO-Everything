# Taming Dragons

A guide to the taming of dragons, and one of the stealable books found in the world.

## Contents

The author's hard-won counsel on taming dragons — the patience required, the dangers courted, and the bond formed with a successfully tamed drake. Part manual, part memoir of singed eyebrows.

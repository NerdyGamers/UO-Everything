# The Rankings of the Trade

A ranking of Britannia's traders and trade houses, and one of the stealable books found in the world.

## Contents

A gossipy, much-disputed ranking of the realm's merchants and trade associations — who is rising, who is falling, and who cheats at weights. New editions are eagerly awaited and hotly contested.

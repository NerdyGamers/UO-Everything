# A Grammar of Orcish

A dictionary and grammar of the Orcish tongue, and one of the stealable books found in the world.

## Contents

A scholarly attempt to systematize the language of Britannia's orcs — common words, phrases, and the rough grammar of Orcish speech. Invaluable for anyone trying to parley with (or eavesdrop on) orc tribes.

# Treatise on Alchemy

A scholarly work on alchemy, and one of the stealable books found in the world.

## Contents

A systematic treatise on the alchemical art — reagents, mortars, and the brewing of potions from heal to explosion. Drier than the practitioner's handbooks, but thorough.

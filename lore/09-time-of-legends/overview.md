# Time of Legends — Eodon and the Shadowguard

**Canon**

Beyond the charted seas lies Eodon, a valley lost to time where tribal peoples dwell among dinosaurs — and where the insectoid Myrmidex swarm threatens to devour everything, while the trials of Shadowguard test the worthiest champions.

## The story

Eodon is a place the maps forgot: a vast valley sealed away from the world, where the old ways never died. Tribal humans hunt and farm as their ancestors did, dinosaurs stalk the jungles, and the valley's strange magics keep it hidden from the empires beyond.

The valley's peace is shattered by the Myrmidex — a relentless insectoid swarm pouring from their hives to consume everything in their path. The tribes, brave but outmatched, turn to outsiders for aid, and adventurers find themselves fighting a war of extermination against an enemy that never stops coming.

Above it all stands Shadowguard, a fortress of trials older than any kingdom. Its rooftop is the proving ground of legends: champions climb through its chambers to face the final test, and those who survive are written into the valley's songs. Eodon rewards the bold — and buries the careless.

## Key figures and factions

- **The tribes of Eodon** — the valley's human peoples, each with their own customs, hunters, and grudges.
- **The Myrmidex** — the swarm: insectoid hordes driven by hunger and their queens, the existential threat of the age.
- **The guardians of Shadowguard** — the trial-keepers of the fortress, and the champions who climb to its roof.

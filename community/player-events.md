# Player Events

Player-run events are the community's own content: auctions, tournaments, weddings, tavern nights, and scavenger hunts organized by players rather than the developers. On many shards they are as much a draw as the game's official content.

## How it works

The classic formats have run for decades. **Auctions** — a player auctioneer takes lots from sellers and hammers them off to a crowd, usually at a house or tavern — move rares and high-end gear. **Tournaments** range from duel brackets to full PvP events with prize purses. **Weddings** are in-character ceremonies, often with custom venues and receptions. **Tavern nights** gather roleplayers and regulars for an evening of social play. **Scavenger hunts and races** send participants across the world for hidden items or checkpoints.

These differ from official content in kind: player events are organized through shard forums, Discord, and in-game bulletin boards, run on the organizer's rules, and live or die on reputation. The developers' own events — EM-run storylines and seasonal content — are documented separately (see [Live Events](../history/live-events.md)); the two scenes overlap constantly, with player events filling the gaps between official arcs.

## Era notes

Player events predate almost every official event system — auctions and tournaments were running within the game's first years. Forum and later Discord organization changed how events are advertised but not how they run.

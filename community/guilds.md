# Guilds

Guilds are Ultima Online's player organizations: persistent groups with a shared name, abbreviation, chat channel, and the ability to declare war on other guilds. They are the backbone of UO's social and political life, from crafting cooperatives to PvP war machines.

## How it works

A guild is founded by a character placing a guildstone (or using the guild menu in later eras) and recruiting members, who wear the guild's name and three-letter abbreviation in their title bar. The guildmaster controls ranks and can appoint members to titles like Emissary or Warlord, which appear after the character's name. Members share a guild chat channel and can set the guild's public charter and recruitment stance.

Guilds can declare **war** on other guilds through mutual or one-sided declarations; warring guilds may attack each other freely without criminal or murder-count consequences (see [Notoriety](../mechanics/notoriety.md)). **Alliances** let multiple guilds band together under a shared alliance name, coordinating wars and chat across guild lines. Guild politics — feuds, mergers, betrayals, and server-wide wars — have driven some of UO's most storied player history.

## Era notes

Guilds date to the earliest eras of the game, when the guildstone was a physical object that could be captured or destroyed. Later publishes moved guild management into menus, added alliances, and hardened guildstones against griefing, but the core loop — recruit, title, war, ally — is unchanged.

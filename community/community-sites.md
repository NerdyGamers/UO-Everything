# Community Knowledge Sites

Ultima Online's documentation has always lived mostly outside the game: fan sites, wikis, forums, and chat communities that catalog mechanics, maps, and item data the client never explains.

## How it works

The community knowledge infrastructure grew up around the game's gaps. **Stratics** (UO Stratics) has been the long-running hub for news, forums, and guides since the early eras. **UOGuide** is the community-maintained wiki documenting items, mechanics, quests, and publishes in detail. The official **UO.com** site and forums carry publish notes, event announcements, and developer communication from Broadsword. Beyond those, each era's players congregate in **Discord servers** — per-shard communities, guild Discords, trade channels, and freeshard discords — plus Reddit communities and independent wikis that cover niche topics like rares collecting or specific expansions.

Because UO predates modern in-game tutorials, these sites are effectively the manual: players learn templates, crafting math, spawn mechanics, and event rules from community documentation rather than from the client. The quality varies — long-running references like UOGuide and Stratics are generally reliable, while random forum posts and old guides may describe mechanics from publishes that no longer apply. Checking the era a guide was written for is a basic survival skill.

## Era notes

Community sites have tracked every era of the game; the shift from official forums to Discord in the 2010s moved day-to-day discussion off the web and into chat servers, which are harder to search but faster for trade and event coordination.

# The PvP Scene

Player-versus-player combat is one of Ultima Online's defining cultures: Felucca field fights, duel pits, and organized tournaments, with a scene that has refined templates, tactics, and etiquette over decades. It is distinct from the faction system, which is one venue for PvP among several (see [Factions](../mechanics/factions.md)).

## How it works

Most PvP happens in **Felucca**, the ruleset where non-consensual combat is legal and murder counts apply (see [Notoriety](../mechanics/notoriety.md)). **Field fighting** — roaming groups clashing over champion spawns, dungeons, and resource spots — is the open-world form. **Duels** are consensual one-on-one or small-group fights, often in guild houses or arenas, used to settle scores and test templates. **PvP tournaments**, run by players or EMs, bracket fighters for prizes and prestige.

The scene runs on templates: purpose-built character builds (mage, archer, dexxer, necro-mage, and their many hybrids) tuned around the [combat](../mechanics/combat-system.md), [spellcasting](../mechanics/spellcasting.md), and item-property systems. Gear, consumables, and split-second reactions matter as much as build. Long-standing etiquette — looting norms, rematch customs, trash talk boundaries — varies by shard but is taken seriously where it exists.

## Era notes

PvP is as old as the game; the Trammel/Felucca split (Renaissance, 2000) concentrated non-consensual PvP in Felucca and reshaped the scene permanently. Publish-era balance changes — special moves, item properties, new skills — have repeatedly redefined the dominant templates.

# Special Moves

Weapon special moves are mana-cost techniques that add effects — armor penetration, bleeding, disarming, knockback, and more — to a weapon swing. Each weapon carries a primary and a secondary move, and the Samurai Empire expansion added a further set tied to Bushido and Ninjitsu.

## How it works

**Requirements.** Activating a special move generally requires 70 or more skill in both the weapon skill and [Tactics](../skills/combat/tactics.md) (community recollection on the exact threshold). Moves cost mana, typically 10–30 points depending on the move (community recollection on individual costs).

**Notable moves.** Common examples include Armor Ignore (bypasses the target's armor), Mortal Strike (wounds that resist healing), Bleed Attack (damage over time), Concussion Blow (bonus damage against high-mana targets), Crushing Blow (large single-hit bonus), Disarm (knocks the weapon from the target's hand), Dismount (unhorses a mounted target), and Whirlwind (strikes surrounding enemies). The exact primary/secondary pairing varies by weapon type.

**Bushido and Ninjitsu moves.** Samurai Empire (2004) added moves such as Lightning Strike (Bushido — a high-accuracy critical strike) and the Ninjitsu animal forms, which transform the user and grant form-specific abilities. These draw on the Bushido ([Bushido](../skills/combat/bushido.md)) and Ninjitsu ([Ninjitsu](../skills/combat/ninjitsu.md)) skills rather than the standard weapon-move list.

**Tactical role.** Special moves turn mana into burst damage or control, which is why warrior templates carry enough mana and mana regeneration to fire them repeatedly. Slow, hard-hitting weapons pair well with single-hit moves like Crushing Blow; fast weapons favor damage-over-time moves like Bleed Attack.

## Era notes

Special moves debuted with Age of Shadows (2003). Samurai Empire (2004) added the Bushido/Ninjitsu sets. Later publishes (notably around Publish 71) reworked several moves' costs and effects.

# Champion Spawns

Champion spawns are staged PvM encounters in Ilshenar and Felucca dungeons. Killing waves of spawn creatures advances an altar through skull-marked tiers until a champion boss emerges.

## How it works
Each spawn site has an altar and a themed set of creatures (undead, spiders, ratmen, and others). Killing spawn creatures advances the encounter through tiers, shown as skulls on the altar; at the final tier the champion appears. The six champions are Barracoon the Piper, Mephitis, Neira the Necromancer, Rikktor, Semidar, and the Harrower — the most powerful of the set (community recollection on Harrower summoning specifics).

When the champion dies, power scrolls are distributed to the top damagers (see [Power Scrolls](power-scrolls.md)). Felucca spawn sites are contested PvP ground, and raiding another group's spawn to steal the champion kill is a long-standing facet pastime.

## Era notes
Introduced with Age of Shadows (2003). Felucca champion spawns are the only source of 120 power scrolls; spawns under Trammel rules cap lower.

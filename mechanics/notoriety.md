# Notoriety

Notoriety is the flag system that marks characters Innocent, Criminal, or Murderer — shown by the color of their name highlight — and governs the consequences of player killing.

## How it works

- **Innocent (blue)** is the default. Attacking an innocent, stealing from one, or similar hostile acts flag you **Criminal (gray)** for a short time; while gray, anyone may attack you freely.
- **Murderer (red):** killing an innocent player grants murder counts. At five or more counts you are a murderer. Counts come in short- and long-term varieties and decay over time (community recollection puts the decay at roughly 8 hours and 40 hours respectively).
- Killing a murderer grants no counts, and fighting back against someone who attacked you first never flags you criminal.
- Guildmates and allies show **green** to each other; declared enemies show **orange**.

The consequences for reds are severe: town guards attack murderers on sight in guarded areas, and a slain murderer suffers **stat loss** — temporarily reduced stats and skills after resurrection. Destroying reds, meanwhile, advances the [Justice virtue](virtues.md).

## Era notes

The flag system is launch-era. The Trammel/Felucca split (Renaissance, 2000) confined most of its bite to Felucca, where non-consensual PvP lives on.

# Karma and Fame

Every character carries two separate reputation ratings: **Karma** (moral standing, from −32,000 to +32,000) and **Fame** (renown, from 0 to about 32,000). Together they decide the titles displayed on your paperdoll.

## How it works

**Karma** rises when you destroy evil creatures and falls when you commit crimes or slay the innocent. It is persistent — it never decays on its own, changing only through your actions.

**Fame** rises when you kill monsters, with stronger foes granting more, and it slowly decays over time — so high fame has to be maintained through continued deeds.

Your paperdoll combines a karma prefix with a fame suffix: paragons of virtue read as *Glorious Lord* or *Glorious Lady*, while the utterly corrupt sink to *Outcast*. (Exact title thresholds are community-documented rather than officially published.)

Karma and fame shape how the world treats you: NPCs react to your standing, and sacrificing fame advances the [Sacrifice virtue](virtues.md). Letting your karma collapse through murder, on the other hand, feeds the [notoriety](notoriety.md) system instead.

## Era notes

Karma and fame date to the launch era, and the paperdoll title display has been the standard way to read another character's standing ever since.

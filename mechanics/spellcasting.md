# Spellcasting

Casting a spell requires mana, reagents (usually), and an uninterrupted casting window. Higher-circle spells take longer to cast and cost more mana, and taking damage mid-cast can disrupt the spell.

## How it works

**Casting time.** Spells are organized into circles (1st through 8th for Magery; other spellbooks have their own structures). Higher circles take longer to cast — roughly an extra quarter-second per circle for Magery (community recollection on exact timings). Faster Casting (FC) from equipment shortens cast time, capped for practical purposes (community recollection: 4 for Magery). Faster Cast Recovery (FCR) shortens the brief recovery delay after a spell completes, letting the caster chain spells more quickly.

**Mana and reagents.** Each spell has a mana cost scaling with its circle; insufficient mana fizzles the cast. Most spells also consume reagents, though the Lower Reagent Cost (LRC) item property can reduce or eliminate reagent use — at 100% LRC no reagents are consumed.

**Interruption.** Taking damage while casting can disrupt the spell, with heavier hits more likely to break concentration. The Protection spell trades casting speed for immunity to interruption. Casting also requires line of sight to the target for targeted spells.

**Effectiveness.** [Evaluating Intelligence](../skills/magic/evaluating-intelligence.md) scales Magery spell damage, [Inscription](../skills/magic/inscription.md) adds a small spell-damage bonus at high skill, and [Meditation](../skills/magic/meditation.md) governs mana regeneration. Magic Resistance ([Magic Resistance](../skills/magic/magic-resistance.md)) lets targets partially resist hostile spells through a contested check.

**Other spellbooks.** Chivalry, Necromancy, Mysticism, Spellweaving, Bushido, and Ninjitsu each have their own casting rules, costs, and secondary-skill requirements — see their skill pages ([Chivalry](../skills/magic/chivalry.md), [Necromancy](../skills/magic/necromancy.md), [Mysticism](../skills/magic/mysticism.md), [Spellweaving](../skills/magic/spellweaving.md)).

## Era notes

Faster Casting and Faster Cast Recovery arrived with the Age of Shadows item overhaul (2003). Later publishes adjusted FC/FCR caps and added LRC as a standard item property.

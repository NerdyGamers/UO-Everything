# Power Scrolls

Power scrolls raise a skill's cap above 100, in steps of 105, 110, 115, and 120. Each scroll is skill-specific and they drop from champion bosses (see [Champion Spawns](champion-spawns.md)).

## How it works
To use a scroll, the skill must already be at its current cap — a character needs 100 real skill to consume a 105 scroll, 105 to consume a 110, and so on (community-documented rule). Each scroll is consumed on use and raises that skill's individual cap to the scroll's value. Power scrolls exist for a wide range of combat, magic, crafting, and trade skills.

120 scrolls drop only from Felucca champion spawns; spawns under Trammel rules cap at 115 (community-documented). A related item, the stat scroll (+5 through +25), raises the total stat cap above its base of 225.

## Era notes
Introduced with Age of Shadows (2003) alongside champion spawns, breaking the original 100-point skill ceiling and reshaping character templates around 120-point skills.

# Factions

Factions are a PvP war system in Felucca. Players join one of four warring factions, fight over town sigils, and earn silver and ranks by killing enemy faction members.

## How it works
Players enlist at their faction's stronghold stone in Felucca. The four factions are the True Britannians, the Council of Mages, the Shadowlords, and Minax (the Following of Minax). Each faction holds a fortified stronghold and contests control of Feluccan towns.

Town control revolves around sigils: faction-aligned towns have sigil posts, and stealing an enemy faction's sigil and carrying it back to your stronghold corrupts it — fully corrupted sigils transfer the town to your faction. Killing enemy faction members earns silver, a faction currency spent at faction vendors on blessed clothing, armor, and other faction gear, plus kill points that feed a rank ladder from 1 to 10 (ranks decay without continued kills, per community recollection). Members elect a Commanding Lord, and leaving a faction carries a waiting period before rejoining (community recollection on exact length).

Faction members fight freely in Felucca, and faction gear stays blessed only while the wearer remains in the faction. See [Notoriety](notoriety.md) for how faction warfare interacts with the criminal system.

## Era notes
Introduced with Renaissance (2000) alongside the Felucca/Trammel split, giving the PvP facet an organized war system. Later publishes adjusted silver costs, rank decay, and sigil mechanics (exact publish numbers vary by source).

# Stats: Strength, Dexterity, Intelligence

The three stats underpin every character: **Strength** (health and carrying), **Dexterity** (stamina and speed), and **Intelligence** (mana).

## How it works

- **Strength:** each point is one hit point. Strength also raises carrying capacity and adds to melee damage.
- **Dexterity:** each point is one stamina. Stamina governs swing speed, and Dexterity speeds up bandaging.
- **Intelligence:** each point is one mana.

The three stats share a **225-point total cap**. Individual stats can rise past 100 (community recollection on the exact per-stat ceiling), limited by the shared cap. Stats increase through skill use — spellcasting raises Intelligence, fighting raises Strength and Dexterity — and each stat carries a lock on the paperdoll: set it to rise, fall, or stay locked to steer gains where you want them.

Items can push stats above their base values; modified stats display in blue while base values show in white.

## Era notes

The 225-point stat cap and the lock-arrow system are long-standing. Stat bonuses on equipment (strength, dexterity, and intelligence on gear) grew greatly in importance with the item-property eras.

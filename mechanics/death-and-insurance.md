# Death and Item Insurance

Death in UO is a setback, not an ending: you rise as a ghost, and how much of your gear comes back with you depends on insurance and blessings.

## How it works

On death you become a **ghost** — unable to touch the world, your speech garbled to the living. Your corpse remains where you fell with everything you carried, free for anyone to loot.

**Resurrection** comes several ways: the 8th-circle Magery spell Resurrection, another player's [Chivalry](../skills/magic/chivalry.md) spell Noble Sacrifice (which costs the caster their own life), NPC healers in town for a gold fee, wandering healers in the wild, or the [Sacrifice virtue](virtues.md), which lets a Knight-tier ghost resurrect themselves.

**Item insurance** is opt-in per item through the context menu: insured gear stays with you through death, for a flat gold fee per item (community recollection puts the fee at 600 gp). **Blessed** items — ethereal mounts, certain quest rewards, clothing touched by a clothing bless deed — never drop at all and need no insurance.

Murderers face an extra penalty on top: [stat loss](notoriety.md) after resurrection.

## Era notes

Item insurance arrived with Age of Shadows (2003); before it, everything lootable on your corpse was simply gone. Blessed new-player and reward items predate it.

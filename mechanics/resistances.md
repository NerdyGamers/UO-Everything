# Resistances

Every creature and player has five resistance values — Physical, Fire, Cold, Poison, and Energy — each reducing incoming damage of the matching type by its percentage.

## How it works

When damage lands, the game checks its damage type. A weapon dealing 50% fire and 50% physical damage, for example, is reduced separately by the target's Fire and Physical resists. Each point of resistance negates roughly one percent of that damage type, so 70 Fire resist means 70% less fire damage taken.

**The five resists:**
- **Physical** — reduced by armor; applies to most weapon damage.
- **Fire** — fire-based spells and weapons.
- **Cold** — cold spells and effects.
- **Poison** — poison damage, including poisoned weapons and poison-field ticks.
- **Energy** — energy-based spells such as Energy Bolt and Chain Lightning.

**Caps.** Player resists are capped at 70 under normal circumstances. Certain temporary effects and item properties can push individual resists above the cap (community recollection on which effects and by how much). There is no meaningful minimum — resists can sit at 0, leaving the character fully exposed to that damage type.

**Sources of resists.** Armor pieces carry the bulk of a character's resists. Magic Resistance skill ([Magic Resistance](../skills/magic/magic-resistance.md)) reduces spell damage through a separate contested check rather than the resist values directly. Spells, potions, and item properties can raise or lower resists temporarily.

## Era notes

The five-resist model arrived with Age of Shadows (2003), replacing the older armor-rating absorption system. Later publishes added resist-modifying item properties and refined how spell damage interacts with resists.

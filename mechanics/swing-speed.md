# Swing Speed

Swing speed determines how quickly a weapon attacks. Every weapon has a base speed, and the actual delay between swings is reduced by the wielder's Stamina and Swing Speed Increase (SSI) from equipment.

## How it works

**Base weapon speed.** Each weapon type has an inherent speed rating — daggers and other light weapons swing fast, heavy weapons like hammers swing slowly but hit harder per blow. The choice between fast/light and slow/heavy is one of the core equipment tradeoffs.

**Stamina.** Higher current Stamina shortens the delay between swings. Because Stamina drains as you take damage and perform actions, a fighter's attack rate can slow down over a long fight — which is why stamina regeneration and stamina-increasing items matter to warrior templates. The [Focus](../skills/combat/focus.md) skill aids stamina recovery.

**Swing Speed Increase.** SSI is an item property that directly reduces swing delay by a percentage. It is one of the most sought-after properties on warrior equipment. The total SSI bonus is capped (community recollection: 60%), and there is a hard floor on how fast any weapon can swing — no combination of stamina and SSI can push attacks faster than one swing every 1.25 seconds (community recollection on the exact floor).

**Practical effect.** Template builders balance weapon speed against damage per hit: a fast weapon with moderate damage can out-damage a slow weapon over time, especially with high SSI and stamina — but slow weapons pair better with high-damage [Special Moves](special-moves.md), which deal their bonus on a single hit.

## Era notes

SSI arrived with the Age of Shadows item-property overhaul (2003). Later publishes tuned the SSI cap and the interaction between stamina and swing delay.

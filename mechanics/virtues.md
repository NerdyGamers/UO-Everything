# Virtues

The Eight Virtues — Honesty, Compassion, Valor, Justice, Sacrifice, Honor, Spirituality, Humility — are Ultima's moral framework, derived from the Three Principles of Truth, Love, and Courage. In UO they are also a gameplay system: performing virtuous acts advances each virtue through the tiers of Seeker, Follower, and Knight, unlocking powers along the way.

## How it works

The system is reached through the virtue icons on the paperdoll. Each virtue advances through related deeds:

- **Compassion** (a heart): gained through NPC escort quests. Knights can resurrect other players, with the health restored scaling by tier.
- **Honesty** (an open hand): designed but never implemented.
- **Honor** (a chalice): gained by honoring foes and slaying them alone. Knights can walk briefly among monsters without being attacked.
- **Humility** (a shepherd's crook): designed but never implemented.
- **Justice** (a set of scales): gained by destroying murderers. Knights can designate champions whose deeds at champion spawns earn the Knight power scrolls.
- **Sacrifice** (a teardrop): gained by sacrificing fame. Knights can resurrect themselves as ghosts.
- **Spirituality** (an ankh): designed but never implemented.
- **Valor** (a sword): gained by fighting at champion spawns. Knights can activate and advance champion spawns.

Player killers cannot use the virtues at all.

## Era notes

The virtue system was introduced in the early 2000s, with virtue quests following in Publish 38. Honesty, Humility, and Spirituality were designed but never activated.

# Mondain's Legacy Quests

Mondain's Legacy added dungeon quest chains across its new dungeons — the Twisted Weald, Blighted Grove, Bedlam, the Labyrinth, the Prism of Light, the Citadel, and the Palace of Paroxysmus — many of them gating access to [peerless encounters](../mechanics/peerless-encounters.md).

## How it works

The signature structure is the **peerless key quest chain**: each peerless boss requires collecting keys or completing a summoning quest in its dungeon before the encounter can be triggered. The commonly cited chains (community recollection on exact steps):

- **Dread Horn** (Twisted Weald), **Lady Melisande** (Blighted Grove), **Shimmering Effusion** (Prism of Light), **Travesty** (Bedlam), **Chief Paroxysmus** (Palace of Paroxysmus).

The **Prism of Light** has its own access chain through the dungeon's crystal puzzles. Completing these chains is the prerequisite for farming peerless rewards — crafting ingredients and named [artifacts](../items/artifacts.md).

## Era notes

Added with Mondain's Legacy (2005). The key- and quest-gated boss model carried forward into later eras' encounter design.

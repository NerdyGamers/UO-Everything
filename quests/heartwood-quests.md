# Heartwood Quests

The Heartwood is the elven quest hub of Ilshenar: a community of elven NPCs offering roughly twenty quests steeped in elven lore, including the chain that teaches the Spellweaving skill.

## How it works

Heartwood quests are standard [quest system](quest-system.md) fare — kill tasks, deliveries, and collections — but with elven flavor: the givers are elves, the stories involve the elven community and its enemies, and several quests reward elven-crafted items. The hub is the densest concentration of lore-flavored quests from the Mondain's Legacy era.

Its most mechanically important quest is the Spellweaving initiation: learning the [Spellweaving](../skills/magic/spellweaving.md) skill requires completing a dedicated quest chain in the Heartwood (community recollection on the exact quest names and steps), making the Heartwood a mandatory pilgrimage for any aspiring arcanist.

## Era notes

The Heartwood and its quests arrived with Mondain's Legacy (2005), alongside the elf race and the Spellweaving skill itself. It remains the primary Spellweaving learning location.

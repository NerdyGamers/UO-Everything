# Community Collections

Community collections are turn-in systems where players donate items to a public institution for points, unlocking titles and rewards as donation totals grow.

## How it works

The flagship example is the **Britain Library collection**: donate books, scrolls, maps, and other written works, earning points scaled to each item's rarity and value. Accumulated points unlock reward tiers — donor titles and, at the highest tiers, special rewards (community recollection on the exact reward list).

Other institutions run collections on the same pattern. Commonly cited examples include the Vesper Museum and the Moonglow Royal Zoo (community recollection on the full list of active collections and their accepted items). Donations are one-way; points are tracked per character or account depending on the collection.

Collections give otherwise vendor-trash oddities — duplicate books, common scrolls, curiosities — a purpose, and they act as a long-term item sink for the shard economy.

## Era notes

Community collections were added in the mid-2000s era as part of the push toward shard-persistent, player-driven content that outlives any single event.

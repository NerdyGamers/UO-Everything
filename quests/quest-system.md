# The Quest System

The quest system is Ultima Online's structured task framework: NPCs offer multi-step objectives tracked in a dedicated quest log, replacing the freeform "figure it out yourself" style of the game's earliest content.

## How it works

Quest-giving NPCs are marked with an exclamation point (!) overhead. Talking to one opens the quest offer gump describing the task and its reward. Accepting adds the quest to the quest log — opened from the paperdoll's quest button — which lists active quests, their objectives, and progress. Objectives range from killing creatures to delivering items to visiting locations; completing them updates the log, and returning to the giver (or finishing the final objective) grants the reward, usually gold, items, or skill-related benefits.

Characters can hold a limited number of active quests and can abandon any quest from the log to free space. Some quests are repeatable; others are once per character, particularly the starter and profession chains. Quest rewards are generally modest for veteran characters but meaningful for new ones.

## Era notes

The quest engine arrived with Mondain's Legacy (2005), alongside the elf race and the [Heartwood](heartwood-quests.md) and [New Haven](new-haven-quests.md) quest hubs. Before it, UO had almost no structured quests — the [escort system](escort-quests.md) and a handful of one-off tasks were the exceptions.

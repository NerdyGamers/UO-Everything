# Escort Quests

Before UO had a quest engine, it had escorts: hire an NPC in one town, walk them to another, collect a small gold reward. It was the closest thing to questing in the game's early years.

## How it works

Certain NPCs in towns ask to be escorted to a destination town. Accepting flags them as your charge and they follow you as you travel. Reaching the named destination completes the escort and pays a modest gold reward scaled to the distance. The NPC must survive the trip — monsters along the road are the main hazard, which made escorts a low-stakes introduction to overland travel for new players.

Escorts are one-off tasks rather than chains: take one, deliver, get paid, repeat. The rewards were never large, but for a brand-new character with empty pockets they were meaningful, and the system doubled as a guided tour of Britannia's towns and roads.

## Era notes

Escort quests date to the game's early eras, well before the structured [quest system](quest-system.md) introduced with Mondain's Legacy (2005). They remain in the game as a legacy system, largely supplanted by richer quest content.

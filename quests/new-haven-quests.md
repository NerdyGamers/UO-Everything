# New Haven Quests

New Haven is Ultima Online's starting city, and its profession quest chains are the on-ramp for every new character: they teach the basics while granting accelerated skill gains and starter gear.

## How it works

New characters begin in New Haven, where NPCs for each profession — warrior, mage, crafter, and others — offer quest chains themed to their trade. Completing quests teaches core mechanics (combat, spellcasting, crafting) and pays out equipment appropriate to the profession, so a new character finishes the chains reasonably outfitted for the wider world.

The key draw is accelerated skill gain: young characters gain skill much faster than normal, up to a threshold (community recollection commonly cites 50 skill points), making New Haven the fastest way to build a new character's foundation. Finishing a profession chain typically awards a choice of reward items suited to the profession.

## Era notes

New Haven arrived with Mondain's Legacy (2005) as the centralized new-player experience, replacing the older scattered starting towns. The accelerated-gain system has been tuned across publishes since, but New Haven remains the standard starting path.

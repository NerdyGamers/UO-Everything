# Ter Mur Quests

The Stygian Abyss expansion made Ter Mur — the gargoyle homeland — a quest hub, with chains covering the gargoyle starting experience, Abyss access, and the imbuing craft.

## How it works

- **Gargoyle starter quests** walk new gargoyles through Ter Mur and the Royal City, introducing the race's abilities (including flight) and the city's layout.
- **Abyss quest chains** gate and guide progression into the Stygian Abyss dungeon itself, leading players through its tiers toward the deeper encounters.
- **Imbuing questlines** teach the [imbuing system](../crafting/imbuing-system.md): unraveling items at the Soul Forge and the ingredient tiers of residue, essence, and relic (community recollection on the exact quest steps).

The chains double as tutorials for the expansion's systems — a player who follows them learns imbuing, gargoyle flight, and the Abyss layout in the intended order, rather than discovering each system in isolation.

## Era notes

Added with Stygian Abyss (2009) alongside the gargoyle race, Ter Mur, and the imbuing craft.

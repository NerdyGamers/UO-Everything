# Virtue Quests

Publish 38 added quests that advance the [Virtues](../mechanics/virtues.md) directly, giving players a reliable path to Seeker, Follower, and Knight tiers instead of relying only on ambient virtuous acts.

## How it works

Each quest targets one virtue:

- **Compassion** — escort quests: guide a helpless NPC to their destination safely. This is the most accessible of the four and overlaps with the general [escort system](escort-quests.md).
- **Honesty** — lost-item quests: find a lost item out in the wilderness and return it to its owner rather than keeping it.
- **Justice** — protection quests: defend an innocent NPC from a murderer who spawns to attack them; defeating the attacker advances Justice.
- **Sacrifice** — giving quests: give up a portion of your own fame for another's benefit (community recollection on the exact exchange mechanic).

The quests are repeatable, and repeated completion is the standard way to climb virtue tiers for virtues that are otherwise slow to gain through normal play.

## Era notes

Added in Publish 38, alongside the virtue system overhaul that turned the eight virtues from roleplay decoration into functional gameplay with Knight-tier rewards.

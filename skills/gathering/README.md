# Gathering Skills

**Canon scope:** Canon (highlight shard-specific tweaks as non-canon when needed).

Gathering skills harvest raw materials from the world — the foundation of the crafting economy. Gatherers work mines, forests, and open water, often in dangerous territory.

## Skills in this group
- [Fishing](fishing.md) — catching fish, treasure, and rarities from water and lava.
- [Lumberjacking](lumberjacking.md) — chopping logs and boards from trees; also boosts axe damage in combat.
- [Mining](mining.md) — digging ore and gems from rock; smelting ore into ingots.

## Cross-cutting notes
- Individual skills cap at 100 (120 with power scrolls); the total skill cap is 700 points.
- Higher skill unlocks rarer resources: colored ores, colored woods, deep-water catches.
- Gathering spots are often contested — miners and lumberjacks work where monsters roam.

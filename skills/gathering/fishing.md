# Fishing

**Canon**

Fishing is the gathering skill for pulling fish, treasure, and rarities from water (and lava) with a fishing pole.

## How it works
Fishermen cast from shore, docks, or boats; deeper water yields better catches. Beyond plain fish, skilled fishers pull up serpents, deep-water elementals, treasure maps in bottles, and — the signature prize — messages in bottles (MIBs) that lead to sunken SOS treasure. [Cooking](../crafting/cooking.md) turns the catch into meals. Special catches include rare fish for taxidermy-style trophies and, in lava, obsidian-elemental encounters.

## Training
Train anywhere with water, working up from shore to deep water as skill rises; a boat helps enormously. MIBs begin appearing at higher skill (community recollection on the exact threshold — verify in-game). No power scrolls are required for basic fishing, but 105–120 scrolls exist for dedicated fishers.

## Synergies
- [Cooking](../crafting/cooking.md) — consumes the catch.
- [Carpentry](../crafting/carpentry.md) — fishing poles are carpenter-made.
- Boating/seafaring skills — deep-water access.

## Era notes
The High Seas expansion (2010) greatly expanded fishing: lobster and crab traps, high-seas encounters, and new bait mechanics. Later publishes added fishing quests with special rewards.

# Lumberjacking


Lumberjacking is the gathering skill for chopping logs and boards from trees with an axe, and it grants a damage bonus when fighting with axes.

## How it works
Lumberjacks chop trees for logs, which become boards for [Carpentry](../crafting/carpentry.md) and shafts for [Fletching](../crafting/fletching.md). Special colored trees yield colored logs — oak, ash, yew, heartwood, bloodwood, and frostwood — used for stronger bows, instruments, and furniture. In combat, lumberjacking adds bonus damage to axe attacks, scaling with skill (community recollection on the exact percentage — verify in-game before relying on it).

## Training
Chop any trees; move to rarer wooded areas as skill rises to find colored trees. Axes are cheap and [Tinkering](../crafting/tinkering.md)-adjacent tools are unnecessary — any axe works. Power scrolls (105–120) drop from champion spawn bosses.

## Synergies
- [Carpentry](../crafting/carpentry.md) — primary consumer of boards.
- [Fletching](../crafting/fletching.md) — shafts and bow staves.
- Axe-based melee builds — the damage bonus stacks with weapon skill.

## Era notes
Colored woods were introduced alongside colored ores in the Renaissance era; heartwood quests and special-wood instruments came with Mondain's Legacy.

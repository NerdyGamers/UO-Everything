# Mining


Mining is the gathering skill for digging ore and gems from mountainsides and caves with a pickaxe or shovel, and smelting ore into ingots.

## How it works
Miners target rock faces to dig ore — iron at low skill, then the colored tiers: dull copper, shadow iron, copper, bronze, gold, agapite, verite, and valorite — plus occasional gems. Ore is smelted at a forge into ingots for [Blacksmithy](../crafting/blacksmithy.md) and [Tinkering](../crafting/tinkering.md); higher skill improves smelting yield. Mining also unearths sand (for glassblowing) and, rarely, special resources. Gargoyle miners can use the racial ability to mine without tools in some eras (community recollection — verify against current publish notes).

## Training
Dig and smelt in a loop; keep a forge nearby. Colored veins appear as skill rises. Power scrolls (105–120) drop from champion spawn bosses and matter for reliable valorite yields.

## Synergies
- [Blacksmithy](../crafting/blacksmithy.md) — primary ingot consumer.
- [Tinkering](../crafting/tinkering.md) — pickaxes, shovels, and smith tools.
- [Imbuing](../crafting/imbuing.md) — mined gems serve as imbuing reagents.

## Era notes
Colored ores date to the Renaissance era; ore elementals that ambush miners were added later, turning mining runs into combat encounters.

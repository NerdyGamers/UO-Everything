# Blacksmithy


Blacksmithy is the craft of forging metal weapons and armor from ingots at an anvil and forge, using a smith's hammer or tongs.

## How it works
Blacksmiths smelt ore into ingots ([Mining](../gathering/mining.md) supplies the ore), then shape them into swords, axes, maces, polearms, and plate, chain, and ring armor. Crafting requires an anvil and a forge nearby; many towns have public smithies. At 100 skill, crafted items can bear the smith's maker's mark, and exceptional-quality items gain durability and damage bonuses. Higher skill unlocks harder recipes and improves success chance on difficult items.

## Training
Train by crafting progressively harder items — daggers and maces early, plate armor later. Ingots are the main cost; miners often train both skills together. Power scrolls (105–120) come from champion spawn bosses and raise the cap for exceptional crafting and difficult recipes.

## Synergies
- [Mining](../gathering/mining.md) — supplies ore and ingots.
- [Tinkering](tinkering.md) — tinker-made tools (tongs, hammers) are used at the forge.
- Bulk Order Deeds from NPC blacksmiths reward large crafting runs with rare hammers and colored metal.

## Era notes
Colored metals (dull copper through valorite) were added with the Renaissance-era mining expansion; each tier crafts stronger armor. Runic hammers from BOD rewards let smiths add magical properties to crafted gear.

# Tailoring


Tailoring is the craft of sewing cloth, leather, and bone armor — plus hats, robes, and containers — from bolts of cloth and cured hides, using a sewing kit.

## How it works
Tailors cut cloth from cotton and flax (gathered in the field — see [wilderness](../wilderness/README.md)) or buy bolts from NPC weavers, and cure hides into leather. Recipes span everyday clothing, leather and studded armor, bone armor, backpacks, and quivers. At 100 skill, crafted items can bear the tailor's maker's mark, and exceptional pieces gain bonuses. Colored leathers (spined, horned, barbed) from tougher creatures craft progressively stronger armor. Bulk Order Deeds from NPC tailors reward large clothing and armor orders with rare cloth and sewing bonuses.

## Training
Train on simple cloth items (shirts, skirts) before moving to leather armor; cloth is cheap and hides come from hunting. Power scrolls (105–120) drop from champion spawn bosses.

## Synergies
- [Blacksmithy](blacksmithy.md) — studded armor needs both trades' materials.
- Hunting/foraging skills — supply hides and leather.
- [Imbuing](imbuing.md) — exceptional tailor-made armor is prime imbue fodder.

## Era notes
Leather dye tubs and colored cloth (from special dyes and holiday gifts) expanded tailoring's cosmetic side in later publishes; elven tailoring recipes arrived with Mondain's Legacy.

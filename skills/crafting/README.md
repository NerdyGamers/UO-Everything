# Crafting Skills

**Canon scope:** Canon (highlight shard-specific tweaks as non-canon when needed).

Crafting skills turn raw materials into finished goods — weapons, armor, tools, food, and magical enhancements. Crafters work at forges, anvils, ovens, and Soul Forges, and supply the gear economy that combat characters depend on.

## Skills in this group
- [Blacksmithy](blacksmithy.md) — forging metal weapons and armor from ingots.
- [Carpentry](carpentry.md) — shaping boards into furniture, containers, instruments, and ships.
- [Cooking](cooking.md) — preparing food from raw ingredients.
- [Fletching](fletching.md) — crafting bows, crossbows, arrows, and bolts.
- [Imbuing](imbuing.md) — adding magical properties to gear at a Soul Forge (Stygian Abyss era).
- [Tailoring](tailoring.md) — sewing cloth, leather, and bone armor and clothing.
- [Tinkering](tinkering.md) — making tools, traps, locks, jewelry, and gadgets.

## Cross-cutting notes
- Individual skills cap at 100 (120 with power scrolls); the total skill cap is 700 points.
- Exceptional-quality crafts (at high skill) gain bonuses and can carry the crafter's maker's mark.
- Bulk Order Deeds (BODs) from NPC crafters reward bulk production runs.
- Crafters depend on [gathering](../gathering/README.md) skills for raw materials: ore, logs, hides, fish.

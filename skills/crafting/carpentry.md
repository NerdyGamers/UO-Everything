# Carpentry


Carpentry is the craft of shaping boards into furniture, containers, weapons, and musical instruments, using saws, hammers, and other woodworking tools.

## How it works
Carpenters turn logs into boards ([Lumberjacking](../gathering/lumberjacking.md) supplies the wood) and craft everything from chairs, tables, chests, and bookshelves to bows' wooden components, fishing poles, and instruments for [Musicianship](../bard/musicianship.md). Many recipes need additional components — cloth from [Tailoring](tailoring.md), ingots, or hinges from [Tinkering](tinkering.md). Exceptional items at high skill gain durability bonuses and can carry the carpenter's maker's mark.

## Training
Train on simple items (wooden shields, footstools) before moving to furniture and instruments. Boards are cheap if gathered yourself; buying them in bulk gets expensive. Power scrolls (105–120) drop from champion spawn bosses.

## Synergies
- [Lumberjacking](../gathering/lumberjacking.md) — supplies plain and colored boards.
- [Tinkering](tinkering.md) — hinges, locks, and clockwork parts for complex pieces.
- [Tailoring](tailoring.md) — cloth for upholstered furniture.

## Era notes
Later publishes added craftable house add-ons, holiday decorations, and — with the High Seas expansion — ship repair and shipwright recipes using the carpentry skill.

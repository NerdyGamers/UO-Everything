# Cooking

**Canon**

Cooking is the craft of preparing food — bread, pies, cakes, and cooked meats — from raw ingredients using an oven, flour mill, or heat source.

## How it works
Cooks grind wheat into flour, combine it with water, honey, fruit, and other ingredients to bake goods, and cook raw fish, poultry, and meat into meals. Food restores stamina and is a staple for travelers; some recipes produce filling multi-bite dishes (like whole cakes). Cooking has no exceptional-quality tier — success is binary — but higher skill unlocks the full recipe list.

## Training
Cooking is one of the cheapest crafts to train: flour, water, and dough cost little, and NPC bakers sell ingredients. Train on bread and muffins, then move to cakes and pies as skill rises.

## Synergies
- [Fishing](../gathering/fishing.md) — raw fish for cooked fish steaks and fish pies.
- [Tinkering](tinkering.md) — tinker-made rolling pins and flour sifters are cooking tools.
- Farmers and butchers supply eggs, milk, and raw meat.

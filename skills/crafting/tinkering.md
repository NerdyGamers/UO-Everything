# Tinkering

**Canon**

Tinkering is the craft of making tools, traps, locks, jewelry, and clockwork gadgets from ingots, using tinker's tools.

## How it works
Tinkers supply the tool economy: sewing kits, smith hammers, saws, fletching tools, cooking utensils, shovels, and pickaxes all come from tinkering. They also craft locks and keys, rings and bracelets, spyglasses, lanterns, and — at high skill — trapped containers that trigger on tampering. Exceptional tools at high skill gain extra uses. Tinkering has no maker's-mark armor line; its value is in consumable tools every other crafter needs.

## Training
Train on inexpensive ingot recipes (locks, tools) in bulk; ingot cost is the main expense, so pairing with [Mining](../gathering/mining.md) helps. Power scrolls (105–120) drop from champion spawn bosses.

## Synergies
- [Mining](../gathering/mining.md) — ingots for every recipe.
- [Blacksmithy](blacksmithy.md), [Carpentry](carpentry.md), [Tailoring](tailoring.md), [Cooking](cooking.md), [Fletching](fletching.md) — all consume tinker-made tools.
- [Lockpicking](../rogue/lockpicking.md) — tinkers make the locks that lockpickers practice on.

## Era notes
Golem crafting (a tinkering recipe producing a controllable construct) dates to the early eras; later publishes added clockwork and holiday gadgets.

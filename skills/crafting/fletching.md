# Fletching


Fletching is the craft of making bows, crossbows, arrows, and bolts from boards, shafts, and feathers.

## How it works
Fletchers cut shafts from boards ([Lumberjacking](../gathering/lumberjacking.md) supplies the wood), then assemble bows and crossbows and fletch ammunition in large batches. Arrows and bolts are consumed in bulk by archers, making fletching the ammunition pipeline for ranged combat. Exceptional bows at high skill gain damage bonuses and can carry the fletcher's maker's mark. Fletcher BODs from NPC bowyers reward bulk ammunition and bow orders.

## Training
Train by cutting shafts and fletching arrows in volume — feathers from harpies and birds are the usual bottleneck. Bows come later as skill rises. Power scrolls (105–120) drop from champion spawn bosses.

## Synergies
- [Lumberjacking](../gathering/lumberjacking.md) — boards for shafts and bows.
- [Archery](../wilderness/archery.md) — archers are the main customers for fletcher-made ammunition.
- [Tinkering](tinkering.md) — some crossbow components.

## Era notes
Elven bowyers and heartwood-crafted bows were added with the Mondain's Legacy era; later publishes added special-wood bows with magical properties.

# Imbuing


Imbuing is the craft of adding or enhancing magical properties on weapons, armor, and jewelry at a Soul Forge, introduced with the Stygian Abyss expansion.

## How it works
Imbuers first **unravel** unwanted magical items to harvest ingredients — Magical Residue from minor items, Enchanted Essence from stronger ones, and Relic Fragments from the most powerful — then spend those ingredients plus gems at a Soul Forge to imbue chosen properties onto an item. Each property has an intensity weight, and an item can hold up to five properties; total intensity is capped, so imbuing is a budgeting exercise. Success chance scales with skill, and failure can destroy the ingredients. Unraveling also trains the skill.

## Training
Train by unraveling loot in bulk — dungeon and champion-spawn loot feeds the ingredient pipeline — then imbuing low-intensity properties. The Soul Forge stands in Ter Mur's Royal City; any character who can reach it can imbue (travel details for non-gargoyle characters are community recollection — verify in-game).

## Synergies
- Loot-generating combat builds — supply unravel fodder.
- [Blacksmithy](blacksmithy.md) / [Tailoring](tailoring.md) — craft exceptional base items to imbue.
- [Mining](../gathering/mining.md) — gems used as imbuing reagents come from mining.

## Era notes
Added with Stygian Abyss (2009). Later publishes adjusted ingredient yields and added new imbueable properties; exact current weights should be checked against recent publish notes.

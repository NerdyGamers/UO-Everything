# Spellweaving

**Canon**

Elven arcane magic of sixteen spells, introduced with Mondain's Legacy (2005) — strongest when woven together.

## How it works
Spellweaving is learned through a quest in Heartwood, per community documentation, and its spells require no reagents. Its signature mechanic is the Arcane Circle: casting Arcane Circle alongside up to five nearby spellweavers empowers the whole group's weaving. Spells include Gift of Renewal, Immolating Weapon, Thunderstorm, Nature's Fury, Summon Fey, Summon Fiend, Wildfire, Essence of Wind, and the execution spell Word of Death. Effectiveness scales with Spellweaving skill; community sources also cite [Evaluating Intelligence](evaluating-intelligence.md) as a secondary factor, though the exact formula is uncertain. Individual skills cap at 100 (120 with power scrolls); the total skill cap is 700 points.

## Training
Repeated casting of low-cost spells; keeping an arcane circle active with fellow weavers supports practical use.

## Synergies
[Evaluating Intelligence](evaluating-intelligence.md) is the commonly cited companion skill; [Meditation](meditation.md) supports sustained casting. Popular on elf mage templates for its utility buffs.

## Era notes
Introduced in Mondain's Legacy (2005). Arcane-circle mechanics and individual spells have been publish-tuned since.

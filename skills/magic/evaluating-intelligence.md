# Evaluating Intelligence

**Canon**

A passive skill that increases spell damage and lets a mage gauge a target's intelligence.

## How it works
Evaluating Intelligence adds to the damage of Magery, Necromancy, and Spellweaving spells — community documentation describes roughly +1% damage per 10 points of skill, though exact scaling has changed across publishes. Used actively on a target, it reveals that target's Intelligence stat. Individual skills cap at 100 (120 with power scrolls); the total skill cap is 700 points.

## Training
It trains passively alongside spellcasting — every offensive spell cast is a gain chance. Dedicated training typically means casting low-cost damage spells on repeat.

## Synergies
Pairs with [Magery](magery.md), [Necromancy](necromancy.md), and [Spellweaving](spellweaving.md) for damage; [Meditation](meditation.md) keeps mana flowing for repeated casts.

## Era notes
The damage contribution of Evaluating Intelligence has been adjusted in several publishes; older formulas found online may not match the current era.

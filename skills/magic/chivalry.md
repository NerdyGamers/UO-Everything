# Chivalry


The paladin's art: ten holy spells powered by karma and tithing, introduced with the Age of Shadows expansion (2003).

## How it works
Chivalry spells are cast from a Book of Chivalry without reagents, but each cast consumes tithing points (gold donated at an ankh), and most spells require positive karma — high karma improves success and power. The ten spells include Consecrate Weapon, Divine Fury, Enemy of One, Holy Light, Sacred Journey (travel without reagents), Remove Curse, Cleanse by Fire, Close Wounds, Dispel Evil, and Noble Sacrifice (a self-sacrificing resurrection). Individual skills cap at 100 (120 with power scrolls); the total skill cap is 700 points.

## Training
Casting low-cost spells such as Consecrate Weapon or Divine Fury; maintaining karma through virtuous deeds and topping up tithing at an ankh.

## Synergies
The classic paladin pairs Chivalry with swordsmanship and tactics; [Meditation](meditation.md) sustains the mana pool. Sacred Journey makes it a favorite travel tool for non-mages.

## Era notes
Introduced in Age of Shadows (2003). Karma requirements and spell effects have been publish-tuned since.

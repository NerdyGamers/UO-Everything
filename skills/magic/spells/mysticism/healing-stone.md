# Healing Stone

A mysticism spell that conjures a stone of mending.

## Casting

Words of power: *Kal In Mani*. Mysticism spell. Mana cost: 4. Minimum Mysticism skill: 0. Requires no reagents.

## Effect

Creates a healing stone only the mystic can use to mend their own wounds.

## Notes

Mysticism spells require no reagents. Taught by mystic vendors in Ter Mur. Introduced with Stygian Abyss (2009). See [Mysticism](../mysticism.md).

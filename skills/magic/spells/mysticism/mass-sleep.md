# Mass Sleep

A mysticism spell that lulls a whole group into slumber.

## Casting

Words of power: *Vas Zu*. Mysticism spell. Mana cost: 14. Minimum Mysticism skill: 45. Requires no reagents.

## Effect

Puts several targets in a small area to sleep, as Sleep does.

## Notes

Mysticism spells require no reagents. Taught by mystic vendors in Ter Mur. Introduced with Stygian Abyss (2009). See [Mysticism](../mysticism.md).

# Sleep

A mysticism spell that lulls the target into slumber.

## Casting

Words of power: *In Zu*. Mysticism spell. Mana cost: 8. Minimum Mysticism skill: 20. Requires no reagents.

## Effect

Puts the target to sleep until harmed or enough time passes; the sleeper cannot attack or cast and moves slowly.

## Notes

Mysticism spells require no reagents. Taught by mystic vendors in Ter Mur. Introduced with Stygian Abyss (2009). See [Mysticism](../mysticism.md).

# Enchant

A mysticism spell that imbues the held weapon with magic.

## Casting

Words of power: *In Ort Ylem*. Mysticism spell. Mana cost: 6. Minimum Mysticism skill: 8. Requires no reagents.

## Effect

Temporarily grants the held weapon a hit-spell effect; at high skill it also grants spell channeling.

## Notes

Mysticism spells require no reagents. Taught by mystic vendors in Ter Mur. Introduced with Stygian Abyss (2009). See [Mysticism](../mysticism.md).

# Spell Trigger

A mysticism spell that bottles a spell for instant use.

## Casting

Words of power: *In Vas Ort Ex*. Mysticism spell. Mana cost: 14. Minimum Mysticism skill: 45. Requires no reagents.

## Effect

Stores one mysticism spell in a spell stone, which can then be triggered to cast it instantly.

## Notes

Mysticism spells require no reagents. Taught by mystic vendors in Ter Mur. Introduced with Stygian Abyss (2009). See [Mysticism](../mysticism.md).

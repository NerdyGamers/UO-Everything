# Nether Cyclone

A mysticism spell that summons a cyclone of nether winds.

## Casting

Words of power: *Grav Hur*. Mysticism spell. Mana cost: 50. Minimum Mysticism skill: 83. Requires no reagents.

## Effect

Calls up a gale of lethal winds that strikes all targets around a location with chaos damage.

## Notes

Mysticism spells require no reagents. Taught by mystic vendors in Ter Mur. Introduced with Stygian Abyss (2009). See [Mysticism](../mysticism.md).

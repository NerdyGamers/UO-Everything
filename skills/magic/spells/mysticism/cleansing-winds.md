# Cleansing Winds

A mysticism spell that washes afflictions away on the wind.

## Casting

Words of power: *In Vas Mani Hur*. Mysticism spell. Mana cost: 20. Minimum Mysticism skill: 58. Requires no reagents.

## Effect

Soothing winds that cure poison, lift curses, and heal the target.

## Notes

Mysticism spells require no reagents. Taught by mystic vendors in Ter Mur. Introduced with Stygian Abyss (2009). See [Mysticism](../mysticism.md).

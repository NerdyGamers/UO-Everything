# Spell Plague

A mysticism spell that infects the target with a creeping magical plague.

## Casting

Mysticism spell. Mana cost: see Notes. Minimum Mysticism skill: 70. Requires no reagents.

## Effect

Afflicts the target with a magical plague that spreads and worsens over time.

## Notes

Exact figures are best confirmed in-game; community recollection places its cost alongside Hail Storm at around 40 mana. Mysticism spells require no reagents. Taught by mystic vendors in Ter Mur. Introduced with Stygian Abyss (2009). See [Mysticism](../mysticism.md).

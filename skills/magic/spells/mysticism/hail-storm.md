# Hail Storm

A mysticism spell that calls down a storm of hail.

## Casting

Words of power: *Kal Des Ylem*. Mysticism spell. Mana cost: 40. Minimum Mysticism skill: 70. Requires no reagents.

## Effect

Pounds the area around the caster with hailstones, damaging nearby enemies.

## Notes

Mysticism spells require no reagents. Taught by mystic vendors in Ter Mur. Introduced with Stygian Abyss (2009). See [Mysticism](../mysticism.md).

# Animated Weapon

A mysticism spell that wakes a weapon to fight on its own.

## Casting

Words of power: *In Jux Por Ylem*. Mysticism spell. Mana cost: 11. Minimum Mysticism skill: 33. Requires no reagents.

## Effect

Conjures a floating animated weapon that attacks nearby foes.

## Notes

Mysticism spells require no reagents. Taught by mystic vendors in Ter Mur. Introduced with Stygian Abyss (2009). See [Mysticism](../mysticism.md).

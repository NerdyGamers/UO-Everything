# Bombard

A mysticism spell that hurls a crushing boulder.

## Casting

Words of power: *Corp Por Ylem*. Mysticism spell. Mana cost: 20. Minimum Mysticism skill: 58. Requires no reagents.

## Effect

Launches a magical boulder that can knock its victim back and briefly stun them.

## Notes

Mysticism spells require no reagents. Taught by mystic vendors in Ter Mur. Introduced with Stygian Abyss (2009). See [Mysticism](../mysticism.md).

# Nether Bolt

A mysticism spell that hurls raw nether energy.

## Casting

Words of power: *In Corp Ylem*. Mysticism spell. Mana cost: 4. Minimum Mysticism skill: 0. Requires no reagents.

## Effect

Fires a bolt of nether energy that strikes the target with chaos damage.

## Notes

Mysticism spells require no reagents. Taught by mystic vendors in Ter Mur. Introduced with Stygian Abyss (2009). See [Mysticism](../mysticism.md).

# Stone Form

A mysticism spell that hardens the caster like living rock.

## Casting

Words of power: *In Rel Ylem*. Mysticism spell. Mana cost: 11. Minimum Mysticism skill: 33. Requires no reagents.

## Effect

Infuses the caster with stone: slower to move, fight, and cast, but resistant to damage with a small melee bonus.

## Notes

Mysticism spells require no reagents. Taught by mystic vendors in Ter Mur. Introduced with Stygian Abyss (2009). See [Mysticism](../mysticism.md).

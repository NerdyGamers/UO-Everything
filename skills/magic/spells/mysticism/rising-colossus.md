# Rising Colossus

A mysticism spell that raises a stone colossus.

## Casting

Words of power: *Kal Vas Xen Corp Ylem*. Mysticism spell. Mana cost: 50. Minimum Mysticism skill: 83. Requires no reagents.

## Effect

Summons a colossal stone titan that attacks nearby foes.

## Notes

Mysticism spells require no reagents. Taught by mystic vendors in Ter Mur. Introduced with Stygian Abyss (2009). See [Mysticism](../mysticism.md).

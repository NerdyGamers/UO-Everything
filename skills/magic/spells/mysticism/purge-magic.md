# Purge Magic

A mysticism spell that strips a foe's magical ward.

## Casting

Words of power: *An Ort Sanct*. Mysticism spell. Mana cost: 6. Minimum Mysticism skill: 8. Requires no reagents.

## Effect

Removes one beneficial magical effect from the target.

## Notes

Mysticism spells require no reagents. Taught by mystic vendors in Ter Mur. Introduced with Stygian Abyss (2009). See [Mysticism](../mysticism.md).

# Eagle Strike

A mysticism spell that looses a spectral eagle.

## Casting

Words of power: *Kal Por Ylem*. Mysticism spell. Mana cost: 9. Minimum Mysticism skill: 20. Requires no reagents.

## Effect

Sends a magical eagle-like projectile raking the target with its talons.

## Notes

Mysticism spells require no reagents. Taught by mystic vendors in Ter Mur. Introduced with Stygian Abyss (2009). See [Mysticism](../mysticism.md).

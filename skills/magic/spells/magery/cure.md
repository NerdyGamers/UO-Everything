# Cure

A second circle magery spell that purges poison from the target.

## Casting

Words of power: *An Nox*. Second circle magery spell. Mana cost: 6. Minimum Magery skill: 15. Reagents: garlic, ginseng.

## Effect

Cures the target of poison; stronger poisons may need repeated casts.

## Notes

One of the original magery spells, dating to the game's launch. See [Magery](../magery.md).

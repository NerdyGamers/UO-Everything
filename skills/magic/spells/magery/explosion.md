# Explosion

A sixth circle magery spell that detonates a delayed blast.

## Casting

Words of power: *Vas Ort Flam*. Sixth circle magery spell. Mana cost: 20. Minimum Magery skill: 72. Reagents: blood moss, mandrake root.

## Effect

Plants a fiery explosion on the target that detonates a moment later.

## Notes

One of the original magery spells, dating to the game's launch. See [Magery](../magery.md).

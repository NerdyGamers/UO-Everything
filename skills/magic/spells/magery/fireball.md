# Fireball

A third circle magery spell that hurls a searing ball of flame.

## Casting

Words of power: *Vas Flam*. Third circle magery spell. Mana cost: 9. Minimum Magery skill: 29. Reagents: black pearl.

## Effect

Launches a bolt of fire that explodes on the target for fire damage.

## Notes

One of the original magery spells, dating to the game's launch. See [Magery](../magery.md).

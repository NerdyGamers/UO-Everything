# Mana Vampire

A seventh circle magery spell that drinks the mana of everyone nearby.

## Casting

Words of power: *Ort Mud*. Seventh circle magery spell. Mana cost: 40. Minimum Magery skill: 86. Reagents: black pearl, blood moss, mandrake root.

## Effect

Drains mana from all valid targets around the caster into the caster's own reserves.

## Notes

One of the original magery spells, dating to the game's launch. See [Magery](../magery.md).

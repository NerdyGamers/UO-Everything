# Dispel

A sixth circle magery spell that banishes summoned creatures.

## Casting

Words of power: *An Ort*. Sixth circle magery spell. Mana cost: 20. Minimum Magery skill: 72. Reagents: garlic, mandrake root, sulfurous ash.

## Effect

Unmakes a summoned creature, sending it back where it came from.

## Notes

One of the original magery spells, dating to the game's launch. See [Magery](../magery.md).

# Telekinesis

A third circle magery spell that manipulates distant objects.

## Casting

Words of power: *Ort Por Ylem*. Third circle magery spell. Mana cost: 9. Minimum Magery skill: 29. Reagents: blood moss, mandrake root.

## Effect

Lets the caster use an object from a distance, such as pulling a lever or lifting an item.

## Notes

One of the original magery spells, dating to the game's launch. See [Magery](../magery.md).

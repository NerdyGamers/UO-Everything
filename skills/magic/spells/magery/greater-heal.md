# Greater Heal

A fourth circle magery spell that mends serious wounds.

## Casting

Words of power: *In Vas Mani*. Fourth circle magery spell. Mana cost: 11. Minimum Magery skill: 43. Reagents: garlic, ginseng, mandrake root, spider's silk.

## Effect

Restores a large amount of health to the target, scaling with skill.

## Notes

One of the original magery spells, dating to the game's launch. See [Magery](../magery.md).

# Earthquake

A eighth circle magery spell that shakes the earth itself.

## Casting

Words of power: *In Vas Por*. Eighth circle magery spell. Mana cost: 50. Minimum Magery skill: 100. Reagents: blood moss, garlic, mandrake root, sulfurous ash.

## Effect

Violently shakes the ground, damaging everything in a wide radius around the caster.

## Notes

One of the original magery spells, dating to the game's launch. See [Magery](../magery.md).

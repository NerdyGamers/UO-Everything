# Summon Creature

A fifth circle magery spell that calls a wild beast to fight.

## Casting

Words of power: *Kal Xen*. Fifth circle magery spell. Mana cost: 14. Minimum Magery skill: 58. Reagents: blood moss, mandrake root, spider's silk.

## Effect

Summons a random creature that fights for the caster for a time.

## Notes

One of the original magery spells, dating to the game's launch. See [Magery](../magery.md).

# Chain Lightning

A seventh circle magery spell that looses lightning that leaps between foes.

## Casting

Words of power: *Vas Ort Grav*. Seventh circle magery spell. Mana cost: 40. Minimum Magery skill: 86. Reagents: black pearl, blood moss, mandrake root, sulfurous ash.

## Effect

Strikes the target with lightning that arcs on to others standing nearby.

## Notes

One of the original magery spells, dating to the game's launch. See [Magery](../magery.md).

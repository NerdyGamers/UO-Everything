# Poison Field

A fifth circle magery spell that spreads a field of venom.

## Casting

Words of power: *In Nox Grav*. Fifth circle magery spell. Mana cost: 14. Minimum Magery skill: 58. Reagents: black pearl, nightshade, spider's silk.

## Effect

Creates a field of poison that afflicts anything crossing it.

## Notes

One of the original magery spells, dating to the game's launch. See [Magery](../magery.md).

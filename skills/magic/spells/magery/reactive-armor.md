# Reactive Armor

A first circle magery spell that turns attackers' blows back on them.

## Casting

Words of power: *Flam Sanct*. First circle magery spell. Mana cost: 4. Minimum Magery skill: 0. Reagents: garlic, spider's silk, sulfurous ash.

## Effect

Surrounds the caster with shimmering armor that reflects a portion of melee damage back at the attacker.

## Notes

One of the original magery spells, dating to the game's launch. See [Magery](../magery.md).

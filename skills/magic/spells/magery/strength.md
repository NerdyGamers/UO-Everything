# Strength

A second circle magery spell that fills the target with vigor.

## Casting

Words of power: *Uus Mani*. Second circle magery spell. Mana cost: 6. Minimum Magery skill: 15. Reagents: mandrake root, nightshade.

## Effect

Temporarily raises the target's strength, increasing melee damage.

## Notes

One of the original magery spells, dating to the game's launch. See [Magery](../magery.md).

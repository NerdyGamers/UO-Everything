# Mark

A sixth circle magery spell that records a location on a rune.

## Casting

Words of power: *Kal Por Ylem*. Sixth circle magery spell. Mana cost: 20. Minimum Magery skill: 72. Reagents: black pearl, blood moss, mandrake root.

## Effect

Inscribes the caster's current location onto a blank recall rune for later Recall and Gate Travel.

## Notes

The rune must be blank; see [Recall](recall.md). One of the original magery spells, dating to the game's launch. See [Magery](../magery.md).

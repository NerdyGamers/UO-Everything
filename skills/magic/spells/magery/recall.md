# Recall

A fourth circle magery spell that carries the caster to a marked rune.

## Casting

Words of power: *Kal Ort Por*. Fourth circle magery spell. Mana cost: 11. Minimum Magery skill: 43. Reagents: black pearl, blood moss, mandrake root.

## Effect

Teleports the caster to the location recorded on a marked recall rune.

## Notes

Pairs with [Mark](mark.md): a blank rune must be marked before it can be recalled to. One of the original magery spells, dating to the game's launch. See [Magery](../magery.md).

# Paralyze

A fifth circle magery spell that freezes the target in place.

## Casting

Words of power: *An Ex Por*. Fifth circle magery spell. Mana cost: 14. Minimum Magery skill: 58. Reagents: garlic, mandrake root, spider's silk.

## Effect

Holds the target motionless for a short duration.

## Notes

One of the original magery spells, dating to the game's launch. See [Magery](../magery.md).

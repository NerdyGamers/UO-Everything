# Arch Cure

A fourth circle magery spell that purges poison from the caster and nearby allies.

## Casting

Words of power: *Vas An Nox*. Fourth circle magery spell. Mana cost: 11. Minimum Magery skill: 43. Reagents: garlic, ginseng, mandrake root.

## Effect

Cures poison on the caster and everyone standing close by.

## Notes

One of the original magery spells, dating to the game's launch. See [Magery](../magery.md).

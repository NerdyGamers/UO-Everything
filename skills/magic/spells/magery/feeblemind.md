# Feeblemind

A first circle magery spell that dulls the target's mind.

## Casting

Words of power: *Rel Wis*. First circle magery spell. Mana cost: 4. Minimum Magery skill: 0. Reagents: ginseng, nightshade.

## Effect

Temporarily lowers the target's intelligence, weakening the power of their spells.

## Notes

One of the original magery spells, dating to the game's launch. See [Magery](../magery.md).

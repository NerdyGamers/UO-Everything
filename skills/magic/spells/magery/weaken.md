# Weaken

A first circle magery spell that saps the target's strength.

## Casting

Words of power: *Des Mani*. First circle magery spell. Mana cost: 4. Minimum Magery skill: 0. Reagents: garlic, nightshade.

## Effect

Temporarily lowers the target's strength, reducing their melee damage.

## Notes

One of the original magery spells, dating to the game's launch. See [Magery](../magery.md).

# Clumsy

A first circle magery spell that temporarily weakens the target's dexterity.

## Casting

Words of power: *Uus Jux*. First circle magery spell. Mana cost: 4. Minimum Magery skill: 0. Reagents: blood moss, nightshade.

## Effect

Lowers the target's dexterity for a short duration, making them easier to hit.

## Notes

One of the original magery spells, dating to the game's launch. See [Magery](../magery.md).

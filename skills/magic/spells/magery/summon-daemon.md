# Summon Daemon

A eighth circle magery spell that binds a daemon to the caster's will.

## Casting

Words of power: *Kal Vas Xen Corp*. Eighth circle magery spell. Mana cost: 50. Minimum Magery skill: 100. Reagents: blood moss, mandrake root, spider's silk, sulfurous ash.

## Effect

Summons a mighty daemon that fights for the caster.

## Notes

One of the original magery spells, dating to the game's launch. See [Magery](../magery.md).

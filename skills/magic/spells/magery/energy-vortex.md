# Energy Vortex

A eighth circle magery spell that summons a living storm of energy.

## Casting

Words of power: *Vas Corp Por*. Eighth circle magery spell. Mana cost: 50. Minimum Magery skill: 100. Reagents: black pearl, blood moss, mandrake root, nightshade.

## Effect

Conjures a crackling vortex that moves on its own, striking the caster's enemies.

## Notes

One of the original magery spells, dating to the game's launch. See [Magery](../magery.md).

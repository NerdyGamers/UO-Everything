# Mass Dispel

A seventh circle magery spell that unmakes every summoned creature nearby.

## Casting

Words of power: *Vas An Ort*. Seventh circle magery spell. Mana cost: 40. Minimum Magery skill: 86. Reagents: black pearl, garlic, mandrake root, sulfurous ash.

## Effect

Banishes summoned creatures in an area around the target.

## Notes

One of the original magery spells, dating to the game's launch. See [Magery](../magery.md).

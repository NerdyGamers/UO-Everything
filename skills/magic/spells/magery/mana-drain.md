# Mana Drain

A fourth circle magery spell that siphons the target's mana.

## Casting

Words of power: *Ort Rel*. Fourth circle magery spell. Mana cost: 11. Minimum Magery skill: 43. Reagents: black pearl, blood moss, mandrake root.

## Effect

Drains mana from the target into the caster.

## Notes

One of the original magery spells, dating to the game's launch. See [Magery](../magery.md).

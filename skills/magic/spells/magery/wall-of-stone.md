# Wall of Stone

A third circle magery spell that raises a barrier of rock.

## Casting

Words of power: *In Sanct Ylem*. Third circle magery spell. Mana cost: 9. Minimum Magery skill: 29. Reagents: blood moss, garlic.

## Effect

Conjures a temporary wall of stone that blocks movement.

## Notes

One of the original magery spells, dating to the game's launch. See [Magery](../magery.md).

# Magic Untrap

A second circle magery spell that safely disarms a magical trap.

## Casting

Words of power: *An Jux*. Second circle magery spell. Mana cost: 6. Minimum Magery skill: 15. Reagents: blood moss, sulfurous ash.

## Effect

Removes a magical trap from a container without setting it off.

## Notes

One of the original magery spells, dating to the game's launch. See [Magery](../magery.md).

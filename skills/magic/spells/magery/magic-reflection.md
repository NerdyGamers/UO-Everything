# Magic Reflection

A fifth circle magery spell that turns hostile spells back on their caster.

## Casting

Words of power: *In Jux Sanct*. Fifth circle magery spell. Mana cost: 14. Minimum Magery skill: 58. Reagents: garlic, mandrake root, spider's silk.

## Effect

Reflects the next harmful spell cast at the target back onto its caster.

## Notes

One of the original magery spells, dating to the game's launch. See [Magery](../magery.md).

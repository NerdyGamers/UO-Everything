# Paralyze Field

A sixth circle magery spell that lays a field that freezes trespassers.

## Casting

Words of power: *In Ex Grav*. Sixth circle magery spell. Mana cost: 20. Minimum Magery skill: 72. Reagents: black pearl, garlic, spider's silk.

## Effect

Creates a field that paralyzes anything crossing it.

## Notes

One of the original magery spells, dating to the game's launch. See [Magery](../magery.md).

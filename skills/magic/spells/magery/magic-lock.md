# Magic Lock

A third circle magery spell that seals a door or container with magic.

## Casting

Words of power: *An Por*. Third circle magery spell. Mana cost: 9. Minimum Magery skill: 29. Reagents: blood moss, garlic, sulfurous ash.

## Effect

Magically locks a door or container; it can then only be opened with the Unlock spell or sufficient lockpicking.

## Notes

One of the original magery spells, dating to the game's launch. See [Magery](../magery.md).

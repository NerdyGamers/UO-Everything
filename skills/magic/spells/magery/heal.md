# Heal

A first circle magery spell that mends minor wounds.

## Casting

Words of power: *In Mani*. First circle magery spell. Mana cost: 4. Minimum Magery skill: 0. Reagents: garlic, ginseng, spider's silk.

## Effect

Restores a small amount of health to the target, scaling with the caster's skill.

## Notes

One of the original magery spells, dating to the game's launch. See [Magery](../magery.md).

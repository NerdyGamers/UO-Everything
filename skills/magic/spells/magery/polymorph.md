# Polymorph

A seventh circle magery spell that reshapes the caster into a beast.

## Casting

Words of power: *Vas Ylem Rel*. Seventh circle magery spell. Mana cost: 40. Minimum Magery skill: 86. Reagents: blood moss, mandrake root, spider's silk.

## Effect

Transforms the caster into the likeness of a creature, changing appearance and some capabilities.

## Notes

One of the original magery spells, dating to the game's launch. See [Magery](../magery.md).

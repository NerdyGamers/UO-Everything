# Energy Field

A seventh circle magery spell that raises a shimmering wall of energy.

## Casting

Words of power: *In Sanct Grav*. Seventh circle magery spell. Mana cost: 40. Minimum Magery skill: 86. Reagents: black pearl, mandrake root, spider's silk, sulfurous ash.

## Effect

Conjures a barrier of crackling energy that blocks passage and harms those who touch it.

## Notes

One of the original magery spells, dating to the game's launch. See [Magery](../magery.md).

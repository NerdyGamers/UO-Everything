# Teleport

A third circle magery spell that blinks the caster a short distance.

## Casting

Words of power: *Rel Por*. Third circle magery spell. Mana cost: 9. Minimum Magery skill: 29. Reagents: blood moss, mandrake root.

## Effect

Instantly moves the caster to a nearby visible location.

## Notes

One of the original magery spells, dating to the game's launch. See [Magery](../magery.md).

# Harm

A second circle magery spell that blasts the target with raw magic.

## Casting

Words of power: *In Mani*. Second circle magery spell. Mana cost: 6. Minimum Magery skill: 15. Reagents: nightshade, spider's silk.

## Effect

Strikes the target with a burst of magical damage at close range.

## Notes

One of the original magery spells, dating to the game's launch. See [Magery](../magery.md).

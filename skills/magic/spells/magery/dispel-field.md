# Dispel Field

A fifth circle magery spell that unmakes magical barriers.

## Casting

Words of power: *An Grav*. Fifth circle magery spell. Mana cost: 14. Minimum Magery skill: 58. Reagents: black pearl, garlic, spider's silk, sulfurous ash.

## Effect

Removes field spells such as walls of stone, fire fields, and energy fields.

## Notes

One of the original magery spells, dating to the game's launch. See [Magery](../magery.md).

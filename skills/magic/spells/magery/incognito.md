# Incognito

A fifth circle magery spell that masks the caster as a stranger.

## Casting

Words of power: *Kal In Ex*. Fifth circle magery spell. Mana cost: 14. Minimum Magery skill: 58. Reagents: blood moss, garlic, nightshade.

## Effect

Disguises the caster as a random stranger, hiding name and appearance for a time.

## Notes

One of the original magery spells, dating to the game's launch. See [Magery](../magery.md).

# Magic Trap

A second circle magery spell that rigs a container with a magical trap.

## Casting

Words of power: *In Jux*. Second circle magery spell. Mana cost: 6. Minimum Magery skill: 15. Reagents: garlic, spider's silk, sulfurous ash.

## Effect

Places a magical trap on a container that fires a bolt at whoever opens it.

## Notes

One of the original magery spells, dating to the game's launch. See [Magery](../magery.md).

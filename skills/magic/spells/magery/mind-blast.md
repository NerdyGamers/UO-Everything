# Mind Blast

A fifth circle magery spell that assaults the target's mind.

## Casting

Words of power: *Por Corp Wis*. Fifth circle magery spell. Mana cost: 14. Minimum Magery skill: 58. Reagents: black pearl, mandrake root, nightshade, sulfurous ash.

## Effect

Damages the target based on the difference between the caster's and the target's intelligence.

## Notes

One of the original magery spells, dating to the game's launch. See [Magery](../magery.md).

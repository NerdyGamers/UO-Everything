# Cunning

A second circle magery spell that sharpens the target's mind.

## Casting

Words of power: *Uus Wis*. Second circle magery spell. Mana cost: 6. Minimum Magery skill: 15. Reagents: mandrake root, nightshade.

## Effect

Temporarily raises the target's intelligence, strengthening their spells.

## Notes

One of the original magery spells, dating to the game's launch. See [Magery](../magery.md).

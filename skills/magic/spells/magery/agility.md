# Agility

A second circle magery spell that quickens the target's reflexes.

## Casting

Words of power: *Ex Uus*. Second circle magery spell. Mana cost: 6. Minimum Magery skill: 15. Reagents: blood moss, mandrake root.

## Effect

Temporarily raises the target's dexterity.

## Notes

One of the original magery spells, dating to the game's launch. See [Magery](../magery.md).

# Reveal

A sixth circle magery spell that exposes the hidden.

## Casting

Words of power: *Wis Quas*. Sixth circle magery spell. Mana cost: 20. Minimum Magery skill: 72. Reagents: blood moss, sulfurous ash.

## Effect

Reveals hidden and invisible creatures and players nearby.

## Notes

One of the original magery spells, dating to the game's launch. See [Magery](../magery.md).

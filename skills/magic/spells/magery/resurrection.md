# Resurrection

A eighth circle magery spell that returns the dead to life.

## Casting

Words of power: *An Corp*. Eighth circle magery spell. Mana cost: 50. Minimum Magery skill: 100. Reagents: blood moss, garlic, ginseng.

## Effect

Brings a ghost back to life at the spot where they died.

## Notes

Only works on player ghosts; see [Chivalry's Noble Sacrifice](../chivalry/noble-sacrifice.md) for the paladin's version. One of the original magery spells, dating to the game's launch. See [Magery](../magery.md).

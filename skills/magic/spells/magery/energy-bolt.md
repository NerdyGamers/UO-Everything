# Energy Bolt

A sixth circle magery spell that fires a bolt of pure energy.

## Casting

Words of power: *Corp Por*. Sixth circle magery spell. Mana cost: 20. Minimum Magery skill: 72. Reagents: black pearl, nightshade.

## Effect

Hurls a bolt of crackling energy at the target for heavy damage.

## Notes

One of the original magery spells, dating to the game's launch. See [Magery](../magery.md).

# Fire Field

A fourth circle magery spell that lays a wall of living flame.

## Casting

Words of power: *In Flam Grav*. Fourth circle magery spell. Mana cost: 11. Minimum Magery skill: 43. Reagents: black pearl, spider's silk, sulfurous ash.

## Effect

Creates a field of fire that burns anything stepping through it for a time.

## Notes

One of the original magery spells, dating to the game's launch. See [Magery](../magery.md).

# Magic Arrow

A first circle magery spell that fires a bolt of magical force.

## Casting

Words of power: *In Por Ylem*. First circle magery spell. Mana cost: 4. Minimum Magery skill: 0. Reagents: sulfurous ash.

## Effect

Launches a bolt of magical energy that strikes the target for modest damage.

## Notes

One of the original magery spells, dating to the game's launch. See [Magery](../magery.md).

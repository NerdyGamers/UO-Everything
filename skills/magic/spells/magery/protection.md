# Protection

A second circle magery spell that steadies the caster's casting under attack.

## Casting

Words of power: *Uus Sanct*. Second circle magery spell. Mana cost: 6. Minimum Magery skill: 15. Reagents: garlic, ginseng, sulfurous ash.

## Effect

Makes the caster's spells harder to interrupt while it lasts, at the cost of lowered physical resistance.

## Notes

One of the original magery spells, dating to the game's launch. See [Magery](../magery.md).

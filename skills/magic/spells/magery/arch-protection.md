# Arch Protection

A fourth circle magery spell that extends magical protection to nearby allies.

## Casting

Words of power: *Vas Uus Sanct*. Fourth circle magery spell. Mana cost: 11. Minimum Magery skill: 43. Reagents: garlic, ginseng, mandrake root, sulfurous ash.

## Effect

Grants the Protection effect to the caster and those nearby.

## Notes

One of the original magery spells, dating to the game's launch. See [Magery](../magery.md).

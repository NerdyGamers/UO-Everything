# Meteor Swarm

A seventh circle magery spell that rains meteors on an area.

## Casting

Words of power: *Kal Vas Xen Corp*. Seventh circle magery spell. Mana cost: 40. Minimum Magery skill: 86. Reagents: blood moss, mandrake root, spider's silk, sulfurous ash.

## Effect

Calls down a storm of meteors that batter everything in the target area.

## Notes

One of the original magery spells, dating to the game's launch. See [Magery](../magery.md).

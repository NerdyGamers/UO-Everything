# Invisibility

A sixth circle magery spell that veils the target from sight.

## Casting

Words of power: *An Lor Xen*. Sixth circle magery spell. Mana cost: 20. Minimum Magery skill: 72. Reagents: blood moss, nightshade.

## Effect

Turns the target invisible until they attack, cast, or otherwise act.

## Notes

One of the original magery spells, dating to the game's launch. See [Magery](../magery.md).

# Mass Curse

A sixth circle magery spell that withers a group of foes.

## Casting

Words of power: *Vas Des Sanct*. Sixth circle magery spell. Mana cost: 20. Minimum Magery skill: 72. Reagents: garlic, mandrake root, nightshade, sulfurous ash.

## Effect

Curses the target and those standing near them, lowering all their stats.

## Notes

One of the original magery spells, dating to the game's launch. See [Magery](../magery.md).

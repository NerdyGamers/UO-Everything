# Create Food

A first circle magery spell that conjures a meal from thin air.

## Casting

Words of power: *In Mani Ylem*. First circle magery spell. Mana cost: 4. Minimum Magery skill: 0. Reagents: garlic, ginseng, mandrake root.

## Effect

Creates a random piece of food at the caster's feet.

## Notes

One of the original magery spells, dating to the game's launch. See [Magery](../magery.md).

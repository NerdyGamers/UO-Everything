# Bless

A third circle magery spell that blesses the target in body and mind.

## Casting

Words of power: *Rel Sanct*. Third circle magery spell. Mana cost: 9. Minimum Magery skill: 29. Reagents: garlic, mandrake root.

## Effect

Temporarily raises all of the target's stats: strength, dexterity, and intelligence.

## Notes

One of the original magery spells, dating to the game's launch. See [Magery](../magery.md).

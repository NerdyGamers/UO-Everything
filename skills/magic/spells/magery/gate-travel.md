# Gate Travel

A seventh circle magery spell that opens a gate others can step through.

## Casting

Words of power: *Vas Rel Por*. Seventh circle magery spell. Mana cost: 40. Minimum Magery skill: 86. Reagents: black pearl, mandrake root, sulfurous ash.

## Effect

Opens a shimmering moongate to a rune-marked location that anyone nearby can enter.

## Notes

The gate stays open briefly; see [Recall](recall.md). One of the original magery spells, dating to the game's launch. See [Magery](../magery.md).

# Lightning

A fourth circle magery spell that calls down a lightning bolt.

## Casting

Words of power: *Por Ort Grav*. Fourth circle magery spell. Mana cost: 11. Minimum Magery skill: 43. Reagents: mandrake root, sulfurous ash.

## Effect

Strikes the target with a bolt of lightning for energy damage.

## Notes

One of the original magery spells, dating to the game's launch. See [Magery](../magery.md).

# Blade Spirits

A fifth circle magery spell that summons a whirling blade spirit.

## Casting

Words of power: *In Jux Hur Ylem*. Fifth circle magery spell. Mana cost: 14. Minimum Magery skill: 58. Reagents: black pearl, mandrake root, nightshade.

## Effect

Conjures a spinning blade spirit that attacks the caster's enemies on its own.

## Notes

One of the original magery spells, dating to the game's launch. See [Magery](../magery.md).

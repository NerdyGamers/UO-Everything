# Curse

A fourth circle magery spell that lays a withering curse on the target.

## Casting

Words of power: *Des Sanct*. Fourth circle magery spell. Mana cost: 11. Minimum Magery skill: 43. Reagents: garlic, nightshade, sulfurous ash.

## Effect

Temporarily lowers all of the target's stats.

## Notes

One of the original magery spells, dating to the game's launch. See [Magery](../magery.md).

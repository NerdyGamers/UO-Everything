# Summon Water Elemental

A eighth circle magery spell that calls a water elemental to serve.

## Casting

Words of power: *Kal Vas Xen An Lor*. Eighth circle magery spell. Mana cost: 50. Minimum Magery skill: 100. Reagents: blood moss, mandrake root, spider's silk.

## Effect

Summons a powerful water elemental that fights for the caster.

## Notes

One of the original magery spells, dating to the game's launch. See [Magery](../magery.md).

# Flamestrike

A seventh circle magery spell that drops a pillar of flame.

## Casting

Words of power: *Kal Vas Flam*. Seventh circle magery spell. Mana cost: 40. Minimum Magery skill: 86. Reagents: spider's silk, sulfurous ash.

## Effect

Calls down a column of fire on the target for heavy damage.

## Notes

One of the original magery spells, dating to the game's launch. See [Magery](../magery.md).

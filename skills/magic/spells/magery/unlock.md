# Unlock

A third circle magery spell that opens magically sealed locks.

## Casting

Words of power: *Ex Por*. Third circle magery spell. Mana cost: 9. Minimum Magery skill: 29. Reagents: blood moss, sulfurous ash.

## Effect

Undoes a Magic Lock on a door or container.

## Notes

One of the original magery spells, dating to the game's launch. See [Magery](../magery.md).

# Poison

A third circle magery spell that infects the target with poison.

## Casting

Words of power: *In Nox*. Third circle magery spell. Mana cost: 9. Minimum Magery skill: 29. Reagents: nightshade.

## Effect

Poisons the target; higher skill inflicts stronger poison.

## Notes

One of the original magery spells, dating to the game's launch. See [Magery](../magery.md).

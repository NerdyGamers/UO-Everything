# Evil Omen

A necromancy spell that marks the target for a crueler next spell.

## Casting

Words of power: *Pas Tym An Sanct*. Necromancy spell. Mana cost: 11. Minimum Necromancy skill: 20. Requires no reagents.

## Effect

The next harmful spell cast on the target lands with enhanced effect.

## Notes

Necromancy spells require no reagents. Many grow stronger with the Spirit Speak skill, and the transformation spells cannot be combined with each other. Introduced with Age of Shadows (2003). See [Necromancy](../necromancy.md).

# Pain Spike

A necromancy spell that wracks the target with sudden agony.

## Casting

Words of power: *In Sar*. Necromancy spell. Mana cost: 5. Minimum Necromancy skill: 20. Requires no reagents.

## Effect

Deals direct damage to the target. Its strength relies on Spirit Speak.

## Notes

Necromancy spells require no reagents. Many grow stronger with the Spirit Speak skill, and the transformation spells cannot be combined with each other. Introduced with Age of Shadows (2003). See [Necromancy](../necromancy.md).

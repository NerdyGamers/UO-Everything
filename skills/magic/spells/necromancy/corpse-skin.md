# Corpse Skin

A necromancy spell that rots the target's flesh against fire and poison.

## Casting

Words of power: *In Aglo Corp Ylem*. Necromancy spell. Mana cost: 11. Minimum Necromancy skill: 20. Requires no reagents.

## Effect

Makes the target take increased damage from fire and poison attacks. Its strength relies on Spirit Speak.

## Notes

Necromancy spells require no reagents. Many grow stronger with the Spirit Speak skill, and the transformation spells cannot be combined with each other. Introduced with Age of Shadows (2003). See [Necromancy](../necromancy.md).

# Strangle

A necromancy spell that chokes the life from the target.

## Casting

Words of power: *In Bal Nox*. Necromancy spell. Mana cost: 29. Minimum Necromancy skill: 65. Requires no reagents.

## Effect

Curses the target so they take poison damage every few seconds until the effect fades. Its strength relies on Spirit Speak.

## Notes

Necromancy spells require no reagents. Many grow stronger with the Spirit Speak skill, and the transformation spells cannot be combined with each other. Introduced with Age of Shadows (2003). See [Necromancy](../necromancy.md).

# Wraith Form

A necromancy spell that reshapes the caster into a wraith.

## Casting

Words of power: *Rel Xen Um*. Necromancy spell. Mana cost: 17. Minimum Necromancy skill: 20. Requires no reagents.

## Effect

Transforms the caster into a wraith, gaining the ability to leech mana with weapon strikes and other spectral traits. Its strength relies on Spirit Speak.

## Notes

Necromancy spells require no reagents. Many grow stronger with the Spirit Speak skill, and the transformation spells cannot be combined with each other. Introduced with Age of Shadows (2003). See [Necromancy](../necromancy.md).

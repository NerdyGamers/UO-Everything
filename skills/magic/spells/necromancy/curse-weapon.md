# Curse Weapon

A necromancy spell that enchants the caster's weapon to drink life.

## Casting

Words of power: *An Sanct Gra Char*. Necromancy spell. Mana cost: 7. Minimum Necromancy skill: 0. Requires no reagents.

## Effect

Briefly enchants the caster's weapon so its strikes drain life from the victim into the caster.

## Notes

Necromancy spells require no reagents. Many grow stronger with the Spirit Speak skill, and the transformation spells cannot be combined with each other. Introduced with Age of Shadows (2003). See [Necromancy](../necromancy.md).

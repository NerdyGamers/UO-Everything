# Mind Rot

A necromancy spell that rots the target's grasp on their own mana.

## Casting

Words of power: *Wis An Ben*. Necromancy spell. Mana cost: 17. Minimum Necromancy skill: 30. Requires no reagents.

## Effect

Forces the target to spend extra mana whenever they cast. Its strength relies on Spirit Speak.

## Notes

Necromancy spells require no reagents. Many grow stronger with the Spirit Speak skill, and the transformation spells cannot be combined with each other. Introduced with Age of Shadows (2003). See [Necromancy](../necromancy.md).

# Exorcism

A necromancy spell that drives summoned spirits back to the void.

## Casting

Words of power: *Ort Corp Grav*. Necromancy spell. Mana cost: 40. Minimum Necromancy skill: 80. Requires no reagents.

## Effect

Damages and can banish summoned creatures; it also sends lingering ghosts in champion spawn regions to the Chaos Shrine. Requires Spirit Speak.

## Notes

Necromancy spells require no reagents. Many grow stronger with the Spirit Speak skill, and the transformation spells cannot be combined with each other. Introduced with Age of Shadows (2003). See [Necromancy](../necromancy.md).

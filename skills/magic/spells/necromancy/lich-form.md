# Lich Form

A necromancy spell that reshapes the caster into a lich.

## Casting

Words of power: *Rel Xen Corp Ort*. Necromancy spell. Mana cost: 23. Minimum Necromancy skill: 70. Requires no reagents.

## Effect

Transforms the caster into a lich with greatly improved mana regeneration and cold affinity, at the cost of fire vulnerability.

## Notes

Necromancy spells require no reagents. Many grow stronger with the Spirit Speak skill, and the transformation spells cannot be combined with each other. Introduced with Age of Shadows (2003). See [Necromancy](../necromancy.md).

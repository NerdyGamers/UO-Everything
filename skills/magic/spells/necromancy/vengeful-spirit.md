# Vengeful Spirit

A necromancy spell that sends a revenant to hunt the target.

## Casting

Words of power: *Kal Xen Bal Beh*. Necromancy spell. Mana cost: 41. Minimum Necromancy skill: 80. Requires no reagents.

## Effect

Summons a revenant that relentlessly hunts the chosen target. Its strength relies on Spirit Speak, and it requires Spirit Speak to cast.

## Notes

Necromancy spells require no reagents. Many grow stronger with the Spirit Speak skill, and the transformation spells cannot be combined with each other. Introduced with Age of Shadows (2003). See [Necromancy](../necromancy.md).

# Animate Dead

A necromancy spell that raises a slain creature to fight again.

## Casting

Words of power: *Uus Corp*. Necromancy spell. Mana cost: 23. Minimum Necromancy skill: 40. Requires no reagents.

## Effect

Brings a dead creature back to unlife; it attacks the caster's enemies for a time.

## Notes

Necromancy spells require no reagents. Many grow stronger with the Spirit Speak skill, and the transformation spells cannot be combined with each other. Introduced with Age of Shadows (2003). See [Necromancy](../necromancy.md).

# Blood Oath

A necromancy spell that binds caster and target in a pact of pain.

## Casting

Words of power: *In Jux Mani Xen*. Necromancy spell. Mana cost: 13. Minimum Necromancy skill: 20. Requires no reagents.

## Effect

Creates a pact between caster and target: for a time, damage the target deals to the caster is reflected back at them. Its strength relies on Spirit Speak.

## Notes

Necromancy spells require no reagents. Many grow stronger with the Spirit Speak skill, and the transformation spells cannot be combined with each other. Introduced with Age of Shadows (2003). See [Necromancy](../necromancy.md).

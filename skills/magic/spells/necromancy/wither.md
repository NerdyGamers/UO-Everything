# Wither

A necromancy spell that spreads killing frost around the caster.

## Casting

Words of power: *Kal Vas An Flam*. Necromancy spell. Mana cost: 23. Minimum Necromancy skill: 60. Requires no reagents.

## Effect

Creates a ring of frost around the caster that deals cold damage to all valid targets.

## Notes

Necromancy spells require no reagents. Many grow stronger with the Spirit Speak skill, and the transformation spells cannot be combined with each other. Introduced with Age of Shadows (2003). See [Necromancy](../necromancy.md).

# Summon Familiar

A necromancy spell that calls a minor familiar to serve.

## Casting

Words of power: *Kal Xen Bal*. Necromancy spell. Mana cost: 17. Minimum Necromancy skill: 30. Requires no reagents.

## Effect

Brings forth a small familiar — a dark wolf, death adder, vampire bat, or wisp — each with its own special ability. Requires Spirit Speak.

## Notes

Necromancy spells require no reagents. Many grow stronger with the Spirit Speak skill, and the transformation spells cannot be combined with each other. Introduced with Age of Shadows (2003). See [Necromancy](../necromancy.md).

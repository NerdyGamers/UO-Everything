# Poison Strike

A necromancy spell that blasts the target with venom.

## Casting

Words of power: *In Vas Nox*. Necromancy spell. Mana cost: 17. Minimum Necromancy skill: 50. Requires no reagents.

## Effect

Strikes the target and adjacent enemies with poison damage.

## Notes

Necromancy spells require no reagents. Many grow stronger with the Spirit Speak skill, and the transformation spells cannot be combined with each other. Introduced with Age of Shadows (2003). See [Necromancy](../necromancy.md).

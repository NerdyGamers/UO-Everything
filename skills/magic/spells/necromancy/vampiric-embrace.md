# Vampiric Embrace

A necromancy spell that reshapes the caster into a vampire.

## Casting

Words of power: *Rel Xen An Sanct*. Necromancy spell. Mana cost: 23. Minimum Necromancy skill: 99. Requires no reagents.

## Effect

Transforms the caster into a vampire: immune to lesser poisons, able to drain life with strikes, with other vampiric traits.

## Notes

Necromancy spells require no reagents. Many grow stronger with the Spirit Speak skill, and the transformation spells cannot be combined with each other. Introduced with Age of Shadows (2003). See [Necromancy](../necromancy.md).

# Horrific Beast

A necromancy spell that unleashes the beast within the caster.

## Casting

Words of power: *Rel Xen Vas Bal*. Necromancy spell. Mana cost: 11. Minimum Necromancy skill: 40. Requires no reagents.

## Effect

Transforms the caster into a horrific beast with greatly improved health regeneration and savage physical attacks.

## Notes

Necromancy spells require no reagents. Many grow stronger with the Spirit Speak skill, and the transformation spells cannot be combined with each other. Introduced with Age of Shadows (2003). See [Necromancy](../necromancy.md).

# Consecrate Weapon

A chivalry spell that blesses the caster's weapon against the foe's weakest ward.

## Casting

Words of power: *Consecrus Arma*. Chivalry spell. Mana cost: 10. Minimum Chivalry skill: 15. Tithing cost: 10. Requires no reagents.

## Effect

For a time, the caster's weapon strikes with whichever damage type the target resists least.

## Notes

Chivalry spells require no reagents but spend tithing points donated at shrines. Their strength grows with the caster's karma — a paladin in good standing casts mightier spells. Requires a Book of Chivalry in the pack. Introduced with Age of Shadows (2003). See [Chivalry](../chivalry.md).

# Sacred Journey

A chivalry spell that carries the caster to a marked rune.

## Casting

Words of power: *Sanctum Viatas*. Chivalry spell. Mana cost: 10. Minimum Chivalry skill: 15. Tithing cost: 15. Requires no reagents.

## Effect

Teleports the caster to a rune-marked location, working like the Recall spell.

## Notes

Chivalry spells require no reagents but spend tithing points donated at shrines. Their strength grows with the caster's karma — a paladin in good standing casts mightier spells. Requires a Book of Chivalry in the pack. Introduced with Age of Shadows (2003). See [Chivalry](../chivalry.md).

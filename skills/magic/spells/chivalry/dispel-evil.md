# Dispel Evil

A chivalry spell that scatters the wicked and the summoned.

## Casting

Words of power: *Dispiro Malas*. Chivalry spell. Mana cost: 10. Minimum Chivalry skill: 35. Tithing cost: 10. Requires no reagents.

## Effect

Causes monsters to flee from the caster and harms necromancers wearing transformed shapes; it can also dismiss hostile summoned creatures nearby.

## Notes

Chivalry spells require no reagents but spend tithing points donated at shrines. Their strength grows with the caster's karma — a paladin in good standing casts mightier spells. Requires a Book of Chivalry in the pack. Introduced with Age of Shadows (2003). See [Chivalry](../chivalry.md).

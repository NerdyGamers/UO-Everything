# Enemy of One

A chivalry spell that declares a single foe-type the caster's sworn enemy.

## Casting

Words of power: *Forul Solum*. Chivalry spell. Mana cost: 20. Minimum Chivalry skill: 45. Tithing cost: 10. Requires no reagents.

## Effect

Greatly increases the caster's damage against one chosen monster type, but doubles damage taken from all others.

## Notes

Chivalry spells require no reagents but spend tithing points donated at shrines. Their strength grows with the caster's karma — a paladin in good standing casts mightier spells. Requires a Book of Chivalry in the pack. Introduced with Age of Shadows (2003). See [Chivalry](../chivalry.md).

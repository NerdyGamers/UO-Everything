# Noble Sacrifice

A chivalry spell that gives the caster's own life-force to save allies.

## Casting

Words of power: *Dium Prostra*. Chivalry spell. Mana cost: 20. Minimum Chivalry skill: 65. Tithing cost: 30. Requires no reagents.

## Effect

Resurrects, cures, and heals allies around the caster, but reduces the caster's own health, stamina, and mana to nearly nothing.

## Notes

Chivalry spells require no reagents but spend tithing points donated at shrines. Their strength grows with the caster's karma — a paladin in good standing casts mightier spells. Requires a Book of Chivalry in the pack. Introduced with Age of Shadows (2003). See [Chivalry](../chivalry.md).

# Divine Fury

A chivalry spell that fills the caster with righteous battle-frenzy.

## Casting

Words of power: *Divinum Furis*. Chivalry spell. Mana cost: 15. Minimum Chivalry skill: 25. Tithing cost: 10. Requires no reagents.

## Effect

Quickens the caster's swing speed, sharpens their aim, and refreshes all stamina, while lowering their defense.

## Notes

Chivalry spells require no reagents but spend tithing points donated at shrines. Their strength grows with the caster's karma — a paladin in good standing casts mightier spells. Requires a Book of Chivalry in the pack. Introduced with Age of Shadows (2003). See [Chivalry](../chivalry.md).

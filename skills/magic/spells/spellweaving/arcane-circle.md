# Arcane Circle

A spellweaving spell that raises a circle that focuses arcane power.

## Casting

Words of power: *Myrshalee*. Spellweaving spell. Mana cost: 24. Minimum Spellweaving skill: 0. Requires no reagents.

## Effect

Creates an arcane focus crystal that strengthens the caster's and nearby allies' later spellweaving.

## Notes

Spellweaving spells require no reagents. Training begins with the Patience or Discipline quests in Heartwood or Sanctuary; further spell scrolls drop from Mondain's Legacy dungeon monsters. Introduced with Mondain's Legacy (2005). See [Spellweaving](../spellweaving.md).

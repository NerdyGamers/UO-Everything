# Attunement

A spellweaving spell that raises a shield against physical blows.

## Casting

Words of power: *Haeldril*. Spellweaving spell. Mana cost: 24. Minimum Spellweaving skill: 0. Requires no reagents.

## Effect

Surrounds the caster with a magical shield that absorbs melee damage.

## Notes

Spellweaving spells require no reagents. Training begins with the Patience or Discipline quests in Heartwood or Sanctuary; further spell scrolls drop from Mondain's Legacy dungeon monsters. Introduced with Mondain's Legacy (2005). See [Spellweaving](../spellweaving.md).

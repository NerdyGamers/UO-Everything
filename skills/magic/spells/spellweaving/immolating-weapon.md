# Immolating Weapon

A spellweaving spell that sets the caster's weapon ablaze.

## Casting

Words of power: *Thalshara*. Spellweaving spell. Mana cost: 32. Minimum Spellweaving skill: 10. Requires no reagents.

## Effect

Enchants the caster's melee weapon with extra fire damage for a short time.

## Notes

Spellweaving spells require no reagents. Training begins with the Patience or Discipline quests in Heartwood or Sanctuary; further spell scrolls drop from Mondain's Legacy dungeon monsters. Introduced with Mondain's Legacy (2005). See [Spellweaving](../spellweaving.md).

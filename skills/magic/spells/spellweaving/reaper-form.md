# Reaper Form

A spellweaving spell that reshapes the caster into a reaper.

## Casting

Words of power: *Tarisstree*. Spellweaving spell. Mana cost: 34. Minimum Spellweaving skill: 24. Requires no reagents.

## Effect

Transforms the caster into a reaper: faster swings, stronger spells, and tougher resists, but slower movement and weakened fire resistance.

## Notes

Spellweaving spells require no reagents. Training begins with the Patience or Discipline quests in Heartwood or Sanctuary; further spell scrolls drop from Mondain's Legacy dungeon monsters. Introduced with Mondain's Legacy (2005). See [Spellweaving](../spellweaving.md).

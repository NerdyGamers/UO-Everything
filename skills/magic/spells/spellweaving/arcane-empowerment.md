# Arcane Empowerment

A spellweaving spell that surges the caster's magic to new heights.

## Casting

Words of power: *Aslavdra*. Spellweaving spell. Mana cost: 50. Minimum Spellweaving skill: 24. Requires no reagents.

## Effect

Briefly empowers the caster's damaging and healing spells and toughens their summons.

## Notes

Spellweaving spells require no reagents. Training begins with the Patience or Discipline quests in Heartwood or Sanctuary; further spell scrolls drop from Mondain's Legacy dungeon monsters. Introduced with Mondain's Legacy (2005). See [Spellweaving](../spellweaving.md).

# Essence of Wind

A spellweaving spell that chills foes to sluggishness.

## Casting

Words of power: *Anathrae*. Spellweaving spell. Mana cost: 40. Minimum Spellweaving skill: 52. Requires no reagents.

## Effect

Deals cold damage and leaves nearby enemies swinging slower and recovering from casts more slowly.

## Notes

Spellweaving spells require no reagents. Training begins with the Patience or Discipline quests in Heartwood or Sanctuary; further spell scrolls drop from Mondain's Legacy dungeon monsters. Introduced with Mondain's Legacy (2005). See [Spellweaving](../spellweaving.md).

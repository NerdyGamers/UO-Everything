# Summon Fiend

A spellweaving spell that calls an imp ally.

## Casting

Words of power: *Nylisstra*. Spellweaving spell. Mana cost: 10. Minimum Spellweaving skill: 38. Requires no reagents.

## Effect

Summons one or more imps that fight under the caster's control. Casting it costs the caster karma.

## Notes

Spellweaving spells require no reagents. Training begins with the Patience or Discipline quests in Heartwood or Sanctuary; further spell scrolls drop from Mondain's Legacy dungeon monsters. Introduced with Mondain's Legacy (2005). See [Spellweaving](../spellweaving.md).

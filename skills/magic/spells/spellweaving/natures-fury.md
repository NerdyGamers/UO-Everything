# Nature's Fury

A spellweaving spell that unleashes a swarm of stinging insects.

## Casting

Words of power: *Rauvvrae*. Spellweaving spell. Mana cost: 24. Minimum Spellweaving skill: 0. Requires no reagents.

## Effect

Releases an uncontrollable swarm of insects that attacks nearby enemies.

## Notes

Spellweaving spells require no reagents. Training begins with the Patience or Discipline quests in Heartwood or Sanctuary; further spell scrolls drop from Mondain's Legacy dungeon monsters. Introduced with Mondain's Legacy (2005). See [Spellweaving](../spellweaving.md).

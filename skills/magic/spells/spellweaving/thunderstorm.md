# Thunderstorm

A spellweaving spell that calls a storm down on everything nearby.

## Casting

Words of power: *Erelonia*. Spellweaving spell. Mana cost: 32. Minimum Spellweaving skill: 10. Requires no reagents.

## Effect

Blasts everything around the caster with energy damage and can leave victims slower to recover their casting.

## Notes

Spellweaving spells require no reagents. Training begins with the Patience or Discipline quests in Heartwood or Sanctuary; further spell scrolls drop from Mondain's Legacy dungeon monsters. Introduced with Mondain's Legacy (2005). See [Spellweaving](../spellweaving.md).

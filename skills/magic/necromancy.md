# Necromancy

**Canon**

Dark magic of seventeen spells, strongest in the hands of the wicked — introduced with Age of Shadows (2003).

## How it works
Necromancy spells use their own reagents (bat wing, grave dust, daemon blood, nox crystal, pig iron). Power scales with Necromancy and [Spirit Speak](spirit-speak.md), and — crucially — with negative karma: a caster at deeply negative karma wields far stronger magic than a virtuous one. Signature magic includes transformative forms (Lich, Vampire, Wraith, Horrific Beast), Wither, Strangle, Pain Spike, Curse Weapon, and Vengeful Spirit. Individual skills cap at 100 (120 with power scrolls); the total skill cap is 700 points.

## Training
Casting low-circle spells repeatedly; maintaining negative karma, which drifts toward zero over time and needs upkeep.

## Synergies
[Spirit Speak](spirit-speak.md) is the mandatory companion skill. Pairs with [Magery](magery.md) or melee on necro-mage and dread-paladin templates; [Meditation](meditation.md) feeds the mana cost.

## Era notes
Introduced in Age of Shadows (2003). Form bonuses and karma scaling have been adjusted in later publishes.

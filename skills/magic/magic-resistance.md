# Magic Resistance


A purely passive defensive skill that reduces spell damage and resists harmful magical effects.

## How it works
Magic Resistance lowers damage taken from offensive spells and gives a chance to fully resist effects such as paralyze, poison, curse, and mana drain. It works automatically — there is no active use. It also factors into resisting some creature special abilities. Community testing describes damage reduction scaling with skill, but exact formulas are era-dependent. Individual skills cap at 100 (120 with power scrolls); the total skill cap is 700 points.

## Training
Traditionally trained by having spells cast on oneself (or standing in damaging spell fields); it gains on resisted hits.

## Synergies
A staple on virtually every template — warriors, tamers, and crafters alike take it to survive enemy casters. It complements [Magery](magery.md)-based offense by blunting return fire.

## Era notes
Resist mechanics, including the interaction between skill level, spell damage, and resist checks, have been tuned across publishes.

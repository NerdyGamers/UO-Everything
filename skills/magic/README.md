# Magic

**Canon**

The Magic group covers Britannia's spellcasting and spell-adjacent skills: the classic schools, their supporting skills, and the crafting arts that feed them.

## Skills

- [Alchemy](alchemy.md) — brewing potions, from healing draughts to explosion flasks.
- [Chivalry](chivalry.md) — paladin holy magic powered by karma and tithing (Age of Shadows, 2003).
- [Evaluating Intelligence](evaluating-intelligence.md) — spell-damage scaling and gauging a target's mind.
- [Inscription](inscription.md) — scribing spell scrolls; empowers the scribe's own magery.
- [Magery](magery.md) — the core art: 64 spells in eight circles.
- [Magic Resistance](magic-resistance.md) — passive defense against hostile magic.
- [Meditation](meditation.md) — mana regeneration, passive and active.
- [Mysticism](mysticism.md) — nether magic of the gargoyles (Stygian Abyss, 2009).
- [Necromancy](necromancy.md) — dark magic, strongest for the wicked (Age of Shadows, 2003).
- [Spellweaving](spellweaving.md) — elven arcane magic woven in circles (Mondain's Legacy, 2005).
- [Spirit Speak](spirit-speak.md) — communion with the dead; empowers necromancy.

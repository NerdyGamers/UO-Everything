# Magery


The core spellcasting skill of Britannia, governing 64 spells arranged in eight circles of increasing power.

## How it works
Each spell belongs to a circle (1–8); higher circles demand higher skill, rising roughly 10 skill points per circle according to community documentation. Casting consumes mana and reagents, unless the caster has enough Lower Reagent Cost on their equipment. A failed cast (a "fizzle") still consumes mana. Taking damage while casting can disrupt the spell, and moving cancels most casts. Individual skills cap at 100 (120 with power scrolls); the total skill cap is 700 points.

## Training
New mages train on low-circle spells, commonly by casting buffs or low-mana spells repeatedly. Power scrolls (105/110/115/120) drop as champion-spawn and peerless rewards, unlocking the skill's full potential at 120.

## Synergies
[Evaluating Intelligence](evaluating-intelligence.md) raises spell damage; [Meditation](meditation.md) restores the mana pool (sized by Intelligence); [Inscription](inscription.md) adds a spell-damage bonus; [Magic Resistance](magic-resistance.md) defends against enemy casters.

## Era notes
Magery predates Ultima Online's launch (1997) and has been rebalanced across many publishes, including spell-damage formulas and casting mechanics. Exact historical numbers vary by era; treat older guides as era-specific.

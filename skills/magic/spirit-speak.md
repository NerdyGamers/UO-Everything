# Spirit Speak


Communion with the dead — and the skill that empowers necromancy.

## How it works
Spirit Speak has two roles. Used actively, it lets the living hear and converse with ghosts. Passively, it amplifies [Necromancy](necromancy.md): spell damage, duration, and the potency of necromantic forms all scale with Spirit Speak alongside Necromancy itself. Individual skills cap at 100 (120 with power scrolls); the total skill cap is 700 points.

## Training
Gains come from active use near corpses or spirits and from casting necromancy spells; it is almost always trained alongside [Necromancy](necromancy.md).

## Synergies
Inseparable from [Necromancy](necromancy.md). Also of use to roleplayers and treasure hunters who want to question the recently departed.

## Era notes
Reworked from its pre-Age of Shadows form — where it mainly served to hear ghosts — into necromancy's companion skill in 2003.

# Inscription

**Canon**

The scribe's craft: copying spells onto scrolls, with a side benefit that empowers the scribe's own magery.

## How it works
Using a scribe's pen and blank scrolls, an inscriber writes spell scrolls that anyone can later cast from. Higher-circle scrolls demand higher Inscription (and Magery). As a combat benefit, Inscription grants a spell-damage bonus to the scribe's own Magery spells — community documentation describes +1% per 10 points of skill (+10% at GM). Individual skills cap at 100 (120 with power scrolls); the total skill cap is 700 points.

## Training
Scribing scrolls in bulk, typically lower-circle scrolls for cost efficiency; each attempt is a gain chance.

## Synergies
Pairs with [Magery](magery.md) for the damage bonus and the Magery requirement behind high-circle scrolls. Finished scrolls are trade goods for any caster.

## Era notes
The spell-damage bonus for inscription was added after launch; its exact value has been publish-tuned.

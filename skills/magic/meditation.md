# Meditation

**Canon**

Governs mana regeneration, both passively and through active meditation.

## How it works
Meditation increases the rate at which mana regenerates. Activating the skill (via the skill button or a macro) puts the character into a meditative state with faster regeneration, broken by movement or combat actions. Armor interferes: pieces carrying a meditation penalty reduce or block active meditation unless the armor has the "mage armor" (medable) property. Individual skills cap at 100 (120 with power scrolls); the total skill cap is 700 points.

## Training
Gains come from successful active meditation ticks. Mages commonly macro meditation between spell casts; wearing medable armor avoids penalties while training in the field.

## Synergies
Essential for every mana user: [Magery](magery.md), [Necromancy](necromancy.md), [Mysticism](mysticism.md), [Spellweaving](spellweaving.md), and [Chivalry](chivalry.md). Intelligence sets the size of the mana pool that Meditation refills.

## Era notes
Meditation is original to UO (1997). Armor meditation penalties and the mage-armor property were refined in later publishes.

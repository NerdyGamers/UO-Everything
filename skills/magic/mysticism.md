# Mysticism


The Stygian Abyss spell school (2009): sixteen spells blending raw nether energy with gargish tradition.

## How it works
Mysticism is cast from a Mystic's Spellbook. Its power draws on three skills: Mysticism itself plus Focus and Imbuing, which jointly scale spell damage and duration. Community documentation describes the three skills as averaged together, but exact weighting is publish-dependent — treat specific numbers as approximate. The sixteen spells include Nether Bolt, Healing Stone, Cleansing Winds, Hail Storm, Nether Cyclone, Bombard, Spell Plague, Spell Trigger, and the Rising Colossus summon. Individual skills cap at 100 (120 with power scrolls); the total skill cap is 700 points.

## Training
Casting low-cost spells such as Nether Bolt or Healing Stone on repeat; the triple-skill dependency makes it one of the most point-hungry schools to maximize.

## Synergies
Focus and Imbuing are the mechanical companions (documented under their own skill groups); [Meditation](meditation.md) sustains casting. The school suits "mystic mage" templates.

## Era notes
Introduced with the Stygian Abyss expansion (2009) alongside the gargoyle race. Spell tuning has continued in later publishes.

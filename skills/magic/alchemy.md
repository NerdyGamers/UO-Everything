# Alchemy


The crafting skill behind potions — healing draughts, cures, explosions, and more.

## How it works
With a mortar and pestle and the proper reagents, an alchemist brews potions. Potion strength (lesser/greater grades) and success chance scale with skill; the most potent concoctions — greater explosion, conflagration, and shrinking potions — require near-GM skill. Individual skills cap at 100 (120 with power scrolls, though few builds take it past GM); the total skill cap is 700 points.

## Training
Brewing potions in bulk; higher-tier potions give better gains but cost more reagents. Reagents are also consumed by [Magery](magery.md), so alchemists often buy or farm in bulk.

## Synergies
Explosion potions are a staple of dexter and thrower templates; heal and cure potions support every fighter. Economically it pairs with [Magery](magery.md) reagent gathering.

## Era notes
Alchemy is original to UO (1997). Potion effects and keg/bottle mechanics have seen publish-era adjustments.

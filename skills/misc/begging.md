# Begging

**Canon**

Begging allows a character to plead with NPCs for coins or small gifts.

## How it works

Target a nearby NPC — typically shopkeepers and other town NPCs — and a successful plea moves them to hand over a small amount of gold. Begging works on NPCs only, not on other players or creatures. Higher skill improves both success chance and generosity. There is a cooldown before the same NPC can be begged again. Individual skills cap at 100 (120 with power scrolls); the total skill cap is 700 points.

## Training

Beg from NPCs in towns repeatedly. Busy bank and shop districts, with many NPCs in reach, are the traditional training grounds.

## Synergies

Begging is largely standalone — a flavor and roleplay skill with no combat or crafting interactions.

## Era notes

Mechanically almost unchanged since launch. It remains a niche skill with no endgame or PvP application.

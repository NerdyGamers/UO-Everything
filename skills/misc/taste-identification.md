# Taste Identification


Taste Identification lets a character sample food, drink, and potions to determine what they are — and whether the food is poisoned.

## How it works

Use the skill on a consumable to identify its type. Its signature use is detecting poisoned food or drink before eating — a successful taste warns of poison, making it the classic countermeasure against poisoned refreshments. Individual skills cap at 100 (120 with power scrolls); the total skill cap is 700 points.

## Training

Taste food and potions repeatedly. Cooks and alchemists historically trained it alongside their crafts, with no shortage of samples.

## Synergies

- [Alchemy](../magic/alchemy.md) — identifying potions.
- [Cooking](../crafting/cooking.md) — identifying food.
- [Poisoning](../rogue/poisoning.md) — the counterplay: spotting poisoned consumables.

## Era notes

Its identification role diminished as the game revealed more item information directly over the years; detecting poisoned food and drink remains its enduring use.

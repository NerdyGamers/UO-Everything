# Cartography

**Canon**

Cartography is the art of mapmaking — most importantly, decoding the treasure maps that lead to buried chests.

## How it works

Use the skill on a treasure map to decode it and reveal the marked location. Maps come in levels (commonly levels 1–6, from crudely to devilishly drawn), each demanding progressively higher skill to decode; exact minimums are community-reported and vary by source, so confirm against a current reference before planning around them. A decoded map is dug up with Mining, and the unearthed chest generally requires Lockpicking and Remove Trap to open safely. Cartographers can also craft blank maps and world maps. (Sea charts for High Seas-era sailing are attributed to Cartography in community sources — exact mechanics uncertain.) Individual skills cap at 100 (120 with power scrolls); the total skill cap is 700 points.

## Training

Craft maps and decode low-level treasure maps repeatedly. Beginners typically start with blank and world maps before moving on to real treasure maps.

## Synergies

- [Mining](../gathering/mining.md) — dig up the decoded chest.
- [Lockpicking](../rogue/lockpicking.md) and [Remove Trap](../rogue/remove-trap.md) — open it safely.
- [Fishing](../gathering/fishing.md) — message-in-a-bottle leads for the nautically inclined.

## Era notes

The core decode–dig–unlock loop is unchanged since the skill's introduction; later eras added higher-level maps and richer chests.

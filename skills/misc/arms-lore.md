# Arms Lore

**Canon**

Arms Lore lets a character judge the quality and condition of weapons and armor, and at high skill reveals more about an item's properties.

## How it works

Target a weapon or piece of armor to receive an assessment of its condition, ranging from pristine to about to fall apart. Higher skill produces more precise readings. Some sources report that very high skill reveals additional detail about magical properties, but the exact thresholds are not consistently documented — treat specific claims with caution. Individual skills cap at 100 (120 with power scrolls); the total skill cap is 700 points.

## Training

Use the skill repeatedly on any weapons or armor. Practitioners often train on their own equipment or on loot, since any valid target works.

## Synergies

- [Anatomy](../combat/anatomy.md) — the classic lore pairing for judging warriors and their gear.
- [Item Identification](item-identification.md) — the two gear-evaluation skills complement each other.
- Crafting skills ([crafting](../crafting/README.md)) — smiths and tailors checking their own work.

## Era notes

Arms Lore's practical role has grown and shrunk with the game's itemization: always useful for checking gear condition, more or less central to evaluating loot depending on era.

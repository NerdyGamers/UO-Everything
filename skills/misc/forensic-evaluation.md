# Forensic Evaluation

**Canon**

Forensic Evaluation lets a character examine corpses to learn about the death — most famously, the name of the killer.

## How it works

Use the skill on a corpse to receive findings about how it died. The signature result names the killer, which once made this skill central to player bounty hunting. Higher skill is reported to reveal additional detail, though exact messages and thresholds are community-recollected rather than officially documented. Individual skills cap at 100 (120 with power scrolls); the total skill cap is 700 points.

## Training

Use the skill on corpses — player, NPC, or creature remains all work. Graveyards and recent battlefields are the traditional practice grounds.

## Synergies

- [Tracking](../rogue/tracking.md) — find the dead and the living alike.
- [Spirit Speak](../magic/spirit-speak.md) — another way to commune with the departed.

## Era notes

Largely unchanged since launch. Its bounty-hunting heyday faded as PvP systems evolved, but the skill itself works as ever.

# Item Identification


Item Identification reveals the hidden properties of magic items.

## How it works

Target an unidentified magic item to learn what enchantments it carries. In the early game this was essential — magic gear appeared generic until identified, and unidentified loot was the norm for every adventurer. Individual skills cap at 100 (120 with power scrolls); the total skill cap is 700 points.

## Training

Identify magic items repeatedly. Monster loot and dungeon chests historically supplied endless practice material.

## Synergies

- [Arms Lore](arms-lore.md) — the two gear-evaluation skills pair naturally.

## Era notes

The Age of Shadows itemization overhaul made most properties visible without identification, sharply reducing this skill's day-to-day role. It remains the classic answer for the rare item that still conceals its nature.

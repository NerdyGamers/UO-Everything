# Wilderness skills

**Canon**

The wilderness group covers living off the land and working with animals: taming, healing, judging, herding, and roughing it.

## Skills
- [Animal Lore](animal-lore.md) — appraise a creature's stats and taming difficulty.
- [Animal Taming](animal-taming.md) — tame wild creatures as pets and mounts.
- [Camping](camping.md) — build campfires and rest for faster recovery.
- [Herding](herding.md) — direct animals with a shepherd's crook.
- [Veterinary](veterinary.md) — heal and cure pets with bandages.

## How the group fits together
The tamer trio — [Animal Taming](animal-taming.md), [Animal Lore](animal-lore.md), [Veterinary](veterinary.md) — is one of the game's classic templates. [Camping](camping.md) and [Herding](herding.md) round out the ranger and shepherd fantasy.

# Camping

**Canon**

Camping lets a character build a campfire from kindling and rest beside it for faster recovery.

## How it works
Use kindling on the ground to build a campfire; sitting or standing near the lit fire accelerates hit point, stamina, and mana regeneration (community recollection on exact rates). It is a flavor skill for wanderers and roleplayers rather than a mechanical powerhouse. Like all skills, it caps at 100 (120 with power scrolls) against the 700-point total skill cap.

## Training
Build campfires repeatedly; gains are straightforward but slow at high levels. Kindling is sold by provisioners.

## Synergies
Thematic with cooking and the ranger/wanderer fantasy; pairs naturally with [Herding](herding.md) on shepherd templates. See [crafting](../../crafting/README.md) for campfire cooking.

## Era notes
A launch skill (1997), largely unchanged since — and commonly cited as one of the least-trained skills in the game (community recollection).

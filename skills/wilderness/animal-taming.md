# Animal Taming


Animal Taming lets a character tame wild creatures as pets and mounts.

## How it works
Target a tamable creature and attempt to tame it; each species has a minimum taming requirement, from trivial for rabbits and birds to over 100 for the most powerful beasts (power scrolls raise the cap to 120). Weakening an aggressive creature first makes difficult tames safer. Tamed pets occupy control slots — a tamer is commonly cited as having 5 (community recollection) — with stronger creatures costing more slots. Pets that hold maximum loyalty for about a week can bond, after which they can be renamed and resurrected (community recollection). Like all skills, it caps at 100 (120 with power scrolls) against the 700-point total skill cap.

## Training
Tame progressively harder creatures; each species gates gains. The usual path runs from farm animals up through bulls, hell hounds, and eventually dragons and greater dragons.

## Synergies
The classic tamer template: Animal Taming, [Animal Lore](animal-lore.md), [Veterinary](veterinary.md), often with magery or peacemaking for control. See [mechanics](../../mechanics/README.md) for pet control slots and bonding.

## Era notes
A launch skill (1997). Pet bonding was added after launch (community recollection).

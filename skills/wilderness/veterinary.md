# Veterinary

**Canon**

Veterinary heals and cures the tamer's pets with bandages — the animal equivalent of Healing.

## How it works
Use bandages and target a pet to restore its hit points; higher skill heals more and can cure poison. Animal Lore is commonly cited as boosting heal strength (community recollection). Skilled veterinarians are also said to be able to resurrect dead bonded pets (community recollection). Bandages are crafted with tailoring. Like all skills, it caps at 100 (120 with power scrolls) against the 700-point total skill cap.

## Training
Heal injured pets repeatedly — keep a pet tanking damage while you bandage it. The gain curve mirrors the Healing skill's.

## Synergies
Completes the tamer trio with [Animal Taming](animal-taming.md) and [Animal Lore](animal-lore.md); no serious tamer goes without it.

## Era notes
A launch skill (1997).

# Animal Lore

**Canon**

Animal Lore reveals detailed information about creatures — stats, skills, temperament, and taming difficulty.

## How it works
Use the skill and target a creature to open its lore display: attributes, resistances, skills, damage, and — for tamable creatures — the minimum taming requirement and current loyalty. Higher skill shows more precise information. It is the tamer's appraisal tool, used to judge a wild creature before attempting to tame it. Like all skills, it caps at 100 (120 with power scrolls) against the 700-point total skill cap.

## Training
Examine every creature you meet; more exotic creatures give better gains at high levels. It trains quickly alongside [Animal Taming](animal-taming.md).

## Synergies
Essential with [Animal Taming](animal-taming.md) and [Veterinary](veterinary.md) — Animal Lore is commonly cited as boosting veterinary healing (community recollection). Warriors also use it to size up dangerous monsters.

## Era notes
A launch skill (1997).

# Herding

**Canon**

Herding directs animals to move to a chosen spot, traditionally with a shepherd's crook.

## How it works
Use the skill targeting a creature, then target a destination; the animal walks there. It works on tamed creatures and some neutral ones (community recollection on the exact creature rules). A shepherd's crook is commonly cited as required or helpful (community recollection). Like all skills, it caps at 100 (120 with power scrolls) against the 700-point total skill cap.

## Training
Herd animals repeatedly; farm animals give steady early gains. One of the least-used skills in the game, kept alive mostly by shepherd roleplayers.

## Synergies
Thematic with [Animal Taming](animal-taming.md) and [Animal Lore](animal-lore.md) on shepherd templates, and with [Camping](camping.md) for the full pastoral fantasy.

## Era notes
A launch skill (1997).

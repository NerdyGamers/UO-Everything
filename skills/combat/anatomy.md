# Anatomy


Anatomy lets a character assess the physical condition of other creatures, and it passively adds damage to melee attacks.

## How it works
Using Anatomy on a target reports its strength and dexterity; higher skill gives more precise readings. In combat, Anatomy contributes a damage bonus to melee swings, stacking with the bonus from [Tactics](tactics.md). The bonus scales with skill (community recollection: roughly +5% damage per 20 points of Anatomy).

## Training
Use the skill on creatures, players, or NPCs. It trains quickly to Grandmaster through repeated use, and power scrolls raise the cap to 120 for a larger damage bonus.

## Synergies
- [Healing](healing.md) — heal amounts and cure strength scale with Anatomy; bandage resurrection is generally attempted with high Anatomy alongside Healing.
- [Tactics](tactics.md) — the two skills together form the standard melee damage package.
- Every melee weapon skill: [Swordsmanship](swordsmanship.md), [Fencing](fencing.md), [Macing](macing.md), [Wrestling](wrestling.md).

## Era notes
Anatomy's dual role (evaluation plus damage) dates to the earliest eras of the game and has remained stable; later publishes kept it as the standard damage companion to Tactics.

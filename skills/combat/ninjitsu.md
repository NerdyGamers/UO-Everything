# Ninjitsu

**Canon** (introduced with the Samurai Empire expansion)

Ninjitsu grants the abilities of the ninja: stealth attacks, misdirection, and animal transformation.

## How it works
Ninjitsu abilities include Focus Attack and Ki Attack (strikes with bonus effects), Backstab (a high-damage strike delivered from hiding), Mirror Images (decoys that absorb incoming attacks), Death Strike (a delayed burst of damage that scales with Ninjitsu), Shadow Jump (short-range teleportation), and Animal Form (transformation into various creatures, each with its own Ninjitsu requirement and benefits). Most abilities cost mana and scale with Ninjitsu skill; several interact with the hiding and stealth skills, with exact interactions being community recollection.

## Training
Use abilities in the field — Animal Form and Mirror Images see constant use and drive gains. Power scrolls raise the cap to 120.

## Synergies
- [Fencing](fencing.md) — the classic ninja weapon pairing.
- Hiding and stealth skills — enable the Backstab and Shadow Jump playstyle.
- [Focus](focus.md) — sustains the mana costs of repeated abilities.

## Era notes

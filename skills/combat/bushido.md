# Bushido

**Canon** (introduced with the Samurai Empire expansion)

Bushido is the samurai's code of combat, granting special abilities fueled by mana and the Perfection mechanic.

## How it works
Practitioners gain access to Bushido abilities: Honorable Execution (a finishing strike against weakened foes), Confidence (restores health and stamina over time), Evasion (a large temporary boost to parry chance), Counter Attack (a chance to strike back when hit), Lightning Strike (a high-accuracy critical strike), and Momentum Strike (hitting multiple nearby foes). Landing honorable kills builds Perfection, which amplifies subsequent damage; exact scaling is community recollection. Ability strength and success chance scale with Bushido skill.

## Training
Use the abilities in live combat — Confidence and Evasion see constant use and drive gains. Power scrolls raise the cap to 120.

## Synergies
- [Parrying](parrying.md) — Evasion directly boosts parry chance, and Counter Attack triggers when struck.
- [Swordsmanship](swordsmanship.md) and [Fencing](fencing.md) — the traditional samurai weapon pairings.
- [Focus](focus.md) — helps sustain the mana costs of repeated abilities.

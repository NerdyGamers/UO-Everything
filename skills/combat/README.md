# Combat skills


The combat group covers the skills used to fight: weapon skills, defensive skills, and the support skills warriors rely on.

The weapon skills — [Archery](archery.md), [Fencing](fencing.md), [Macing](macing.md), [Swordsmanship](swordsmanship.md), [Throwing](throwing.md), [Wrestling](wrestling.md) — determine hit chance with their weapon families and pair with [Tactics](tactics.md) for damage. [Anatomy](anatomy.md) adds melee damage and lets fighters evaluate foes. [Parrying](parrying.md) blocks incoming attacks. [Healing](healing.md) keeps fighters alive with bandages. [Focus](focus.md) passively regenerates stamina and mana. [Bushido](bushido.md) and [Ninjitsu](ninjitsu.md), added with Samurai Empire, grant special combat abilities.

Each skill caps at 100 individually (120 with power scrolls) against a 700-point total skill cap. See [mechanics](../../mechanics/README.md) for hit-chance and damage formulas.

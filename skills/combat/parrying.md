# Parrying

**Canon**

Parrying gives a chance to block incoming melee and ranged attacks.

## How it works
Each incoming attack may be parried, negating it entirely. The chance scales with Parrying skill and dexterity, and shields improve the odds; the exact formula is community recollection. Parry is rolled after the attacker's hit chance is contested, so it layers on top of evasion from the defender's weapon skill. [Bushido](bushido.md)'s Evasion ability temporarily raises parry chance by a large amount.

## Training
Stand in combat and take hits — the traditional method is having a second character or a low-damage creature attack you while you hold a shield. Power scrolls raise the cap to 120.

## Synergies
- [Bushido](bushido.md) — Evasion and Counter Attack are built around being struck.
- Any melee weapon skill; sword-and-board templates pair it with [Swordsmanship](swordsmanship.md) or [Macing](macing.md).
- [Healing](healing.md) — blocked attacks buy uninterrupted bandage time.

## Era notes
A long-standing defensive skill. Its interaction with the special-move system and with Bushido was refined in later eras.

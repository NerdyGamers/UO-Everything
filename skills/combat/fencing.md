# Fencing


Fencing governs piercing weapons: daggers, the kryss, spears, war forks, and lances.

## How it works
Fencing weapons trend toward fast swing speeds and include some of the quickest weapons in the game. Hit chance follows the standard weapon-skill contest, and [Tactics](tactics.md) adds damage. Special moves associated with fencing weapons include Armor Ignore, Bleed Attack, and Infectious Strike; the exact weapon-to-move mapping is community recollection.

## Training
Fight progressively tougher creatures; the fast swing rate makes fencing one of the quicker weapon skills to raise. Power scrolls raise the cap to 120.

## Synergies
- [Tactics](tactics.md) and [Anatomy](anatomy.md) — the standard melee damage pairing.
- [Healing](healing.md) — survivability for close-range fighters.
- [Parrying](parrying.md) — common on dex-based fencer templates.

## Era notes
One of the original weapon skills. The special-move system of later eras gave fencing weapons distinct identities, notably fast weapons carrying Bleed Attack.

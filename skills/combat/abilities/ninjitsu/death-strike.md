# Death Strike

A delayed Ninjitsu burst that punishes a fleeing target.

## Mechanics

Requires around 85 Ninjitsu and costs 30 mana. Five seconds after the strike lands, the target takes damage based on how many steps they have moved since the attack and the average of the ninja's hiding and stealth skills — running away greatly increases the damage. Ranged weapons deal half the normal Death Strike damage.

## Notes

Introduced with the Samurai Empire expansion. The signature ninja finisher, and the traditional way to train Ninjitsu from 85 to 120. See [Ninjitsu](../../ninjitsu.md).

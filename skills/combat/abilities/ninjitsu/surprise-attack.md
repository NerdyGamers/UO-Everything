# Surprise Attack

A Ninjitsu strike from stealth that cripples the target's defenses.

## Mechanics

Requires around 60 Ninjitsu, costs 20 mana, and must be initiated while stealthed. Inflicts a defense penalty on the opponent for a short duration.

## Notes

Introduced with the Samurai Empire expansion. A setup strike for the stealth-assassin playstyle, often followed by heavier attacks. See [Ninjitsu](../../ninjitsu.md).

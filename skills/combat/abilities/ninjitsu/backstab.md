# Backstab

A high-damage Ninjitsu strike delivered from stealth.

## Mechanics

Requires around 40 Ninjitsu, costs 30 mana, and must be initiated while stealthed. Applies a damage bonus matching the ninja's Ninjitsu skill.

## Notes

Introduced with the Samurai Empire expansion. The classic opener for the stealth-assassin playstyle, pairing with the hiding and stealth skills. See [Ninjitsu](../../ninjitsu.md).

# Ki Attack

A Ninjitsu strike whose damage grows with the distance the ninja travels to deliver it.

## Mechanics

Requires around 80 Ninjitsu and costs 25 mana. The ninja initiates the attack, then must quickly close the distance to the target; the farther traveled to deliver the blow, the higher the damage bonus. Demands precise timing and movement.

## Notes

Introduced with the Samurai Empire expansion. See [Ninjitsu](../../ninjitsu.md).

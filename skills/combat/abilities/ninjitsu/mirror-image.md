# Mirror Image

A Ninjitsu misdirection that creates decoys to absorb incoming attacks.

## Mechanics

Requires around 20 Ninjitsu and costs 10 mana. Each image absorbs a single enemy attack before disappearing; a ninja can maintain up to 4 mirror images at once, making it hard to tell which figure is real.

## Notes

Introduced with the Samurai Empire expansion. A constant-use ability that traditionally trains Ninjitsu from about 50 to 75 — ninja attack their own images to dismiss them. See [Ninjitsu](../../ninjitsu.md).

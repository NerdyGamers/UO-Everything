# Focus Attack

A Ninjitsu strike that adds a small damage bonus and amplifies the weapon's magical properties.

## Mechanics

Requires around 30 Ninjitsu and costs 10 mana. Beyond the damage bonus, it increases the effectiveness of any magical properties on the weapon used for the attack.

## Notes

Introduced with the Samurai Empire expansion. Traditionally used to train Ninjitsu from about 75 to 85. See [Ninjitsu](../../ninjitsu.md).

# Shadowjump

A Ninjitsu short-range teleport performed while hidden.

## Mechanics

Requires around 50 Ninjitsu, costs 15 mana, and must be initiated while stealthed. The ninja jumps a considerable distance while hidden; whether they stay hidden on landing depends on their stealth skill.

## Notes

Introduced with the Samurai Empire expansion. The ninja's repositioning tool, central to the stealth playstyle alongside hiding and stealth. See [Ninjitsu](../../ninjitsu.md).

# Animal Form

A Ninjitsu transformation that turns the ninja into a beast, granting animal-like bonuses.

## Mechanics

Costs 10 mana; each of the 12 forms has its own minimum Ninjitsu requirement, from 0 for rabbit and rat up to 99 for unicorn and ki-rin. Forms grant bonuses such as stealth skill (rabbit, rat), regeneration (cat, dog), poison on strikes (giant serpent) or when struck at close range (bullfrog), movement speed (ostard, llama), and hit chance plus hit points (wolf, bake-kitsune). While transformed, the ninja cannot use special attacks or cast most spells.

## Notes

Introduced with the Samurai Empire expansion. Rabbit and rat forms are a common way to train the early skill. See [Ninjitsu](../../ninjitsu.md).

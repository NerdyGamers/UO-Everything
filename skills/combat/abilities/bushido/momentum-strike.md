# Momentum Strike

A Bushido attack that sweeps through multiple nearby foes at once.

## Mechanics

Requires around 90 Bushido and costs 10 mana. Strikes the primary target and additional nearby enemies, making it the samurai's crowd-fighting ability. Effectiveness and reliability scale with Bushido skill.

## Notes

Introduced with the Samurai Empire expansion. The final ability samurai learn, traditionally used to train Bushido from 105 to 120. See [Bushido](../../bushido.md).

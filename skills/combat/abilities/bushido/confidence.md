# Confidence

A defensive Bushido stance that restores health and stamina when the samurai blocks attacks, while greatly increasing hit point regeneration for a short time.

## Mechanics

Requires around 25 Bushido and costs 10 mana. Once activated, the stance lasts about 15 seconds; each successful block during that time restores a small amount of hits and stamina, with the amounts scaling with Bushido skill. Exact restoration values are community recollection.

## Notes

A staple training ability — samurai traditionally raise Bushido from 30 to 60 by casting Confidence repeatedly. Introduced with the Samurai Empire expansion. Pairs well with [Parrying](../../parrying.md), since blocks trigger the restoration.

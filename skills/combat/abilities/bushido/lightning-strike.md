# Lightning Strike

A Bushido attack that strikes with greatly improved accuracy and a chance to ignore the target's armor.

## Mechanics

Requires around 50 Bushido and costs 10 mana. Grants a +50 hit-chance bonus on the attack, and a hit has a chance to bypass armor entirely — community recollection puts the critical chance at 0% at 50 skill rising to roughly 20% at 120. Reliability improves with Bushido; below about 90 skill the ability can fizzle.

## Notes

Introduced with the Samurai Empire expansion. The workhorse Bushido attack and a common training method from 50 skill upward. See [Bushido](../../bushido.md).

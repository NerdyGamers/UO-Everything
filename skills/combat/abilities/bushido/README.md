# Bushido Abilities

The six special abilities of the [Bushido](../../bushido.md) skill, introduced with the Samurai Empire expansion.

- [Confidence](confidence.md) — defensive stance restoring health and stamina on blocks
- [Counter Attack](counter-attack.md) — answer the next block with an immediate strike
- [Evasion](evasion.md) — greatly raised parry chance; avoids direct spell damage
- [Honorable Execution](honorable-execution.md) — finishing strike against weakened foes
- [Lightning Strike](lightning-strike.md) — high-accuracy strike with a chance to ignore armor
- [Momentum Strike](momentum-strike.md) — hit multiple nearby foes at once

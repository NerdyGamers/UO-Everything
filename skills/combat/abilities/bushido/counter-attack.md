# Counter Attack

A defensive Bushido stance that answers the next successful block with an immediate counterattack.

## Mechanics

Requires around 40 Bushido and costs 5 mana. The stance lasts about 30 seconds; on the next successful block, if the samurai has a weapon equipped, they immediately swing back at the attacker, ending the stance. Damage returned is based on the weapon's damage and the samurai's Parrying and Bushido skill. Requires an equipped weapon or shield.

## Notes

Introduced with the Samurai Empire expansion. A classic training ability, traditionally used from roughly 60 to 75 skill. Works hand in hand with [Parrying](../../parrying.md).

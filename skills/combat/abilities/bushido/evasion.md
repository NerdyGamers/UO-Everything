# Evasion

A defensive Bushido stance that greatly raises the samurai's chance to parry — the only way in the game to entirely avoid direct spell damage.

## Mechanics

Requires around 60 Bushido and costs 10 mana. For a few seconds after activation, parry chance is multiplied (community recollection puts the increase at about half again the normal chance), and successful evasion checks can reduce single-target spell damage to zero. Requires an equipped weapon or shield.

## Notes

Introduced with the Samurai Empire expansion. Traditionally used to train Bushido from about 75 to 105. Synergizes with [Parrying](../../parrying.md).

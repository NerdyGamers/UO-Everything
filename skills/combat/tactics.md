# Tactics

**Canon**

Tactics increases damage dealt with weapon attacks and gates the use of special moves.

## How it works
Every weapon skill benefits: Tactics adds a damage bonus to melee, archery, and throwing attacks, with the bonus scaling with skill (exact scaling is community recollection). Special moves — weapon techniques such as Armor Ignore, Mortal Strike, and Disarm — generally require 70 or more skill in both the weapon skill and Tactics (community recollection on the exact threshold). Tactics is therefore near-mandatory on any warrior template.

## Training
Gains come from dealing damage in combat, so it trains naturally alongside whatever weapon skill is in use. Power scrolls raise the cap to 120 for maximum damage.

## Synergies
- Every weapon skill: [Swordsmanship](swordsmanship.md), [Fencing](fencing.md), [Macing](macing.md), [Archery](archery.md), [Throwing](throwing.md), [Wrestling](wrestling.md).
- [Anatomy](anatomy.md) — the standard companion damage skill for melee fighters.

## Era notes
Its damage role is unchanged since launch. The special-move system of later eras made the ~70-point threshold a key template breakpoint.

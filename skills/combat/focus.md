# Focus

**Canon**

Focus is a passive skill that regenerates stamina and mana.

## How it works
Focus is always active: it steadily restores stamina and mana, with faster regeneration at higher skill (exact rates are community recollection). Unlike active regeneration skills, it requires no activation and works in combat, making it a quiet backbone of templates that burn mana or stamina on abilities and special moves.

## Training
Focus rises passively through combat and adventuring rather than through a dedicated training loop; many players raise it incidentally while training other skills (community recollection). Power scrolls raise the cap to 120.

## Synergies
- [Bushido](bushido.md) and [Ninjitsu](ninjitsu.md) — helps pay the mana costs of repeated abilities.
- [Parrying](parrying.md) and weapon skills — stamina sustains swing rate and special-move use.
- [Healing](healing.md) — stamina recovery supports kiting between bandages.

## Era notes
Introduced later than the original skill set (community recollection: alongside the Samurai Empire-era systems). Its regeneration role has been stable since.

# Throwing

**Canon** (introduced with the Stygian Abyss expansion)

Throwing governs thrown ranged weapons such as boomerangs, cyclones, and soul glaives.

## How it works
Throwing attacks at range like [Archery](archery.md), but thrown weapons are not consumed as ammunition (community recollection). Hit chance is contested against the defender's combat skills as with other weapon skills, and [Tactics](tactics.md) adds damage. It arrived alongside the gargoyle race in Stygian Abyss and remains the newest weapon skill in the game.

## Training
Attack creatures at range; it trains much like Archery. Power scrolls raise the cap to 120.

## Synergies
- [Tactics](tactics.md) — damage on every throw.
- [Healing](healing.md) — the standard survival pairing for ranged fighters.
- [Focus](focus.md) — stamina recovery for sustained throwing and special moves.

## Era notes
Added with Stygian Abyss — the first new weapon skill since the game's launch. Its fundamentals have been stable since introduction.

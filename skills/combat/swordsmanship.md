# Swordsmanship


Swordsmanship governs bladed weapons: swords, katanas, axes, and polearms such as the halberd.

## How it works
Swordsmanship covers the largest weapon family in the game, from fast blades to heavy two-handed axes. Hit chance follows the standard weapon-skill contest, and damage scales with [Tactics](tactics.md) and [Anatomy](anatomy.md). Special moves associated with swordsman weapons include Double Strike, Armor Ignore, Mortal Strike, and Whirlwind; the exact weapon-to-move mapping is community recollection.

## Training
Fight progressively tougher foes; the wide weapon selection lets swordsmen match weapons to targets. Power scrolls raise the cap to 120.

## Synergies
- [Tactics](tactics.md) and [Anatomy](anatomy.md) — the standard melee damage pairing.
- [Healing](healing.md) and [Parrying](parrying.md) — the classic sword-and-board survival core.
- [Bushido](bushido.md) — the samurai's weapon skill.

## Era notes
One of the original weapon skills. Expansion-era weapon additions, such as the Tokuno blades of Samurai Empire, steadily expanded its roster without changing the fundamentals.

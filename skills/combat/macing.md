# Macing

**Canon**

Macing governs blunt weapons: maces, war hammers, mauls, quarter staves, clubs, and scepters.

## How it works
Macing weapons are generally slower but hit hard, and the family is associated with stamina-draining and crushing effects. Special moves found on macing weapons include Crushing Blow, Concussion Blow, and Disarm; the exact weapon-to-move mapping is community recollection. Damage scales with [Tactics](tactics.md) and [Anatomy](anatomy.md) as with other melee skills.

## Training
The standard melee progression against progressively tougher foes. Power scrolls raise the cap to 120.

## Synergies
- [Tactics](tactics.md) and [Anatomy](anatomy.md) — the standard melee damage pairing.
- [Parrying](parrying.md) — mace-and-shield is a classic defensive pairing.
- [Healing](healing.md) — survivability between heavy swings.

## Era notes
One of the original weapon skills. The special-move system of later eras gave macing a control-and-disruption identity built around stamina drain and crushing blows.

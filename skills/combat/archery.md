# Archery


Archery governs ranged attacks with bows and crossbows.

## How it works
Each shot consumes one unit of ammunition — arrows for bows, bolts for crossbows — and hit chance is contested against the defender's combat skills as with melee. Archers strike from range, staying clear of melee retaliation, but depend on ammunition supply and a clear line of fire. [Tactics](tactics.md) adds damage to shots, and item properties such as Hit Chance Increase and Damage Increase apply normally. Some archery weapons can use the Moving Shot special move, firing while on the move (community recollection).

## Training
Fire at progressively tougher creatures; ammunition cost makes low-level game the economical route early on. Power scrolls raise the cap to 120.

## Synergies
- [Tactics](tactics.md) — damage on every shot.
- [Healing](healing.md) — the standard survival pairing for dex-based archers.
- [Focus](focus.md) — stamina recovery for sustained firing and special moves.

## Era notes
A core skill since launch. Samurai Empire-era additions such as the yumi expanded the weapon roster; the fundamentals of ammunition and range have not changed.

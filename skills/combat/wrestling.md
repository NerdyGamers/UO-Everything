# Wrestling


Wrestling governs unarmed combat — fighting with fists.

## How it works
Strikes land based on the standard hit-chance contest, with damage increased by [Tactics](tactics.md) and, for melee, [Anatomy](anatomy.md). Wrestling also has a defensive role: with no weapon equipped, Wrestling skill substitutes for a weapon skill when defending, making unarmed characters harder to hit (community recollection on the exact mechanic). It is the fallback combat skill for spellcasters and anyone caught without a weapon.

## Training
Fight unarmed; gains come with landed blows. It trains slowly compared to armed skills, and power scrolls raise the cap to 120.

## Synergies
- [Tactics](tactics.md) and [Anatomy](anatomy.md) — damage, as with other melee skills.
- Spellcasting templates — commonly carried as a defensive skill by mages.
- [Healing](healing.md) — survivability in close quarters.

## Era notes
One of the original skills. Its defensive use for unarmed characters dates to the earliest eras of the game.

## Sources

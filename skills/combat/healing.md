# Healing


Healing governs the use of bandages to restore health, cure poison, and resurrect the dead.

## How it works
Applying a bandage to a target restores health after a short delay; the amount healed scales with Healing and [Anatomy](anatomy.md). Bandages can also cure poison, with success chance scaling with skill, and can resurrect dead players — resurrection is generally attempted with high Healing and Anatomy together (community recollection: around 80 of each). Bandaging yourself while taking damage or while poisoned can cause the attempt to slip and fail (community recollection).

## Training
Heal yourself, allies, or pets; curing poison and resurrecting give gains at higher levels. Power scrolls raise the cap to 120.

## Synergies
- [Anatomy](anatomy.md) — the required pairing; boosts heal amounts and enables resurrection.
- Every combat template — Healing is the standard survival skill for warriors of all weapon types.
- [Parrying](parrying.md) — blocking buys uninterrupted bandage time.

## Era notes
Bandage timing, delay, and slip behavior have been adjusted across publishes; specific numbers are not verified here.

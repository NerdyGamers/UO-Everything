# Provocation

**Canon**

Provocation is the bard skill for inciting two creatures to attack each other by playing an inflammatory tune.

## How it works
The bard targets two creatures and attempts to provoke them into fighting; success is checked against [Musicianship](musicianship.md) plus Provocation versus the targets' barding difficulty. Provoked creatures battle until one dies or the effect ends, letting the bard clear spawns without landing a blow — the classic bard-farmer playstyle. Both targets must be provokable (not all creatures are), and failure can turn both on the bard. An instrument is required.

## Training
Train on progressively tougher creature pairs as skill rises; gains come from successful and failed attempts alike (community recollection on optimal training pairs — verify in-game). Power scrolls (105–120) come from champion spawn bosses and later felucca content.

## Synergies
- [Musicianship](musicianship.md) — required base skill.
- [Discordance](discordance.md) — discord one target first, then provoke for faster kills.
- [Peacemaking](peacemaking.md) — peace to escape when a provoke goes wrong.

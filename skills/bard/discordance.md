# Discordance

**Canon**

Discordance is the bard skill for weakening a target creature — lowering its stats, resists, and combat effectiveness — by playing a discordant tune.

## How it works
The bard targets a creature and attempts to discord it; success is checked against [Musicianship](musicianship.md) plus Discordance versus the target's barding difficulty. A discorded creature suffers reduced strength, dexterity, intelligence, resists, and damage for the effect's duration, making it far easier to kill. Discordance is the bard's premier PvM debuff and a staple of tamer and solo-farmer templates. It requires an instrument in hand and line of effect; failure can draw aggression.

## Training
Train on progressively tougher creatures as skill rises, starting with low-difficulty animals. Discordance gains come from attempts, so macros on suitable targets are the traditional route (community recollection on optimal training mobs — verify in-game). Power scrolls (105–120) come from champion spawn bosses and later felucca content.

## Synergies
- [Musicianship](musicianship.md) — required; attempts check both skills.
- [Peacemaking](peacemaking.md) / [Provocation](provocation.md) — the full bard toolkit shares the instrument and musicianship base.
- Animal Taming templates — discord softens targets for taming and killing.

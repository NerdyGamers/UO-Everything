# Musicianship


Musicianship is the foundational bard skill — the ability to play an instrument well — and a prerequisite for the other bard skills: [Discordance](discordance.md), [Peacemaking](peacemaking.md), and [Provocation](provocation.md).

## How it works
Every bard ability first checks Musicianship, then the specific bard skill, against the target's barding difficulty. Low musicianship means failed songs regardless of the other skill's level, so bards train it first. Instruments (lutes, harps, drums, tambourines — made by [Carpentry](../crafting/carpentry.md)) are required in hand; damaged or missing instruments cause failures. Musicianship itself has no direct combat effect — it enables the rest of the bard's kit.

## Training
Play any instrument repeatedly; gains come quickly at low levels and slow toward grandmaster. Because every bard attempt checks it, musicianship trains passively while training the other bard skills. Power scrolls (105–120) drop from champion spawn bosses.

## Synergies
- [Discordance](discordance.md), [Peacemaking](peacemaking.md), [Provocation](provocation.md) — all depend on it.
- [Carpentry](../crafting/carpentry.md) — instruments, including exceptional and special-wood ones.

## Era notes
No major mechanical changes across eras; bard masteries added new uses for high musicianship (community recollection — verify against current publish notes).

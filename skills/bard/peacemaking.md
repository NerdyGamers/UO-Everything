# Peacemaking

**Canon**

Peacemaking is the bard skill for calming creatures (and, in limited cases, players) — stopping them from attacking — by playing a soothing tune.

## How it works
The bard plays an instrument to attempt area or targeted peace; success is checked against [Musicianship](musicianship.md) plus Peacemaking versus each target's barding difficulty. Peaced creatures stop fighting and wander calmly for the effect's duration, giving the bard room to escape, heal, or reposition. It is a defensive staple for bards and tamers working dangerous spawns. Attempting peace on players has heavy restrictions and short effect (community recollection on exact PvP rules — verify in-game).

## Training
Train by peacing progressively tougher creatures, as with the other bard skills; gains come from attempts. Power scrolls (105–120) come from champion spawn bosses and later felucca content.

## Synergies
- [Musicianship](musicianship.md) — required base skill.
- [Discordance](discordance.md) / [Provocation](provocation.md) — the offensive bard skills; peacemaking is the defensive third.
- Animal Taming templates — peace first, then tame or kill safely.

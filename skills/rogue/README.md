# Rogue skills


The rogue group covers skullduggery: staying unseen, getting into locked things, and relieving others of their belongings. These nine skills form the backbone of thief, assassin, treasure-hunter, and scout templates.

## Skills
- [Detecting Hidden](detecting-hidden.md) — reveal hidden creatures and players.
- [Hiding](hiding.md) — vanish from sight.
- [Lockpicking](lockpicking.md) — open locked doors and containers.
- [Poisoning](poisoning.md) — coat weapons (and food) with poison.
- [Remove Trap](remove-trap.md) — disarm trapped containers.
- [Snooping](snooping.md) — peek into another character's backpack.
- [Stealing](stealing.md) — take items from another character's backpack.
- [Stealth](stealth.md) — move while hidden.
- [Tracking](tracking.md) — locate nearby mobiles by category.

## How the group fits together
The classic thief runs [Snooping](snooping.md) → [Stealing](stealing.md) under cover of [Hiding](hiding.md) and [Stealth](stealth.md). Treasure hunters swap theft for [Lockpicking](lockpicking.md) and [Remove Trap](remove-trap.md) on dungeon chests. Assassins add [Poisoning](poisoning.md); scouts add [Tracking](tracking.md) and [Detecting Hidden](detecting-hidden.md) to hunt the hidden.

# Stealth


Stealth allows a hidden character to move without being revealed.

## How it works
While hidden (via [Hiding](hiding.md)), Stealth grants a limited number of steps before concealment breaks; the step budget scales with skill (community recollection: roughly skill ÷ 10 steps). Running, attacking, casting, or most other actions still reveal the character, and heavy armor reduces effectiveness. The skill works passively — no activation beyond being hidden. It requires substantial Hiding to function (community recollection: 80+ Hiding is the practical minimum). Like all skills, it caps at 100 (120 with power scrolls) against the 700-point total skill cap.

## Training
Hide, then walk; gains come from successful stealthed steps. Train Hiding first, since Stealth is useless without it.

## Synergies
Inseparable from [Hiding](hiding.md). Thief ([Snooping](snooping.md), [Stealing](stealing.md)), assassin ([Poisoning](poisoning.md)), and scout ([Tracking](tracking.md)) templates all build on the pair. Countered by [Detecting Hidden](detecting-hidden.md).

## Era notes
Introduced with Ultima Online: Renaissance (2000).

# Hiding


Hiding lets a character vanish from sight, becoming invisible to other players and most creatures.

## How it works
Activating the skill makes a Hiding check; on success the character is hidden. While hidden, nearly any action reveals the character — moving (without [Stealth](stealth.md)), attacking, casting, or speaking. Taking damage also breaks concealment. Hidden characters can still be exposed by [Detecting Hidden](detecting-hidden.md), the Reveal spell, or area damage. Like all skills, it caps at 100 (120 with power scrolls) against the 700-point total skill cap.

## Training
Use the skill repeatedly; new characters can train to about 30 from NPC trainers. Gains come from successful hides, slowing as you approach grandmaster.

## Synergies
Required foundation for [Stealth](stealth.md). Combines with [Snooping](snooping.md) and [Stealing](stealing.md) on classic thief templates, with [Lockpicking](lockpicking.md) and [Remove Trap](remove-trap.md) for dungeon chest runners, and with magery for mages who hide to escape combat.

## Era notes
A launch skill (1997). The Stealth skill, added in Renaissance (2000), gave hiders the ability to move unseen.

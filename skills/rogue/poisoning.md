# Poisoning

**Canon**

Poisoning applies poison to bladed weapons (and, in classic play, food and drink), giving attacks a chance to poison the victim.

## How it works
Use the skill on a poison potion, then on the weapon, to coat it. Poison comes in five strengths — Lesser, Regular, Greater, Deadly, Lethal — and the strength you can apply scales with skill. On a successful hit, the victim must resist or suffer damage over time; higher skill raises both the chance to poison and the level delivered. Poisoned weapons show a greenish hue. Like all skills, it caps at 100 (120 with power scrolls) against the 700-point total skill cap.

## Training
Apply progressively stronger poisons; each tier gates gains. Lethal poison is commonly cited as requiring near-grandmaster skill (community recollection).

## Synergies
The classic assassin template: Poisoning with Fencing, [Hiding](hiding.md), and [Stealth](stealth.md). Pairs with anatomy and tactics for maximum melee effect. Victims cure poison with cure potions or the Cure/Arch Cure spells; see [mechanics](../../mechanics/README.md).

## Era notes
A launch skill (1997). Lethal poison was added after launch (community recollection).

# Stealing


Stealing takes items directly from another character's backpack.

## How it works
After [Snooping](snooping.md) a target's pack, use Stealing and target an item. The item's weight must be within your stealable limit, which scales with skill (community recollection: roughly 1 stone per 10 skill, about 10 stones at grandmaster). Blessed, insured, or otherwise unstealable items cannot be taken. Stealing from an innocent in a guarded area is a criminal act — expect the guards and a notoriety hit. Like all skills, it caps at 100 (120 with power scrolls) against the 700-point total skill cap.

## Training
Steal from creatures, NPCs, or cooperative players, working up to heavier items. High-end thieves practice in the wilderness of Felucca, where the rules are looser.

## Synergies
The classic thief template: [Snooping](snooping.md), Stealing, [Hiding](hiding.md), [Stealth](stealth.md), rounded out with [Detecting Hidden](detecting-hidden.md) against rival thieves. See [mechanics](../../mechanics/README.md) for criminal acts and notoriety.

## Era notes
A launch skill (1997). Item insurance, introduced with Age of Shadows (2003), sharply reduced the impact of theft by letting players protect their gear.

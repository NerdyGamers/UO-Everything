# Lockpicking

**Canon**

Lockpicking opens locked doors and containers using lockpicks crafted by tinkers.

## How it works
With lockpicks in hand, target a locked door or container; success is checked against the lock's difficulty. Harder locks — dungeon chests, paragon chests — demand high skill, and failed attempts can break the lockpick. The Unlock spell (3rd circle magery) is the spellcaster's alternative for simple locks. Like all skills, it caps at 100 (120 with power scrolls) against the 700-point total skill cap.

## Training
Practice on locked boxes of increasing difficulty; dungeon chests provide the high-end gains. Progress slows markedly past 90, and 120 power scrolls are prized by dedicated thieves and treasure hunters. Paragon chests are commonly cited as needing roughly 95+ skill (community recollection).

## Synergies
Almost always trained alongside [Remove Trap](remove-trap.md) — dungeon chests are frequently both locked and trapped — plus [Hiding](hiding.md) and [Stealth](stealth.md) for reaching them unmolested. [Detecting Hidden](detecting-hidden.md) helps spot ambushes at the chest.

## Era notes
A launch skill (1997). Paragon chests, introduced with the Paragon system in the Age of Shadows era (2003), gave grandmaster locksmiths their toughest targets.

# Remove Trap

**Canon**

Remove Trap disarms trapped chests and containers — dart, poison-needle, and explosive traps.

## How it works
Use the skill on a trapped container; success disarms the trap, while failure can spring it. Trap strength scales with the container's level, so dungeon and paragon chests demand high skill. The Telekinesis spell (3rd circle magery) offers a safer alternative by triggering the trap from a distance. Magically trapped containers (the Magic Trap spell) are a separate matter. Like all skills, it caps at 100 (120 with power scrolls) against the 700-point total skill cap.

## Training
Disarm trapped boxes of increasing difficulty; dungeon chests are the standard high-end training. Almost always trained together with [Lockpicking](lockpicking.md).

## Synergies
Core treasure-hunter kit: [Lockpicking](lockpicking.md), Remove Trap, [Detecting Hidden](detecting-hidden.md), usually with [Hiding](hiding.md) for the approach.

## Era notes

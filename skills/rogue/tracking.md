# Tracking


Tracking locates nearby creatures, NPCs, and players, listing them by category.

## How it works
Using the skill opens a tracking menu of detected mobiles grouped by type — animals, monsters, humanoids, players, and so on — with direction and distance. Detection range scales with skill. It reveals presence and bearing rather than exact identity, and hidden targets generally still require [Detecting Hidden](detecting-hidden.md) to expose. Like all skills, it caps at 100 (120 with power scrolls) against the 700-point total skill cap.

## Training
Track repeatedly in populated areas; monster-filled dungeons give steady gains.

## Synergies
Scout and ranger templates pair it with [Hiding](hiding.md) and [Detecting Hidden](detecting-hidden.md). Hunters combine it with [Animal Lore](../wilderness/animal-lore.md) to size up prey before engaging.

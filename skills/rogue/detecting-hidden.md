# Detecting Hidden


Detecting Hidden reveals creatures and players concealed by [Hiding](hiding.md) or [Stealth](stealth.md).

## How it works
Using the skill and targeting a location or mobile attempts to expose anything hidden nearby. Success is checked against the target's Hiding skill — a more skilled hider is harder to reveal. Detection range grows with skill. It is the mundane counterpart to the Reveal spell (3rd circle magery). Like all skills, it caps at 100 (120 with power scrolls) against the 700-point total skill cap.

## Training
Practice on a willing friend using Hiding, or sweep dungeons where hidden creatures lurk. Gains come from attempts against targets near your skill level, successful or not.

## Synergies
Pairs with [Hiding](hiding.md) and [Stealth](stealth.md) on thief and scout templates, and with [Tracking](tracking.md) for hunters who want to find — and expose — their quarry. PvP characters often carry it to counter stealth attackers. See [mechanics](../../mechanics/README.md) for how reveal checks resolve.

## Era notes
The skill predates Stealth, which arrived with Ultima Online: Renaissance (2000). Before that, Hiding alone was countered by Detecting Hidden and the Reveal spell.

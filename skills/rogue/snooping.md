# Snooping


Snooping lets a character peek inside another character's backpack — the necessary first step before [Stealing](stealing.md).

## How it works
Use the skill and target a nearby character to open their pack for inspection. The victim may notice the attempt ("You notice someone trying to snoop in your pack"), with the notice chance falling as skill rises. Snooping itself is not a criminal act, but what usually follows is. Like all skills, it caps at 100 (120 with power scrolls) against the 700-point total skill cap.

## Training
Snoop the packs of players, NPCs, or a cooperative friend. Gains come from attempts against targets near your skill level.

## Synergies
Prerequisite partner to [Stealing](stealing.md) — useless without it, essential with it. Usually combined with [Hiding](hiding.md) and [Stealth](stealth.md) on thief templates.

## Era notes
A launch skill (1997).

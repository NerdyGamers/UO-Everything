# Renaissance (2000)

Renaissance, released May 2000, made the single most consequential change in Ultima Online's history: it split the world in two. Britannia was mirrored into Trammel — where non-consensual PvP was abolished — and Felucca, which kept the original rules.

## What happened

- **The facet split.** Every landmass was duplicated. Trammel allowed only consensual PvP (duels, guild wars, [factions](../mechanics/factions.md)); Felucca kept unrestricted player killing with all its risks — and its better resources and rewards.
- **The names** come from Britannia's two moons in series lore: Trammel the orderly, Felucca the chaotic.
- **The great migration.** The overwhelming majority of players moved to Trammel, draining Felucca's population almost overnight. The wilderness went from crowded and dangerous to empty.
- **Factions.** The four-faction warfare system (Minax, True Britannians, Shadowlords, Council of Mages) launched alongside this era, giving Felucca's remaining fighters structured, large-scale PvP over sigils and towns.

## Lasting impact

The split ended UO's first age of unrestricted PvP and created the game's two-audience structure ever since: [Trammel and Felucca](../locations/trammel-felucca.md) as parallel worlds, one for the PvM and crafting majority, one for risk, reward, and PvP. Arguments about whether the split saved or ruined the game still run hot decades later.

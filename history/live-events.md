# Live Events and the EM Program

Beyond publishes and expansions, Ultima Online's history is written week by week on individual shards — through the Event Moderator program, story arcs, invasions, and holiday events that make each shard's timeline its own.

## What happened

- **The EM program:** Event Moderators are volunteer staff assigned to specific shards, running live in-character events — from tavern nights to full invasions. The program began in the late 2000s (community recollection on the exact start) and became the backbone of shard culture: regular, human-run content no publish schedule could replicate.
- **Story arcs and invasions:** EMs run multi-week storylines — towns besieged, factions maneuvering, mysteries unfolding — often with player actions feeding back into how the arc resolves. Major arcs have reshaped shard fiction and left behind commemorative items.
- **Holiday events:** Halloween and winter holiday events recur yearly, with trick-or-treating, themed dungeons, and collectible gifts. They are among the most consistently attended events on every shard.
- **Publish-adjacent events:** expansions and publishes frequently launch with live event prologues — invasions or crises that set the stage in-fiction before the new content goes live (the Blackrock crises are the classic example).

## Lasting impact

The EM program is why "UO history" isn't one timeline but dozens: each shard has its own wars, heroes, and scandals, remembered by its players and sometimes enshrined in [community](../community/README.md) lore. For many veterans, the events *are* the game — the systems are just the stage.

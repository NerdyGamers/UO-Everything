# Expansions 2004–2015

After [Age of Shadows](age-of-shadows.md) redefined itemization, Ultima Online's next decade of expansions added new lands, races, skills, and systems at a steady clip — each one layering onto the game rather than replacing what came before.

## What happened

- **Samurai Empire (2004)** opened the [Tokuno Islands](../locations/tokuno-islands.md), a Japanese-themed landmass, and introduced the [Bushido](../skills/combat/bushido.md) and [Ninjitsu](../skills/combat/ninjitsu.md) skills along with new weapons and armor. It gave warrior templates two entirely new playstyles.
- **Mondain's Legacy (2005)** added the elven race and the [Heartwood](../quests/heartwood-quests.md) quest hub, the [Spellweaving](../skills/magic/spellweaving.md) skill, new Ilshenar dungeons (Bedlam, Blighted Grove, the Palace of Paroxysmus, the Prism of Light), the modern quest engine, and [peerless encounters](../mechanics/peerless-encounters.md).
- **Stygian Abyss (2009)** brought the gargoyle race and their city [Ter Mur](../locations/termur.md), the Stygian Abyss dungeon, and three new skills: [Mysticism](../skills/magic/mysticism.md), [Imbuing](../skills/crafting/imbuing.md), and [Throwing](../skills/combat/throwing.md). Imbuing in particular reshaped endgame gearing toward crafted suits.
- **High Seas (2010)**, released as a smaller "booster" rather than a full expansion, added player ships with cannons, sea combat and piracy, and expanded fishing.
- **Time of Legends (2015)** opened the valley of [Eodon](../locations/eodon.md) and the Shadowguard roof encounter, with new tamables, craftables, and high-end encounters.

## Lasting impact

Every system from this stretch is still live: gargoyles and elves remain playable races, imbuing and reforging define the crafted-gear economy, and Ter Mur, Tokuno, and Eodon are permanent parts of the world. The era established the pattern of additive expansions that the game follows to this day.

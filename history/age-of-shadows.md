# Age of Shadows (2003)

Age of Shadows, released February 2003, rebuilt Ultima Online's itemization from the ground up — the property system, item insurance, custom housing, and a new continent. The modern game is built on its foundation.

## What happened

- **The item property revolution.** Magic items moved to the intensity-based [property system](../items/item-properties.md) — Hit Chance Increase, Damage Increase, Faster Casting, resists, luck, and dozens more — that still defines gear today.
- **Item insurance.** Pay gold per item and it returns to your backpack on death instead of dropping to your corpse (see [item flags](../items/item-flags.md)).
- **Custom housing.** The house designer let players build homes tile by tile, replacing fixed classic house types for most players — and sparking a decorating culture that never stopped.
- **Malas.** A new landmass with the paladin city of Luna and the necromancer city of Umbra, and a frantic land rush for housing plots.
- **New professions.** [Chivalry](../skills/magic/chivalry.md) (paladins) and [Necromancy](../skills/magic/necromancy.md) joined the skill list.
- **The Doom Gauntlet and artifacts.** Named, fixed-property [artifacts](../items/artifacts.md) as rewards from the game's hardest content gave endgame players something to chase beyond the property lottery.

## Lasting impact

Nearly every system a modern player touches — properties, insurance, custom homes, artifacts — dates to Age of Shadows. It turned UO from a game about the world into a game about the gear, for better and worse, and its systems survived every expansion since.

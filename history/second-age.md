# The Second Age (1998)

The Second Age, released October 1998, was Ultima Online's first retail expansion — sold as a physical boxed product — and it added an entire new continent: the Lost Lands.

## What happened

- **The Lost Lands.** A new landmass reached through passages from Britannia, with its own towns (Delucia, Papua), dungeons, and creatures. For the first time, the world had a frontier beyond the original map.
- **Treasure hunting.** The expansion brought deciphered treasure maps and buried chests into the game, creating a permanent profession loop for explorers.
- **Expansions as retail products.** Selling a boxed expansion for a subscription online game was itself a novelty — and it established the rhythm UO would follow for decades: each era adds land, and land means housing, hunting grounds, and new frontiers.

## Lasting impact

The Lost Lands remain part of the world, and treasure hunting never left. More importantly, The Second Age proved the expansion model worked: players would pay for more world, and each new landmass would reshape the economy and the map.

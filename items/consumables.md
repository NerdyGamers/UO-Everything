# Consumables

Consumables are the ammunition of daily play: reagents, bandages, potions, and the assorted tools that get used up in the course of fighting, healing, and traveling.

## How it works

**Reagents** — black pearl, blood moss, garlic, ginseng, mandrake root, nightshade, spider's silk, sulfurous ash — power [spellcasting](../mechanics/spellcasting.md). [Lower Reagent Cost](item-properties.md) and reagent-free casting methods reduce consumption, but every serious mage still travels with a stocked reagent bag.

**Bandages** fuel [Healing](../skills/combat/healing.md): closing wounds in combat, curing poison, and resurrecting the dead. They are cheap, light, and consumed constantly by warrior templates.

**Potions** — heal, cure, refresh, strength, agility, nightsight, explosion, and more — are brewed with [Alchemy](../skills/magic/alchemy.md) and stored in **kegs** for bulk use. Greater heal and cure potions are combat staples; explosion potions double as thrown weapons.

**Magical foods** from [cooking](../crafting/cooking-system.md) — enchanted apples, grapes of wrath, magical fish pies — are consumables with buff effects rather than just hunger relief.

**Ninja tools** feed [Ninjitsu](../skills/combat/ninjitsu.md): **smoke bombs** let a ninja vanish mid-fight, and shuriken serve as thrown weapons. Recall scrolls round out the travel kit for characters without magery.

## Era notes

Kegs and the bulk potion economy date to the earliest eras. Magical foods accreted over many publishes, and ninja consumables arrived with Samurai Empire (2004).

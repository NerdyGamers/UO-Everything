# Mark of Travesty

A tribal mask dropped by Travesty, known for its paired skill bonuses.

## How it is obtained

Rare drop from [Travesty](../../../mobiles/bosses/peerless/travesty.md), the changeling peerless of Bedlam.

## Properties

A tribal mask granting two complementary skill bonuses of +10 each — for example music and provocation together; per UOGuide's Travesty loot table the pair always complements.

## Notes

Valued by bards and skill-focused templates for the free skill points. See [Artifacts](../../artifacts.md).

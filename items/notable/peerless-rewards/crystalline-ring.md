# Crystalline Ring

A mage-oriented ring and a signature drop of the Shimmering Effusion.

## How it is obtained

Drops from the [Shimmering Effusion](../../../mobiles/bosses/peerless/shimmering-effusion.md), one of the Mondain's Legacy peerless bosses.

## Properties

Geared toward casters, with faster-casting-oriented bonuses (community recollection; exact values are not stated here).

## Notes

A fixture of necromage suit discussions, often worn alongside the Pendant of the Magi. See [Artifacts](../../artifacts.md).

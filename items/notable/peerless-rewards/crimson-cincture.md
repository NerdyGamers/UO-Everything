# Crimson Cincture

A half apron counted among the most sought-after peerless artifacts, prized by melee fighters for its dexterity and combat bonuses.

## How it is obtained

Drops from the peerless bosses — community artifact lists mark it as obtainable from all of them, and it is most commonly associated with [Dread Horn](../../../mobiles/bosses/peerless/dread-horn.md).

## Properties

Worn in the waist slot, where almost nothing else carries item properties. Its bonuses lean toward dexterity, accuracy, and defense (community recollection; exact values are not stated here).

## Notes

A staple of dexer suits. It pairs with the Tangle as the two property-bearing waist-slot options — the Cincture being the fighter's choice and the Tangle the mage's. See [Artifacts](../../artifacts.md) and [Peerless Encounters](../../mechanics/peerless-encounters.md).

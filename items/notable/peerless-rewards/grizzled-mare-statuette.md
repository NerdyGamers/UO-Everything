# Grizzled Mare Statuette

A pet statuette dropped by the Monstrous Interred Grizzle.

## How it is obtained

Rare drop from the [Monstrous Interred Grizzle](../../../mobiles/bosses/peerless/monstrous-interred-grizzle.md).

## Properties

Double-clicking the statuette releases a bonded, fully trained skeletal steed that does not decay over time.

## Notes

One of the peerless pet rewards; a Mondain's Legacy upgrade is required to activate the statuette.

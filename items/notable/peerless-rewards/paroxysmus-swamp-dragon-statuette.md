# Paroxysmus Swamp Dragon Statuette

A pet statuette dropped by Chief Paroxysmus.

## How it is obtained

Rare drop from [Chief Paroxysmus](../../../mobiles/bosses/peerless/chief-paroxysmus.md).

## Properties

Double-clicking the statuette releases a bonded, fully trained swamp dragon with unbreakable armor.

## Notes

One of the peerless pet rewards; a Mondain's Legacy upgrade is required to activate the statuette.

# Albino Squirrel Imprisoned in a Crystal

A pet crystal dropped by Lady Melisande.

## How it is obtained

Rare drop from [Lady Melisande](../../../mobiles/bosses/peerless/lady-melisande.md).

## Properties

Double-clicking the crystal releases a bonded pure-white squirrel pet.

## Notes

One of the peerless pet rewards; a Mondain's Legacy upgrade is required to activate the crystal. See [Peerless Encounters](../../mechanics/peerless-encounters.md).

# Scepter of the Chief

A scepter dropped by Chief Paroxysmus.

## How it is obtained

Drops from [Chief Paroxysmus](../../../mobiles/bosses/peerless/chief-paroxysmus.md), the peerless of the Palace of Paroxysmus.

## Properties

A mace-class weapon; specific property details are not well documented in community sources.

## Notes

One of the named peerless weapons; see [Artifacts](../../artifacts.md).

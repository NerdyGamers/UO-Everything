# Gauntlets of the Grizzle

One of five matching armor pieces dropped by the Monstrous Interred Grizzle.

## How it is obtained

Drops from the [Monstrous Interred Grizzle](../../../mobiles/bosses/peerless/monstrous-interred-grizzle.md), the peerless of Bedlam.

## Properties

Part of the Grizzle armor set — gauntlets, greaves, skull helm, tunic, and vambraces. Specific property details are not well documented in community sources.

## Notes

Collected as a matching set; the full set is a peerless trophy. See [Artifacts](../../artifacts.md).

# An Imprisoned Dog

A pet crystal dropped by Travesty.

## How it is obtained

Rare drop from [Travesty](../../../mobiles/bosses/peerless/travesty.md).

## Properties

Double-clicking the crystal releases a bonded dog that shape-shifts like a changeling.

## Notes

One of the peerless pet rewards; a Mondain's Legacy upgrade is required to activate the crystal. See [Peerless Encounters](../../mechanics/peerless-encounters.md).

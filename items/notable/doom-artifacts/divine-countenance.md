# Divine Countenance

A white tribal mask, a Doom artifact and one of the original Gauntlet rewards. It is a defensive mask for spellcasters.

## How it is obtained

Awarded as a Doom Gauntlet artifact for killing Dark Fathers, Darknight Creepers, Fleshrenderers and Impalers, Shadow Knights, or Abyssmal Horrors. Artifacts are placed directly into the backpack rather than looted. It carries the standard Gauntlet artifact rarity of 11.

## Properties

Reflect physical damage, intelligence bonus, mana regeneration, and lower mana cost, with strong energy resist.

## Notes

The reflected damage plus mana sustain makes it a defensive pick for mage templates. Note: the current UOGuide page no longer shows its property table, so the properties above are community-documented rather than confirmed from the primary reference — verify in game.

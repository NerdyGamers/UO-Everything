# Blade of the Righteous

A longsword Doom artifact. Unlike most Gauntlet artifacts it is not a boss drop — it is stolen from within Doom.

## How it is obtained

A Doom equipment stealable (artifact rarity 10), not a Gauntlet boss reward. It spawns in the Gauntlet's healer room and must be taken with the Stealing skill.

## Properties

Demon slayer, hit life leech, hit point increase, damage increase, and use best weapon skill.

## Notes

The demon slayer property with life leech makes it a strong choice against demonic foes, and use-best-weapon-skill lets non-swordsmen wield it effectively. Its stealable origin makes it scarcer than the boss-drop artifacts.

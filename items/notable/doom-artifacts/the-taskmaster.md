# The Taskmaster

A war fork Doom artifact, one of the original Gauntlet rewards. It deals poison damage and spreads poison around its target.

## How it is obtained

Awarded as a Doom Gauntlet artifact for killing Dark Fathers, Darknight Creepers, Fleshrenderers and Impalers, Shadow Knights, or Abyssmal Horrors. Artifacts are placed directly into the backpack rather than looted. It carries artifact rarity 10, one step below the standard Gauntlet rarity of 11.

## Properties

Hit poison area, dexterity bonus, hit chance increase, and damage increase. It deals poison-type damage.

## Notes

The area poison hit makes it distinctive among the Gauntlet weapons. A long-standing bug produced two variants of the item, one with a broken Disarm special move — check which version you hold, per community recollection.

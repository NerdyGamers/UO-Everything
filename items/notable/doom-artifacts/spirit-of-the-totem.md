# Spirit of the Totem

A black bear mask Doom artifact, one of the original Gauntlet rewards. It is famous for a hit chance increase well above what normal items allow.

## How it is obtained

Awarded as a Doom Gauntlet artifact for killing Dark Fathers, Darknight Creepers, Fleshrenderers and Impalers, Shadow Knights, or Abyssmal Horrors. Artifacts are placed directly into the backpack rather than looted. It carries the standard Gauntlet artifact rarity of 11.

## Properties

Strength bonus, reflect physical damage, hit chance increase, and physical resist.

## Notes

UOGuide cites this mask as a flagship example of Doom artifacts breaking normal item-property limits. It remains a prized melee headpiece for the accuracy and reflected damage.

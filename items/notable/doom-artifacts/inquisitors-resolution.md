# Inquisitor's Resolution

A pair of blue platemail gloves, a Doom artifact. It is stolen from Doom's first level rather than earned from Gauntlet bosses.

## How it is obtained

A Doom equipment stealable (artifact rarity 10), not a Gauntlet boss reward. It spawns on Doom's first level in a hidden room near the dungeon exit, which requires completing the Doom lever puzzle to reach. It must be taken with the Stealing skill.

## Properties

Faster cast recovery, lower mana cost, and mage armor, with cold and energy resists.

## Notes

Faster cast recovery on a gloves slot is exceptionally rare, which is what makes this piece prized for mage templates. UOGuide has re-categorized it under Doom stealables rather than Gauntlet artifacts, so treat the classification accordingly.

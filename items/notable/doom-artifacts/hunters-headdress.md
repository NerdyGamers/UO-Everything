# Hunter's Headdress

A pale green deer mask, a Doom artifact and one of the original Gauntlet rewards. It is the archer's headpiece.

## How it is obtained

Awarded as a Doom Gauntlet artifact for killing Dark Fathers, Darknight Creepers, Fleshrenderers and Impalers, Shadow Knights, or Abyssmal Horrors. Artifacts are placed directly into the backpack rather than looted. It carries the standard Gauntlet artifact rarity of 11.

## Properties

Archery skill bonus, hit chance increase, dexterity bonus, night sight, and high cold resist.

## Notes

The archery bonus plus accuracy and night sight in one slot makes it a long-standing staple for archer templates.

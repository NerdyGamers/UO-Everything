# Hat of the Magi

A white wizard's hat Doom artifact, one of the original Gauntlet rewards. It boosts intelligence, mana recovery, and spell damage.

## How it is obtained

Awarded as a Doom Gauntlet artifact for killing Dark Fathers, Darknight Creepers, Fleshrenderers and Impalers, Shadow Knights, or Abyssmal Horrors. Artifacts are placed directly into the backpack rather than looted. It carries the standard Gauntlet artifact rarity of 11.

## Properties

Intelligence bonus, mana regeneration, spell damage increase, and strong poison and energy resists.

## Notes

A staple mage headpiece. Since Publish 94 it can also be used in crafting Dr. Spector's Lenses, per community documentation.

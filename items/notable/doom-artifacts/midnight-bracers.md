# Midnight Bracers

A pair of black bone arms, a Doom artifact and one of the original Gauntlet rewards. They are the necromancer's bracers.

## How it is obtained

Awarded as a Doom Gauntlet artifact for killing Dark Fathers, Darknight Creepers, Fleshrenderers and Impalers, Shadow Knights, or Abyssmal Horrors. Artifacts are placed directly into the backpack rather than looted. It carries the standard Gauntlet artifact rarity of 11.

## Properties

Necromancy skill bonus, spell damage increase, mage armor, and resists.

## Notes

The necromancy bonus on an armor piece is rare, making these a natural fit for necromancer templates pairing the skill with spell damage.

# Armor of Fortune

A studded tunic Doom artifact, one of the original Gauntlet rewards. It is the best-known luck armor piece in the game.

## How it is obtained

Awarded as a Doom Gauntlet artifact for killing Dark Fathers, Darknight Creepers, Fleshrenderers and Impalers, Shadow Knights, or Abyssmal Horrors. Artifacts are placed directly into the backpack rather than looted. It carries the standard Gauntlet artifact rarity of 11.

## Properties

A very large luck bonus, lower reagent cost well above the normal item cap, and defense chance increase.

## Notes

The luck bonus is the draw — it is a centerpiece of luck suits for improving loot rolls. The reagent cost reduction exceeding the normal cap is another example of Doom artifacts breaking property limits.

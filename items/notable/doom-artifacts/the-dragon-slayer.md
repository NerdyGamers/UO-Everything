# The Dragon Slayer

A lance Doom artifact and one of the original Gauntlet rewards. As the name suggests, it is built for hunting dragons.

## How it is obtained

Awarded as a Doom Gauntlet artifact for killing Dark Fathers, Darknight Creepers, Fleshrenderers and Impalers, Shadow Knights, or Abyssmal Horrors. Artifacts are placed directly into the backpack rather than looted. It carries the standard Gauntlet artifact rarity of 11.

## Properties

Dragon slayer, use best weapon skill, damage increase, luck, and fire resist. It deals energy damage.

## Notes

The dragon slayer property plus use-best-weapon-skill makes it a classic dragon-hunting weapon, effective even for characters without deep lance training. See [Item Properties](../../item-properties.md) for how slayer properties work.

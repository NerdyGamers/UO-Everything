# Shadow Dancer Leggings

A pair of black leather leggings, a Doom artifact and one of the original Gauntlet rewards. They are the thief's leggings.

## How it is obtained

Awarded as a Doom Gauntlet artifact for killing Dark Fathers, Darknight Creepers, Fleshrenderers and Impalers, Shadow Knights, or Abyssmal Horrors. Artifacts are placed directly into the backpack rather than looted. It carries the standard Gauntlet artifact rarity of 11.

## Properties

Stealing bonus, stealth bonus, and strong poison, energy, and physical resists.

## Notes

The large stealing and stealth bonuses make these the signature leg armor for thief templates; few other items boost both skills at once.

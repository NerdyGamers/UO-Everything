# Ornate Crown of the Harrower

A green bone helmet, a Doom artifact and one of the original Gauntlet rewards. It is a regeneration-focused helm for fighters.

## How it is obtained

Awarded as a Doom Gauntlet artifact for killing Dark Fathers, Darknight Creepers, Fleshrenderers and Impalers, Shadow Knights, or Abyssmal Horrors. Artifacts are placed directly into the backpack rather than looted. It carries the standard Gauntlet artifact rarity of 11.

## Properties

Damage increase, stamina regeneration, hit point regeneration, and high poison resist.

## Notes

The dual regeneration mods make it a sustain-oriented choice for melee templates that want to recover stamina and health mid-fight.

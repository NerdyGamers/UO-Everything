# Ornament of the Magician

A blue bracelet Doom artifact, one of the original Gauntlet rewards. It is a pure spellcaster's bracelet.

## How it is obtained

Awarded as a Doom Gauntlet artifact for killing Dark Fathers, Darknight Creepers, Fleshrenderers and Impalers, Shadow Knights, or Abyssmal Horrors. Artifacts are placed directly into the backpack rather than looted. It carries the standard Gauntlet artifact rarity of 11.

## Properties

Faster cast recovery, faster casting, lower mana cost, and lower reagent cost, with energy resist.

## Notes

The combination of casting speed and mana/reduction mods makes it a core bracelet for mage templates. See [Artifacts](../../artifacts.md) for the broader artifact systems.

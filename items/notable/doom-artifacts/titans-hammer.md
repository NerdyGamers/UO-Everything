# Titan's Hammer

A war hammer Doom artifact. Like the Blade of the Righteous, it is stolen rather than earned from Gauntlet bosses.

## How it is obtained

A Doom equipment stealable (artifact rarity 10), not a Gauntlet boss reward. It spawns in Doom and must be taken with the Stealing skill.

## Properties

Hit energy area, strength bonus, hit chance increase, and damage increase.

## Notes

The area energy hit gives it crowd-clearing character unusual for a war hammer, and the strength bonus stacks well with melee damage templates.

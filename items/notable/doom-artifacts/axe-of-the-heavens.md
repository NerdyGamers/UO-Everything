# Axe of the Heavens

A double axe Doom artifact, one of the original Gauntlet rewards. It mixes lightning offense with defensive mods.

## How it is obtained

Awarded as a Doom Gauntlet artifact for killing Dark Fathers, Darknight Creepers, Fleshrenderers and Impalers, Shadow Knights, or Abyssmal Horrors. Artifacts are placed directly into the backpack rather than looted. It carries the standard Gauntlet artifact rarity of 11.

## Properties

Hit lightning, hit chance increase, defense chance increase, and damage increase.

## Notes

The combination of a lightning hit effect with both accuracy and defense mods makes it a well-rounded melee choice rather than a pure damage weapon.

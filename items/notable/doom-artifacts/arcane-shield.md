# Arcane Shield

A tear kite shield Doom artifact, one of the original Gauntlet rewards. It is a defensive shield built for spellcasters.

## How it is obtained

Awarded as a Doom Gauntlet artifact for killing Dark Fathers, Darknight Creepers, Fleshrenderers and Impalers, Shadow Knights, or Abyssmal Horrors. Artifacts are placed directly into the backpack rather than looted. It carries the standard Gauntlet artifact rarity of 11.

## Properties

Spell channeling, faster casting, defense chance increase, and night sight.

## Notes

A rare combination of mage casting mods on a shield, letting casters gain defense chance without giving up spellcasting — a staple for defensive mage templates.

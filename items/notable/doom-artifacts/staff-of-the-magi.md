# Staff of the Magi

A black staff Doom artifact, one of the original Gauntlet rewards. It is a weapon designed to let mages fight in melee.

## How it is obtained

Awarded as a Doom Gauntlet artifact for killing Dark Fathers, Darknight Creepers, Fleshrenderers and Impalers, Shadow Knights, or Abyssmal Horrors. Artifacts are placed directly into the backpack rather than looted. It carries the standard Gauntlet artifact rarity of 11.

## Properties

Mage weapon with no skill penalty, spell channeling, faster casting, and damage increase. It deals energy damage.

## Notes

Because it is a mage weapon without the usual skill penalty and allows spell channeling, it suits battlemage templates that mix melee swings with casting.

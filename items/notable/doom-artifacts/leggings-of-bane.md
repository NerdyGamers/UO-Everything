# Leggings of Bane

A pair of green chainmail leggings, a Doom artifact and one of the original Gauntlet rewards. They are built for melee accuracy and poison protection.

## How it is obtained

Awarded as a Doom Gauntlet artifact for killing Dark Fathers, Darknight Creepers, Fleshrenderers and Impalers, Shadow Knights, or Abyssmal Horrors. Artifacts are placed directly into the backpack rather than looted. It carries the standard Gauntlet artifact rarity of 11.

## Properties

Hit chance increase at the maximum intensity found on a single item, stamina increase, and very high poison resist.

## Notes

A premier fighter's leg slot for the accuracy alone. Since Publish 96 they can also be taken to a blacksmith to craft the Britches of Warding, per community documentation.

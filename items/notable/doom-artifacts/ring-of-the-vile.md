# Ring of the Vile

A ring Doom artifact, one of the original Gauntlet rewards. It boosts dexterity and stamina recovery with strong poison resistance.

## How it is obtained

Awarded as a Doom Gauntlet artifact for killing Dark Fathers, Darknight Creepers, Fleshrenderers and Impalers, Shadow Knights, or Abyssmal Horrors. Artifacts are placed directly into the backpack rather than looted. It carries the standard Gauntlet artifact rarity of 11.

## Properties

Dexterity bonus, stamina regeneration, hit chance increase, and poison resist.

## Notes

The stamina regeneration and dexterity bonus make it a strong ring for melee fighters who want to keep swinging; the poison resist is a useful extra.

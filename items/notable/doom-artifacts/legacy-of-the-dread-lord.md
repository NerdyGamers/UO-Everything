# Legacy of the Dread Lord

A bardiche-class Doom artifact, one of the original Gauntlet rewards. It is unusual as a two-handed melee weapon built for spellcasters.

## How it is obtained

Awarded as a Doom Gauntlet artifact for killing Dark Fathers, Darknight Creepers, Fleshrenderers and Impalers, Shadow Knights, or Abyssmal Horrors. Artifacts are placed directly into the backpack rather than looted. It carries artifact rarity 10, one step below the standard Gauntlet rarity of 11.

## Properties

Spell channeling, faster cast recovery, swing speed increase, and damage increase.

## Notes

The spell channeling on a heavy two-handed weapon makes it a fit for mage-warrior templates that want to cast without swapping weapons. Community recollection holds it as one of the earliest examples of a weapon breaking normal item-property limits.

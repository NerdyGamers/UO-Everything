# Rune Beetle Carapace

The Rune Beetle Carapace is a platemail do, a Major artifact of the Treasures of Tokuno events. It is a chest piece built for spellcasters, trading raw resistance for mana and casting mods.

## How it is obtained

During the Treasures of Tokuno events, minor artifacts dropped into the packs of players killing creatures around the Tokuno Islands (cranes excepted). Ten minor artifacts could be turned in to Ihara Soko, the Imperial Minister of Trade in Zento, for a major artifact of the player's choice, even if the minors were damaged through use.

## Properties

Mana increase 10, mana regeneration 3, and lower mana cost 15%, with resists of 5 physical, 3 fire, 14 cold, 3 poison, and 14 energy, for a total resistance of 39. Lower requirements 100% and the mage armor property, durability 255.

## Notes

Introduced with the original 2005 Treasures of Tokuno event (Publish 30). The total resistance of 39 is low for a chest piece, but few chest pieces offer this combination of caster benefits, which made it popular with mages and special-move fighters alike. It is named for the rune beetle of the Tokuno Islands.

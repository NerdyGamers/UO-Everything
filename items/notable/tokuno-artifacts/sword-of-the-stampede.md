# Sword of the Stampede

The Sword of the Stampede is a no-dachi, a Major artifact of the Treasures of Tokuno events. It is a heavy two-handed sword built around the harm spell and cold damage.

## How it is obtained

During the Treasures of Tokuno events, minor artifacts dropped into the packs of players killing creatures around the Tokuno Islands (cranes excepted). Ten minor artifacts could be turned in to Ihara Soko, the Imperial Minister of Trade in Zento, for a major artifact of the player's choice, even if the minors were damaged through use.

## Properties

Hit harm 100%, hit chance increase 10%, and damage increase 60%, dealing all cold damage. Base damage 16-18 at 3.5 seconds swing speed, strength requirement 40, durability 255. Special moves: Crushing Blow and Riding Swipe.

## Notes

Introduced with the original 2005 Treasures of Tokuno event (Publish 30). It uses the swordsmanship skill. The guaranteed harm proc on every hit gave it its stampede reputation among warriors.

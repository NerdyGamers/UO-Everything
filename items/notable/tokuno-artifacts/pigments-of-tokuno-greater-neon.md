# Pigments of Tokuno (Greater, Neon)

The greater neon Pigments of Tokuno are dye bottles, a Major artifact of the Treasures of Tokuno events. Like other Tokuno dyes, they color magical items such as weapons and armor, working much like a dye tub.

## How it is obtained

Available only during the first Treasures of Tokuno event in 2005. Ten minor artifacts turned in to Ihara Soko, the Imperial Minister of Trade in Zento, earned a major artifact of the player's choice. Each bottle came with 50 uses; a dye with no charges remaining disappears and cannot be turned in.

## Properties

Ten bright neon shades, 50 uses per bottle: Berserker Red, Rum Red, Fire Orange, Paragon Gold, Invulnerability Blue, Luna White, Dryad Green, Nox Green, Violet Courage Purple, and Shadow Dancer Black.

## Notes

At 50 charges per bottle, the neon set is the most generous of the Tokuno dyes. The lesser pigment of the first event was an uncolored bottle that removed coloration rather than a neon set.

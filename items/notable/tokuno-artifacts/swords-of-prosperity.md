# Swords of Prosperity

The Swords of Prosperity are a daisho, a Major artifact of the Treasures of Tokuno events. They are a mage-friendly weapon pairing spellcasting mods with a large luck bonus.

## How it is obtained

During the Treasures of Tokuno events, minor artifacts dropped into the packs of players killing creatures around the Tokuno Islands (cranes excepted). Ten minor artifacts could be turned in to Ihara Soko, the Imperial Minister of Trade in Zento, for a major artifact of the player's choice, even if the minors were damaged through use.

## Properties

Spell channeling, mage weapon with no skill penalty, luck 200, and faster casting 1, dealing all fire damage. Base damage 13-15 at 2.75 seconds swing speed, strength requirement 40, durability 255.

## Notes

The 200 luck is the standout property, making it a staple of luck suits, while spell channeling with no mage penalty lets a mage use magery skill for defense. Community recollection counts it among the most coveted Tokuno majors.

# Darkened Sky

The Darkened Sky is a kama, a Major artifact of the Treasures of Tokuno events. It is a fast two-handed fencing weapon built around lightning strikes and cold damage.

## How it is obtained

During the Treasures of Tokuno events, minor artifacts dropped into the packs of players killing creatures around the Tokuno Islands (cranes excepted). Ten minor artifacts could be turned in to Ihara Soko, the Imperial Minister of Trade in Zento, for a major artifact of the player's choice, even if the minors were damaged through use.

## Properties

Hit lightning 60%, swing speed increase 25%, and damage increase 50%, dealing half cold and half energy damage. Base damage 9-11 at 2 seconds swing speed, strength requirement 15, durability 255. Special moves: Whirlwind Attack and Defense Mastery.

## Notes

Introduced with the original 2005 Treasures of Tokuno event (Publish 30). As a two-handed weapon it uses the fencing skill. The hit lightning proc paired with a fast swing speed made it a popular fencer weapon in its era.

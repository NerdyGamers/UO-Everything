# Kasa of the Raj-In

The Kasa of the Raj-In is a kasa, a Major artifact of the Treasures of Tokuno events. It is a mage-oriented headpiece prized for its unusually high total resistances and its boost to spell damage.

## How it is obtained

During the Treasures of Tokuno events, minor artifacts dropped into the packs of players killing creatures around the Tokuno Islands (cranes excepted). Ten minor artifacts could be turned in to Ihara Soko, the Imperial Minister of Trade in Zento, for a major artifact of the player's choice, even if the minors were damaged through use.

## Properties

Spell damage increase 12%, with resists of 12 physical, 17 fire, 21 cold, 17 poison, and 17 energy, for a total resistance of 84. Strength requirement 10, durability 255.

## Notes

Introduced with the original 2005 Treasures of Tokuno event (Publish 30). The total of 84 resistance on a single head-slot item is exceptionally high, which made it a coveted mage headpiece. A comparable caster headpiece from a different source is the Hat of the Magi, covered under the Doom artifacts.

# Treasures of Tokuno Major Artifacts

The choice rewards of the Treasures of Tokuno events. During the events, minor artifacts dropped into the packs of players killing creatures around the Tokuno Islands (cranes excepted); the more creatures killed, the more likely an artifact would appear. Ten minor artifacts could be turned in to Ihara Soko, the Imperial Minister of Trade in Zento, for a major artifact of the player's choice, even if the minors were damaged through use. An unlocked Chest of Heirlooms could not be turned in.

The original event ran in 2005 with Publish 30. A second Treasures of Tokuno event followed, adding the metallic pigment set.

# Tome of Lost Knowledge

The Tome of Lost Knowledge is a light blue spellbook, a Major artifact of the Treasures of Tokuno events. It is one of the strongest mage spellbooks of its era, combining magery skill, intelligence, spell damage, and mana cost reduction.

## How it is obtained

During the Treasures of Tokuno events, minor artifacts dropped into the packs of players killing creatures around the Tokuno Islands (cranes excepted). Ten minor artifacts could be turned in to Ihara Soko, the Imperial Minister of Trade in Zento, for a major artifact of the player's choice, even if the minors were damaged through use.

## Properties

Magery +15, intelligence bonus 8, spell damage increase 15%, and lower mana cost 15%. It has no weight.

## Notes

Introduced with the original 2005 Treasures of Tokuno event (Publish 30). Aside from the Tome of Enlightenment, the minor-artifact spellbook of the same events, it was the only spellbook without the blessed property, so owners were advised to insure it.

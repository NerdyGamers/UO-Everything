# Wind's Edge

Wind's Edge is a tessen, a Major artifact of the Treasures of Tokuno events. It is one of the fastest-swinging artifact weapons of its era, built for mana leech and defense.

## How it is obtained

During the Treasures of Tokuno events, minor artifacts dropped into the packs of players killing creatures around the Tokuno Islands (cranes excepted). Ten minor artifacts could be turned in to Ihara Soko, the Imperial Minister of Trade in Zento, for a major artifact of the player's choice, even if the minors were damaged through use.

## Properties

Hit mana leech 25%, defense chance increase 10%, swing speed increase 50%, and damage increase 50%, dealing all energy damage. Base damage 10-12 at 2 seconds swing speed, strength requirement 10, durability 255.

## Notes

Introduced with the original 2005 Treasures of Tokuno event (Publish 30). It uses the mace fighting skill. The 50% swing speed increase on an already fast 2-second base made it one of the most popular Tokuno weapons.

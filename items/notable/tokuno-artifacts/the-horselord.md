# The Horselord

The Horselord is a yumi, a Major artifact of the Treasures of Tokuno events. It is an archer's bow with a double slayer property, valued for hunting elementals and reptiles.

## How it is obtained

During the Treasures of Tokuno events, minor artifacts dropped into the packs of players killing creatures around the Tokuno Islands (cranes excepted). Ten minor artifacts could be turned in to Ihara Soko, the Imperial Minister of Trade in Zento, for a major artifact of the player's choice, even if the minors were damaged through use.

## Properties

Elemental slayer and reptile slayer, dexterity bonus 5, mana regeneration 1, luck 125, and damage increase 50%, dealing all physical damage. Base damage 16-20 at 4.5 seconds swing speed, range 10, strength requirement 35, durability 255. Special moves: Armor Pierce and Double Shot.

## Notes

Introduced with the original 2005 Treasures of Tokuno event (Publish 30). It uses the archery skill. The dual slayer combination is unusual on a single bow and made it a favored hunting weapon.

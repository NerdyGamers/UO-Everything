# Pigments of Tokuno (Greater, Metal)

The greater metallic Pigments of Tokuno are dye bottles, a Major artifact of the Treasures of Tokuno events. Like other Tokuno dyes, they color magical items such as weapons and armor, working much like a dye tub.

## How it is obtained

Available only during the second Treasures of Tokuno event. Ten minor artifacts turned in to Ihara Soko, the Imperial Minister of Trade in Zento, earned a major artifact of the player's choice. Each bottle came with 10 uses; a dye with no charges remaining disappears and cannot be turned in.

## Properties

Ten metallic shades, 10 uses per bottle. The shades are remembered as Faded Coal, Coal, Midnight Coal, Faded Gold, Faded Bronze, Storm Bronze, Rose, Faded Rose, Deep Rose, and Subdued Coal.

## Notes

The lesser counterpart of this set, the earthen pigments, dropped directly as minor artifacts with a single use each. Community recollection holds that most of the metallic colors could later be purchased through the Japanese UO website with 50 charges per bottle.

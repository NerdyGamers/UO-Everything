# Swords of Prosperity

The Swords of Prosperity are a named greater artifact from the Treasures of Tokuno collection — a mage's sword combining spell channeling with a large Luck bonus. Despite the festive-sounding name, they are Tokuno artifacts, not holiday gifts.

## How it is obtained

A greater-tier artifact from the Treasures of Tokuno event, earned through that event's artifact system. It is a two-handed weapon using the Swordsmanship skill.

## Properties

Spell channeling with no mage-weapon skill penalty, a high Luck bonus (200), faster casting, and all fire damage — a combination that made it a popular mage weapon.

## Notes

The plural name is the official one ("Swords of Prosperity"), though players often shorten it. See [Item Properties](../../item-properties.md) for the Luck and channeling mechanics it relies on.

# Aegis of Grace

The Aegis of Grace is a Mondain's Legacy minor artifact helmet notable for appearing in two forms: a dragon helm for humans or a circlet for elves.

## How it is obtained

Drops from named monsters in the nine Mondain's Legacy dungeons, appearing directly in the character's backpack. Peerless bosses can also drop minor artifacts on their corpses, which must be looted.

## Properties

Defense chance increase (20%) and a Resisting Spells bonus, with self repair — a combination suited to melee and archer templates. Its resists lean heavily toward energy, the least common damage type in player-versus-player combat, a noted drawback in older guides.

## Notes

One of the few artifacts with alternate racial forms. The dragon-helm art was changed at some point after the item's introduction (reported as Publish 39), per community recollection.

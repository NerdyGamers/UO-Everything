# Dupre's Shield

Dupre's Shield is an anniversary chaos shield honoring Dupre, the paladin companion of the Avatar who led the people of Sosaria against Minax at Trinsic. It was dedicated to UO's third year, September 1999 to September 2000.

## How it is obtained

Introduced as an 8th Anniversary gift, claimable with an 8th Anniversary Token (later also via Heritage Tokens); it has also appeared in the Ultima Store. It is blessed.

## Properties

A Parrying bonus (+5), hit point increase and regeneration, and small resists. It can be dyed with Pigments of Tokuno.

## Notes

Often paired with the Virtue Armor set as a thematic complement, since Dupre embodies the paladin virtues. Dupre is one of the classic companions from the Ultima series; see the [Virtues](../../../mechanics/virtues.md) he represents.

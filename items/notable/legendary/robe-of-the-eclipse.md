# Robe of the Eclipse

The Robe of the Eclipse is a Mondain's Legacy minor artifact robe worn mainly for its Luck bonus. Its elven counterpart is the Robe of the Equinox — the same item in elven form.

## How it is obtained

Drops from named monsters in the Mondain's Legacy dungeons, appearing directly in the character's backpack. Peerless bosses can also drop minor artifacts on their corpses, which must be looted.

## Properties

A Luck 95 robe with minimal other modifiers; the human and gargoyle form is the Eclipse, the elven form the Equinox.

## Notes

One of the few artifacts with a racial counterpart split — Eclipse for humans and gargoyles, Equinox for elves. See [Item Properties](../../item-properties.md) for how Luck works.

# Legendary and Named Items

Named and legendary items are one-of-a-kind or limited-run pieces tied to Ultima's characters, anniversaries, and events — distinct from the repeatable artifact sets. They carry story significance alongside their properties.

## Articles

- [Jaana's Staff](jaanas-staff.md) — Virtue Artifact gnarled staff named for the Avatar's companion, prized for its Luck.
- [Swords of Prosperity](sword-of-prosperity.md) — Treasures of Tokuno greater artifact mage sword.
- [Robe of the Eclipse](robe-of-the-eclipse.md) — Mondain's Legacy minor artifact luck robe, with the elven Robe of the Equinox as its counterpart.
- [Aegis of Grace](aegis-of-grace.md) — Mondain's Legacy minor artifact helmet in dragon-helm or circlet form.
- [Geoffrey's Axe](geoffreys-axe.md) — Vesper Museum collection replica honoring Geoffrey of Jhelom.
- [Dupre's Shield](dupres-shield.md) — 8th Anniversary chaos shield honoring Dupre's stand against Minax.
- [Ethereal Mounts](ethereal-mounts.md) — veteran-reward summoned mounts in statuette form.

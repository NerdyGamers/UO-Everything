# Jaana's Staff

Jaana's Staff is a named artifact gnarled staff from the Virtue Artifact set, the artifact collection introduced for Ultima Online's 10th Anniversary. It is a mage-oriented weapon prized for carrying one of the highest Luck bonuses on any weapon.

## How it is obtained

Drops from creatures slain in the Anti-Virtue dungeons as part of the Virtue Artifact system. It appears directly in the character's backpack with a system message rewarding valor in defeating the fallen creature. Drop chances improve the longer it has been since the character's last such artifact.

## Properties

Spell channeling with a reduced mage-weapon penalty, a very high Luck bonus (220), and defense chance increase. It is a two-handed weapon using the Mace Fighting skill.

## Notes

Jaana was one of the Avatar's companions in the classic Ultima games. The staff belongs to the same anniversary artifact family as the [Virtue Armor set](../../artifacts.md); see [Item Properties](../../item-properties.md) for how Luck works.

# Ethereal Mounts

Ethereal mounts are translucent summoned mounts obtained chiefly as veteran rewards. Each arrives as a statuette; double-clicking it summons the mount and seats the rider after a short summoning delay. They cannot be killed, and dismounting returns them to statue form.

## How it is obtained

Veteran reward picks at various account-age tiers. The long-standing set includes the ethereal horse, llama, ostard, unicorn, ki-rin, ridgeback, beetle, and swamp dragon; later tiers added the rideable polar bear, cu sidhe, reptalon, hiryu, tiger, war boar, tarantula, hell hound, and dragon — with later anniversary rewards reported as usable at any account age. Some are also sold in the Ultima Store (such as the Charger of the Fallen), and certain vanity pet statuettes can be converted to ethereal-style mounts.

## Properties

They function as mounts without taming or feeding, and the statue form keeps them in the backpack rather than the stables. Stamina regenerates while standing still. They can be disrupted while summoning.

## Notes

One of the most recognizable veteran perks and a long-standing status symbol of account age. See [Veteran Rewards](../../../shards/veteran-rewards.md) for the reward system.

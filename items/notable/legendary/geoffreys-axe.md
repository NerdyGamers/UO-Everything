# Geoffrey's Axe

Geoffrey's Axe is a replica executioner's axe awarded by the Museum of Vesper community collection, named for Geoffrey — the Jhelom fighter who embodied the Virtue of Valor and traveled with the Avatar.

## How it is obtained

An 800,000-point reward from the Vesper Museum collection; players earn points by donating to the museum and spend them on the replica.

## Properties

A random super slayer, Strength Bonus 10, Luck 150, with hit chance and damage increase and some fire resist. Despite being an axe, it uses the Swordsmanship skill. It can be dyed with Pigments of Tokuno but cannot be enhanced.

## Notes

Geoffrey was one of the Avatar's companions in the classic Ultima games. The axe sees occasional practical use in the Doom Gauntlet. See [Community Collections](../../../quests/community-collections.md) for how the museum reward system works.

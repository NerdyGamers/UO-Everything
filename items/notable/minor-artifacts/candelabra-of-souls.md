# Candelabra of Souls

A decorative candelabra minor artifact.

## How it is obtained

Awarded for defeating a Leviathan at sea, or found in a level 6 or 7 treasure map chest. The artifact appears directly in the backpack.

## Properties

A light source with no combat properties - pure decoration.

## Notes

Part of the seafaring minor set.

For the broader artifact picture, see [Artifacts](../../items/artifacts.md) and the
[Doom Gauntlet majors](../doom-artifacts/).

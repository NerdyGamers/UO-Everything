# Traditional Minor Artifacts

The traditional minor artifacts are the original pre-rarity artifact set: they are so named because
they were the first artifacts available without the Artifact Rarity label. They predate the
[Doom Gauntlet majors](../doom-artifacts/) and are generally less powerful, though some -
particularly the two Leviathan exclusives - are far rarer and more valuable.

They come from three sources, and some artifacts are exclusive to certain sources:

- **Paragons** - roughly one in twenty non-champion monsters in Ilshenar spawns as an empowered
  Paragon; defeating one can place a minor artifact directly in your pack, with odds tied to the
  creature's fame and your Luck.
- **Leviathans** - the great sea monster summoned in deep water with a rare fabled fishing net;
  the caller has a 25% chance at an artifact and heavy damage-dealers can also be rewarded.
- **Treasure maps** - every level 6 (Ingeniously drawn) or level 7 (Diabolically drawn) chest
  holds a single traditional minor artifact.

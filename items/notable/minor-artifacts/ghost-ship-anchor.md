# Ghost Ship Anchor

A decorative anchor, one of the two Leviathan-specific traditional minor artifacts.

## How it is obtained

Awarded for defeating a Leviathan, the great sea monster summoned in deep water with a rare fabled fishing net. The fisher who calls the beast has a chance at an artifact, and those who deal heavy damage to it can also be rewarded; high Luck improves the odds. Like other minor artifacts, it is placed directly into the backpack rather than looted.

## Properties

No combat properties - pure decoration.

## Notes

The Ghost Ship Anchor and the Seahorse Statuette are the two artifacts exclusive to the Leviathan, making them extremely rare given how hard the nets are to come by. During the Spring Cleaning 2008 event they were worth 25,000 turn-in points, against 500 for any other traditional minor - fifty times the value.

For the broader artifact picture, see [Artifacts](../../items/artifacts.md) and the
[Doom Gauntlet majors](../doom-artifacts/).

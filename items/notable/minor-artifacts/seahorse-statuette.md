# Seahorse Statuette

A decorative seahorse statuette, one of the two Leviathan-specific traditional minor artifacts.

## How it is obtained

Awarded for defeating a Leviathan, the great sea monster summoned in deep water with a rare fabled fishing net. The fisher who calls the beast has a chance at an artifact, and those who deal heavy damage to it can also be rewarded; high Luck improves the odds. Like other minor artifacts, it is placed directly into the backpack rather than looted.

## Properties

No combat properties - pure decoration.

## Notes

The Seahorse Statuette and the [Ghost Ship Anchor](ghost-ship-anchor.md) are the two artifacts exclusive to the Leviathan, making them extremely rare. During the Spring Cleaning 2008 event they were worth 25,000 turn-in points, against 500 for any other traditional minor.

For the broader artifact picture, see [Artifacts](../../artifacts.md) and the
[Doom Gauntlet majors](../doom-artifacts/).

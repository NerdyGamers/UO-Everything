# Cavorting Club

A club minor artifact.

## How it is obtained

Awarded from any of the three traditional minor sources: defeating a Paragon in Ilshenar, fighting a Leviathan at sea, or digging up a level 6 or 7 treasure map chest. The artifact appears directly in the backpack.

## Properties

Community recollection holds hit lower defense.

## Notes

Obtainable from paragons, Leviathans, and treasure map chests alike.

For the broader artifact picture, see [Artifacts](../../items/artifacts.md) and the

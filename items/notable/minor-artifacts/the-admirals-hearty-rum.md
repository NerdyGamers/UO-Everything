# The Admiral's Hearty Rum

A bottle of rum minor artifact.

## How it is obtained

Awarded for defeating a Leviathan at sea, or found in a level 6 or 7 treasure map chest. The artifact appears directly in the backpack.

## Properties

Drinkable. Community recollection holds it grants a temporary strength buff when drunk.

## Notes

A fixture of the seafaring minor set alongside the [Candelabra of Souls](candelabra-of-souls.md) and [Captain Quacklebush's Cutlass](captain-quacklebushs-cutlass.md).

For the broader artifact picture, see [Artifacts](../../artifacts.md) and the
[Doom Gauntlet majors](../doom-artifacts/).

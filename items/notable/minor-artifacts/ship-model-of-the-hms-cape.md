# Ship Model of the H.M.S. Cape

A decorative model of the ship H.M.S. Cape.

## How it is obtained

Awarded for defeating a Leviathan at sea, or found in a level 6 or 7 treasure map chest. The artifact appears directly in the backpack.

## Properties

No combat properties - pure decoration.

## Notes

Part of the seafaring minor set.

For the broader artifact picture, see [Artifacts](../../artifacts.md) and the
[Doom Gauntlet majors](../doom-artifacts/).

# Orcish Visage

An orc helm minor artifact.

## How it is obtained

Awarded as a minor artifact for defeating a Paragon - the roughly one-in-twenty empowered form that non-champion monsters in Ilshenar can spawn as. The artifact appears directly in the pack, with the odds tied to the creature's fame and the slayer's Luck.

## Properties

Community recollection holds a strength bonus at the cost of intelligence.

## Notes

One of the original paragon minor artifacts from Publish 27 (2004).

For the broader artifact picture, see [Artifacts](../../items/artifacts.md) and the
[Doom Gauntlet majors](../doom-artifacts/).

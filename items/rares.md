# Rares Collecting

Rares are items valued not for their stats but for their scarcity and story: one-of-a-kind server artifacts, misprints, event prizes, and holiday gifts that can never be generated again.

## How it works

The classic categories:

- **Server births** — items that spawned once due to bugs or early-era mechanics and were never reproducible. The most famous and valuable rares in the game.
- **Event items** — prizes from EM-run events, invasions, and storyline arcs, often personalized or dated to the occasion.
- **Holiday gifts** — annual rares given out at holidays, collected year over year; complete year-sets command premiums.
- **Misprints** — items with wrong names, hues, or graphics from data errors, frozen in their mistaken state.

Collectors value provenance as much as the object: which shard and event it came from, who owned it, and whether its history is documented. Because rares can't be farmed, the market runs on reputation and auction rather than any fixed price list, and fakes are a perennial problem — experienced collectors verify hue, graphic, and history before paying.

Rares live outside the [loot system](loot-system.md) and the [property system](item-properties.md): their worth is cultural, not mechanical.

## Era notes

Server births mostly date to the game's earliest years, when looser data controls let unique items slip through. EM events and holiday gifts have kept the rares scene alive in every era since.

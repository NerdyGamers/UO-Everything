# Jewelry

Rings and bracelets carry no resists and no armor, yet they are often the most build-defining items a character wears. Jewelry is where the key caster and template properties concentrate.

## How it works

A character wears one ring and one bracelet. Magical jewelry rolls properties from the full [item property(item-properties.md) pool, and the most sought-after pieces stack several of the properties that are hard to fit elsewhere:

- **Faster Casting (FC)** — shortens spell cast times; capped, so jewelry is the usual place to reach it.
- **Faster Cast Recovery (FCR)** — shortens the recovery between spells.
- **Lower Mana Cost (LMC)** — reduces spell mana costs, stacking with armor.
- **Lower Reagent Cost (LRC)** — chance to cast without consuming reagents; a full LRC suit frees mages from carrying regs.
- **Skill bonuses** — jewelry commonly carries +skill on multiple skills, letting templates exceed 100 in key skills without power scrolls.
- **Stats** — Strength, Dexterity, and Intelligence bonuses round out suits.

Because rings and bracelets accept the same property pool as other gear but occupy slots with no defensive role, they become the flexible slots in suit construction: whatever a template is missing — cast speed, mana efficiency, skill points — jewelry supplies. High-end jewelry comes from [loot](loot-system.md), [runic crafting](../crafting/runic-tools.md), [reforging](../crafting/reforging.md), and [imbuing](../crafting/imbuing-system.md), and perfect pieces are among the most traded items in the game.

## Era notes

Faster Casting, FCR, LMC, and LRC are Age of Shadows-era properties (2003); jewelry's role as the caster's flexible slot dates to that system.

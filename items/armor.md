# Armor

Armor is worn in six body slots — head, neck (gorget), arms, gloves, chest, and legs — plus a shield slot. Every piece contributes resists, and armor sets define a character's defenses as much as their skills do.

## How it works

Armor comes in types with escalating protection: leather, studded leather, bone, chainmail, ringmail, and plate. Plate pieces carry the highest resists but require more [Strength](../skills/mechanics/../../mechanics/stats.md) and stamina to wear effectively; leather is light and favored by mages and archers. The five resists — [physical, fire, cold, poison, energy](../mechanics/resistances.md) — appear on each piece, and a suited character layers them toward the 70-per-type cap.

Key properties beyond raw resists:
- **Lower Mana Cost (LMC)** — reduces the mana each spell consumes; a cornerstone of mage suits.
- **Mage Armor** — lets armor be worn with full meditation and spellcasting effectiveness, enabling armored mages.
- **Luck** — influences loot quality while worn.
- Crafted armor can be [exceptional](../crafting/crafting-basics.md) and [enhanced](../crafting/enhancing.md); endgame pieces are often [runic](../crafting/runic-tools.md), [reforged](../crafting/reforging.md), or [imbued](../crafting/imbuing-system.md).

**Shields** occupy the hand slot and trade offense for defense: they contribute resists and the [Defense Chance Increase](../items/item-properties.md) (DCI) property, and they are the focus of the [Parrying](../skills/combat/parrying.md) skill.

## Era notes

Mage Armor and LMC are later-era additions layered onto the original resist-based armor system. Medable (meditation-friendly) armor predates the Mage Armor property.

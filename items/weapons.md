# Weapons

Weapons define a character's offense: their damage range, swing speed, and which [special moves](../mechanics/special-moves.md) they can perform. Each weapon belongs to a weapon skill — Swordsmanship, Fencing, Macing, Archery, or Throwing — and using it trains that skill.

## How it works

Weapon classes differ in damage, speed, and handedness. Swords tend toward balanced damage; fencing weapons are fast; macing weapons are slow and hard-hitting; bows and crossbows attack at range; thrown weapons (added with Stygian Abyss) need no ammunition. Base damage is modified by [Tactics](../skills/combat/tactics.md), [Anatomy](../skills/combat/anatomy.md), Strength, and the [Damage Increase](../items/item-properties.md) (DI) property.

Each weapon carries two [special moves](../mechanics/special-moves.md) — techniques like Armor Ignore, Mortal Strike, and Whirlwind — gated behind skill thresholds and costing mana. Magical weapons add properties: Hit Chance Increase, Swing Speed Increase, Hit Life/ Mana/ Stamina Leech, and [slayers](#slayers).

**Slayers** are weapons (and spellbooks) with bonus damage against creature families: silver (undead), reptilian slayer, arachnid doom, and so on. **Super slayers** cover broad families (e.g., Repond for humanoids, Undead for all undead); lesser slayers target specific creatures. Slayer weapons double damage against their targets (community recollection on the exact multiplier) and are essential for efficient farming and champion spawns.

## Era notes

Special moves and most item properties arrived with Age of Shadows (2003), transforming weapons from stat sticks into build-defining gear. Throwing and gargish weapons came with Stygian Abyss (2009).

# Item Properties

Magical properties are the modifiers that make one sword worth a fortune and another worth unraveling. Every magic item in Ultima Online is a bundle of named properties, each with an intensity range and a weight that counts against the item's total budget.

## How it works

The major properties fall into a few families. **Hit Chance Increase (HCI)** and **Defense Chance Increase (DCI)** improve the attacker's and defender's odds in the hit-chance contest (community recollection puts the player caps at 45% each). **Damage Increase (DI)** raises weapon damage up to a 100% cap. **Swing Speed Increase (SSI)** shortens swing delay (cap commonly cited as 60%). **Faster Casting** and **Faster Cast Recovery** speed up spellcasting (caps commonly cited as 4 and 6). **Lower Mana Cost (LMC)** reduces spell mana cost (40% cap); **Lower Reagent Cost (LRC)** reduces reagent consumption up to 100%. **Luck** improves loot rolls with diminishing returns and no hard cap. **Resists** add to the five armor resists, subject to the 70-point player cap per resist. Stat bonuses (Strength, Dexterity, Intelligence), hit-point/mana/stamina increases, and skill bonuses round out the common set.

Each property has an intensity range — a 40% DI ring is near the top of that property's range. Items also carry a total intensity budget: the more (and stronger) the properties, the higher the budget, which is why a five-property item with maxed stats is exponentially rarer than a two-property one (exact budget math is community-documented). Crafters manipulate this through [runic tools](../crafting/runic-tools.md), [enhancing](../crafting/enhancing.md), [reforging](../crafting/reforging.md), and the [imbuing system](../crafting/imbuing-system.md), which assigns each property a weight against a per-item cap.

## Era notes

The property system arrived with Age of Shadows (2003), which replaced the older magic-item scheme with the modern intensity-based one. Later eras added properties and refined caps, and luck's role in loot rolls grew over time.

# Artifacts

Artifacts are named, fixed-property items awarded for defeating the game's hardest content. Unlike crafted or imbued gear, an artifact's properties are set by design — what makes one desirable is the combination, not the roll.

## How it works

Artifact sources, roughly in historical order:

- **Doom Gauntlet artifacts** — the original artifact system. Clearing the rooms of the Doom Gauntlet and defeating its bosses awards artifacts like the Ornament of the Magician, the Ring of the Vile, and the Axe of the Heavens, each with a distinctive property package.
- **Peerless rewards** — [peerless encounters](../mechanics/peerless-encounters.md) drop ingredients and named rewards (e.g., Crimson Cincture from Dread Horn); some are assembled from keys and ingredients rather than dropped whole.
- **Champion spawn and event rewards** — later eras added artifacts to champ loot tables, seasonal events, and live arcs, broadening the pool well beyond Doom.
- **Rares** — distinct from artifacts: one-off or extremely limited items, often decorative, documented separately (see [rares](rares.md)).

Artifacts differ from player-made gear in kind: a [runic](../crafting/runic-tools.md) or [imbued](../crafting/imbuing-system.md) piece is customized to a template, while an artifact offers a fixed, often unique combination — some properties appear on artifacts that cannot be crafted or imbued at all. Many best-in-slot suits mix artifacts for their irreplaceable pieces with crafted gear for everything else. Because artifacts come from contested, difficult content, they are also among the most traded and counterfeited-adjacent items in the game's economy (verify provenance on high-value trades).

## Era notes

The Doom artifact system dates to the early 2000s; peerless and event artifacts accreted through Mondain's Legacy (2005) and every era since.

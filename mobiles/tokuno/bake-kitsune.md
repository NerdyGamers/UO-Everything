# Bake Kitsune

A fox spirit of Tokuno folklore given flesh, the bake kitsune is a swift, cunning predator wreathed in foxfire.

## Behavior
The bake kitsune fights with bites and searing flame, darting in and out of reach. It is a tamable creature, but only the most skilled tamers can bond with one. Tamed bake kitsune are prized for their speed and their fiery breath in combat.

## Habitat
Found in the wilderness of the [Tokuno Islands](../../locations/tokuno-islands.md), often near shrines and forested areas.

## Loot
Standard gold and Tokuno-area loot; nothing uniquely notable is widely documented.

# Deathwatch Beetle

A large, armored beetle native to Tokuno, named for the ticking sound it makes — an omen of death in island folklore.

## Behavior
The deathwatch beetle is a hardy melee fighter with a thick carapace that shrugs off physical blows. It is tamable, and tamed specimens serve as durable, if slow, mounts and fighters.

## Habitat
Found in caves and wilderness across the [Tokuno Islands](../../locations/tokuno-islands.md).

## Loot
Standard gold and low-tier loot; its carapace is not harvestable as a crafting material as far as community records show.

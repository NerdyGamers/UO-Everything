# Kappa

A turtle-like water imp of Tokuno folklore, the kappa lurks in rivers and coastal shallows.

## Behavior
Kappas are amphibious fighters that strike from the water's edge, using claws and a surprising turn of speed in their element. They are aggressive and often found in small groups.

## Habitat
Rivers, ponds, and shorelines of the [Tokuno Islands](../../locations/tokuno-islands.md).

## Loot
Standard low-tier gold and loot; no widely documented unique drops.

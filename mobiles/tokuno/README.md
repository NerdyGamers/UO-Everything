# Tokuno

The bestiary of the Tokuno Islands, drawn from Japanese yokai folklore and the island's martial traditions — fox spirits and snow maidens alongside dojo warriors and tribal miners. Several of its creatures are prized tamables, and its dungeons host the Treasures of Tokuno campaigns.

## Mobiles

- [Bake Kitsune](bake-kitsune.md) — tamable fox spirit wreathed in foxfire
- [Deathwatch Beetle](deathwatch-beetle.md) — tamable armored beetle of ill omen
- [Fan Dancer](fan-dancer.md) — bladedancer of the dojo, known for disarming foes
- [Hiryu](hiryu.md) — great draconic predator; a premier tamed fighter
- [Lesser Hiryu](lesser-hiryu.md) — smaller cousin of the hiryu, also tamable
- [Kappa](kappa.md) — amphibious water imp of rivers and shores
- [Oni](oni.md) — towering horned brute of mountain passes
- [Revenant Lion](revenant-lion.md) — undead spectral lion of haunted wilds
- [Rune Beetle](rune-beetle.md) — spellcasting beetle; a valued mage-support tame
- [Tsuki Wolf](tsuki-wolf.md) — tamable moon-pale pack hunter
- [Yomotsu Elder](yomotsu-elder.md) — spellcasting leader of the yomotsu tribes
- [Yomotsu Warrior](yomotsu-warrior.md) — tribal melee fighter of the Yomotsu Mines
- [Yomotsu Priest](yomotsu-priest.md) — curse-hurling spiritual caste of the yomotsu
- [Lady of the Snow](lady-of-the-snow.md) — yuki-onna boss atop the Fan Dancer Dojo

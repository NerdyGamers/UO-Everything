# Fan Dancer

Graceful bladedancers of the Tokuno dojos, the fan dancers wield razor-edged war fans with lethal precision.

## Behavior
Fan dancers are skilled melee combatants known for disarming their opponents mid-fight — losing your weapon to one is a common and dangerous surprise. They fight in groups and are aggressive toward intruders.

## Habitat
The Fan Dancer Dojo in the [Tokuno Islands](../../locations/tokuno-islands.md), where they guard the halls leading to deeper chambers.

## Loot
Gold and occasional Tokuno-themed items; their war fans are sometimes recovered as decorative pieces.

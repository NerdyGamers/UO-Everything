# Tsuki Wolf

A moon-pale wolf of the Tokuno highlands, the tsuki wolf hunts in packs under the island moon.

## Behavior
Tsuki wolves are fast pack hunters that use bites and numbers to bring down prey. They are tamable and make swift, loyal hunting companions.

## Habitat
Highlands and forests of the [Tokuno Islands](../../locations/tokuno-islands.md).

## Loot
Standard low-tier loot; hides and meat where applicable. More commonly tamed than killed.

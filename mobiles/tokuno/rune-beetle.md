# Rune Beetle

An intelligent, spellcasting beetle whose carapace bears natural runic markings, the rune beetle is a prized tame.

## Behavior
Unlike its purely physical cousins, the rune beetle casts offensive spells and can corrupt an opponent's defenses, weakening them for follow-up attacks. It is tamable by skilled tamers and valued as a mage-support pet.

## Habitat
Caves and dungeons of the [Tokuno Islands](../../locations/tokuno-islands.md).

## Loot
Standard loot for its tier; more often tamed than killed.

# Lesser Hiryu

A smaller cousin of the great hiryu, the lesser hiryu is still a formidable draconic predator.

## Behavior
Like its larger kin, the lesser hiryu fights with claws and flame breath. It is tamable at a lower skill threshold than the full hiryu, making it an accessible stepping stone for tamers working toward the greater beast.

## Habitat
Wilderness areas of the [Tokuno Islands](../../locations/tokuno-islands.md), sometimes ranging near hiryu territory.

## Loot
Standard loot for its tier; more commonly tamed than killed.

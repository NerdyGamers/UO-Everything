# Lady of the Snow

A powerful yuki-onna — a snow spirit of Tokuno legend — the Lady of the Snow rules the heights of the Fan Dancer Dojo.

## Behavior
The Lady of the Snow is a boss-tier encounter fought at the top of the dojo. She commands ice and cold, striking with freezing magic, and is far deadlier than the fan dancers below. Parties should prepare cold resistance and expect a hard fight.

## Habitat
The summit of the Fan Dancer Dojo in the [Tokuno Islands](../../locations/tokuno-islands.md).

## Loot
Boss-tier loot; exact drops are community-documented — verify in game before farming.

# Yomotsu Elder

The aged leaders of the yomotsu tribes, elders command respect — and magic — among their kind.

## Behavior
Yomotsu elders supplement their tribe's warriors with spellcasting, hurling offensive magic at intruders while their kin close to melee. They are typically found deeper in yomotsu territory, guarded by warriors.

## Habitat
The Yomotsu Mines in the [Tokuno Islands](../../locations/tokuno-islands.md).

## Loot
Gold, reagents, and occasional tribal trinkets. Drop details are community-documented — verify in game.

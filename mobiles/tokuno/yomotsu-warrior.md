# Yomotsu Warrior

Stocky, goblin-like tribal fighters, yomotsu warriors defend their mines with crude but effective ferocity.

## Behavior
Yomotsu warriors are aggressive melee fighters that swarm intruders in numbers. Individually they are unremarkable; in a pack, backed by priests and elders, they are dangerous.

## Habitat
The Yomotsu Mines in the [Tokuno Islands](../../locations/tokuno-islands.md).

## Loot
Gold and low-tier loot; tribal weapons and trinkets are sometimes recovered.

# Yomotsu Priest

The spiritual caste of the yomotsu tribes, priests tend their kin's dark rites and hurl curses at invaders.

## Behavior
Yomotsu priests fight primarily with magic, supporting warriors and elders with offensive spells. Like the elders, they are usually found deeper within yomotsu-held areas, shielded by warriors.

## Habitat
The Yomotsu Mines in the [Tokuno Islands](../../locations/tokuno-islands.md).

## Loot
Gold, reagents, and occasional tribal items. Drop details are community-documented — verify in game.

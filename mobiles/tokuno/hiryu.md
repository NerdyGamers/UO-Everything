# Hiryu

A great draconic predator of the Tokuno wilds, the hiryu is among the most powerful creatures a tamer can bond with.

## Behavior
The hiryu attacks with claws, teeth, and a gout of flame. It is tamable only by grandmaster-level tamers and is widely regarded as one of the premier tamed fighters — strong, fast, and able to take punishment.

## Habitat
Remote wilderness regions of the [Tokuno Islands](../../locations/tokuno-islands.md).

## Loot
As a tamable predator, it is more often tamed than slain; slain hiryu yield standard high-tier loot.

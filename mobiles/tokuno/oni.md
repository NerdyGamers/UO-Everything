# Oni

A towering horned ogre-demon of Tokuno legend, the oni is one of the islands' most feared brutes.

## Behavior
Oni rely on overwhelming physical force — massive club-like blows that can crush armor and bone. They are tough, hit hard, and shrug off weaker attacks. Fighters should expect a long, punishing melee.

## Habitat
Remote wilderness and mountain passes of the [Tokuno Islands](../../locations/tokuno-islands.md).

## Loot
Gold and mid-to-high-tier loot; oni are sometimes farmed for Tokuno-area drops. Exact drop tables are community-documented — verify in game.

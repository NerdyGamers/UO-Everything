# Revenant Lion

The vengeful spirit of a slain lion, the revenant lion stalks Tokuno as an undead predator.

## Behavior
Revenant lions fight with spectral claws and teeth, moving with unnatural speed. As undead, they are subject to the usual banes of the unliving. They are aggressive and territorial.

## Habitat
Haunted wilds and ruins of the [Tokuno Islands](../../locations/tokuno-islands.md).

## Loot
Standard undead loot — gold, gems, and reagents. No widely documented unique drops.

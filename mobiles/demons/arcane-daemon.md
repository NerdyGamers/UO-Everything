# Arcane Daemon

A daemon of the Prism of Light, wreathed in crystalline arcane energy rather than hellfire. Where common demons burn, the arcane daemon refracts.

## Behavior

Casts arcane-flavored offensive magic and fights with the strength of a greater demon — tougher than a common [Demon](demon.md). Aggressive toward intruders in its crystalline halls. The [Demon Slayer](../../items/item-properties.md) property applies.

## Habitat

The Prism of Light dungeon, among the crystal formations.

## Loot

Community recollection associates them with crystal and arcane-themed drops; verify in game.

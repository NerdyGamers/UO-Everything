# Imp

A small, mischievous lesser demon. Weak individually, but imps rarely travel alone and their spellcasting makes them a genuine nuisance in groups.

## Behavior

Casts low-level offensive spells from a distance and nips at close range. Aggressive but fragile — most adventurers swat them aside, though a swarm can still surprise the careless. [Demon Slayer](../../items/item-properties.md) weapons apply.

## Habitat

Upper dungeon levels: Despise, Covetous, and similar shallow haunts.

## Loot

Very little — small amounts of gold and the occasional trinket.

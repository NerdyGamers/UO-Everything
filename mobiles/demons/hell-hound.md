# Hell Hound

A demonic canine wreathed in flame, best known for the gout of fire it breathes at its prey.

## Behavior

Bites and breathes fire, and tends to hunt in packs, so one hell hound often means several. Aggressive; the fire breath makes them disproportionately dangerous for their size. [Demon Slayer](../../items/item-properties.md) applies.

## Habitat

The Fire Dungeon, Hythloth, and other volcanic or infernal regions.

## Loot

Modest gold and standard dungeon loot.

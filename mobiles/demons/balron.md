# Balron

A greater demon — larger, tougher, and far more dangerous than the common [Demon](demon.md). One of the classic high-end predators of the deep dungeons.

## Behavior

Casts powerful offensive spells and strikes with devastating melee attacks. Aggressive and relentless; a serious threat even to well-equipped groups. The [Demon Slayer](../../items/item-properties.md) property applies.

## Habitat

Destard, Hythloth, and other deep dungeon reaches where the strongest creatures lair.

## Loot

Gold and magic items, fitting its station; hunted as much for the challenge as the reward.

# Demons

Horned, winged, and ill-tempered: Britannia's demons range from the mischievous imp to the mighty balron. What they share is aggression, a taste for spellcasting, and vulnerability to the **Demon Slayer** weapon property — the standard answer to anything with horns. They haunt the world's infernal corners: Hythloth, the Fire Temple, the Doom Gauntlet, and the Stygian Abyss.

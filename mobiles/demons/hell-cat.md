# Hell Cat

A small demonic feline with a fiery temperament — and one of the few demons that can be tamed, making it a prized pet for tamers who want something with claws and attitude.

## Behavior

Fast and aggressive in the wild, striking quickly at intruders. Tamed specimens make fierce, loyal pets; see [Animal Taming](../../skills/wilderness/animal-taming.md) for the requirements, which are steep. [Demon Slayer](../../items/item-properties.md) weapons apply against wild ones.

## Habitat

Volcanic and infernal areas; related predator hellcats prowl the Twisted Weald (per community recollection).

## Loot

Minimal — hell cats are hunted for taming, not treasure.

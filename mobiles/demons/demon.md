# Demon

The classic red-skinned demon of Britannia's dungeons: a horned, winged spellcaster with a barbed tail, and the archetype the whole family is measured against.

## Behavior

Fights with a mix of heavy melee attacks and offensive spells, and is aggressive toward anything that wanders into its territory. Dangerous to unprepared travelers but a routine hunt for equipped warriors. Weapons with the [Demon Slayer](../../items/item-properties.md) property are especially effective.

## Habitat

Deeper dungeon levels — Hythloth, the Fire Temple, and other infernal corners of the world, usually near lava flows and demonic architecture.

## Loot

Gold and standard dungeon loot; magic equipment on occasion.

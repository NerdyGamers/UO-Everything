# Gargoyle Assassin

A stealthy gargoyle killer that stalks the shadows of the Stygian Abyss.

## Behavior

Assassins use hiding and stealth to approach unseen, then strike with the race's throwing weapons and quick melee. They are fragile compared to gargoyle destroyers but dangerous for the surprise of their opening attack. Community recollection notes poisoned or otherwise enhanced strikes, though details vary.

## Habitat

The Stygian Abyss dungeon. See [Dungeons](../../locations/dungeons.md).

## Loot

Carried weapons and gear; nothing uniquely notable is reliably documented.

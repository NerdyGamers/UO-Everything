# Gargoyle

The winged humanoid race native to Ter Mur, met both as NPCs and as hostile warriors in the Stygian Abyss.

## Behavior

Gargoyles fight at range with boomerang-like throwing weapons and cast mysticism spells. They can fly, letting them cross terrain other fighters cannot. Hostile gargoyles are aggressive and often appear in groups, supporting each other with spells.

## Habitat

Ter Mur and the Stygian Abyss dungeon. See [Ter Mur](../../locations/termur.md) and [Dungeons](../../locations/dungeons.md).

## Loot

Carried throwing weapons and armor, plus the usual coin and reagents of a humanoid foe.

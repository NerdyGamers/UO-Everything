# Unbound Energy Vortex

A wild, masterless energy vortex crackling through the Stygian Abyss.

## Behavior

Like the summoned spell of the same name, it lashes out with bolts of raw energy at anything nearby. Being unbound, it has no master to call it off — community recollection describes it as aggressive and indiscriminate.

## Habitat

The Stygian Abyss dungeon. See [Dungeons](../../locations/dungeons.md).

## Loot

None reliably documented.

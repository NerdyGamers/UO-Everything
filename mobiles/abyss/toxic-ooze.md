# Toxic Ooze

A poisonous slime reported in the depths of the Stygian Abyss.

## Behavior

Like other oozes it engulfs and corrodes, and its touch carries a venomous bite. Community recollection places it among the Abyss's mid-tier hazards — slow, but punishing to the careless.

## Habitat

The Stygian Abyss dungeon. See [Dungeons](../../locations/dungeons.md).

## Loot

Nothing reliably documented beyond standard ooze loot.

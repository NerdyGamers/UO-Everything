# Gargoyle Destroyer

A hulking gargoyle champion of the Stygian Abyss, far stronger than the common gargoyle warriors found near Ter Mur.

## Behavior

Destroyers close to melee and hit extremely hard, shrugging off damage that would drop lesser gargoyles. They retain the race's throwing attacks and mysticism, making them dangerous at any range. Community recollection places them among the toughest standard spawns in the Abyss.

## Habitat

The deeper levels of the Stygian Abyss dungeon. See [Dungeons](../../locations/dungeons.md).

## Loot

Richer humanoid loot in line with their strength; community reports mention gargoyle equipment and valuables.

# Fire Gargoyle

A gargoyle wreathed in flame, found in the volcanic reaches of the Stygian Abyss.

## Behavior

Fire gargoyles combine the race's throwing attacks with fire-based assaults, and their strikes carry a burning edge. As community recollection has it, they are among the more punishing mid-tier spawns of the Abyss's fire regions.

## Habitat

The volcanic areas of the Stygian Abyss dungeon. See [Dungeons](../../locations/dungeons.md).

## Loot

Standard gargoyle loot; nothing uniquely notable is reliably documented.

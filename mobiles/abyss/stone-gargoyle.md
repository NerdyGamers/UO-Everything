# Stone Gargoyle

A hulking, stone-skinned gargoyle guardian reported in the Stygian Abyss.

## Behavior

Stone gargoyles are slow but enormously durable, shrugging off blows that would fell flesh-and-blood foes. Community recollection describes them as straightforward melee brutes — little subtlety, a great deal of staying power.

## Habitat

The Stygian Abyss dungeon. See [Dungeons](../../locations/dungeons.md).

## Loot

Nothing reliably documented beyond standard loot for a creature of its strength.

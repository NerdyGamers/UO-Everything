# Acid Elemental

A corrosive elemental of the Stygian Abyss that hurls burning acid at its foes.

## Behavior

Acid elementals bombard targets with acid bolts and leave searing pools where their attacks land. Standing in the acid is punishing, so fights against them are about positioning as much as damage. They are tough, high-end spawns and not to be underestimated.

## Habitat

The Stygian Abyss dungeon. See [Dungeons](../../locations/dungeons.md).

## Loot

Standard elemental loot — gems and reagents are the usual take; no signature drop is reliably documented.

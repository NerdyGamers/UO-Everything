# Enslaved Gargoyle

A gargoyle held in bondage within the Stygian Abyss, forced to labor or fight for its captors.

## Behavior

Enslaved gargoyles fight like their free kin — throwing weapons and mysticism — but are generally weaker and less organized. In-fiction they are captives of the abyssal powers, a detail community retellings tie to the faction strife of the Stygian Abyss storyline.

## Habitat

The Stygian Abyss dungeon. See [Dungeons](../../locations/dungeons.md).

## Loot

Modest humanoid loot; little of note beyond carried gear.

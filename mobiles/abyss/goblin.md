# Goblin

Small, cunning humanoid raiders found in the Stygian Abyss.

## Behavior

Goblins attack in packs, using numbers and quick strikes rather than individual strength. They are cowardly alone but bold in groups, and will swarm an isolated target.

## Habitat

The Stygian Abyss dungeon. See [Dungeons](../../locations/dungeons.md).

## Loot

Meager pickings — coin and trinkets in line with their size.

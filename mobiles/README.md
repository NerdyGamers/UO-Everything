# Mobiles

Every creature, monster, and boss of Britannia and beyond — organized by family. Tamable creatures note it in their article; bosses live under [Bosses](bosses/peerless/README.md).

- [Undead](undead/README.md) — skeletons, liches, wraiths, and the skeletal dragon
- [Demons](demons/README.md) — demons, balrons, imps, hell hounds, and the arcane daemon
- [Animals](animals/README.md) — bears, wolves, deer, farm animals, and the horse
- [Reptiles and Arachnids](reptiles/README.md) — spiders, serpents, drakes, dragons, and wyrms
- [Elementals](elementals/README.md) — the summoned and wild elementals, plus the efreet
- [Fey and Magical Beasts](fey/README.md) — wisps, pixies, centaurs, cu sidhe, kirin, and unicorns
- [Humanoids](humanoids/README.md) — orcs, giants, ratmen, juka and meer, ophidians and terathans, savages
- [Tokuno](tokuno/README.md) — hiryu, oni, yomotsu, and the yokai of the Tokuno Islands
- [The Abyss](abyss/README.md) — gargoyles and the creatures of Ter Mur and the Stygian Abyss
- [Sea](sea/README.md) — krakens, leviathans, serpents, and pirates
- [Peerless Bosses](bosses/peerless/README.md) — the keyed boss encounters
- [Champion Spawn Bosses](bosses/champions/README.md) — the champion spawn bosses and the Harrower

# Dread Spider

A far larger and more venomous cousin of the giant spider, the dread spider is one of the most dangerous arachnids in the world.

## Behavior
Dread spiders hit hard and deliver a potent poison with their bite. They are aggressive and quick, and hunters are advised to carry greater cure potions. They are reported as **tamable** at high [Animal Taming](../../skills/wilderness/animal-taming.md) skill, making them a prestige pet for dedicated tamers — community recollection; verify current requirements in game.

## Habitat
Found in the deep Underworld and around the Prism of Light, among other high-end hunting grounds.

## Loot
Beyond standard gold and reagents, dread spiders are hunted for the challenge; no signature artifact is associated with them.

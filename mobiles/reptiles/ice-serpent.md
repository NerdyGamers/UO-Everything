# Ice Serpent

A serpent of the frozen wastes whose bite carries the chill of the deep cold.

## Behavior
Ice serpents attack with fangs and cold-based attacks, and resist frost. They are aggressive pack hunters in icy regions. Tamability is uncertain — verify in game (community recollection).

## Habitat
Frozen dungeons and the ice-bound wilderness of the far north.

## Loot
Ice serpents drop modest gold and gems; they are a hazard of cold-weather hunting rather than a prized target.

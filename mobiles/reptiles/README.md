# Reptiles and Arachnids

Scaled, fanged, and eight-legged: the dragons, serpents, and arachnids of the world. Most of these creatures fall under the **Reptilian Death** slayer group, and the dragons among them are the crown jewels of [Animal Taming](../../skills/wilderness/animal-taming.md) — from the humble drake to the mighty greater dragon.

# Giant Beetle

A massive beetle prized by travelers as a beast of burden rather than feared as a predator.

## Behavior
Giant beetles are aggressive in the wild but become famously useful once tamed. They are **tamable** and widely used as pack animals: items placed in a tamed beetle's pack weigh significantly less, making them a favorite of miners, lumberjacks, and treasure hunters. They can also be ridden.

## Habitat
Found in wilderness areas across the facets; tamers seek them wherever beetles spawn in numbers.

## Loot
Wild beetles carry little of note; their value is as a tamed companion, not as a hunt target.

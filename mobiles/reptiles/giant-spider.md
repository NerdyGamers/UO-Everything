# Giant Spider

A large aggressive arachnid whose venomous bite makes it a staple of low- and mid-level hunting grounds.

## Behavior
Giant spiders attack on sight with a melee bite that can poison. They are fast for their size and often hunt in packs near their nests. They are **tamable**, and tamed specimens are sometimes kept as guards or novelty pets rather than serious combat companions.

## Habitat
Found in spider caves and dungeons across the facets, most famously the spider rooms of [Destard](../../locations/dungeons.md). They also appear in wilderness forests.

## Loot
Giant spiders carry little of value beyond what they were carrying in life; hunters visit them mainly for the sport and for spider silk in some eras.

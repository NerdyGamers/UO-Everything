# White Wyrm

A frost-breathing wyrm of the frozen wastes, as beautiful as it is deadly.

## Behavior
White wyrms attack with claws and a blast of freezing breath. They are **tamable** at high [Animal Taming](../../skills/wilderness/animal-taming.md) skill, and are prized by tamers who want a dragon with a cold theme. In the wild they are a serious hunt.

## Habitat
Icy regions and cold dungeons, including the ice-bound hunting grounds of the far north.

## Loot
White wyrms drop gold, gems, and magic items; their scales are sought by crafters.

# Drake

A lesser dragon — smaller than a true dragon but still a fire-breathing menace to the unprepared.

## Behavior
Drakes attack with claws, bite, and a gout of flame. They are a classic mid-tier hunting target and a stepping stone for tamers: they are **tamable** at moderate [Animal Taming](../../skills/wilderness/animal-taming.md) skill, making them a popular first dragon for aspiring tamers.

## Habitat
Dragon lairs and volcanic wilderness, most famously the upper levels of [Destard](../../locations/dungeons.md).

## Loot
Drakes carry gold and gems, and occasionally magic items; they are also a source of dragon scales for [tailors](../../skills/crafting/tailoring.md) in some eras.

# Greater Dragon

The apex of dragonkind — larger, tougher, and more destructive than any ordinary dragon.

## Behavior
Greater dragons possess enormous health, crushing melee attacks, and a fearsome fire breath. They are **tamable**, but only by grandmaster-level tamers, and a tamed greater dragon is widely regarded as the most powerful tamable pet in the game. Hunting one wild is an endgame undertaking.

## Habitat
The deepest reaches of [Destard](../../locations/dungeons.md) and other high-end dragon lairs.

## Loot
Greater dragons drop rich hoards of gold, gems, and high-end magic items, befitting their status.

# Lava Serpent

A serpent wreathed in flame, at home in volcanic caverns.

## Behavior
Lava serpents attack with fangs and searing heat, and are resistant to fire. They are aggressive and often found near lava flows. Tamability is uncertain — community recollection varies, so verify in game before attempting.

## Habitat
Volcanic dungeons and lava-filled caverns.

## Loot
Lava serpents drop modest gold and gems; they are hunted mainly by travelers passing through their territory.

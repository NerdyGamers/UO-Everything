# Shadow Wyrm

A rare wyrm wreathed in shadow, darker and more sinister than its kin.

## Behavior
Shadow wyrms fight like ancient wyrms but with a shadowy aspect; they are exceptionally tough and aggressive. They are **not tamable**. Their rarity makes encountering one a notable event for dragon hunters.

## Habitat
Rare spawns in deep dragon lairs, reported in the depths of [Destard](../../locations/dungeons.md) (community recollection).

## Loot
Shadow wyrms drop rich hoards comparable to ancient wyrms.

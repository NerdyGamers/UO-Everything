# Dragon

The iconic fire-breathing dragon of Britannia — powerful, proud, and the benchmark against which tamers measure themselves.

## Behavior
Dragons fight with rending claws and a devastating breath of flame, and can take a great deal of punishment. They are **tamable**, but only by tamers of high skill, and a tamed dragon remains one of the most respected combat pets. Wild dragons are a serious hunt requiring preparation and often a group.

## Habitat
The deep chambers of [Destard](../../locations/dungeons.md), the dragon lands of the Lost Lands, and other volcanic lairs.

## Loot
Dragons hoard gold, gems, and magic items; their scales are prized by armor crafters.

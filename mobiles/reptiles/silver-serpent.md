# Silver Serpent

A swift, gleaming serpent known for its speed and its venomous bite.

## Behavior
Silver serpents are fast and aggressive, striking with fangs that can poison. They are dangerous in numbers and best handled with cure potions ready. Tamability is uncertain — verify in game (community recollection).

## Habitat
Most famously the serpent-infested depths of [Covetous](../../locations/dungeons.md).

## Loot
Silver serpents drop gold and gems; Covetous hunters clear them for the dungeon's richer prizes beyond.

# Ancient Wyrm

An elder dragon of immense age and power, dwelling in the deepest dragon lairs.

## Behavior
Ancient wyrms are among the most dangerous single creatures in the game: tremendous health, brutal melee, and searing fire breath. They are **not tamable**. Hunters typically tackle them in groups with slayer weapons and heavy preparation.

## Habitat
The lowest depths of [Destard](../../locations/dungeons.md).

## Loot
Ancient wyrms guard some of the richest standard loot in the game — large gold piles, gems, and powerful magic items.

# Scorpion

A desert-dwelling arachnid whose stinging tail delivers a painful poison.

## Behavior
Scorpions attack with claws and a venomous tail sting. They are moderately tough and often found in groups. They are **tamable**, and make serviceable low-level pets for new tamers.

## Habitat
Arid regions, notably the deserts of the Lost Lands and the lands around Terathan Keep.

## Loot
Scorpions carry little beyond modest gold; they are hunted mainly by tamers and travelers passing through their territory.

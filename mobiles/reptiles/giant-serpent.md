# Giant Serpent

An enormous constrictor snake, the mundane cousin of the magical serpents.

## Behavior
Giant serpents attack with crushing bites and are a solid low- to mid-level hunt. They are **tamable** at low [Animal Taming](../../skills/wilderness/animal-taming.md) skill, making them an easy early pet for new tamers.

## Habitat
Jungles, swamps, and dungeon wilderness areas across the facets.

## Loot
Giant serpents carry little of value; they are hunted for training and by tamers seeking an easy tame.

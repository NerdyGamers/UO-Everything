# Serpentine Dragon

A sinuous dragon of the Stygian Abyss, distinct from the winged dragons of the surface world.

## Behavior
Serpentine dragons are fast, aggressive, and hit hard with bite and breath attacks. They are **not tamable** (community recollection). They are typically hunted by parties exploring the Abyss.

## Habitat
The Stygian Abyss, in the gargoyle homelands of [Ter Mur](../../locations/termur.md).

## Loot
Serpentine dragons drop gold, gems, and magic items appropriate to an Abyss-level hunt.

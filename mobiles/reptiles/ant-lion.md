# Ant Lion

A burrowing insectoid ambusher that lurks near Solen ant colonies.

## Behavior
Ant lions burst from the ground to attack prey, fighting with powerful mandibles. They are sturdy mid-level opponents. They are reported as **tamable** (community recollection), though they are an uncommon choice of pet.

## Habitat
Found around Solen hives and in sandy wilderness areas where the ant colonies tunnel.

## Loot
Ant lions drop modest gold and occasionally the Solen-related items of their territory; they are hunted mainly by travelers crossing hive lands.

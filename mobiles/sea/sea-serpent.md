# Sea Serpent

The classic terror of Britannia's fishermen — a large, aggressive serpent pulled from the deep.

## Behavior

Aggressive on sight. Sea serpents are closely tied to sunken treasure: fishing up a message in a bottle and hauling in the chest frequently brings a serpent up with it, and they are known to guard wreck sites.

## Habitat

Deep water, well away from shore, and around shipwreck treasure locations.

## Loot

Gold, gems, and magic items. Serpents pulled up with bottled treasure may carry treasure maps or guard the sunken chest.

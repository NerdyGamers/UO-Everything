# Sea Mobiles

The creatures and seafarers of Britannia's oceans — serpents, krakens, leviathans, and the pirates who sail among them.

## Sea combat

Fighting at sea means fighting from ships: High Seas-era vessels carry cannons, and many sea monsters are fished up from deep water rather than found on land. Bring a boat, watch your footing, and expect the water itself to fight back. See also [High Seas](../../lore/08-high-seas/overview.md).

# Leviathan

A boss-tier sea monster introduced with the High Seas booster, summoned by fishermen using special fishing nets.

## Behavior

The leviathan never comes alone: it surfaces with a pack of spawn — krakens, sea serpents, and water elementals — making the fight a group effort or a serious challenge for a well-prepared solo hunter. It attacks with blasts of water. On rare occasions the ancient Scalis appears in its place, a still tougher encounter.

## Habitat

The open ocean. Leviathans are summoned by throwing a special fishing net, itself fished up from the sea.

## Loot

Magic items, gems, and gold, plus rare decorative drops — the ghost ship anchor and the seahorse statuette are community-documented as extremely rare finds.

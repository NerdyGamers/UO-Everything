# Deep Sea Serpent

A larger, tougher cousin of the common sea serpent, found only in the deepest waters.

## Behavior

Like the sea serpent but significantly stronger — a genuine threat to fishermen and a respectable fight for hunters. Aggressive on sight.

## Habitat

The deepest ocean water, fished up far from any shore.

## Loot

Gold, gems, and magic items, generally richer than the common serpent's haul.

# Pirate

Hostile human seafarers found aboard the pirate ships that sail Britannia's oceans.

## Behavior

Pirates attack on sight, defending their vessels with melee weapons. Pirate captains are tougher versions that lead the crew. Fighting pirates is a staple of High Seas ship combat — expect to deal with the ship and its defenders together.

## Habitat

Pirate ships on the open sea.

## Loot

Gold, gems, and magic items taken from the crew's plunder.

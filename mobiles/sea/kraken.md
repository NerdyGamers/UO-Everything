# Kraken

One of the most dangerous creatures in Britannia's oceans — a giant, tentacled beast that deep-water fishermen pull up with their lines.

## Behavior

Extremely aggressive and hard-hitting, striking with powerful tentacle attacks. It carries high fame and karma, and it cannot be tamed. Krakens are often found in the company of lesser sea serpents when they surface.

## Habitat

Deep ocean water, well away from shore. Most commonly encountered when fished up by fishermen working far out at sea.

## Loot

Gold, gems, and magic items.

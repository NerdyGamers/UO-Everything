# Medusa

Medusa is a Peerless boss encounter of the Stygian Abyss — the gorgon of myth, serpent-haired and lethal, lairing in the dungeon's depths.

## The encounter
Medusa's lair lies within the Stygian Abyss dungeon, reached through the Underworld or the Tomb of Kings. She was added with the Stygian Abyss expansion. Keys are gathered from the Abyss's inhabitants, per community recollection. See [Peerless Encounters](../../../mechanics/peerless-encounters.md) and [Ter Mur](../../../locations/termur.md).

## Behavior
Medusa is known for her petrifying gaze — meeting her eyes can turn an attacker to stone, so the fight demands careful positioning. She is also a dangerous spellcaster and melee combatant in her own right. Ranged attackers who can strike without meeting her gaze fare best, per community recollection.

## Loot
Abyss peerless ingredients and artifacts.

# Shimmering Effusion

The Shimmering Effusion is the Peerless boss of the Prism of Light — a fascinated creature of light and reflection dwelling in the dungeon's crystalline caves.

## The encounter
The Prism of Light is entered magically, and its keys are widely considered the hardest of all the Peerless key sets to collect. The effusion waits past wisps and shadow wisps, guarded by Fetid Essences, Corporeal Brumes, and Mantra Effervescences. See [Peerless Encounters](../../../mechanics/peerless-encounters.md) and [Dungeons](../../../locations/dungeons.md).

## Behavior
A slow, grinding fight — the effusion absorbs punishment while its attendants harass the party. Community reports describe the key run, not the boss itself, as the true challenge of this encounter.

## Loot
Peerless ingredients, including crystals used in making soulstone fragments (per community reports), plus a rare chance at a ferret imprisoned in a crystal — a bonded, fully trained ferret.

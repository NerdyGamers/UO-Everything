# Dread Horn

Dread Horn is the Peerless boss of the Twisted Weald in Ilshenar — a corrupted, powerful equine-like beast dwelling on an island just west of the weald's swamp.

## The encounter
Six keys are required: Blighted Cotton, Thorny Briar, Gnaw's Fang, Irk's Brain, Lissith's Silk, and Sabrix's Eye. Four come from the weald's named mini-bosses — Sabrix and Lissith (spiders), Irk (a changeling), and Gnaw (a dire wolf) — while the cotton and briar are picked up from the ground. The keys are dropped on the Statue of the Fey and exchanged for the master key, Essence of Wind, which carries a short timer. Using it transports the party to Dread Horn's island, where they have two hours to kill him before he vanishes. See [Peerless Encounters](../../../mechanics/peerless-encounters.md) and [Ilshenar](../../../locations/ilshenar.md).

## Behavior
Dread Horn fights with relentless, constant poisoning — the steady poison damage can make it difficult to keep pets healed through the fight. He is fast and punishing at close range.

## Loot
Peerless ingredients and artifacts, plus a rare chance at pet statuettes or power crystals.

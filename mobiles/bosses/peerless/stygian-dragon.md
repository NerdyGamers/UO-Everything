# Stygian Dragon

The Stygian Dragon is a Peerless boss of the Stygian Abyss — an enormous dragon dwelling beyond a sealed temple in the dungeon's depths.

## The encounter
Entry requires four Draconic Orbs, which drop from the fairy dragons near the Stygian Dragon gate; one orb is sacrificed at each of four altars in the temple to the west, opening a gate to the dragon's chamber. See [Peerless Encounters](../../../mechanics/peerless-encounters.md) and [Ter Mur](../../../locations/termur.md).

## Behavior
The dragon opens with a terror attack like a crimson dragon's and paralyzes attackers who close in. It moves fast, strikes for heavy damage even through strong suits, and quickly dispels or kills summoned creatures. Community reports describe the encounter as time-limited — linger too long and the dragon simply vanishes.

## Loot
Abyss peerless ingredients and artifacts.

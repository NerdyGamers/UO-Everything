# Chief Paroxysmus

Chief Paroxysmus is the Peerless boss of Paroxysmus' Lair in Ilshenar — a massive ratman chieftain.

## The encounter
Keys are gathered from the lizardmen and ratmen infesting the lair and its approaches, per community recollection, then exchanged for the master key to his chamber. The encounter dates to the Mondain's Legacy expansion. See [Peerless Encounters](../../../mechanics/peerless-encounters.md) and [Ilshenar](../../../locations/ilshenar.md).

## Behavior
Paroxysmus is infamous for devouring nearby creatures — including players' pets — to restore his own health, so the standard advice is to stable mounts and leave pets behind. He hits brutally hard in melee.

## Loot
Peerless ingredients and artifacts, plus a rare chance at a Paroxysmus Swamp Dragon Statuette — a bonded, fully trained swamp dragon with unbreakable armor.

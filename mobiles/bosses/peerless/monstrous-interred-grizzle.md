# Monstrous Interred Grizzle

The Monstrous Interred Grizzle is the Peerless boss of Bedlam in Malas — a reanimated monstrosity stitched together by the necromancers of the former academy, who are still struggling to bring their creation back under control.

## The encounter
Bedlam was once a prestigious academy for necromancers; now its halls hold bone knights, patchwork skeletons, rotting corpses, and gore fiends. Keys for the Grizzle are gathered from the dungeon's undead, per community recollection. The encounter dates to the Mondain's Legacy expansion. See [Peerless Encounters](../../../mechanics/peerless-encounters.md), [Malas](../../../locations/malas.md), and [Dungeons](../../../locations/dungeons.md).

## Behavior
A massive undead bruiser — slow but devastating in melee, backed by the dungeon's necromancers and their minions.

## Loot
Peerless ingredients and artifacts, plus a rare chance at a Grizzled Mare Statuette — a bonded, fully trained skeletal steed that does not decay.

# Navrey Night-Eyes

Navrey Night-Eyes is a Peerless boss of the Underworld — a gigantic spider, mistress of web and shadow, lairing in the caverns above the Stygian Abyss proper.

## The encounter
Navrey's lair lies in the Underworld, the goblin- and undead-haunted level reached through the Fire Island volcano, below which the Abyss itself waits. Keys are gathered from the Underworld's spiders and their kin, per community recollection. The Underworld itself requires completing a quest chain to enter, per UOGuide's Stygian Abyss notes. See [Peerless Encounters](../../../mechanics/peerless-encounters.md) and [Ter Mur](../../../locations/termur.md).

## Behavior
A huge venomous spider — webs, poison, and crushing melee. Community recollection emphasizes clearing her spider brood before engaging her directly.

## Loot
Abyss peerless ingredients and artifacts. Her webs are also famously stealable — Navrey's Web appears among the Stygian Abyss stealable artifacts.

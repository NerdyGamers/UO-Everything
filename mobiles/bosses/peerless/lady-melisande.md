# Lady Melisande

Lady Melisande is the Peerless boss of the Blighted Grove in Malas — a corrupted dryad-like being bound to an ancient, poisoned Yew tree.

## The encounter
Adventurers enter the grove by passing beneath the great Yew's exposed roots, fighting through harpies, changelings, bog things, and hydras to gather the keys that open her lair. Enslaved and insane dryads and satyrs serve her within the tree. The encounter dates to the Mondain's Legacy expansion. See [Peerless Encounters](../../../mechanics/peerless-encounters.md) and [Malas](../../../locations/malas.md).

## Behavior
Melisande fights with blighted nature magic and poison — every aspect of her grove is poisonous, and the fight keeps that theme, with her enslaved servants joining in.

## Loot
Peerless ingredients and artifacts, plus a rare chance at an albino squirrel imprisoned in a crystal — a bonded, pure-white squirrel.

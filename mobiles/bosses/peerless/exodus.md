# Exodus

Exodus is counted among the boss encounters of the Stygian Abyss — a powerful entity fought in the dungeon's depths.

## The encounter
Exodus is reached in the depths of the Stygian Abyss dungeon, accessed through the Underworld or the Tomb of Kings. It belongs to the Stygian Abyss expansion's top tier of boss fights. Key sources and access details are community-documented; verify current before attempting. See [Peerless Encounters](../../../mechanics/peerless-encounters.md) and [Ter Mur](../../../locations/termur.md).

## Behavior
Community recollection describes a heavily armored, hard-hitting boss fight typical of the Abyss's top tier — bring a group and expect a long fight. Verified detail on its exact mechanics is scarce, so check current community guides before attempting.

## Loot
Abyss peerless ingredients and artifacts.

# Travesty

Travesty is the Peerless boss of the Citadel in the Tokuno Islands — an ultimate doppelganger that has seized control of the Black Order, the elite guild of thieves and assassins.

## The encounter
Reaching Travesty means fighting through the Citadel's trapped halls to collect a series of colored keys; community reports describe the red key's chamber as guarded by endlessly spawning assassins, making stealth, honor, or a fast group run the standard approach. The keys open the way to the boss chamber. See [Peerless Encounters](../../../mechanics/peerless-encounters.md) and [Tokuno Islands](../../../locations/tokuno-islands.md).

## Behavior
True to its name, Travesty is a shapeshifter, known for assuming the forms of those who face it. It is a fast, punishing fighter, and community reports describe it targeting tamers aggressively — bringing pets is widely considered risky.

## Loot
Peerless ingredients and artifacts, plus a rare chance at An Imprisoned Dog — a bonded dog that shape-shifts like a changeling.

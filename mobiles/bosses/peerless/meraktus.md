# Meraktus the Tormented

Meraktus the Tormented is the Peerless boss of the Tomb of Kings in Ter Mur — a tormented undead champion (his rare pet drop, the Tormented Minotaur Statuette, suggests a minotaur's form) haunting the gargoyle kings' burial place.

## The encounter
The Tomb of Kings is the final resting place of the gargoyle kings and one of the entrances to the Stygian Abyss. Keys for Meraktus are gathered from the tomb's undead guardians, per community recollection. The encounter dates to the Mondain's Legacy expansion. See [Peerless Encounters](../../../mechanics/peerless-encounters.md) and [Ter Mur](../../../locations/termur.md).

## Behavior
A punishing undead melee fighter — community reports describe him as no easy kill, dealing heavy physical damage with undead resilience.

## Loot
Peerless ingredients and artifacts, plus a rare chance at a Tormented Minotaur Statuette, per community reports.

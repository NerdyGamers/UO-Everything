# Slasher of Veils

The Slasher of Veils is a Peerless boss of the Stygian Abyss — a void demon waiting in the Chamber of Virtue for victims of its incomprehensible power.

## The encounter
The Slasher dwells on the deepest level of the Abyss dungeon, reached through the Underworld or the Tomb of Kings. Keys are gathered from the Abyss's void-touched inhabitants, per community recollection. See [Peerless Encounters](../../../mechanics/peerless-encounters.md) and [Ter Mur](../../../locations/termur.md).

## Behavior
Like Chief Paroxysmus, the Slasher devours nearby creatures — including player pets — to restore its health, so pets are a liability in this fight; community advice is to stable or dismiss them beforehand. It is a fast, hard-hitting demon, and the Chamber of Virtue setting gives the fight its name — a demon of the void waiting where virtue is tested.

## Loot
Abyss peerless ingredients and artifacts.

# Peerless Bosses

The Peerless are Ultima Online's keyed boss encounters: each requires gathering a set of timed keys from the surrounding area and exchanging them for a master key, which opens a time-limited fight against a single powerful boss. See [Peerless Encounters](../../../mechanics/peerless-encounters.md) for the full system.

## Mondain's Legacy peerless
- [Dread Horn](dread-horn.md): corrupted beast of the Twisted Weald; six keys, constant poison.
- [Travesty](travesty.md): doppelganger ruler of the Citadel's Black Order in Tokuno.
- [Lady Melisande](lady-melisande.md): corrupted dryad of the Blighted Grove's ancient Yew.
- [Shimmering Effusion](shimmering-effusion.md): creature of light in the Prism of Light; notoriously hard keys.
- [Chief Paroxysmus](chief-paroxysmus.md): ratman chieftain of Ilshenar; devours pets to heal.
- [Meraktus the Tormented](meraktus.md): undead champion of the Tomb of Kings in Ter Mur.
- [Monstrous Interred Grizzle](monstrous-interred-grizzle.md): reanimated monstrosity of Bedlam.

## Stygian Abyss bosses
- [Slasher of Veils](slasher-of-veils.md): void demon of the Chamber of Virtue; devours pets to heal.
- [Exodus](exodus.md): boss encounter of the Abyss depths.
- [Medusa](medusa.md): the gorgon; beware her petrifying gaze.
- [Stygian Dragon](stygian-dragon.md): sealed behind four orb-fed altars; terror attack and paralysis.
- [Navrey Night-Eyes](navrey-night-eyes.md): giant spider of the Underworld, mistress of web and shadow.

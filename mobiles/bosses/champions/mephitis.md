# Mephitis

The arachnid champion of the Shame champion spawn, a colossal spider dripping with venom.

## The encounter
Mephitis appears at the climax of the [Shame champion spawn](../../../locations/dungeons.md), preceded by waves of spiders, scorpions, terathan, and other arachnids. Poison is the theme of the entire spawn, and coming prepared with cures or poison immunity is close to mandatory. See [Champion Spawns](../../../mechanics/champion-spawns.md) for how the altar and skull system works.

## Behavior
Mephitis is a heavy poison threat: her attacks and breath apply strong poison, and she is surrounded by venomous adds from the spawn waves. The fight punishes anyone without poison mitigation. Ranged attackers and tamers tend to fare better than melee fighters standing in her poison. Her physical hits are significant but it is the poison pressure that defines the encounter.

## Loot
Mephitis drops gold, magic items, and her champion skull, a trophy item. Power scrolls are associated with champion spawn completion in Felucca.

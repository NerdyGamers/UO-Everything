# Semidar

The demon champion of the Hythloth champion spawn, a succubus-like fiend added to the champion rotation after the original four.

## The encounter
Semidar appears at the climax of the [Hythloth champion spawn](../../../locations/dungeons.md), preceded by waves of demons, imps, balrons, and other fiends. She was introduced later than the original four champions, giving the demon-themed spawn its own boss. The waves hit hard with fire and physical damage, and the confined Hythloth altar area makes positioning awkward. See [Champion Spawns](../../../mechanics/champion-spawns.md) for how the altar and skull system works.

## Behavior
Semidar is a hybrid threat: powerful melee attacks combined with dangerous spellcasting, including life-draining magic that punishes careless attackers. Her demon adds, particularly balrons in the later waves, demand attention even after she appears. Groups generally treat her as one of the harder champion fights, with crowd control on the adds being as important as damage on Semidar herself.

## Loot
Semidar drops gold, magic items, and her champion skull, a trophy item. Power scrolls are associated with champion spawn completion in Felucca.

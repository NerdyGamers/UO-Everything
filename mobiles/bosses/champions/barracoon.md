# Barracoon

The ratman champion of the Despise champion spawn, a hulking ratman king commanding waves of ratkin.

## The encounter
Barracoon appears at the climax of the [Despise champion spawn](../../../locations/dungeons.md), which cycles through escalating waves of ratmen, ratman mages, and ratman archers. He is generally considered the easiest of the champions, and the Despise spawn is a common entry point for players learning the champion system. See [Champion Spawns](../../../mechanics/champion-spawns.md) for how the altar and skull system works.

## Behavior
Barracoon fights in melee with heavy physical attacks and is accompanied by the remaining ratmen from the spawn. The fight is straightforward for a prepared group: control the adds, keep distance or tank him directly, and he falls quickly. He has no notable special mechanics beyond his brute strength, which is why Despise runs are often used to farm champion completions.

## Loot
Barracoon drops gold, magic items, and his champion skull, a trophy item. Power scrolls are associated with champion spawn completion in Felucca.

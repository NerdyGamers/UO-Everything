# Primeval Lich

The Primeval Lich is one of the two champion-spawn bosses of the Stygian Abyss — an ancient undead sorcerer whose spawn draws adventurers into the dungeon's depths.

## The encounter
Unlike the keyed Peerless fights, the Primeval Lich is worked up through a champion spawn in the Abyss: killing the spawn's waves advances its level until the lich itself appears. It was added with the Stygian Abyss expansion as one of the dungeon's two champion spawns. See [Champion Spawns](../../../mechanics/champion-spawns.md) and [Ter Mur](../../../locations/termur.md).

## Behavior
A classic lich taken to extremes — heavy necromantic spellcasting backed by the spawn's undead guardians. Dispelling and focused damage are the community's standard advice.

## Loot
Champion-spawn loot: power scrolls, artifacts, and Abyss ingredients.

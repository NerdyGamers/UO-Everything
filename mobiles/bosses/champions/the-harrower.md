# The Harrower

A tentacled horror and the secret sixth champion, able to manifest in Felucca dungeons after other champions fall. One of the most feared encounters in the game.

## The encounter
The Harrower has no fixed altar. After a champion is defeated in [Felucca](../../../locations/trammel-felucca.md), there is a chance the Harrower manifests in that dungeon instead of the expected skull reward — a surprise second boss for the victors. It is a deliberate escalation: the group that just finished a champion fight must immediately face something worse.

## Behavior
The Harrower attacks with sweeping tentacles that deal devastating area damage, and its true form is far tougher than any of the five altar champions. Community recollection consistently ranks it among the hardest fights in classic UO: the tentacle strikes punish clustered groups, and its durability means the fight drags on while adds and respawns pressure the party. Only well-prepared groups attempt it intentionally, and many encounters with it are accidents.

## Loot
The Harrower drops gold, magic items, and its skull. Its rarity as an encounter makes any of its drops notable trophies.

# Neira

The undead champion of the Destard champion spawn, a dark wisp necromancer wreathed in shadow.

## The encounter
Neira appears at the climax of the [Destard champion spawn](../../../locations/dungeons.md), preceded by waves of skeletons, zombies, ghouls, liches, and other undead. The spawn leans heavily on necromancy and cold damage, so undead-slaying weapons and protection from magic serve players well. See [Champion Spawns](../../../mechanics/champion-spawns.md) for how the altar and skull system works.

## Behavior
Unlike the melee-brawler champions, Neira is a spellcaster: she casts powerful necromancy and damaging spells from range and can summon or animate undead support. Interrupting her casting and closing distance quickly matters more here than in most champion fights. Her fragility relative to the other champions is offset by how much damage her magic puts out if she is left to cast freely.

## Loot
Neira drops gold, magic items, and her champion skull, a trophy item. Power scrolls are associated with champion spawn completion in Felucca.

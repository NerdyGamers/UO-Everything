# Rikktor

The serpent champion of the Lost Lands champion spawn, a massive serpent commanding the ophidian and serpent kin.

## The encounter
Rikktor appears at the climax of the [Lost Lands champion spawn](../../../locations/lost-lands.md), preceded by waves of snakes, giant serpents, ophidians, and terathan. The spawn mixes fast melee attackers with ophidian spellcasters, and poison shows up throughout. See [Champion Spawns](../../../mechanics/champion-spawns.md) for how the altar and skull system works.

## Behavior
Rikktor is one of the physically toughest champions: enormous melee damage backed by poison, with adds that include ophidian mages casting from behind the front line. Groups typically clear the ophidian casters first, then focus Rikktor down while managing his poison. His raw damage output makes him a step up from Barracoon and Neira in difficulty, as community recollection generally holds.

## Loot
Rikktor drops gold, magic items, and his champion skull, a trophy item. Power scrolls are associated with champion spawn completion in Felucca.

# Champion Spawn Bosses

The six champions of the champion spawn system: five altar bosses plus the Harrower. Champions are the climax of altar-based spawn events in Felucca dungeons, where killing waves of themed spawn creatures advances the altar until the champion appears — see [Champion Spawns](../../../mechanics/champion-spawns.md) for the full system.

## Article index

- [Mephitis](mephitis.md) — the arachnid champion of the Shame spawn
- [Neira](neira.md) — the undead champion of the Destard spawn
- [Rikktor](rikktor.md) — the serpent champion of the Lost Lands spawn
- [Semidar](semidar.md) — the demon champion of the Hythloth spawn
- [The Harrower](the-harrower.md) — the secret sixth champion, manifesting in Felucca dungeons after other champions fall
- [Primeval Lich](primeval-lich.md) — the lich champion spawn of the Stygian Abyss

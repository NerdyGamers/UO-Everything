# Abyssal Infernal

The Abyssal Infernal is one of the two champion-spawn bosses of the Stygian Abyss — a towering demon that caps the Abyss's infernal spawn.

## The encounter
The Abyssal Infernal is worked up through a champion spawn in the Stygian Abyss dungeon rather than through keys: clearing its demonic waves raises the spawn level until the infernal appears. It was added with the Stygian Abyss expansion as one of the dungeon's two champion spawns. See [Champion Spawns](../../../mechanics/champion-spawns.md) and [Ter Mur](../../../locations/termur.md).

## Behavior
A massive brawling demon — heavy melee damage with demonic spell support from its spawn. Community advice treats it as a group fight.

## Loot
Champion-spawn loot: power scrolls, artifacts, and Abyss ingredients.

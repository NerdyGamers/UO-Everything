# Mountain Goat

The mountain goat is a sure-footed herd animal found on Britannia's high peaks, more skittish than dangerous.

## Behavior
Mountain goats are generally non-aggressive, fleeing from danger rather than fighting. Cornered, they will butt with their horns.
Mountain goats would rather flee than fight, bounding up slopes no pursuer can follow. When cornered they lower their heads and charge, and their horns can surprise an overconfident hunter.

## Habitat
Mountainous regions, including the peaks around Minoc and other high terrain.
High mountain terrain — the peaks around Minoc, the ranges of the Lost Lands, anywhere the air is thin and the footing treacherous.

## Notes
Tamable with [Animal Taming](../../skills/wilderness/animal-taming.md). They are a novelty pet for tamers who favor mountain creatures. They yield hides and meat.
More a curiosity than a combat pet, the mountain goat appeals to tamers who collect unusual creatures. They are hardy and sure-footed, and their presence in a stable is a conversation piece.

# Eagle

The eagle is a majestic bird of prey that nests in Britannia's mountains.

## Behavior
Eagles are aggressive predators that swoop on smaller creatures and unwary travelers. They attack with beak and talons.
Eagles circle high, then stoop at terrifying speed, talons extended. They are aggressive toward smaller creatures and will attack travelers who approach their nests.

## Habitat
Mountain peaks and high cliffs across Britannia.
Mountain peaks, sea cliffs, and high crags. Their nests are found in the most inaccessible places in Britannia.

## Notes
Tamable with [Animal Taming](../../skills/wilderness/animal-taming.md). Tamed eagles are swift flying-attack pets, though they cannot be ridden. They yield feathers and meat.
Among the few tamable flying creatures, eagles hold a special place for tamers. They cannot be ridden, but a tamed eagle is a swift and striking aerial companion, and their feathers are useful to fletchers.

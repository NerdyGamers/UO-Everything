# Black Bear

The black bear is the smallest of Britannia's bears, a shy forest dweller that is less aggressive than its larger relatives.

## Behavior
Black bears are generally less aggressive than brown or grizzly bears, though they will defend themselves. They are quicker but weaker in a fight.
Black bears are timid compared to their larger cousins and often prefer to be left alone. When cornered or protecting cubs, however, they fight with the same claws and teeth as any bear.

## Habitat
Forests across Britannia, often in the same regions as brown bears.
Common throughout Britannia's forests, often overlapping with brown bear territory. They are the bear most travelers actually meet.

## Notes
Tamable with [Animal Taming](../../skills/wilderness/animal-taming.md) at a low skill level, making them one of the easiest bears to tame. They yield hides and meat when butchered.
Their low taming requirement makes black bears one of the gentlest introductions to [Animal Taming](../../skills/wilderness/animal-taming.md). New tamers often practice on black bears before moving up to brown bears and beyond.

# Animals

Britannia's animals range from harmless livestock to deadly predators. Many are tamable with [Animal Taming](../../skills/wilderness/animal-taming.md), serving as mounts, guards, and companions, and most yield hides, meat, or other resources when butchered.

- [Grizzly Bear](grizzly-bear.md): The largest, most dangerous bear; a formidable pet.
- [Black Bear](black-bear.md): The smallest bear, an easy early tame.
- [Polar Bear](polar-bear.md): White-furred predator of the frozen north.
- [Wolf](wolf.md): Pack predator and quick starter pet.
- [Dire Wolf](dire-wolf.md): A larger, vicious breed of wolf.
- [Timber Wolf](timber-wolf.md): Hardy forest wolf between common and dire in strength.

- [Cougar](cougar.md): Agile tawny predator of the mountains.
- [Mountain Goat](mountain-goat.md): Sure-footed herd animal of the high peaks.

- [Hart](hart.md): Antlered male deer.
- [Rabbit](rabbit.md): Harmless woodland creature; the classic first tame.
- [Eagle](eagle.md): Majestic mountain bird of prey.
- [Chicken](chicken.md): Harmless farm bird.
- [Cow](cow.md): Placid farm animal of Britannia's pastures.

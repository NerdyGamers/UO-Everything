# Hind

The hind is the female of the deer, a gentle and swift forest animal.

## Behavior
Hinds are passive and flee from danger. They are among the most peaceful creatures in Britannia's woods.
Hinds startle easily and bolt at the first sign of danger, white tails flashing. They never fight; their only defense is speed.
Hinds gather in small herds for safety, and the whole herd bolts as one when a single animal startles. Their flight is silent and swift.

## Habitat
Forests and meadows across Britannia.
Forest clearings, meadows, and glades across Britannia. Where there are harts, there are hinds nearby.

## Notes
Tamable with [Animal Taming](../../skills/wilderness/animal-taming.md) at a low skill level, a common first tame for new tamers. They yield hides and meat.
The classic first tame. New tamers learn the rhythm of [Animal Taming](../../skills/wilderness/animal-taming.md) on hinds and rabbits before moving to anything with teeth. Gentle, quick, and utterly harmless as pets.

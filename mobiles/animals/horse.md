# Horse

The horse is Britannia's most important animal: the standard mount for travel, war, and trade.

## Behavior
Wild horses are skittish but not aggressive, fleeing from danger. They are swift runners.
Wild horses are alert and skittish, bolting at sudden movement but calming quickly. Stallions can be spirited, but horses as a rule are not fighters.

## Habitat
Plains and meadows across Britannia, often in herds.
Open plains and grasslands across Britannia, roaming in herds. Wild herds are a common sight in the central plains.

## Notes
Tamable with [Animal Taming](../../skills/wilderness/animal-taming.md) and rideable once tamed — horses are the backbone of overland travel in Britannia. They come in several colors, with some rarer hues prized by collectors. They yield hides and meat.
The horse is the single most important animal in Britannian life. Tamed and ridden, horses carry travelers, knights, and merchants across the land. Rare hues are prized by collectors, and a well-bred horse is a status symbol. No adventurer's stable is complete without one.

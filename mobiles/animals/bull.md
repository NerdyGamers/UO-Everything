# Bull

The bull is the aggressive male of the cattle, far more dangerous than the placid cow.

## Behavior
Bulls are aggressive and will charge travelers who approach. Their horns make them a genuine threat to the unprepared.
Bulls paw the ground, lower their heads, and charge. Their horns gore deep, and they are far stronger than their placid appearance suggests. A bull is the one farm animal that demands respect.

## Habitat
Pastures and farms, wherever cattle are kept.
The same pastures as cows, usually one bull to a herd.

## Notes
Tamable with [Animal Taming](../../skills/wilderness/animal-taming.md), requiring more skill than the cow. A tamed bull is a surprisingly sturdy pet. They yield hides and meat.
Tamers who want a bovine with backbone choose the bull. They require more [Animal Taming](../../skills/wilderness/animal-taming.md) skill than cows and make surprisingly durable pets — slow, but immensely strong.

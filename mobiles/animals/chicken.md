# Chicken

The chicken is a harmless farm bird found around towns and farms across Britannia.

## Behavior
Chickens are completely passive, wandering farmyards and clucking. They are prey for nothing in particular and a threat to no one.
Chickens peck at the ground, squawk at nothing in particular, and scatter in comic panic when startled. They are the least threatening creature in Britannia.

## Habitat
Farms and towns, including the farms around Britain and Trinsic.
Farmyards in every settled region — Britain, Trinsic, Skara Brae, and countless smaller steadings.

## Notes
Tamable with [Animal Taming](../../skills/wilderness/animal-taming.md) at minimal skill. They yield feathers and meat when butchered. Mostly a flavor creature of rural Britannia.
Chickens exist mostly as set dressing for rural life, but they are tamable and butcherable like any animal. Every farm has them; every traveler ignores them. They yield feathers and meat.

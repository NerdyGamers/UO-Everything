# Sheep

The sheep is a gentle wool-bearing farm animal, one of the most economically useful creatures in Britannia.

## Behavior
Sheep are completely passive, grazing peacefully in pastures. They flee from danger.
Sheep graze, bleat, and follow the flock. They have no defenses and no inclination to develop any. Wolves consider them a food group.

## Habitat
Pastures and farms across Britannia, famously around Yew and Skara Brae.
Pastures across Britannia, most famously the rolling fields around Yew and Skara Brae.

## Notes
Tamable with [Animal Taming](../../skills/wilderness/animal-taming.md). Sheep are the classic source of wool for tailors, sheared for the cloth trade. They yield hides and meat when butchered.
Sheep are economically vital: their wool feeds the tailoring trade, spun into yarn and woven into cloth. Shepherds and tailors alike depend on flocks. Tamable, docile, and profitable — the perfect farm animal.

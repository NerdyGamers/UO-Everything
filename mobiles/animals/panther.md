# Panther

The panther is a sleek jungle predator, fast and deadly, prized by tamers for its speed.

## Behavior
Aggressive and quick, panthers stalk their prey and strike fast. They are dangerous to travelers caught unaware in the jungle.
Panthers are ambush hunters, striking from cover with blinding speed. Their claws rake deep, and they are agile enough to dodge clumsy swings. In the jungle they are the thing travelers fear most after the snakes.

## Habitat
Jungles and tropical regions, including the Lost Lands.
Tropical jungles, most notably in the Lost Lands. They prowl the undergrowth where visibility is poor and escape routes are few.

## Notes
Tamable with [Animal Taming](../../skills/wilderness/animal-taming.md). Tamed panthers are valued as fast attack pets. They yield hides and meat.
Tamers prize panthers for their speed — a tamed panther is one of the fastest attack pets available at its tier. They require respectable [Animal Taming](../../skills/wilderness/animal-taming.md) skill but repay it with style.

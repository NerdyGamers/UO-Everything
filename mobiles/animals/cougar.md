# Cougar

The cougar is a tawny mountain predator, agile and aggressive.

## Behavior
Cougars are aggressive hunters that stalk travelers in hilly and mountainous terrain. They rely on speed and surprise.
Cougars stalk from rocky outcrops and pounce, relying on surprise. They are less inclined to prolonged fights than wolves, preferring to end things quickly or melt away.
Their tawny coats blend with dry grass and rock, making them hard to spot until they move. Travelers in the foothills learn to watch the ridgelines.

## Habitat
Mountains and foothills across Britannia.
Mountain slopes, canyons, and rocky foothills across Britannia and the Lost Lands.

## Notes
Tamable with [Animal Taming](../../skills/wilderness/animal-taming.md). They make swift pets for tamers. They yield hides and meat.
A swift and elegant pet for tamers who prefer speed over brute force. Cougars tame through [Animal Taming](../../skills/wilderness/animal-taming.md) and make fine scouts and skirmishers.

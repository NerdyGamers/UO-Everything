# Grizzly Bear

The grizzly bear is the largest and most dangerous of Britannia's bears, a powerful predator that commands respect from travelers and tamers alike.

## Behavior
Highly aggressive, grizzlies attack on sight and hit far harder than their smaller cousins. They are solitary hunters with a fearsome reputation among low-level adventurers.
Grizzlies rear up and maul with devastating swipes. Unlike smaller bears they seem to relish a fight, and an unprepared traveler can be killed in a few blows. They are among the most feared mundane predators in the wilds.

## Habitat
Deep forests and wilderness areas, including the Lost Lands. They roam the same woodlands as brown bears but are less common.
Grizzlies prefer remote wilderness — the deep forests of the Lost Lands, the wilder corners of Britannia's mainland, and anywhere far from town guards. Encounters are less frequent than with brown bears but far more memorable.

## Notes
Tamable with [Animal Taming](../../skills/wilderness/animal-taming.md), requiring notably higher skill than the brown bear. A tamed grizzly is a formidable early-game tank pet. They yield hides and meat.
Taming a grizzly is a rite of passage for tamers moving into mid-tier pets. They require solid [Animal Taming](../../skills/wilderness/animal-taming.md) skill and patience, as they do not go quietly. A bonded grizzly is a loyal and punishing guard.

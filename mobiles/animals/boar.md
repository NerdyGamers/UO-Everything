# Boar

The boar is a tusked forest animal, bad-tempered and willing to charge.

## Behavior
Boars are aggressive and will attack travelers with their tusks. They are tougher than they look.
Boars are irritable and territorial, charging at intruders with tusks lowered. They are fast over short distances and tougher than their size suggests.

## Habitat
Forests across Britannia.
Forests across Britannia, rooting through the undergrowth for food.

## Notes
Tamable with [Animal Taming](../../skills/wilderness/animal-taming.md). They make hardy low-level pets. They yield hides and meat.
A solid low-tier combat pet for tamers, boars are aggressive enough to be useful and common enough to replace. They yield hides and meat, and their curved tusks are a minor trophy of the hunt.
Young tamers sometimes underestimate boars until a tusked charge sends them running. They are a good lesson in respecting even humble creatures.

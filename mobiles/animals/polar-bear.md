# Polar Bear

The polar bear is a white-furred predator adapted to the frozen north, as dangerous as any bear in warmer climes.

## Behavior
Aggressive like other bears, polar bears attack travelers on sight. Their white coats make them easy to spot against the snow but no easier to fight.
Polar bears hunt seals and travelers alike with the same single-minded aggression as other bears. In the snow they are deceptively quick, and their pale coats make them hard to spot until close.

## Habitat
Icy regions, most famously the Ice Island (Dagger Isle) north of Britannia and other frozen areas.
The Ice Island north of Britannia is their stronghold, along with other frozen reaches. Few other predators share their territory, so they rule it unchallenged.

## Notes
Tamable with [Animal Taming](../../skills/wilderness/animal-taming.md). A tamed polar bear serves as a sturdy cold-climate pet. They yield hides and meat.
Tamers who brave the cold prize polar bears as distinctive pets. They tame like other bears through [Animal Taming](../../skills/wilderness/animal-taming.md) and make hardy companions for adventurers working the northern isles.

# Timber Wolf

The timber wolf sits between the common wolf and the dire wolf in strength, a hardy forest predator.

## Behavior
Aggressive pack hunters, timber wolves behave much like common wolves but are tougher in a fight.
Timber wolves are stockier and hardier than common wolves, fighting with a stubborn persistence. They run in packs and defend their territory fiercely.

## Habitat
Forests across Britannia, particularly the deeper woods.
Dense forests across Britannia, particularly old-growth woods where the trees stand thick.

## Notes
Tamable with [Animal Taming](../../skills/wilderness/animal-taming.md). They make solid pets for tamers progressing past common wolves. They yield hides and meat.
Timber wolves fill the gap between common wolves and dire wolves for progressing tamers. They are dependable, unglamorous pets — the workhorses of the mid-tier taming ladder.
Because they are common and replaceable, timber wolves are popular practice pets for tamers learning to manage bonded animals and veterinary care in the field.

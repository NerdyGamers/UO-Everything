# Brown Bear

The brown bear is a common forest predator found across Britannia's woodlands. Sturdy and aggressive when provoked, it is one of the first challenging creatures a new tamer pursues.

## Behavior
Brown bears are aggressive and will attack travelers who wander too close. They fight with claws and teeth, relying on raw strength rather than speed. They are solitary or found in small numbers.
Brown bears are not particularly fast, so a traveler on foot can sometimes outrun one, but in close quarters their swipes hit hard enough to trouble lightly armored adventurers. They show no interest in anything but driving intruders off or making a meal of them.

## Habitat
Forests throughout Britannia, including the woods around Britain, Yew, and Skara Brae. They favor wooded areas with cover.
They den in the deeper woods and are a familiar sight to lumberjacks and miners working near forest edges. Brown bears avoid towns but will wander surprisingly close to settled land.

## Notes
Tamable with [Animal Taming](../../skills/wilderness/animal-taming.md) at a modest skill level, making them a popular early taming target. They yield hides and raw meat when butchered. Bears respond to [Veterinary](../../skills/wilderness/veterinary.md) like other pets.
Because they tame at a modest skill level, brown bears are a stepping stone between rabbits and deer and the tougher predators. A tamed bear follows and guards reliably, and many tamers keep one as a first real combat pet.

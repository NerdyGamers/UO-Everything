# Dire Wolf

The dire wolf is a larger, far more vicious breed of wolf, a serious threat to unprepared travelers.

## Behavior
Aggressive and powerful, dire wolves hunt in packs like their smaller kin but hit much harder. They are among the more dangerous common predators in the wilderness.
Dire wolves hunt like wolves but fight like monsters. Their bites tear through light armor, and they pursue fleeing prey far longer than common wolves. Packs of dire wolves are a serious threat even to seasoned travelers.

## Habitat
Wilderness areas across Britannia, often deeper in the wilds than common wolves.
Deeper wilderness, the Lost Lands, and the wilder dungeon approaches. Where common wolves thin out, dire wolves take over.

## Notes
Tamable with [Animal Taming](../../skills/wilderness/animal-taming.md), requiring higher skill than the common wolf. A tamed dire wolf is a strong mid-tier pet. They yield hides and meat.
A meaningful step up in [Animal Taming](../../skills/wilderness/animal-taming.md), dire wolves reward the effort with genuine combat power. Tamers value them as fast, hard-hitting pets that can keep pace with mounted travelers.

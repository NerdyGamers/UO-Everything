# Wolf

The wolf is a pack predator found in forests and plains across Britannia, known for hunting in groups and harrying travelers.

## Behavior
Wolves are aggressive and often travel in packs, making them dangerous despite their individual weakness. They are fast and persistent hunters.
Wolves coordinate in packs, surrounding prey and taking turns lunging. A lone wolf is a nuisance; a pack of five or six can overwhelm a traveler who stops to fight. They are fast and relentless once they pick up a scent.

## Habitat
Forests and open wilderness throughout Britannia and the Lost Lands.
Wolves range across forests, plains, and foothills throughout Britannia and the Lost Lands. Their howls are a familiar sound of the wilderness at night.

## Notes
Tamable with [Animal Taming](../../skills/wilderness/animal-taming.md) at a low skill level. Tamed wolves make quick, loyal pets for beginning tamers. They yield hides and meat.
One of the first pack animals a tamer can control, wolves teach new tamers about managing multiple pets. Tamed wolves are swift and loyal, and a small pack of them is a respectable escort for a young adventurer.

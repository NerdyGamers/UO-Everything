# Cow

The cow is a placid farm animal, a staple of Britannia's pastures and a source of hides and meat.

## Behavior
Cows are completely passive and wander grazing. They never attack.
Cows graze placidly and regard the world with mild interest. Nothing bothers a cow. They do not fight, flee only slowly, and generally behave as though danger were a rumor.

## Habitat
Pastures and farms across Britannia, often near towns.
Pastures across Britannia, wherever farmers have fenced good grazing land.

## Notes
Tamable with [Animal Taming](../../skills/wilderness/animal-taming.md). Cows are associated with dairy in Britannian farm life. They yield hides and meat when butchered.
Cows are the backbone of Britannia's pastoral economy in the fiction of the world — milk, meat, and hides. Tamable and docile, they are sometimes kept as decorative livestock by roleplayers and homeowners with pasture space.
In the fiction of Britannian farm life, cows provide milk as well as meat and hides, and dairy farms dot the countryside around the major cities.

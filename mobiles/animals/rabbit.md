# Rabbit

The rabbit is a small, harmless woodland creature, notable mostly as the weakest tamable animal in Britannia.

## Behavior
Rabbits are completely passive and flee from everything. They pose no threat to anyone.
Rabbits nibble grass and freeze at shadows. Their entire survival strategy is noticing danger early and being somewhere else. They have no attack whatsoever.

## Habitat
Meadows and forests across Britannia.
Meadows, gardens, and forest edges across Britannia — anywhere green and quiet.

## Notes
Tamable with [Animal Taming](../../skills/wilderness/animal-taming.md) at minimal skill — often a new tamer's very first tame. They yield a small amount of meat and hides. A traditional rite of passage for tamers.
The traditional first tame of every tamer in Britannia. Rabbits require almost no [Animal Taming](../../skills/wilderness/animal-taming.md) skill, and successfully taming one is a small ceremony every tamer remembers. Useless in a fight; priceless as a beginning.

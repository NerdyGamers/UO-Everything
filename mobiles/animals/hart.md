# Hart

The hart is the male of the deer, distinguished by its antlers and slightly bolder temperament.

## Behavior
Harts are mostly passive but will defend themselves with their antlers if cornered. They are swift and graceful.
Harts are bolder than hinds and will stand their ground when cornered, lashing out with antlered heads. They still prefer flight, but a wounded hart can be dangerous to the careless.

## Habitat
Forests and meadows across Britannia, alongside hinds.
The same forests and meadows as hinds, often seen grazing at dawn and dusk.

## Notes
Tamable with [Animal Taming](../../skills/wilderness/animal-taming.md). Like hinds, they are easy early tames. They yield hides and meat.
A small step up from the hind, harts teach tamers that even prey animals demand respect. They remain easy tames and make swift, graceful companions.
During the rutting season harts can be bolder still, and even experienced hunters give a cornered hart room. As pets they remain swift and graceful rather than fierce.

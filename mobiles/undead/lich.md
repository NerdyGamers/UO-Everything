# Lich

An undead wizard who cheated death through dark magic: the lich retains its full spellcasting in undeath and commands lesser undead as servants. The classic dungeon spellcaster and the bane of careless adventurers.

## Behavior

Casts a full offensive spellbook — fireballs, lightning, poison, and worse — while skeletal servants screen it from melee. Dangerous at range and well-guarded up close; closing quickly, interrupting its casting, or picking off its guards first are all valid approaches.

## Habitat

Undead dungeons ([Deceit](../../locations/dungeons.md), Despise), ancient towers, and anywhere a wizard died refusing to let go.

## Loot

Reagents, scrolls, spellbooks, gold, and magic equipment. A solid farming target for a mage stocking supplies.

# Shade

A living shadow: an undead spirit of pure darkness that stalks the deep places of the world. Shades are elusive, deadly, and frustratingly hard to hit.

## Behavior

A fast, evasive melee attacker that seems to slip around blades. Difficult to strike with mundane weapons; magic and silver are recommended (community recollection). Its shadowy nature makes it a priority target before it picks off the wounded.

## Habitat

Deep dungeons and dark ruins. Community recollection places shades in the dungeons of Ilshenar and Malas.

## Loot

Gold, gems, and magic items.

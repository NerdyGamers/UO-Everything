# Spectre

A more powerful and malevolent spirit than the wraith: a spectre's touch chills to the bone, and its hatred of the living is absolute. Where wraiths haunt, spectres rule.

## Behavior

A strong, fast spectral melee fighter with draining attacks. Considerably more dangerous than a wraith — a serious opponent for mid-level adventurers. Bring silver or enchanted weapons, and do not fight one while already weakened.

## Habitat

Deep dungeon levels, haunted crypts, and the darker corners of Ilshenar's dungeons.

## Loot

Gold, gems, and magic equipment worthy of the risk.

# Skeleton

The most basic undead warrior of Britannia: animated bones that rise in graveyards, crypts, and dungeons. Skeletons are the first undead most adventurers meet, and the most common product of [Necromancy](../../skills/magic/necromancy.md).

## Behavior

Skeletons fight with simple melee attacks and no real tactics, often rising in packs. Necromancers raise them as disposable servants and dungeon guardians. Individually weak, but dangerous in numbers or to the unprepared traveler.

## Habitat

Graveyards (notably the Britain graveyard), crypts, and the upper levels of undead dungeons such as [Deceit](../../locations/dungeons.md) and Despise. They appear wherever necromancers practice their craft.

## Loot

Bones and the occasional low-value weapon or armor piece. Nothing of note — skeletons are fought for training, not profit.

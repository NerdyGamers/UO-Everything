# Skeletal Dragon

The animated bones of a dragon: a flying undead horror with a dragon's strength and none of its mercy. Among the most feared undead in Britannia — and, remarkably, tamable.

**Tamable** — see [Animal Taming](../../skills/wilderness/animal-taming.md). As a pet it is one of the most powerful tamables in the game, requiring grandmaster-level taming skill (community recollection — verify current requirements).

## Behavior

Fights as a dragon: crushing melee, fiery breath, and enormous health, with undead resistances layered on top. Whether faced in a dungeon or ridden into battle as a tamed mount, it demands respect.

## Habitat

Deep dungeons and dragon graveyards. Community recollection places the most infamous lairs in the deep dungeons of the Lost Lands and Ilshenar.

## Loot

A dragon's hoard in undead form: large amounts of gold, gems, and magic equipment.

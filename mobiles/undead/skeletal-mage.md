# Skeletal Mage

A skeleton that retains the spellcasting it knew in life. Skeletal mages hurl offensive magic from behind their bony brethren, making them priority targets in any undead pack.

## Behavior

Casts damaging spells — fireballs, lightning, and the like — while keeping its distance behind skeletal warriors. Fragile in melee but dangerous if ignored; experienced dungeon crawlers kill the mage first. Often found mixed into groups of skeletons and skeletal knights.

## Habitat

Undead dungeons such as [Deceit](../../locations/dungeons.md) and Despise, and wherever liches keep skeletal servants.

## Loot

Reagents, scrolls, and gold. A decent source of low-circle scrolls for a young mage stocking a spellbook.

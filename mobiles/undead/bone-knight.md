# Bone Knight

A powerful undead knight of the Doom Gauntlet: a skeleton warrior wreathed in dark magic, notably tougher than the skeletal knights of Deceit. Bone knights are the gauntlet's heavy infantry.

## Behavior

A heavy melee fighter with high health and strong armor, often fighting alongside Bone Magi and other gauntlet horrors. Hits hard and absorbs punishment; a serious opponent that demands a prepared fighter or a coordinated group.

## Habitat

The Doom Gauntlet in Malas — the gauntlet's undead wing, fought on the brutal road toward the Dark Fathers.

## Loot

Gauntlet-tier loot: gold, equipment, and progress toward the Doom [artifacts](../../items/artifacts.md) that draw adventurers into the gauntlet in the first place.

# Ancient Lich

The eldest and most terrible of liches: an ancient lich is a dungeon-ending threat — a wizard-king whose power only grew in the centuries since its death. Few undead inspire more dread.

## Behavior

A devastating spellcaster with enormous health and layers of undead guardians. Fights like a raid boss: sustained, high-damage magic that punishes mistakes. Bring a group, or bring your absolute best — and even then, have an escape plan.

## Habitat

The deepest chambers of the great undead dungeons; sealed vaults and forgotten throne rooms where something was locked away for good reason.

## Loot

Top-tier dungeon loot: valuable magic equipment, large amounts of gold, and rare reagents.

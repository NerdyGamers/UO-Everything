# Zombie

The shambling corpse: slow, stupid, and relentless. Zombies are the weakest of the true undead, raised by novice necromancers and found wherever the dead rest uneasy.

## Behavior

A slow melee attacker with no tactics whatsoever — it walks at you and claws. Individually trivial; packs can still overwhelm the careless. Often the first undead a young adventurer faces in a graveyard at night.

## Habitat

Graveyards, crypts, and the fringes of undead dungeons. Common wherever necromancers have been active.

## Loot

Little beyond scraps and the occasional coin. Zombies are fought for training, not treasure.

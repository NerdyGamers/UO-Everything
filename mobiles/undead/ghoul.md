# Ghoul

A corpse-eating undead that haunts crypts and battlefields. Ghouls are faster and more aggressive than zombies, with a taste for the recently dead — and the merely living, given the chance.

## Behavior

An aggressive melee attacker, noticeably quicker than a zombie. Often found in packs, feeding on corpses. More dangerous than its shambling cousins and not to be underestimated by young adventurers.

## Habitat

Crypts, graveyards, and the upper levels of dungeons like [Deceit](../../locations/dungeons.md) and Covetous.

## Loot

Gold and low-end equipment — occasionally something worthwhile taken from a victim.

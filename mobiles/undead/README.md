# Undead

The restless dead of Britannia: corpses and spirits animated by [Necromancy](../../skills/magic/necromancy.md), ancient curses, or the sheer refusal to die. Undead share common traits — the [Chivalry](../../skills/magic/chivalry.md) ability Turn Undead repels and damages them, silver weapons strike them with bonus effect, and they haunt the same dark places: [Deceit](../../locations/dungeons.md), Despise, graveyards, and sealed crypts across every facet.

## Index

- [Skeleton](skeleton.md) — animated bones; the first undead every adventurer meets
- [Skeletal Knight](skeletal-knight.md) — armored undead soldier, stronger and better equipped
- [Skeletal Mage](skeletal-mage.md) — spellcasting skeleton; kill it first
- [Bone Knight](bone-knight.md) — Doom Gauntlet heavy infantry, far tougher than its cousins
- [Mummy](mummy.md) — cursed preserved corpse; slow, tough, diseased
- [Zombie](zombie.md) — shambling corpse; weak alone, dangerous in packs
- [Ghoul](ghoul.md) — corpse-eater; faster and more aggressive than a zombie
- [Wraith](wraith.md) — restless spirit; fast, draining, resistant to mundane steel
- [Spectre](spectre.md) — greater malevolent spirit; a serious mid-level threat
- [Shade](shade.md) — living shadow; elusive and deadly in the deep dark
- [Lich](lich.md) — undead wizard retaining full spellcasting; commands lesser undead
- [Lich Lord](lich-lord.md) — master lich ruling whole crypts; a boss-tier fight
- [Ancient Lich](ancient-lich.md) — eldest lich; a dungeon-ending threat
- [Skeletal Dragon](skeletal-dragon.md) — undead dragon; among the most feared — and tamable

# Wraith

A restless spirit given murderous form: a floating, spectral undead that drains the warmth from the living. Wraiths are a step above the common walking dead — quick, hateful, and hard to put down with ordinary steel.

## Behavior

A fast melee attacker whose touch carries a draining chill (community recollection on exact mechanics). Resistant to mundane weapons; silver or enchanted weapons work best. A genuine threat to the mid-level adventurer.

## Habitat

Haunted dungeons — [Deceit](../../locations/dungeons.md), Despise — and cursed ruins where violent deaths left their mark.

## Loot

Gold, gems, and occasional magic items.

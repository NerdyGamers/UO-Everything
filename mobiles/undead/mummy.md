# Mummy

A preserved corpse animated by ancient curses: slow, tough, and diseased. Mummies shamble through forgotten tombs, and their touch is infamous for spreading a rotting affliction.

## Behavior

A slow but durable melee fighter that simply refuses to fall down quickly. Its attacks can inflict disease-like effects (community recollection — verify in game). Best handled at range, with silver weapons, or with a quick overwhelming kill before the rot sets in.

## Habitat

Ancient tombs and desert ruins. Community recollection places mummies in the Lost Lands region and in deep dungeon crypts, though exact spawns vary.

## Loot

Bandages (fittingly), gold, and the occasional tomb trinket.

# Lich Lord

A lich of exceptional power and age: lich lords command whole crypts of undead and cast with the strength of a master wizard. Where a lich is a dungeon threat, a lich lord is a dungeon's master.

## Behavior

A heavy spellcaster with high health and strong resistances, typically surrounded by skeletal knights and mages. A boss-tier fight for a group, or for a well-prepared solo mage-slayer with silver and a plan. Its magic hits harder and its servants hit back.

## Habitat

The deeper levels of [Deceit](../../locations/dungeons.md) and other major undead dungeons; ancient throne rooms and sealed crypts.

## Loot

High-end reagents, scrolls, magic weapons and armor, and substantial gold.

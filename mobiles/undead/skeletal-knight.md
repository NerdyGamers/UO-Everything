# Skeletal Knight

An armored undead warrior: a skeleton clad in corroded plate, wielding real weapons with genuine martial skill. Skeletal knights are the soldiers of undead hosts — stronger, tougher, and far better equipped than common skeletons.

## Behavior

Fights as a competent melee warrior with sword and shield, sometimes in organized groups guarding crypt chambers. Noticeably more accurate and durable than a skeleton; a fair challenge for a developing warrior and a good sparring target before deeper dungeon delves.

## Habitat

[Deceit](../../locations/dungeons.md) and other undead dungeons, ancient battlefields, and sealed crypts. Often found on the deeper levels where common skeletons thin out.

## Loot

Weapons, armor pieces, and gold. Occasionally carries genuinely usable equipment taken from fallen adventurers.

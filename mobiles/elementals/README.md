# Elementals

Living embodiments of the classical elements — earth, air, fire, water, and poison — plus their wilder kin. Five of them (earth, air, fire, water, poison) can be summoned through [Magery](../../skills/magic/magery.md) at increasing circle; the rest are wild-only and uniformly more dangerous. Weapons bearing the Elemental Slayer property deal bonus damage to all of them.

## Index

- [Earth Elemental](earth-elemental.md) — the sturdy 4th-circle summon; slow, durable, crushes in melee
- [Air Elemental](air-elemental.md) — the swift 5th-circle summon; fast strikes, hard to pin down
- [Fire Elemental](fire-elemental.md) — the 6th-circle summon; searing fire damage in melee
- [Water Elemental](water-elemental.md) — the well-rounded 7th-circle summon; cold-touched strikes, reportedly casts
- [Poison Elemental](poison-elemental.md) — the feared 8th-circle summon; poisons on hit
- [Blood Elemental](blood-elemental.md) — wild-only crimson horror of Ilshenar's dungeons; reportedly life-draining
- [Snow Elemental](snow-elemental.md) — wild-only frozen shambler of the Ice Dungeon
- [Efreet](efreet.md) — wild-only genie of living flame; holds the Fire Temple in Ilshenar

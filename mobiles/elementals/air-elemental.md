# Air Elemental

A swirling vortex of living wind, faster than any of its elemental kin.

## Behavior

Where the earth elemental endures, the air elemental evades: it is exceptionally quick, striking rapidly in melee and proving hard to pin down. It lacks the raw durability of earth or fire, so fights against it are chases rather than sieges.

## Habitat

Wild air elementals circle the air-themed reaches of [Shame](../../locations/dungeons.md). Hostile mages reportedly summon them as well (community recollection).

## Loot

Gold and the occasional gem. Little else of note.

## Summoning

Summonable through [Magery](../../skills/magic/magery.md) (5th circle). Favored when speed matters more than staying power.

# Water Elemental

A surging column of living water, deceptively graceful and harder-hitting than it looks.

## Behavior

Fights in melee with cold-touched strikes (community recollection) and is reported to cast offensive spells as well, making it the most magically inclined of the common elementals. Durable and well-rounded — the closest thing to a generalist among the summoned five.

## Habitat

Wild water elementals dwell in the water-themed reaches of [Shame](../../locations/dungeons.md).

## Loot

Gold and the occasional gem. Little else of note.

## Summoning

Summonable through [Magery](../../skills/magic/magery.md) (7th circle). Often considered the strongest all-rounder of the summonable elementals.

# Poison Elemental

A seething, toxic mass — the most dangerous of the elementals a mage can summon.

## Behavior

Its melee strikes poison, and it exudes a poisonous presence that punishes anyone fighting it up close. High durability combined with constant poison pressure makes it a war of attrition the elemental usually wins. Cure potions or magic are close to mandatory.

## Habitat

Wild poison elementals infest the poison-themed reaches of [Shame](../../locations/dungeons.md) and are reported in the Blighted Grove (community recollection).

## Loot

Gold and the occasional gem. Little else of note.

## Summoning

Summonable through [Magery](../../skills/magic/magery.md) (8th circle) — the highest-circle elemental summon, and the most feared of the five.

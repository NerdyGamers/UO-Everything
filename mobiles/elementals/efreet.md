# Efreet

A towering genie of living flame — proud, cruel, and far beyond a mage's power to summon.

## Behavior

A devastating melee fighter, and reported to hurl fire magic at its enemies as well (community recollection). It is intelligent and aggressive, and treats intruders in its domain as prey rather than threats.

## Habitat

The Fire Temple in [Ilshenar](../../locations/ilshenar.md) — its stronghold, shared with fire elementals and worse.

## Loot

Gold, gems, and reportedly magic items on occasion (community recollection).

## Sources

- Community bestiaries (UOGuide, Stratics)
- In-game observation

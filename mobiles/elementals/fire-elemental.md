# Fire Elemental

A walking inferno given shape, wreathed in perpetual flame.

## Behavior

Aggressive in melee, and its strikes carry searing fire damage on top of physical force. Tougher than air or earth elementals, it is a genuine threat to the underprepared — fire resistance matters more here than against any other common elemental.

## Habitat

Wild fire elementals haunt the fire-themed reaches of [Shame](../../locations/dungeons.md) and are reported in Destard and other volcanic haunts (community recollection).

## Loot

Gold, the occasional gem, and reportedly sulfurous ash (community recollection).

## Summoning

Summonable through [Magery](../../skills/magic/magery.md) (6th circle). A popular damage-dealer among the summoned elementals.

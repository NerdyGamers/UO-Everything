# Snow Elemental

A shambling figure of packed snow and ice, native to the frozen depths.

## Behavior

Fights in melee with cold damage; slow and straightforward, but at home in numbers in its frozen habitat. Cold resistance blunts the worst of it.

## Habitat

The Ice Dungeon in the Lost Lands — its primary and best-known home (community recollection).

## Loot

Gold. Little else of note.

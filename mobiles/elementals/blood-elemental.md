# Blood Elemental

A crimson horror of congealed gore — wild, unsummonable, and far deadlier than the elementals mages command.

## Behavior

Enormously durable with punishing melee attacks; it is reported to drain the life of its victims to sustain itself (community recollection). Unlike the five summonable elementals, it cannot be called or controlled — only killed, and only with preparation.

## Habitat

Found in the dungeons of [Ilshenar](../../locations/ilshenar.md), reportedly in the Sorcerer's Dungeon region (community recollection). Not encountered in Britannia proper.

## Loot

Gold and gems; reportedly magic items on occasion (community recollection).

- Community bestiaries (UOGuide, Stratics)

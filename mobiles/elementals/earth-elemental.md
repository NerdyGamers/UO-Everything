# Earth Elemental

A hulking mass of animated rock and soil, and the first elemental a mage learns to call.

## Behavior

Slow but tremendously durable, the earth elemental relies on crushing melee blows and high physical resistance rather than speed or magic. Summoned ones make dependable front-line tanks; wild ones are straightforward brawls of attrition. They are the sturdiest of the five summonable elementals.

## Habitat

Wild earth elementals inhabit the earth-themed reaches of [Shame](../../locations/dungeons.md). Hostile spellcasters are also known to summon them mid-fight — evil mages and liches reportedly call earth elementals to their aid (community recollection).

## Loot

Gold, and reportedly iron ore and the occasional gem. Little else of note.

## Summoning

Summonable through [Magery](../../skills/magic/magery.md) (4th circle). A common early choice for tamers of the arcane looking for a durable companion.

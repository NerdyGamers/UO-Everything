# Weaver

Cloth-workers of the weaving trade, added in the later crafting eras. Weavers sell cloth and woven goods.

## Wares

Weavers stock bolts of cloth and woven goods — the tailor's raw material sold at the source.

## Era note

Weavers are a later addition to the town roster, arriving with the crafting expansions of the early 2000s rather than the classic launch set.

# Architect

Town NPCs associated with house construction and deeds. Community recollection holds that architects dealt in house deeds and placement paperwork in the classic towns, standing near banks and trade districts.

## Role

Architects are part of the town service furniture alongside bankers and provisioners. Their exact stock varied by era; like many service NPCs, they also answer to greetings and can be asked about their trade.

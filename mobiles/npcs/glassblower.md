# Glassblower

Artisans of the glass trade, added with the Samurai Empire era. They sell the tools and materials of glassblowing.

## Wares

Glassblowers stock blow pipes and the makings of glass items — bottles, pitchers, and decorative glassware. Their trade arrived with the broader crafting expansions of the early 2000s.

## Era note

Glassblowers are a later addition to the town roster, appearing with the Samurai Empire expansion era rather than the classic launch set.

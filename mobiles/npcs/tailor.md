# Tailor

Town clothiers selling clothing and cloth, and teaching [tailoring](../../skills/crafting/tailoring.md). A cornerstone of the crafting trades.

## Wares

Tailors sell shirts, pants, dresses, hats, and bolts of cloth in the standard styles and colors. Every new character's wardrobe starts here.

## Training

They teach tailoring to new students for the usual fee, as does the tailor guildmaster.

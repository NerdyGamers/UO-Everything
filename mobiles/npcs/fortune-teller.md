# Fortune Teller

Wandering seers — often gypsy fortune tellers — who read palms and teach the medical skills. Found at gypsy camps and in town squares.

## Training

Fortune tellers teach [anatomy](../../skills/combat/anatomy.md), [healing](../../skills/combat/healing.md), and forensic evaluation to those who ask, for the usual fee. Healers, bloodletters, and the healer guildmaster teach the same skills.

## Role

Part of the gypsy flavor of the roads and camps, fortune tellers add mystique to the wandering life alongside gypsies and rangers.

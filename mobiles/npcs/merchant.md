# Merchant

Generic traders found in towns and at crossroads, buying and selling a mixed stock of goods. The catch-all shopkeeper of the classic towns.

## Wares

Merchants carry an eclectic mix — food, basic tools, clothing, and sundries — whatever the town needs. They are the classic "general store" NPC.

## Training

They teach item identification to those who ask, and merchant quest-givers hand out escort work to travelers.

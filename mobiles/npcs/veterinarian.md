# Veterinarian

Animal doctors found near the stables, tending the realm's mounts and pets. The medical counterpart to the animal trainer.

## Services

Veterinarians treat injured animals and — in the later eras — resurrect dead pets, per community recollection. They are the healer's answer for the [animal taming](../../skills/wilderness/animal-taming.md) lifestyle.

## Training

They teach [veterinary](../../skills/wilderness/veterinary.md) and animal lore to those who ask, for the usual fee.

# Necromancer

Practitioners of the dark arts who keep shop in Umbra, the necromancer city of Malas. They sell necromancy reagents and teach the forbidden skills.

## Wares

Necromancers sell the reagents of [necromancy](../../skills/magic/necromancy.md) — grave dust, daemon blood, nox crystals, and the rest — along with the tools of spirit speak.

## Training

They teach necromancy, spirit speak, and the supporting magical skills to students bold enough to ask.

## Era note

Necromancer vendors belong to the Age of Shadows era, arriving with Malas and the city of Umbra rather than the classic launch set.

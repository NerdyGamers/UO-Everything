# Blacksmith

The classic town smithy, selling and repairing weapons and armor. One of the most important service NPCs for warriors.

## Wares and repairs

Blacksmiths sell serviceable weapons and armor and — more importantly — repair damaged equipment for a fee. Before player crafters took over the economy, the town blacksmith was every warrior's quartermaster.

## Training

They teach [blacksmithy](../../skills/crafting/blacksmithy.md) and arms lore to new students. Armorers, weaponsmiths, and iron workers teach the same skills.

# Herbalist

Town shopkeepers dealing in herbs, roots, and natural remedies. A fixture of the market districts.

## Wares

Herbalists sell the herbs and natural ingredients used in [alchemy](../../skills/magic/alchemy.md) and [cooking](../../skills/crafting/cooking.md) — a greener counterpart to the alchemist's reagent shelf.

## Training

They teach alchemy and cooking to new students for the usual fee.

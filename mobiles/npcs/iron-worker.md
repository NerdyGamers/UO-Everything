# Iron Worker

Town metalworkers dealing in iron goods and weapons of the simpler sort. A cut below the blacksmith in prestige, but a staple of the industrial districts.

## Wares

Iron workers sell iron ingots' worth of finished goods — tools, simple weapons, and metal fittings. Their prices suit the working adventurer.

## Training

They teach blacksmithy, fencing, mace fighting, and parrying to new students for the usual fee — a broad martial-industrial curriculum shared with the weapons trainers.

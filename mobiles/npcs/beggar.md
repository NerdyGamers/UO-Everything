# Beggar

Wandering unfortunates found in the streets of the classic towns, instantly recognizable by their cries of "Alms for the poor!" and "Spare some change, guv'nor?" They are the practice targets of the [begging](../../skills/misc/begging.md) skill.

## Begging

A character with the begging skill can beg from a beggar for a few gold pieces — a classic, if meager, income for new characters. Beggars wander the town streets and respond to passersby.

## Hireable beggars

Beggars can also be hired as followers through the hireling system, where they serve as cheap, weak companions. See [hireable](hireable.md).

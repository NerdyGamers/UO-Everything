# Stone Crafter

Artisans working stone into furniture and decorative pieces. A specialty crafting trade of the towns.

## Wares

Stone crafters sell stone furniture — tables, chairs, and decorative stonework for houses and gardens.

## Training

They teach [carpentry](../../skills/crafting/carpentry.md) to those who ask, sharing the furniture-maker's curriculum with carpenters.

# Minter

The coiners of the realm, found at the mint in Nujel'm. Minters are the moneyers behind Britannia's gold economy.

## Role

Minters stand at the Nujel'm mint, the palatial home of the currency trade. Community recollection holds they dealt in the exchange and handling of coinage in the classic economy.

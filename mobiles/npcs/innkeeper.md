# Innkeeper

The hosts of Britannia's inns, renting rooms and keeping the common areas lively. Every major town has its inn, and the innkeeper is its heart.

## Services

Innkeepers rent rooms to travelers — a classic, if largely symbolic, service — and keep the inn's common room stocked. Community recollection holds that innkeepers, like tavern keepers, sold the "contract of employment" used to place player vendors.

# Noble

Well-dressed aristocrats of the towns and courts — and frequent escort quest-givers on the roads. Nobles add high-society flavor to the classic cities.

## Role

Nobles stroll the wealthy districts and appear as "quest giver" escortables, asking adventurers to see them safely to their destinations. Their payouts follow the standard escort rewards.

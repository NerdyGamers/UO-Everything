# Cook

Town NPCs of the kitchen trade, selling prepared food and cooking supplies. Found in taverns, inns, and market districts.

## Wares

Cooks sell cooked meals — roasts, stews, and baked dishes — along with the ingredients of [cooking](../../skills/crafting/cooking.md). They teach cooking to new students, as do bakers and herbalists.

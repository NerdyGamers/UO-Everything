# Armorer

Town shopkeepers specializing in ready-made armor, from leather tunics to full plate suits. A first stop for new warriors who cannot yet craft their own.

## Wares

Armorers sell the standard armor types — leather, studded, ringmail, chainmail, and platemail pieces — along with shields. Quality is serviceable rather than exceptional; player-crafted and looted armor outclasses it quickly.

## Training

They teach [blacksmithy](../../skills/crafting/blacksmithy.md) and arms lore to new students for the usual fee. Blacksmiths, weaponsmiths, and iron workers teach the same skills.

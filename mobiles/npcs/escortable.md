# Escortable

Wandering quest-giver NPCs — merchants, nobles, brides and grooms, wandering mages, messengers, seekers of adventure, and peasants — who ask passing adventurers to escort them to a destination. A classic feature of New Haven and the older towns.

## Escort quests

An escortable titled "quest giver" asks to be accompanied to a named destination, such as an inn or a city gate. Accepting the quest makes them follow; arriving at the destination pays a gold reward — community recollection holds the classic payout at 500 gold — and grants compassion virtue gains for the first few escorts each day.

## Training

Many escortable types double as skill teachers of their own trade — merchants, mages, and the rest teach what they know when asked.

# Carpenter

Town woodworkers selling furniture, wooden shields, and carpentry tools. A cornerstone of the crafting economy.

## Wares

Carpenters sell chairs, tables, chests, wooden shields, and the tools of the trade — saws, hammers, and jointing planes. Their furniture stocks the houses of Britannia.

## Training

They teach [carpentry](../../skills/crafting/carpentry.md) and lumberjacking to new students for the usual fee.

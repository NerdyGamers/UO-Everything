# Animal Trainer

The stablemasters of Britannia's towns, found at the stables near most city gates. They board pets for a small fee and teach the animal skills.

## Stabling

Animal trainers stable mounts and pets for a modest gold fee per animal, keeping them safe while the owner travels. They are the backbone of the [animal taming](../../skills/wilderness/animal-taming.md) lifestyle — every tamer visits them.

## Training

They teach animal lore, animal taming, and [veterinary](../../skills/wilderness/veterinary.md) care, each up to a modest level for the usual training fee. Fur traders, ranchers, and rangers teach overlapping parts of the same skill set.

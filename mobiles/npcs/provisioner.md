# Provisioner

The general-store keepers of Britannia — and the classic first shop for every new character. Provisioners sell a bit of everything an adventurer needs to get started.

## Wares

Provisioners stock the adventurer's basics: backpacks, torches and lanterns, bedrolls, kindling, food, bottles, and simple tools. Their famous willingness to buy almost anything also made them the classic fence for a new character's first loot.

## Era note

The provisioner is among the oldest NPC types in the game, present in the towns since the earliest eras of Britannia.

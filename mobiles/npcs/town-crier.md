# Town Crier

The news-bearers of the classic towns, shouting announcements in the streets. Town criers are the broadcast system of old Britannia.

## Crying news

Town criers shout town news and event announcements to all in earshot. Community recollection holds that players could pay a crier to broadcast their own messages — the original public announcement system.

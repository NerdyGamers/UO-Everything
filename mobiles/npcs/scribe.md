# Scribe

Town shopkeepers of the written word, selling blank scrolls, books, and writing supplies, and teaching [inscription](../../skills/magic/inscription.md).

## Wares

Scribes sell blank scrolls by the bundle — the raw material of every spellbook — along with books and writing materials.

## Training

They teach inscription and evaluating intelligence to new students for the usual fee. Mages and the mage guildmaster teach the same skills.

# Bowyer

Town shopkeepers of the archery trade, selling bows, crossbows, and arrows. In classic UO there is no separate fletcher NPC — the bowyer covers bows and fletching alike.

## Wares

Bowyers stock bows, crossbows, heavy crossbows, and arrows in quantity. Archers resupply here, and the stock refreshes over time.

## Training

They teach archery and bowcraft/fletching to new students for the usual fee. Rangers and the ranger guildmaster teach archery as well.

# Shipwright

The boat-builders of the dockside districts, selling ships to sailors and adventurers bound for the high seas.

## Wares

Shipwrights sell ship deeds — small boats, sloops, and larger vessels — the keys to Britannia's oceans. Every sea voyage begins at the shipwright's counter.

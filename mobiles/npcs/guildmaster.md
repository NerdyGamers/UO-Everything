# Guildmaster

The masters of the trade guilds — mage, warrior, thief, ranger, bard, tinker, tailor, blacksmith, miner, fisher, and merchant guildmasters — who train their guild's skills to higher levels than ordinary teachers.

## Training

Guildmasters teach their guild's skills up to 40.0 — well beyond what a regular NPC teacher offers — for the standard fee of about 10 gold per point. The mage guildmaster, for example, is the classic route to a starting magery foundation, and the warrior guildmaster covers the melee skills.

## Guildhalls

Guildmasters stand in their guildhalls, found in the larger towns. Joining a guild is a rite of passage for new characters, and the guildmaster is its face.

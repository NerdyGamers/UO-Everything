# Mapmaker

Town shopkeepers of the cartographer's trade, selling maps and sea charts and teaching [cartography](../../skills/misc/cartography.md).

## Wares

Mapmakers sell local maps, sea charts, and blank scrolls for the cartographer's pen. Treasure hunters and sailors are their regulars.

## Training

They teach cartography to new students for the usual fee.

# Alchemist

Town shopkeepers who sell alchemy reagents, empty bottles, and finished potions, and teach [alchemy](../../skills/magic/alchemy.md) to new students.

## Wares

Alchemists stock the classic reagents — black pearl, blood moss, garlic, ginseng, mandrake root, nightshade, spider's silk, and sulfurous ash — along with mortars and pestles and a rotating shelf of potions. Stock and prices vary by town; community recollection holds that some alchemists carried the rarer Pagan reagents in the earliest eras.

## Training

Greeting an alchemist and asking to train teaches alchemy, usually up to a modest skill level for a fee of about 10 gold per point. Gypsies, herbalists, and tinkers teach the same skill to comparable levels.

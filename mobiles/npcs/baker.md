# Baker

Town shopkeepers who sell bread, baked goods, and cooking supplies. A staple of every city's food trade alongside butchers and cooks.

## Wares

Bakers stock loaves of bread, cakes, muffins, and kindred baked goods, plus flour and other [cooking](../../skills/crafting/cooking.md) staples. Hungry travelers and roleplayers keep them in business.

## Training

They teach cooking to new students for the usual fee, as do cooks and herbalists.

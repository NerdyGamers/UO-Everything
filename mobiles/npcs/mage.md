# Mage

Town shopkeepers of the arcane trade, selling reagents and scrolls, and teaching [magery](../../skills/magic/magery.md). Found in mage shops and guild districts across the towns.

## Wares

Mages sell the full reagent shelf and a selection of spell scrolls — the consumables every spellcaster burns through. Their shops are as vital to mages as smithies are to warriors.

## Training

They teach magery, evaluating intelligence, inscription, meditation, and resisting spells to new students, typically to higher starting levels than most teachers. The mage guildmaster teaches the same skills further.

## Wandering mages

Wandering mages roam the roads and appear as escort quest-givers, distinct from the shopkeepers. They teach meditation to travelers who ask.

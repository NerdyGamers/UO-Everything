# NPCs

The non-player characters of Britannia's towns and roads — the shopkeepers, healers, guards, and wanderers who make the world feel lived in. These are the classic service NPCs of the OSI towns: they sell goods, teach skills, resurrect the dead, guard the peace, and hand out escort work. For the monstrous humanoids, see [humanoids](../humanoids/README.md).

# Fisherman

Town and dockside NPCs of the fishing trade, selling the day's catch and teaching [fishing](../../skills/gathering/fishing.md).

## Wares

Fishermen sell raw fish of the common varieties — a cheap food source and, for cooks, an ingredient. Their stalls cluster around docks and harbors.

## Training

They teach fishing to new students for the usual fee, as does the fisher guildmaster. Wandering healers are also said to teach fishing to travelers.

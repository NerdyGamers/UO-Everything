# Ranger

Woodsmen of the wilds, at home on the forest paths between towns. Rangers are among the most capable wandering teachers in the game.

## Training

Rangers teach archery, animal taming, animal lore, tracking, camping, and hiding — the full woodsman's curriculum — each to a respectable level for the usual fee. The ranger guildmaster teaches the same skills further.

## Role

Rangers patrol the wilderness rather than keeping shops, and their breadth of teaching makes them a favorite stop for new characters building outdoor skills.

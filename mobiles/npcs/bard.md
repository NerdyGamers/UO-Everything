# Bard

Town NPCs of the musical trade who sell instruments and teach the bardic arts. Found in taverns and cultural districts.

## Wares

Bards sell the classic instruments — lutes, lap harps, tambourines, and drums. A new bard character's first stop is usually the local bard's shop.

## Training

They teach [musicianship](../../skills/bard/musicianship.md), peacemaking, provocation, and discordance to new students for the usual fee. The bard guildmaster teaches the same skills to higher levels.

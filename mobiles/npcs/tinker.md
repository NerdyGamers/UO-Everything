# Tinker

Town tinkerers selling tools, lockpicks, and clever devices, and teaching [tinkering](../../skills/crafting/tinkering.md).

## Wares

Tinkers sell the tools of a dozen trades — lockpicks, tools, scales, and ingenious gadgets. Their shops are the hardware stores of Britannia.

## Training

They teach tinkering and alchemy to new students for the usual fee, as does the tinker guildmaster.

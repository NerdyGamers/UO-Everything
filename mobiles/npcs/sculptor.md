# Sculptor

Town artisans carving statues and decorative stonework. Part of the cultural trades with artists.

## Wares

Sculptors sell statues and decorative pieces for houses and gardens — the ornament trade of Britannia.

## Training

They teach [anatomy](../../skills/combat/anatomy.md) to those who ask, alongside artists, healers, and bloodletters.

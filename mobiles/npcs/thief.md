# Thief

Light-fingered rogues who wander the towns, notorious for stealing from unwary adventurers. A hazard of city life since the classic eras.

## Behavior

Thieves lift gold and items from passing players — the reason experienced travelers keep valuables in their bank boxes. They are a classic town nuisance rather than a shopkeeper.

## Training

Thieves teach the rogue's curriculum — stealing, snooping, lockpicking, poisoning, hiding, stealth, remove trap, and detecting hidden — to those who ask, as does the thief guildmaster.

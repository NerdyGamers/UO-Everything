# Tavern Keeper

The hosts of Britannia's taverns, distinct from the player-placeable [barkeeper](barkeeper.md). Tavern keepers are fixtures of the city's drinking establishments.

## Services

Tavern keepers sell food and drink and keep the tavern lively. Community recollection holds that tavern keepers, like innkeepers, sold the "contract of employment" used to place player vendors — about 1500 gold, per the classic vendor guides.

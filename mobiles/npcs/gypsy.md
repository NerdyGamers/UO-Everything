# Gypsy

Wandering road-folk found in camps and along the highways of [Britannia](../../locations/britannia.md). Gypsies are among the most versatile skill teachers in the game.

## Training

Gypsies teach a remarkable range of skills — alchemy, begging, camping, hiding, and more — each to a modest level for the usual fee. Their camps are worth seeking out for a new character building a foundation.

## Variants

Gypsy animal trainers and gypsy fortune tellers appear at the camps, teaching the animal and medical skills respectively.

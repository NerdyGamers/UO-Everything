# Guard

The armed protectors of Britannia's towns — warrior guards with halberds and archer guards with bows — who enforce the peace of the guard zones. Shouting "guards!" in town brings swift and lethal justice.

## Guard zones

Inside town guard zones, calling "guards" summons a guard who instantly strikes down criminals and murderers. This made the classic towns safe havens and shaped the game's risk geography — danger lived outside the walls.

## Variants

Warrior guards patrol on foot with polearms; archer guards cover from range. Order and Chaos guards stand watch at the Order and Chaos shrines, relics of the faction wars.

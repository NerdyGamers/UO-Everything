# Weaponsmith

Town smiths specializing in weapons rather than armor. The warrior's armory alongside the blacksmith and armorer.

## Wares

Weaponsmiths sell swords, axes, maces, polearms, and daggers of serviceable quality — the standard armory stock for new fighters.

## Training

They teach [blacksmithy](../../skills/crafting/blacksmithy.md) and arms lore to new students for the usual fee.

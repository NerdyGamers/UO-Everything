# Farmer

Rural NPCs working the fields outside the towns, selling produce and teaching the occasional hardy skill.

## Wares

Farmers sell fruits, vegetables, and farm goods — the raw ingredients of [cooking](../../skills/crafting/cooking.md). Their farms dot the countryside of [Britannia](../../locations/britannia.md).

## Training

Unusually, farmers teach resisting spells ([magic resistance](../../skills/magic/magic-resistance.md)) to those who ask — to a low level, per community recollection — alongside the occasional other rustic skill.

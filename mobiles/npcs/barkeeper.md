# Barkeeper

Player-placeable tavern NPCs created from a barkeep deed. Unlike the town [tavern keeper](tavern-keeper.md), who is a fixture of the city's tavern, a barkeeper serves in a player's own house.

## Placement

Community recollection holds that a barkeep deed, once placed inside a house the placer owns, creates a barkeeper who can be named, dressed, and given a custom greeting. Barkeeps serve drinks and add tavern atmosphere to player-run establishments.

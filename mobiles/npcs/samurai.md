# Samurai

Warriors of the Tokuno Islands, added with the Samurai Empire expansion. Samurai NPCs teach [bushido](../../skills/combat/bushido.md), the way of the honorable warrior.

## Training

Samurai teach bushido to those who ask, for the usual fee — the gateway skill to the expansion's warrior arts.

## Era note

Samurai are a Samurai Empire era addition, found in Zento and the [Tokuno Islands](../../locations/tokuno-islands.md) rather than the classic Britannian towns.

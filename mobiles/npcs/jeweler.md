# Jeweler

Town shopkeepers of the gem and jewelry trade, selling rings, bracelets, necklaces, and earrings.

## Wares

Jewelers stock the classic jewelry types in gold and silver, set with the gem varieties of the realm. Beyond adornment, their wares feed the appraisal trade.

## Training

They teach item identification to those who ask — the skill of appraising loot — alongside bankers and merchants.

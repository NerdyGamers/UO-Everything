# Artist

Town NPCs of the painter's trade, found in the cultural districts of the larger cities. Artists teach [anatomy](../../skills/combat/anatomy.md) to those who ask, alongside bloodletters, healers, and sculptors.

## Role

Artists are flavor NPCs as much as anything — part of the living-town backdrop that made the classic cities feel inhabited. Their stock, where they keep a shop, runs to the decorative rather than the practical.

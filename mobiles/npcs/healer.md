# Healer

The beloved healers of Britannia, in two classic forms: shopkeeper healers who run town clinics, and wandering healers who roam the roads with their staves. Both resurrect the dead — the most important service in the game.

## Resurrection

Healers resurrect dead adventurers, returning them to life with their belongings. Shop healers work from town clinics; wandering healers roam the wilderness, a welcome sight to a stranded ghost. Community recollection holds that wandering healers ask a modest fee, scaled to the character's standing.

## Healing services

Shop healers cure poison and disease and sell bandages — the tools of the [healing](../../skills/combat/healing.md) skill. They teach anatomy and healing to students, as does the healer guildmaster.

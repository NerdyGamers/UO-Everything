# Hireable

Mercenary NPCs — fighters, mages, thieves, bards, rangers, paladins, sailors, peasants, and even beggars — who can be hired as followers for a daily wage. A classic way for a lone adventurer to buy some company in a fight.

## Hiring

Saying the right words to a hireable NPC engages their services for a daily gold wage, deducted automatically. They follow, fight beside their employer, and can be dismissed. Fighters and paladins are the classic bodyguards; mages, bards, rangers, and thieves offer lighter support.

## Era note

Hirelings are a classic system, present since the early eras of the game.

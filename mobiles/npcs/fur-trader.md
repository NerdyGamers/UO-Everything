# Fur Trader

Frontiersmen dealing in furs, hides, and the products of the hunt. Found at the edges of towns and in wilderness outposts.

## Wares

Fur traders buy and sell furs and hides — the raw material of leatherworking. Hunters and tamers are their natural customers.

## Training

They teach animal lore, animal taming, and [camping](../../skills/wilderness/camping.md) to new students for the usual fee.

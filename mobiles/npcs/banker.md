# Banker

The keepers of every town bank, and among the most-visited NPCs in the game. Bankers give access to each character's bank box — the secure storage every adventurer depends on.

## Banking

Saying "bank" near a banker opens the character's bank box, where gold, checks, and valuables can be stored safely. Bankers stand behind the counter of the bank building found in nearly every town across [Britannia](../../locations/britannia.md).

## Training

Bankers teach item identification to those who ask, alongside jewelers and merchants — a handy skill for sorting loot.

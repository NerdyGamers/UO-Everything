# Unicorn

The unicorn is the pure white horned steed of the deep forest — a creature of light that judges the hearts of those it meets.

## Behavior

Unicorns attack travelers with negative karma on sight, goring them with their horns. Like wisps, they are drawn to punish the wicked rather than threaten the innocent.

## Habitat

Deep forest groves in Trammel and Felucca.

## Notes

Unicorns are tamable with [Animal Taming](../../skills/wilderness/animal-taming.md), but only female characters can tame and ride them — the counterpart to the kirin, which only males can tame and ride (see [Kirin](kirin.md)). Tamed unicorns are immune to poison and will attempt to cure their owner when poisoned. They are vulnerable to the fey slayer property.

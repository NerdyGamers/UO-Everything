# Fey and Magical Beasts

The fey are Britannia's magical wild creatures — beings of the deep forest and the elven lands, bound together by the **fey slayer** property. Their heartland is the [Twisted Weald](../../locations/ilshenar.md) in Ilshenar, Mondain's Legacy's fey forest. Karma runs through the group like a fault line: wisps and unicorns attack the wicked on sight, while changelings and their kin are evil-aligned predators.

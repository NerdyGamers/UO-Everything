# Kirin

The kirin — sometimes spelled ki-rin — is the eastern unicorn: a scaled, dragon-like steed of legend, swift and steeped in magic.

## Behavior

Kirin are spellcasting creatures that defend themselves with magic as well as hooves. When tamed and ridden, a kirin will call down the forces of nature — a bolt of lightning — against anything that badly wounds its rider.

## Habitat

Ilshenar, notably the Ki-Rin Passage, and the [Tokuno Islands](../../locations/tokuno-islands.md).

## Notes

Kirin are tamable with [Animal Taming](../../skills/wilderness/animal-taming.md). Only male characters can tame and ride ki-rins — the counterpart to the unicorn, which only females can tame and ride (see [Unicorn](unicorn.md)). They are vulnerable to the fey slayer property.

# Pixie

Pixies are small winged fey, quick and mischievous, and far more dangerous than their size suggests.

## Behavior

Pixies are aggressive spellcasters that attack travelers with offensive magic. They are often encountered in groups, where several pixies casting at once can overwhelm the unprepared.

## Habitat

The [Twisted Weald](../../locations/ilshenar.md) in Ilshenar, Mondain's Legacy's fey heartland.

## Notes

Pixies are not tamable. They fall under the fey slayer property.

# Wisp

A wisp is a floating orb of living light that drifts through Britannia's deep forests, trailing a soft blue-white glow.

## Behavior

Wisps are peaceful toward travelers of good standing, but they attack anyone with negative karma on sight. They strike from a distance with bolts of energy and are difficult to hit thanks to their small, floating form.

## Habitat

Forest groves, most famously the woods around Yew. They appear in both Trammel and Felucca.

## Notes

Wisps are not tamable. They are vulnerable to the fey slayer property, which groups them with the other magical creatures of the wilds.

# Centaur

Centaurs are half-human, half-horse fey warriors — proud, territorial, and deadly at range.

## Behavior

Centaurs attack with bows, raining arrows on intruders before they can close the distance. They are aggressive and stand their ground rather than fleeing.

## Habitat

The [Twisted Weald](../../locations/ilshenar.md) in Ilshenar, with some found in deep forest elsewhere.

## Notes

Centaurs are not tamable. They are vulnerable to the fey slayer property.

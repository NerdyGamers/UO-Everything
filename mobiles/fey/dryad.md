# Dryad

Dryads are tree-spirits of the deep forest — fey bound to the woodlands they protect.

## Behavior

Dryads are aggressive spellcasters that attack intruders with magic. Like the other fey of the Weald, they defend their territory fiercely.

## Habitat

The [Twisted Weald](../../locations/ilshenar.md) in Ilshenar.

## Notes

Dryads are not tamable. They are vulnerable to the fey slayer property.

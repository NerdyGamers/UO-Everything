# Reptalon

The reptalon is a lesser dragon-kin — a reptilian predator with draconic blood, smaller than a true dragon but no less fierce.

## Behavior

Reptalons attack with claws, teeth, and a breath of fire. They are aggressive hunters and hit hard for their size.

## Habitat

The [Twisted Weald](../../locations/ilshenar.md) in Ilshenar.

## Notes

Reptalons are tamable with [Animal Taming](../../skills/wilderness/animal-taming.md), requiring high skill. They make strong, hard-hitting pets for tamers who prefer a dragon-kin companion.

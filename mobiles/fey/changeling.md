# Changeling

The changeling is a shapeshifting fey of the swamps, known for its Mimic ability — it takes the form of other creatures, making it difficult to know what you are truly facing.

## Behavior

Changelings are evil-aligned and highly dangerous spellcasters. They attack with powerful magic and use their mimicry to confuse prey. They are among the most feared inhabitants of the Weald.

## Habitat

The swamps of the [Twisted Weald](../../locations/ilshenar.md) in Ilshenar.

## Notes

Changelings are not tamable. Several named changelings roam the Weald — Spite, Guile, and Irk. Irk is notable for carrying "Irk's Brain," a key used in the [Dread Horn peerless encounter](../../mechanics/peerless-encounters.md). Changelings fall under the fey slayer property.

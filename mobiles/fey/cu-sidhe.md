# Cu Sidhe

The cu sidhe — "fairy dog" in the old elven tongue — is a great white hound of the elves, and one of the most prized tamable creatures in Britannia.

## Behavior

In the wild, cu sidhe are powerful and aggressive predators. They fight with tooth and claw and are far tougher than their sleek appearance suggests.

## Habitat

The [Twisted Weald](../../locations/ilshenar.md) in Ilshenar, the elven continent.

## Notes

Cu sidhe are tamable with [Animal Taming](../../skills/wilderness/animal-taming.md), requiring high skill. They are rideable, share the canine pack instinct, and are known for their healing gift: a cu sidhe will heal its own wounds and attempt to heal its owner when the owner is hurt. This makes them one of the strongest and most sought-after pets in the game.

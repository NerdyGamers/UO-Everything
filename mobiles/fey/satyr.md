# Satyr

Satyrs are goat-legged fey revelers — wild, aggressive, and dangerous when encountered in their home woods.

## Behavior

Satyrs are fierce fighters that attack travelers on sight. Community recollection describes them as capable ranged and melee combatants that punish anyone wandering the Weald unprepared.

## Habitat

The [Twisted Weald](../../locations/ilshenar.md) in Ilshenar.

## Notes

Satyrs are not tamable. They fall under the fey slayer property.

# Meer Warrior

The defenders of the Meer, graceful fighters holding the line against the Juka invasion.

## Behavior

Meer warriors are skilled melee combatants, quicker and more agile than their Juka counterparts. They fight in defense of their groves and settlements. Exact stats are community recollection.

## Habitat

Meer lands in [Ilshenar](../../locations/ilshenar.md), around Lakeshire and the contested borderlands.

## Loot

Gold and finely made weapons and armor, with a fair chance of magic items.

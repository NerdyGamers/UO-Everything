# Orcish Mage

The spellcasting caste of orc-kind, frail in melee but dangerous at range.

## Behavior

Orcish mages cast offensive spells at their targets while avoiding close combat. They are the priority target in any orc pack — kill the mage first, then mop up the melee fighters. Vulnerable to Repond-type [slayer](../../items/item-properties.md) weapons.

## Habitat

Found with other orcs in the Orc Fort near [Yew](../../locations/britannia.md) and the Orc Cave in the [Lost Lands](../../locations/lost-lands.md).

## Loot

Gold, reagents, and magic items; mages sometimes carry [spellbooks](../../items/spellbooks.md) and scrolls.

# Ratman Mage

The spellcasting caste of rat-kind, lending magical support to the swarm.

## Behavior

Ratman mages cast offensive spells from behind the pack's melee fighters. Like the [orcish mage](orcish-mage.md), they are the priority target — frail up close, dangerous at range. Vulnerable to ratman-slaying properties where available.

## Habitat

Ratman warrens in dungeon upper levels, notably [Shame](../../locations/dungeons.md).

## Loot

Gold, reagents, and the occasional magic item or scroll.

# Orc Captain

A veteran orc leader, stronger and better armed than the rank and file of its tribe.

## Behavior

Orc captains lead orc packs and fight as heavy melee combatants. They are durable opponents for mid-level warriors and hit hard enough to punish the careless. Vulnerable to Repond-type [slayer](../../items/item-properties.md) weapons.

## Habitat

Orc camps and forts, notably the Orc Fort near [Yew](../../locations/britannia.md) and orc holdings in the [Lost Lands](../../locations/lost-lands.md).

## Loot

Gold, better-than-average weapons and armor, and a reasonable chance of magic items — captains are worth checking.

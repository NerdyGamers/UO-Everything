# Ophidian Avenger

Serpentine warriors of the Ophidian zealots, locked in holy war with the Terathans.

## Behavior

Ophidian avengers are aggressive melee fighters, striking fast with bladed weapons. They are the rank and file of the Ophidian crusade and attack intruders on sight. Vulnerable to reptile-slaying [slayer](../../items/item-properties.md) properties where available.

## Habitat

The Ophidian Lair and surrounding war-torn lands of the [Lost Lands](../../locations/lost-lands.md).

## Loot

Gold and ophidian-crafted weapons and armor, with the usual chance of magic items.

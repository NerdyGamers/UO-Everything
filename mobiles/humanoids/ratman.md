# Ratman

Rat-like humanoids that swarm the upper levels of Britannia's dungeons in chittering packs.

## Behavior

Ratmen are quick, aggressive melee fighters that attack in groups. Individually weak, they are dangerous when they surround a target. They have no spells, though their packs can include more specialized kin. Vulnerable to ratman-slaying properties where available.

## Habitat

Dungeon upper levels — [Shame](../../locations/dungeons.md) is the classic ratman warren.

## Loot

Gold and low-end gear; ratmen are hunted more for sport and slayer progress than profit.

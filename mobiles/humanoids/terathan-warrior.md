# Terathan Warrior

Insectoid soldiers of the Terathan hive, the Ophidians' ancient enemies.

## Behavior

Terathan warriors are fast, aggressive melee fighters with chitinous natural armor. They swarm intruders near their hive and hit harder than their size suggests. Vulnerable to arachnid-slaying [slayer](../../items/item-properties.md) properties where available.

## Habitat

Terathan Keep and the hive lands of the [Lost Lands](../../locations/lost-lands.md).

## Loot

Gold and terathan chitin pieces, plus the usual chance of magic items.

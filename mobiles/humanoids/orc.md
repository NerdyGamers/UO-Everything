# Orc

Pig-faced tribal humanoids and one of Britannia's oldest monster staples, found in war camps and caves across the world.

## Behavior

Orcs are straightforward melee fighters that attack on sight, often spawning in groups around their camps. They fight with crude weapons and little tactics, making them manageable for young warriors but dangerous in numbers. They take extra damage from Repond-type [slayer](../../items/item-properties.md) weapons.

## Habitat

The most famous orc stronghold is the Orc Fort north of [Yew](../../locations/britannia.md), with additional camps and the Orc Cave in the [Lost Lands](../../locations/lost-lands.md).

## Loot

Gold, low-end weapons and armor, and occasionally minor magic items. Orc camps are a classic early hunting ground.

# Kobold

Small, cunning humanoids of the deep places, known for mischief as much as malice.

## Behavior

Kobolds are quick, sneaky melee fighters that prefer ambush and numbers over open battle. They are individually weak but can overwhelm the unwary in their warrens. Details of their combat abilities are community recollection — verify in game.

## Habitat

Deep underground regions, associated with the Stygian Abyss era's underworld areas near [Ter Mur](../../locations/termur.md).

## Loot

Gold and small weapons; kobolds are minor quarry.

# Ratman Archer

Rat-kind skirmishers that harass intruders with ranged attacks.

## Behavior

Ratman archers fire arrows from the back of the pack while their melee kin close in. They force adventurers to deal with ranged pressure alongside the swarm. Vulnerable to ratman-slaying properties where available.

## Habitat

Ratman warrens in dungeon upper levels, notably [Shame](../../locations/dungeons.md).

## Loot

Gold, bows and arrows, and occasional low-end magic items.

# Juka Lord

A commander of the Juka war host, among the most dangerous humanoids of Ilshenar.

## Behavior

Juka lords are elite warriors with high health and heavy damage output, leading Juka forces into battle. Only well-prepared adventurers should challenge them. Exact abilities are community recollection — verify in game.

## Habitat

Juka strongholds in [Ilshenar](../../locations/ilshenar.md), in the war-torn Exodus lands.

## Loot

Substantial gold and quality magic items, fitting a warlord.

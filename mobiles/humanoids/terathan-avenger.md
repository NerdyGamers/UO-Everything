# Terathan Avenger

Elite soldiers of the Terathan hive, deadlier kin of the common warrior.

## Behavior

Terathan avengers are tougher, harder-hitting versions of terathan warriors, leading hive defense. They are fast and relentless in melee. Vulnerable to arachnid-slaying [slayer](../../items/item-properties.md) properties where available.

## Habitat

The depths of Terathan Keep in the [Lost Lands](../../locations/lost-lands.md).

## Loot

Gold, terathan chitin, and a good chance of magic items.

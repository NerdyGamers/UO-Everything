# Ophidian Shaman

The priest-casters of the Ophidian faith, guiding their warriors' crusade with serpent magic.

## Behavior

Ophidian shamans cast offensive spells in support of avenger war parties. Like other humanoid casters, they are the priority target — dangerous at range, frail in melee. Vulnerable to reptile-slaying [slayer](../../items/item-properties.md) properties where available.

## Habitat

The Ophidian Lair in the [Lost Lands](../../locations/lost-lands.md).

## Loot

Gold, reagents, and magic items.

# Juka Mage

Spellcasters of the militaristic Juka, the warlike race locked in ancient conflict with the Meer.

## Behavior

Juka mages support Juka war parties with offensive magic, casting from behind their warriors. They are disciplined fighters — take them out before they can wear you down with spells. Their exact resistances and spell lists are community recollection.

## Habitat

[Ilshenar](../../locations/ilshenar.md), in the territories of the Juka war with the Meer, particularly around the Exodus lands.

## Loot

Gold, reagents, and Juka-associated gear and magic items.

# Titan

Towering giants among the most powerful common humanoids in Britannia.

## Behavior

Titans are elite melee juggernauts with enormous health and crushing attacks. Only well-equipped adventurers should engage them, and even then the fight is long. They have no ranged or magical offense. Vulnerable to Repond-type [slayer](../../items/item-properties.md) weapons.

## Habitat

The deepest dungeon reaches — titans dwell in the depths of [Despise](../../locations/dungeons.md).

## Loot

Rich gold and a strong chance of high-quality magic items; titans are one of the classic lucrative humanoid hunts.

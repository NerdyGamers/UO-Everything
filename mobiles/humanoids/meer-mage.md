# Meer Mage

Spellcasters of the Meer, the nature-attuned race defending their lands against the Juka.

## Behavior

Meer mages weave nature-flavored magic in defense of their people, supporting Meer warriors much as Juka mages support theirs. They are dangerous at range and frail up close. Exact spell lists are community recollection.

## Habitat

Meer territories in [Ilshenar](../../locations/ilshenar.md), notably around Lakeshire and the lands contested with the Juka.

## Loot

Gold, reagents, and Meer-associated magic items.

# Troll

Large, brutish humanoids known for shrugging off wounds, a classic dungeon-dweller of early Britannia.

## Behavior

Trolls are slow but heavy melee hitters. They are famously resilient, soaking up damage that would fell lesser creatures. Keep distance or bring strong offense; they have no ranged attack. Vulnerable to Repond-type [slayer](../../items/item-properties.md) weapons, and to troll-slaying properties where available.

## Habitat

Mountainous regions and dungeons — most famously [Despise](../../locations/dungeons.md), where trolls have lurked since launch.

## Loot

Gold and occasional magic items; trolls are a steady, unglamorous hunt.

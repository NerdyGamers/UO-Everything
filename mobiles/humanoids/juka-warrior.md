# Juka Warrior

The disciplined foot soldiers of the Juka, a martial race bred for war against the Meer.

## Behavior

Juka warriors are tough, organized melee fighters that hold formation better than most humanoids. They hit hard and take punishment well — treat them as elite orc-kind in terms of threat. Their exact stats are community recollection.

## Habitat

[Ilshenar](../../locations/ilshenar.md), in Juka-held lands of the war with the Meer, around the Exodus territories.

## Loot

Gold and well-made weapons and armor, with a fair chance of magic items.

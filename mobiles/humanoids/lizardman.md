# Lizardman

Reptilian humanoids that haunt swamps and the upper reaches of dungeons.

## Behavior

Lizardmen are agile melee fighters, quicker than they look. They attack in small groups and are a staple of early dungeon exploration. Vulnerable to reptile-slaying [slayer](../../items/item-properties.md) properties where available.

## Habitat

Swampy regions and dungeon entrances — [Despise](../../locations/dungeons.md) and the swamps of the [Lost Lands](../../locations/lost-lands.md), among others.

## Loot

Gold and low-to-mid-tier weapons and armor.

# Savage Rider

A savage warrior mounted on a savage ridgeback, combining cavalry speed with tribal ferocity.

## Behavior

Savage riders charge their enemies on ridgeback mounts, hitting hard in the initial clash. Slaying the rider leaves the mount behind — savage ridgebacks are tamable by skilled tamers (see [Animal Taming](../../skills/wilderness/animal-taming.md)). Rider stats are community recollection.

## Habitat

Savage tribal lands in [Ilshenar](../../locations/ilshenar.md) and the [Lost Lands](../../locations/lost-lands.md).

## Loot

Gold and tribal gear; the real prize is often the tamable ridgeback itself.

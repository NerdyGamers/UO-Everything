# Humanoids

The humanoid family covers Britannia's tribal and giant-kin monsters: orcs, ogres, trolls, ratmen, the warring races of the Lost Lands and Ilshenar, and a few oddities. Many fall under dedicated [slayer](../../items/item-properties.md) groups — orc slaying, ogre thrashing, troll slaughter, and ratman slaying properties target their respective kin, while the broader Repond group covers the classic giant and orc breeds.

## Orcs

- [Orc](orc.md) — tribal pig-faced warriors of the Yew Orc Fort and Lost Lands
- [Orc Brute](orc-brute.md) — a larger, harder-hitting breed of orc
- [Orc Captain](orc-captain.md) — veteran leader of orc war parties
- [Orcish Lord](orcish-lord.md) — powerful orc chieftain
- [Orcish Mage](orcish-mage.md) — spellcasting caste; kill it first

## Giants

- [Troll](troll.md) — resilient dungeon brutes, famous in Despise
- [Ettin](ettin.md) — two-headed giant that strikes twice as fast
- [Ogre](ogre.md) — massive club-wielding heavy hitter
- [Ogre Lord](ogre-lord.md) — chieftain of ogre-kind, a classic high-end hunt
- [Cyclops](cyclops.md) — one-eyed giant of the deep dungeons
- [Titan](titan.md) — towering juggernaut among the strongest common humanoids

## Ratmen

- [Ratman](ratman.md) — swarming rat-like humanoids of Shame's upper levels
- [Ratman Mage](ratman-mage.md) — spellcasting support for the pack
- [Ratman Archer](ratman-archer.md) — ranged skirmishers of the warren

## Juka and Meer

- [Juka Mage](juka-mage.md) — spellcasters of the militaristic Juka
- [Juka Warrior](juka-warrior.md) — disciplined foot soldiers of the Juka war host
- [Juka Lord](juka-lord.md) — commander of the Juka, a dangerous foe
- [Meer Mage](meer-mage.md) — nature-attuned casters defending Meer lands
- [Meer Warrior](meer-warrior.md) — graceful defenders against the Juka

## Ophidians and Terathans

- [Ophidian Avenger](ophidian-avenger.md) — serpentine zealot warriors of the holy war
- [Ophidian Shaman](ophidian-shaman.md) — priest-casters of the Ophidian faith
- [Terathan Warrior](terathan-warrior.md) — insectoid hive soldiers
- [Terathan Avenger](terathan-avenger.md) — elite defenders of Terathan Keep

## Savages

- [Savage](savage.md) — fierce tribal warriors of the savage lands
- [Savage Rider](savage-rider.md) — mounted savage; its ridgeback is tamable
- [Savage Shaman](savage-shaman.md) — tribal spellcasters backing the warriors

## Misc

- [Lizardman](lizardman.md) — reptilian humanoids of swamps and dungeon mouths

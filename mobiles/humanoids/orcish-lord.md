# Orcish Lord

A powerful orc chieftain, among the toughest of the orc-kind humanoids.

## Behavior

Orcish lords are elite melee fighters with high health and heavy hits, dangerous to all but well-prepared warriors. They lead the largest orc war parties. Vulnerable to Repond-type [slayer](../../items/item-properties.md) weapons.

## Habitat

The deepest parts of orc strongholds — the Orc Fort near [Yew](../../locations/britannia.md) and the Orc Cave in the [Lost Lands](../../locations/lost-lands.md).

## Loot

Substantial gold and a good chance of magic weapons and armor, fitting for a chieftain.

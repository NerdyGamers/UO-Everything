# Cyclops

One-eyed giants of the deep dungeons, strong melee fighters with a fearsome reputation.

## Behavior

Cyclopes are powerful, straightforward melee combatants — slow, hard-hitting, and very durable. They have no spells or ranged attacks, so the fight is a test of damage output and survivability. Vulnerable to Repond-type [slayer](../../items/item-properties.md) weapons.

## Habitat

Deep dungeon levels, most famously [Despise](../../locations/dungeons.md), with some populations in the [Lost Lands](../../locations/lost-lands.md).

## Loot

Gold and a solid chance of magic items; cyclopes are a worthwhile mid-to-high-end hunt.

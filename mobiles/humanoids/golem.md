# Golem

A clockwork construct of metal and magic, built by tinkers to serve as a guardian.

## Behavior

Golems are slow, immensely durable melee fighters. They are constructs rather than living creatures, so they are immune to many effects that target the living (poison and similar, by community recollection). Player-crafted golems can be controlled by their makers; wild ones attack intruders.

## Habitat

Crafted by tinkers anywhere; wild golems are found guarding ruins and dungeons.

## Loot

Ingots and mechanical parts — golems are sometimes harvested for their metal rather than hunted for gold.

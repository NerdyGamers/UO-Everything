# Savage Shaman

The tribal spellcasters of the savages, backing their warriors with primitive magic.

## Behavior

Savage shamans cast offensive spells from behind the tribe's warriors. As with other humanoid casters, kill the shaman first — they are frail in melee but punishing at range. Exact abilities are community recollection.

## Habitat

Savage tribal lands in [Ilshenar](../../locations/ilshenar.md) and the [Lost Lands](../../locations/lost-lands.md).

## Loot

Gold, reagents, and occasional magic items.

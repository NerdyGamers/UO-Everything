# Ogre

Massive club-wielding brutes, a step up from trolls in raw striking power.

## Behavior

Ogres are among the hardest-hitting common humanoids, capable of seriously hurting under-armored characters in a single blow. They are slow and have no ranged attack, so kiting or strong melee defense wins the day. Vulnerable to Repond-type [slayer](../../items/item-properties.md) weapons, and to ogre-thrashing properties where available.

## Habitat

Dungeons and wild lands — [Despise](../../locations/dungeons.md) is the classic ogre ground.

## Loot

Gold and weapons and armor, sometimes of decent quality.

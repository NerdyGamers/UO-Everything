# Orc Brute

A larger, tougher breed of orc that hits noticeably harder than the common variety.

## Behavior

Orc brutes are pure melee brawlers with higher strength and health than standard orcs. They still lack any ranged or magical ability, so the standard tactics apply: keep them at range or wear them down in melee. Vulnerable to Repond-type [slayer](../../items/item-properties.md) weapons.

## Habitat

Found alongside other orcs in the Orc Fort near [Yew](../../locations/britannia.md) and the Orc Cave in the [Lost Lands](../../locations/lost-lands.md).

## Loot

Gold and mid-tier melee weapons and armor, with a better chance of magic items than common orcs.

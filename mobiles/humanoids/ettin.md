# Ettin

A two-headed giant that attacks with both heads, effectively striking twice as often as a single-headed foe.

## Behavior

Ettins are aggressive melee fighters whose twin heads give them a rapid attack rate for their size. They are tough but lack any special abilities — a straightforward brawl. Vulnerable to Repond-type [slayer](../../items/item-properties.md) weapons.

## Habitat

Dungeons and wilderness giant territories, including [Despise](../../locations/dungeons.md).

## Loot

Gold and mid-tier weapons and armor, with the usual chance of magic items.

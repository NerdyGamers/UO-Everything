# Brigand

Human outlaws who ambush travelers on Britannia's roads and in its wilds.

## Behavior

Brigands are human bandits that attack on sight, using melee and sometimes archery. They often lurk near roads and spawn in camps, making them a hazard for the unwary traveler. Being human, they fall to humanoid-targeting effects rather than monster slayers.

## Habitat

Roads, forests, and hidden camps across [Britannia](../../locations/britannia.md) — wherever travelers pass.

## Loot

Gold and the weapons and armor they carry, sometimes of decent quality.

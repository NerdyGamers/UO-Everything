# Savage

Tribal warriors of the savage lands, fierce and territorial fighters.

## Behavior

Savages are aggressive melee combatants that attack intruders in their territory on sight. They fight with primitive weapons and considerable ferocity. Their exact stats are community recollection.

## Habitat

Savage tribal lands — the savage territories of [Ilshenar](../../locations/ilshenar.md) and parts of the [Lost Lands](../../locations/lost-lands.md).

## Loot

Gold, tribal weapons, and occasional magic items.

# Ogre Lord

The chieftain of ogre-kind: enormously strong, durable, and a genuine threat to seasoned adventurers.

## Behavior

Ogre lords hit with devastating force and absorb tremendous punishment. They are pure melee, but their damage output demands respect — bring healing, armor, or range. Vulnerable to Repond-type [slayer](../../items/item-properties.md) weapons.

## Habitat

The deeper reaches of giant-inhabited dungeons such as [Despise](../../locations/dungeons.md).

## Loot

Large amounts of gold and a good chance of quality magic items, befitting one of the classic high-end humanoid hunts.

# Valley of Eodon

The Valley of Eodon is the dinosaur-filled tribal landmass introduced with Time of Legends — a lost-world valley of jungles, tar pits, and warring tribes, capped by one of the game's most involved multi-stage encounters.

## What happens there

Eodon's wilderness teems with dinosaurs of every size, from raptors to the largest sauropods — prime [taming](../skills/wilderness/animal-taming.md) and hunting territory. The valley's tribes (including the Barrab and Jukari, per community documentation) war with each other and with the insectoid **Myrmidex**, who infest the Myrmidex Pit caverns.

The centerpiece is the **Shadowguard**: a multi-stage rooftop-style encounter (community recollection on its exact mechanics — verify against a current reference) that demands a coordinated group and rewards some of the era's best loot. Eodon's [quests](../quests/termur-quests.md)-adjacent tribal questlines introduce the valley's factions.

## Era notes

Introduced with Time of Legends (2015), the most recent full expansion landmass. Its encounter design — especially the Shadowguard — set the template for later multi-phase boss fights.

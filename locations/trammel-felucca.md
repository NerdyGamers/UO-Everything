# Trammel and Felucca

Trammel and Felucca are mirror facets sharing Britannia's geography under opposite rules: Trammel forbids non-consensual PvP, while Felucca is open PvP with greater rewards.

## How it works

**Trammel** (often "Tram") is the safe mirror: player killing is impossible, so it became the home of resource gathering, crafting, housing, and new players. New Haven sits here, and most everyday commerce happens on this side.

**Felucca** ("Fel") is the dangerous mirror: open player-vs-player combat, murder counts, and reds (see [Notoriety](../mechanics/notoriety.md)). In exchange, Felucca offers what Trammel doesn't: 105–120 power scrolls (120s are Felucca-only), [champion spawns](../mechanics/champion-spawns.md), higher resource yields, and faction warfare (see [Factions](../mechanics/factions.md)). Most endgame PvM and all serious PvP happen here.

Travel between the facets is via moongates and recall (see [Travel](travel.md)). The split means every Britannian location exists twice — a Trammel Britain and a Felucca Britain — with different populations, economies, and dangers.

## Era notes

Introduced with Ultima Online: Renaissance (2000) as the answer to rampant player killing: one facet kept the original open-PvP rules, the other made PvP consensual.

# Britannia

Britannia is the original continent of Ultima Online and still the heart of the game: the eight cities, the classic dungeons, and most of the landmarks players learn first all sit here.

## How it works

The eight cities anchor the continent: **Britain** (the capital and original trade hub), **Trinsic** (the paladin city), **Vesper**, **Minoc** (the mining town), **Yew** (the deep-woods town), **Skara Brae** (the ranger town), **Moonglow** (the mage city on Verity Isle), and **Jhelom** (the warrior town). Smaller settlements include Cove, Nujel'm, Serpent's Hold, and Ocllo.

Beneath the surface run the classic dungeons — Covetous, Deceit, Despise, Destard, Hythloth, Shame, and Wrong among them — each with its own creature mix and history (see [Dungeons](dungeons.md)).

Britannia exists on both the Trammel and Felucca facets: the same geography under different rules (see [Trammel and Felucca](trammel-felucca.md)). New Haven, the starter town with accelerated skill gain, sits on the Trammel side. Passages hidden in Britannia's dungeons lead down to the [Lost Lands](lost-lands.md), the subterranean facet added by The Second Age.

## Era notes

Britannia is the launch landmass (1997). The Second Age (1998) opened the Lost Lands beneath it; Renaissance (2000) split the continent across the Trammel/Felucca mirror.

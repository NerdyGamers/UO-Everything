# Ilshenar

Ilshenar is the elven continent introduced with Mondain's Legacy — a single-facet landmass of sacred groves, elven settlements, and some of the game's toughest dungeons.

## How it works

Ilshenar is home to the elves. **Heartwood**, the elven city, hosts the Heartwood quest hub and the Spellweaving initiation chain (see [Heartwood quests](../quests/heartwood-quests.md)).

The continent's dungeons anchor endgame PvM, most of them peerless encounters (see [Peerless encounters](../mechanics/peerless-encounters.md)): the **Twisted Weald** (Dread Horn), the **Palace of Paroxysmus** (Paroxysmus), **Bedlam** (Travesty), the **Blighted Grove** (Melisande), and the **Prism of Light** (Shimmering Effusion). The central dungeon holds Exodus (community recollection on the exact layout).

Facet rules are stricter here than elsewhere: recall and gating are restricted in Ilshenar's dungeons (community recollection on the exact boundaries), so expeditions generally go in on foot.

## Era notes

Added with Mondain's Legacy (2005) alongside the elven race and the Spellweaving skill.

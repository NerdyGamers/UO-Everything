# Tokuno Islands

The Tokuno Islands are the Samurai Empire archipelago: an island chain of dojos, bamboo forests, and Bushido/Ninjitsu content.

## How it works

The main islands are **Isamu-Jima** (home to Zento, the principal city), **Makoto-Jima**, and **Homare-Jima**. The islands are the birthplace of the [Bushido](../skills/combat/bushido.md) and [Ninjitsu](../skills/combat/ninjitsu.md) skills, and their aesthetics — paper walls, rock gardens, dojos — set them apart from every other landmass.

Notable sites include the **Fan Dancer Dojo** and the Yomotsu Mines, home to Yomotsu elementals and colored ore (community recollection on details). The islands have their own champion spawns (community recollection on the exact roster; see [Champion spawns](../mechanics/champion-spawns.md)). **Tokuno dyes** — special dyes from the islands — are prized by decorators (community recollection on acquisition).

## Era notes

Added with Samurai Empire (2004) alongside the Bushido and Ninjitsu skills.

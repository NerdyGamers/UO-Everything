# Travel

Getting around Britannia is a system in itself: moongates, recall magic, runebooks, holy journeys, and seafaring ships each move characters across facets and continents under different rules.

## How it works

- **Moongates** — the eight glowing gates scattered across Britannia. Stepping through sends you to another gate's location; in Trammel they link the facet's cities, and in Felucca they reach the same spots under Felucca rules. Gate destinations shift with the moon phases in classic style.
- **Recall, Mark, Gate Travel** — the [Magery](../skills/magic/magery.md) travel spells: Mark records a rune, Recall teleports the caster to it, Gate Travel opens a two-way gate for a party. Reagents required (or [Lower Reagent Cost](../items/item-properties.md)).
- **Runebooks and recall runes** — crafted by [scribes](../crafting/inscription-craft.md): runebooks hold marked runes and let casters recall or gate from the book directly, replacing pocketfuls of loose runes.
- **Sacred Journey** — the [Chivalry](../skills/magic/chivalry.md) travel spell, teleporting the paladin to shrines and virtue locations.
- **Boats and the High Seas** — ships sail the seas between continents: tillermen, holds, cannons, and pirates. The High Seas booster made seafaring a full system with ship combat and sea bosses.

## Era notes

Moongates and recall are launch-era systems. Runebooks, Sacred Journey, and the High Seas ship system each arrived with later expansions and redefined how players move.

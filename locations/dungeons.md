# Classic Dungeons

Britannia's classic dungeons are the original seven: Covetous, Deceit, Despise, Destard, Hythloth, Shame, and Wrong. Each has its own character, spawn tables, and reputation — and each has been reworked at least once across the game's publishes.

## What happens there

- **Despise** — ogre lords and earth elementals in the upper levels; later revamped with deeper, harder regions.
- **Destard** — the dragon dungeon: drakes, dragons, and the dragon's lair at its heart. A classic tamer and slayer destination.
- **Hythloth** — the deep daemon dungeon: daemons, balrons, and succubi in its lower levels; long the endgame of the original dungeon set.
- **Shame** — earth elementals and the route to deeper revamped levels added in later publishes.
- **Deceit** — liches and evil mages; the stealthy spellcaster's dungeon.
- **Wrong** — the prison dungeon of ogres and trolls near Britain; revamped with new regions in later eras.
- **Covetous** — harpies and undead on the coast; also significantly revamped.

Dungeon revamps in the 2010s-era publishes rebuilt several of these dungeons level by level, adding new spawns, bosses, and loot while keeping the original geography recognizable (community recollection on exact revamp timing — verify against publish notes).

## Era notes

The original seven date to launch-era UO. The dungeon revamp program of later publishes modernized Despise, Shame, Wrong, and Covetous in particular.

# Ter Mur and the Stygian Abyss

Ter Mur is the floating-island homeland of the gargoyles, and beneath it yawns the Stygian Abyss — the largest dungeon in Ultima Online. Together they were the centerpiece of the Stygian Abyss expansion and remain the home of imbuing, the gargoyle race, and some of the game's hardest encounters.

## What happens there

The **Royal City** of Ter Mur is the gargoyle capital: its centerpiece is the **Soul Forge** (and the Queen's Soul Forge with its success bonus), where [imbuing](../crafting/imbuing-system.md) happens. The city also hosts the gargoyle starter [quests](../quests/termur-quests.md) and the flying-mount stables.

Below the city lies the **Underworld**, a transitional dungeon area, and beneath that the **Stygian Abyss** proper — a sprawling multi-region dungeon with the Abyssal Infernal areas, the Tomb of Kings, and lairs for bosses including the Medusa, the Primeval Lich, Navrey Night-Eyes, and the Stygian Dragon (community recollection on the exact boss roster — verify against a current reference). The Abyss is where [peerless](../mechanics/peerless-encounters.md)-tier encounters and high-end imbuing ingredients live.

## Era notes

Introduced with Stygian Abyss (2009) alongside the gargoyle race and the imbuing system. It shifted endgame crafting and PvM toward Ter Mur for years after launch.

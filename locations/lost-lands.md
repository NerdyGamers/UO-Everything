# The Lost Lands

The Lost Lands are the second landmass of Britannia, a desert-and-jungle continent attached to the same world as the mainland cities. They arrived with The Second Age (1998) and have been the wild frontier of Britannia ever since.

## What happens there

The inhabited corners are **Delucia** (the fur-trader town) and **Papua** (a village settlement). The wilderness between them holds the **Terathan Keep** (home of the terathan warriors and avengers), ophidian camps, desert wildlife, and several [champion spawn](../mechanics/champion-spawns.md) sites that make the region a Felucca PvP hunting ground.

The **Hidden Valley** shelters unicorns and ki-rin for tamers willing to make the trip. The dungeon **Khaldun** lies within the Lost Lands as well. Because the Lost Lands share Britannia's facet rules, Trammel's are guarded and safe while Felucca's are contested — the same geography plays very differently on the two facets (see [Trammel and Felucca](trammel-felucca.md)).

## Era notes

One of the original expansions' landmasses (The Second Age, 1998). Champion spawns were layered onto its wilderness in later eras, turning it into a Felucca power-scroll hunting ground.

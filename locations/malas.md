# Malas

Malas is the Age of Shadows landmass: a dark, starlit continent built for housing, trade, and the Doom Gauntlet.

## How it works

Two cities dominate: **Luna**, the paladin city whose bank became the game's main trade hub, and **Umbra**, the necromancer city — holy warriors and necromancers staring at each other across the same continent.

Malas hosts the **Doom Gauntlet** (see [Artifacts](../items/artifacts.md)), the original artifact dungeon, and the **Labyrinth**, a hedge-maze dungeon (community recollection on details). The continent opened with vast tracts of placeable land, triggering the custom-housing land rush (see [Housing](../housing/README.md)).

Malas is a single facet — it was never mirrored like Britannia. Recall and travel work normally here (see [Travel](travel.md)).

## Era notes

Added with Age of Shadows (2003) alongside custom housing, the item-property system, and the Necromancy and Chivalry skills.
